## Land area description

This area is located within the Obabika River watershed. The values and uses in this area include remote tourism lodge, the large lake with a degraded natural lake trout population, and the significant canoe routes adjacent to Obabika River Waterway Park. Additional values include the limited public motorized access, the OFSC snowmobile trail (Sudbury-Temagami connection), and the existing and heritage portages.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Inland Ontario Lakes Designated for Lake Trout Management (2006) accessible in the Source of Direction portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The land use intent is to maintain the remote tourism and park related values, the quality canoe routes, and the backcountry recreational values associated with Obabika Lake while enhancing the lake trout fishery, and providing opportunities for forestry and mining related activities.Concerns include the effectiveness of Goulard gate in continuing to prevent public motorized access and the increased use of semi-remote lakes. Additional concerns include the demand for additional motorized access to the lake, the park-related values in north-end of the area requiring appropriate management, and the use of portages as ATV trails.The management area objectives are to prevent roads within 350-metres of park boundaries, to restrict public motorized access, to maintain existing canoe routes and retain the potential for new routes, to rehabilitate lake trout based fish populations, and to ensure limited access to Obabika Lake.The strategies include planning access and developing road use strategies to restrict public motorized access and minimize conflicts while also developing a use management strategy through the FMP process to confirm the most effective means of controlling access on Goulard Road. Additional strategies include fostering partnerships to resolve access issues, and applying viewscape management and Area of Concern guidelines to resource extraction activities. As part of the Crown Land Recreation Plan Temagami Integrated Planning process, a strategy was developed to prohibit further use of existing portage trails by ATVs within this management area.
