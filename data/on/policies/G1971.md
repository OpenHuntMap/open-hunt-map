## Land area description

This area is located within the Temagami River watershed. The values and uses of this area include the remote road access for tourism operations and local anglers and hunters.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Inland Ontario Lakes Designated for Lake Trout Management (2006) accessible in the Source of Direction portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The land use intent is to provide opportunities for forestry and mining related activities while mitigating impacts on backcountry recreation and remote tourism. Additionally, the intent is to maintain and enhance fish and wildlife habitats while retaining sugar maple, red oak, and yellow birch forests.The concerns include the potential for unplanned motorized access to Cross Lake and Wasaksina Lake as well as the unorganized Crown land camping on Hangstone Lake.The management area objectives include retaining tolerant hardwood stands within the management area as well as the remote tourism values of Hangstone Lake and other areas. The objectives also include retaining opportunities for remote commercial tourism.The strategies include managing access to minimize conflicts as well as restricting development of Crown land camping sites at access to Hangstone Lake. The strategies also include not permitting public road access to Cross Lake and Wasaksina Lake from this management area as well as applying viewscape and Area of Concern planning to extractive activities.

## Permitted uses — preface

Land use and resource management activities for this area are also governed by the policies of the Temagami Land Use Plan (TLUP) (1997). The policies from the TLUP are integrated into this area report. Refer to the TLUP for background information and additional details.
