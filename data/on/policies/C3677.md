## Land area description

Jog Lake Conservation Reserve is located 70 km north of Highway 11 between the towns of Hearst and Longlac. Jog Lake is a large, circular, bog-rimmed lake, which is contained entirely within this conservation reserve. This reserve contains excellent examples of boreal muskeg surrounding Jog Lake. This conservation reserve lies within Hill Site District 2E-1, an area that is characterized by muskeg plan underlined by clay.The key natural heritage values within the conservation reserve include the following: · A large, pristine provincially significant landscape of continuous patterned and raised peat plateau complex;· A radial patterned drainage flowing out from Jog Lake; and,· A deciduous swamp/forest located on a hill south of the Watistiguam River.Jog Lake conservation reserve was regulated in 1997.

## Land use intent

To protect natural heritage values, this area is afforded the legal protection as a conservation reserve under O.Reg. 315/07 made under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act. This area will be managed as a conservation reserve in accordance with the Conservation Reserves Policy (1997). All protected areas within the Far North of Ontario are subject to the provisions of the Far North Act 2010
