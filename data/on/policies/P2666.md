## Land area description

MacLeod Provincial Park (74 hectares) is located 10 kilometres east of Geraldton on Hwy 11. It was regulated in 1963.MacLeod Provincial Park is recreation class park. MacLeod Provincial Park contributes to the achievement of the recreation and tourism objectives established for the provincial parks system. In addition, it contributes to the representation of one historical theme and one theme segment.

## Land use intent

The intent is to manage this park as a recreation class provincial park, in accordance with MNR and Ontario Parks Policy.
