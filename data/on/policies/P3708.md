## Land area description

Landforms in the park include a large moraine and drumlin field. There is a sensitive speckled trout population of interest to sport fishing enthusiasts. Winisk River Provincial Park is an extensive waterway class park that stretches north from Winisk Lake to Polar Bear Provincial Park. The 390 km long park is part of a larger canoe route that extends to the Hudson Bay coast. The community of Webequie actively uses the park for traditional resource harvesting activities There are no visitor facilities. Anyone planning to visit this remote northern park should have prior wilderness and white-water experience. It is accessible by float plane only.Location: About 340 km north of Geraldton and some 500 km north of Thunder Bay. The park was first regulated in 1969. The boundary was amended in 1998.

## Land use intent

This waterway class park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act, 2006. All protected areas within the Far North of Ontario are subject to the provisions of the Far North Act 2010
