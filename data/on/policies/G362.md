## Land area description

This is the largest management area and occurs throughout the Bracebridge Area of Parry Sound District.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Within this area, resource management will be directed toward multiple use management. Standard management practices combined with the concept of sequential use will enable MNR to take full advantage of the potential of the natural resources.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Bracebridge District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
