## Land area description

This area encompasses 5,618 hectares of high quality, road accessible aggregate deposits. Municipal planning controls affect those areas within the Hearst Planning Area.

## Land use intent

The priority in this area is the protection and utilization of aggregate resources.All pits will be rehabilitated once extraction is completed. Other uses such as agriculture may be permitted if they do not conflict with aggregate operations or preclude the future utilization of aggregate resources.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Hearst District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
