## Land area description

Lake Panache angling and cottaging capabilities are fairly good. Crown land in the area is under sustainable forest licence (SFL) primarily to supply local mills.Recreation related activities in the area include small craft boating, canoeing, warm and cold water sport fishing, camping, cottaging and several commercial tourist establishments, including a marina. The land use pattern on the lakeshore has been dictated by access. The Whitefish Lake Indian Reserve dominates the northeastern section of Panache Lake. Several blocks of land have been issued under a sustainable forest licence to supply local mills.This area is on the edge of a large moose wintering area centered in Bevin and Halifax Townships and at the extreme western edge of the elk range, which extends all the way to Eighteen Mile Island and the French River Mouth. INCO Limited operates the Wabagishik generating station on the Vermilion River at Lome Falls. This plant provides supplementary hydro-electric power to meet industrial needs. This will continue to be a recognized use. The dam effectively maintains satisfactory water levels for fisheries and recreational purposes. Over the years, an obvious conflict has developed between cottagers and the quarrying operation on the south shore and the crushing operation on the north shore. Littering, the cutting of trees and vandalism have also resulted in conflicts between cottagers and day-use activities.A portion of this area is located within the Killarney Signature Site and Great Lakes Heritage Coast Signature Site, two of 9 such areas featured in the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999). Signature Sites are identified for their range of natural and recreational values and their potential to contribute to future recreation and tourism. A portion of this area is within the Killarney park addition and a portion is north of lake panache which is outside of the Killarney SSThis area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Lake Panache is a significant recreational lake in Sudbury District. Ministry program direction will ensure the protection of lake trout populations and habitat from development and use pressures. Both private and public recreation, aggregate extraction, mining, hydro-electric power generation, and forestry, will continue to dominate land uses in this area. Where conflicts arise between recreation and resource extraction activities, the impact on recreation values will be minimized, wherever possible.In those areas referred to as the Mineral, Industrial Mineral and Agricultural Reserves in the Sudbury Regional Official Plan, all other uses including urban, rural residential, forestry, wildlife management, etc. which do not preclude future extraction, may also be permitted. The mining rights to the islands in Lake Panache were withdrawn from staking under the Mining Act in 1926.Management direction will be complementary with the adjacent Killarny Lakeland and Headwaters Provincial Park.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Sudbury District Guidelines (1983).
