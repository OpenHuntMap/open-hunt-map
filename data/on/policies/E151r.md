## Land area description

The area was formerly within the McConnell Lakes Recreation Area. The area supports highquality recreation use (backcountry recreation, fishing, camping, hunting, etc.), and contains highquality forest resources. It includes lakes such as Boland, Serene, Sucker, Susy, LaSalle andReynolds. The Boland Lake campground is also included in the area. The area abuts the JockoRivers Waterway Park which is located to the south and west.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designatedlakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via theSource of Direction portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Manage this area to protect high quality recreation and tourism values, while managing the areafor its high quality forest and other resource values. The Boland Lake Area is to be managed as ageneral recreation area with resources production. Boland Lake has a campground with 36 sitesand a day-use area. Any planning of activities adjacent to any provincial park or conservationreserve will consider the management intent and direction of these protected areas. Managementof this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Land Use Strategy (1999).

## Permitted uses — preface

Recreation enhanced management areas have been applied to areas with high recreational useof significant recreation values for activities such as angling, hunting, motorized and pedestriantrail use, and canoeing. The enhanced management area can be used for a wide range ofrecreation values and management intents, ranging from areas where relatively substantialrecreation development is permitted, to areas providing low-density, high-quality recreation in anatural setting. Where the recreation enhanced management area has been identified to protectremote recreation values, all activities need to be carried out in such a way as to maintain orenhance the remote recreation qualities.
