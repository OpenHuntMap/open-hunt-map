## Land area description

This nature reserve class provincial park comprises part of a larger wetland complex, located 3 kilometres east of Stoco Lake in Ecodistrict 6E-9. The wetland is in a shallow depression between limestone plateaus at the head of a valley that served as a meltwater channel during early postglacial times. The park consists mainly of fen and cedar swamp communities, with a mosaic of sedge meadows, shrub thickets, marl ponds and islands of stunted cedars and larches. The park supports a rich assortment of both plants and animals, including several provincially or regionally rare species.Stoco Fen Provincial Nature Reserve was regulated as a provincial park in 1985. In 2014, 100 ha of land, purchased through a partnership between the Nature Conservancy of Canada and the Ministry of Natural Resources and Forestry, was added to the park through regulation. In 2017, an additional 150 ha of land donated by Quinte Conservation was added to the park. The addition of these lands to the park provides increased protection for park values, including regionally and provincially significant species, provincially significant wetland, and headwater areas.

## Land use intent

This provincial nature reserve was established to protect its provincially significant life science features.
