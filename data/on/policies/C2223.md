## Land area description

This area includes all islands in Onaman Lake, and portions of the northern shoreline. The site contains representative landform and vegetation types, including mixed forest types and wetlands on weakly broken ground moraine. The islands are important woodland caribou habitat.Onaman Lake is considered a tourism lake. The Onaman Lake Fisheries Management Plan provides policies that will result in a sustainable multiple use fishery.This area was regulated as a conservation reserve on June 23, 2000.This area is subject to the Range Management Policy in Support of Woodland Caribou Conservation and Recovery (2014). The Range Management Policy is accessible via the “Source of Direction.”

## Land use intent

Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Land Use Strategy (1999).Onaman Lake is also managed under the Onaman Lake Fisheries Management Plan, 1999-2008.

## Permitted uses — preface

Uses and management activities not listed in the following table are governed by existing conservation reserve policy and the Onaman Lake Conservation Reserve Statement of Conservation Interest (July, 2003). Any new uses, and commercial activities associated with conservation reserves, will be considered on a case by case basis, and they must pass a test of compatibility to be acceptable. Compatibility is normally determined through a planning process.The following tables aim to present current information. However, with the passage of new legislation, updates to provincial policy and the development of protected areas management direction documents, certain content elements may be out of date.

## Addendum

Management of this conservation reserve is carried out under the provisions of the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act and within the context of conservation reserve policy.In addition to the provincial and regional policies, the following local policies will apply to the management of Onaman Lake:When feasible, permanent forest access roads will be located a minimum of 1.6 kilometres from the lake.
