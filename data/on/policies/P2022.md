## Land area description

Windy Lake Provincial Park is located on Highway 144, 53 kilometres north of Sudbury. Located on the north shore of Windy Lake which includes two long sand beaches bordered by a flat plain that rises up to a sand plateau.Camping and day-use activities such as swimming, boating, hiking and fishing, sailing, windsurfing, water-skiing and canoeing occur in the park. The park and surrounding area have a network of cross-country ski trails in the winter.Windy Lake Provincial Park was regulated as a provincial park in 1959.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Windy Lake Provincial Park will be managed according to Provincial Park policy and Park Management Plan (1988). Management of the park will be to provide camping and day-use opportunities while protecting the park environment and locally significant features.
