## Land area description

The Nungesser Lake North Enhanced Management Area includes the central portion ofNungesser Lake and lands extending north. This area complements the protection intent of theadjoining Pringle Lake Dedicated Protected Area and the Nungesser River Cultural WaterwayDedicated Protected Area. The Nungesser and Pringle Lake areas exhibit earth science featuresof ‘fans’ or ‘outwash deltas’ associated with the Lac Seul Moraine, and are characterized bynotable natural and cultural values. Numerous woodland caribou calving islands are documented.First Nation people conduct various traditional activities for livelihood and cultural purposes.Development in the area includes several mining claims. Management boundaries relevant to thearea include bear management areas, baitfish blocks, traplines, and a wildlife management unit.

## Land use intent

The intent is to sustain the high quality fish and wildlife resources of Nungesser Lake andsurrounding area. The area is also dedicated to new opportunities eco-cultural tourism andrecreation while sustaining the cultural landscape, and provides for mineral sector opportunities.The Nungesser Lake North Enhanced Management Area has also been identified to protectremote recreation and tourism interests and complement protection interests of the adjacentPringle Lake Dedicated Protected Area, including woodland caribou conservation. Forestry willnot be pursued as an opportunity in this area.

## Permitted uses — preface

Management will also be governed by the Land Use Strategy for the Whitefeather Forest andAdjacent Areas and MNR policies.All activities need to be carried out so that the fish and wildlife resources of this area aremaintained or enhanced.
