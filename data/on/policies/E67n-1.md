## Land area description

These Enhanced Management Areas have been established to protect the headwaters of Algonquin Provincial Park. These areas all contain high value timber resources and contain a variety of recreational landscapes. The Ballantyne Township portion of this Enhanced Management Area also has a number of eco-adventure tourism operations providing backcountry recreation opportunities and canoe access to Algonquin Park.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

These areas have been designated to protect the water quality of rivers flowing into Algonquin Provincial Park. Most land use activities and resource management practices permitted within the General Use Area are permitted within this Enhanced Management area, as these activities and practices do not generally have an adverse impact on watershed water quality. It is not the intent of this policy to manage these lands as potential future additions to Algonquin Provincial Park.All land use activities and resource management practices must be conducted in a manner that protects the water quality of these watersheds. In particular:· with the implementation of the forest management planning process and applicable forest management planning guidelines and practices, forest management is compatible with the protection of water quality;· existing and improved guidelines for access road planning and construction and aggregate extraction will be implemented to protect water quality;· mineral exploration and development is permitted within this designation. However, it is recognized that controls and monitoring will need to be applied to mineral exploration and development activities within this Enhanced Management Area.Watershed boundaries for this Enhanced Management Area have been refined through more precise mapping. Further planning will identify watershed boundaries for lower order watercourses flowing into the Park and will apply the above provisions.In addition to managing these areas through the above provisions, these areas will also be managed for the provision of backcountry recreation and tourism opportunities.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Land Use Strategy (1999) and the Bracebridge District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
