## Land area description

This area lies between White Otter Lake and Finlayson Lake, north of the Town of Atikokan. The boundaries of the area are the Bending Lake Highway to the south and west and the Hardtack Lake road system to the east. Portions of the Crossroute Forest and Sapawe Forest comprise this area.Present uses include resource extraction, recreation and commercial tourist development. There are cottages, commercial outpost camps and main lodges in the area. Two hydro line corridors cross the area. Development is sparse.Existing activities include logging, mineral exploration, trapping, commercial bait fish operations, aggregate extraction, recreation and tourism. Recreational use of the area by both residents and nonresidents is high for canoeing, angling and hunting.The Atomic Energy of Canada Limited drill exploration site at Forsberg Lake is of concern to some people of the Atikokan area.The following are designated as tourism lakes: Sedgewick, Rutter, Pine, North of Sedgewick, Rollingstone, Irene, Little Gull, Doan, Gamble, Riverview, Fire Island, Pyramid, Pekagoning, Clearwater West, Bow, East of Ricemere, Roman, South of Bow, Below Bow, Mae, Eye and Mount lakes.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Logging operations will be of primary importance in this area, the maintenance of the lake trout, moose and deer populations, and the enhancement of associated tourism and recreational opportunities will also be a priority. The area is partly in the Crossroute Forest, and partly in the Sapawe Forest.Areas of Concern will be identified to ensure the protection of the lake trout fishery and associated tourism opportunities. Road access to the area will be restricted to ensure a low level of general public use.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Atikokan District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
