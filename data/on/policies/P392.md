## Land area description

Regulated in 1985, Opeongo River Provincial Park is classified as a Waterway Park. It is regulated under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act. Opeongo River’s mixed hardwood forest shorelines are dominated by white pine, sugar maple, poplar, and white birch. Wet meadows and shoreline habitats characteristic of Madawaska’s sand plan sand plain are situated near the River’s confluence with the Madawaska River.Non operating park administrated by Ontario Parks.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Originally identified in the 1983 Bancroft District Land Use Guidelines as a candidate Provincial Park.Established in 1985 as a Provincial Park, the purpose of the Park is to protect it’s natural and cultural values and recreational character. Sport hunting is permitted by regulation under the Fish & Wildlife Conservation Act.The Park contains four hunt camps (LUPs) and one trapping cabin.
