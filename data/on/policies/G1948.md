## Land area description

These are several large deposits of high quality crushable mineral aggregate, (predominantly gravel) along the Highway 11 corridor. No private lands are included.

## Land use intent

The primary use of these areas is the extraction of mineral aggregate. Other sequential uses may occur when the resource has been removed. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in North Bay District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
