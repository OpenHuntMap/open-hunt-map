## Land area description

This area is located in the northern section of Lake of the Woods. This area is the most heavily cottaged area of the lake and as a result, receives the majority, of the lake's recreational use. This area includes the Clearwater and Echo Bay areas of Lake of the Woods and development is controlled through the Clearwater Bay Restricted Area Order. Other resource use such as forestry and mining in the area is very limited although sport and commercial fishing are actively pursued. There are 10 main base lodges in this area. The Rat Portage and Washagamis Indian Reserves are located in this area.Portions of the Lake of the Woods Islands Conservation Reserve are located in this part of the Lake. The Vernon Nature Area, situated on patented Crown land, is also located within this area. The Vernon Nature Area is managed for low-impact public recreational and educational self-guided use through the Vernon Nature Area Management Plan. Lake of the Woods is a significant tourism lake.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

General recreation will be the dominant use in this area. Crown land recreation will be encouraged and facilities may be developed in response to demonstrated need. All other resource uses will be required to recognize and accommodate the recreational role of the area in any future development proposals. The unique and valuable character of Lake of the Woods will be maintained.
