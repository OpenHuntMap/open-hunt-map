## Land area description

Wasaga Beach Provincial Park is situated entirely within the Town of Wasaga Beach. The park is located 38 km north of Barrie and approximately 133 km north of Toronto. This 14-kilometre sandy beach on Georgian Bay attracts thousands of day users every summer. The park protects a provincially significant system of parabolic dunes and associated features. Endangered piping plovers have recently successfully nested on the beach. Wasaga beach Provincial Park was regulated in 1959.

## Land use intent

This recreational class park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act.
