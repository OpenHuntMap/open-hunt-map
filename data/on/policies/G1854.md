## Land area description

The area consists of a chain of lakes from Rat Lake in the north to Beaverhouse Lake in the south. Access to the area is by a road in Arnold Township. There is also an existing road through the Township of Gauthier to the Misema River. Gauthier Township and McVittie Township are both within municipal areas. There is a small Indian settlement on Misema Lake. Forest activity is ongoing within and adjacent to this area. Mineral potential is high to medium. The area is a popular recreation area for local residents, particularly for fishing and cottaging. The Howard-Misema Lake chain is a designated canoe route. Approximately 60 cottage lots are located in the area. There are two cultural/historic sites in the area. A waterfowl production site is located on Kennedy Lake.

## Land use intent

The intent for this area is to provide for water based recreation activities. In shoreline areas recreation will have priority. Resource extraction will have priority in non-shoreline areas. Development proposals and resource extraction plans will give consideration to effects on general recreation activities and fisheries and wildlife habitat. Urban development allowed. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Kirkland Lake District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
