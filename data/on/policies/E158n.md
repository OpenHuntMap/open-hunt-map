## Land area description

The McConnell Moraine is a seemingly random jumble of sand and gravel deposits that lie underthe forested hills of this area, is part of an interlobate moraine that extended south to Huntsvilleand northeast into Quebec. This spectacular collection of glacial features is part of the mostextensive landscape of its kind in eastern Canada. This site of earth science significance is in sitedistrict 5E-6. The section includes the area between Threetrails, Wyse and Little McDougal lakes.Wyse and Little McDougal lakes are presently stocked with cold water fish species and Threetailslake with lake trout. There is a 42 camp site campground and access point on the south shore ofLittle McDougal Lake operated since 2003 by private contractors on behalf of MNR .This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designatedlakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via theSource of Direction portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The protection of the earth science values will receive priority. No aggregate extraction ispermitted. However, existing quarry operations on leased claims or permits will be permitted tocontinue. Any proposed planning on activities adjacent to any conservation reserve will considerthe management intent and direction of these protected areas. MNR is responsible for themanagement of Crown land and natural resources in unorganized townships. Management of thisarea is also governed by the general policies contained in the Land Use Strategy (1999).
