## Hunting

Not addressed in this policy.

Provincial season, licence, tag and firearm rules still apply, as do municipal discharge by-laws. Verify before hunting.

## Land area description

This area covers the urban areas of the District such as Spanish, Massey, Webbwood, McKerrow, Nairn, Espanola, Little Current, Gore Bay and Manitowaning. The main Ministry interest in these areas revolves around the regulation of inappropriate development on hazardous lands (i.e. Lake Huron's flood plain).A portion of this area is located within the Great Lakes Heritage Coast Signature Site. This area includes multiple urban areas and they are not in the Signature Site. (e.g. Espanola, Webbwood, McKerrow, etc).

## Land use intent

MNR will support normal urban uses to develop in this area. Disposition of Crown land will occur through consultation with the local municipal council and, normally, only in accordance with official plan policies.Crown Land Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Espanola District Land Use Guidelines (1983).

## Addendum

Permitted uses will be determined by the various municipalities and where appropriate through approved planning documents.
