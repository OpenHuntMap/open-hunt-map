## Land area description

This area covers the two important cottaging and tourist areas of the North Channel known as Bay of Islands and McGregor Bay. Bay of Islands extends from Whitefish Falls west to Eastern Island, while McGregor Bay extends from the mainland south to and including from Point. The eastern portion of McGregor Bay and Iroquois Bay abuts Killarney Coast and Islands Provincial Park . The major existing uses are recreational oriented. They include cottaging, marinas, commercial tourist establishments and small service outlets. Iroquois Bay supports one of the last two remaining populations of native Lake Huron lake trout.This area is located within the Great Lakes Heritage Coast Signature Site, one of 9 such areas featured in the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999). Signature Sites are identified for their range of natural and recreational values and their potential to contribute to future recreation and tourism.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Ministry management efforts will be primarily directed towards maintaining the status quo in this area - various forms of public and private recreational uses will be supported. Management activities will be compatible with Killarney Coast and Islands Provincial Park. Onshore mining development may expand underground, outward under Lake Huron, subject to existing international agreements.Specific management practices may be applied to Iroquois Bay to preserve and rehabilitate its native lake trout population.
