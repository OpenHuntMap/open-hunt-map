## Land area description

Important winter deer habitat is situated in the ten geographic townships centered around the Village of Iron Bridge. Scattered throughout this area are numerous sites of varying size which offer deer shelter, food sources and open areas for travel, grazing and sunning. Other land uses in the area include forestry, recreation, cottaging and rural residential development. Hydro generating stations are located at Red Rock Lake and Tunnel Lake.Coastal portions of this area are located within the Great Lakes Heritage Coast Signature Site, one of 9 such areas featured in the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999). Signature Sites are identified for their range of natural and recreational values and their potential to contribute to future recreation and tourism.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

MNR will manage Crown lands and will attempt to influence the use of private lands in a manner which will benefit the wintering deer populations. Forest management practices will play a large part in maintaining or improving the habitat. The existing pattern of land use will be preserved, but new development and disposition of Crown land will consider the impact on deer populations.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Blind River District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
