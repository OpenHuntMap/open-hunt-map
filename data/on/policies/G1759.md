## Land area description

This critical aggregate area includes several high quality aggregate areas. Gravel extraction operations of the District are located in these areas. Most of these critical aggregate lands are Crown; however, significant amounts of private land are encountered in Glackmeyer, Lamarche, Hanna, Fournier and Clute Townships. Existing structural developments here are those associated with agricultural, cottaging and rural residential uses. In the Smooth Rock Falls and Cochrane areas, there is a conflict between aggregate extraction and cottaging on some lakes, most notably Departure and Palangio Lakes. The potential also exists for conflicts on other lakes such as Indian, Sand and Rawcourt. The forest industry can potentially conflict with critical aggregate areas. Aggregate deposits are often among the more productive sites for regeneration. However, due to the need for gravel, regeneration may have to be delayed until the gravel has been removed.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.This area is subject to the Range Management Policy in Support of Woodland Caribou Conservation and Recovery (2014). The Range Management Policy is accessible via the “Source of Direction.”

## Land use intent

The priority for these areas is the protection and utilization of the aggregate resource. Aggregate extraction may be subject to municipal planning controls in Haggart, Glackmeyer and Lamarche Townships. Further structural development is not encouraged in these areas. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Cochrane District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
