## Land area description

This area includes the community of Nestor Falls, now part of the Township of Sioux Narrows-Nestor Falls. It is developed as a tourist service centre and eight lodges are located in the area. There are very few other land uses in the area although several cottages are located on Lake of the Woods and Kakagi Lake.Water Management Areas have been removed from this Policy Report area as revisions, to more accurately reflect First Nation reserve lands affected by flooding.

## Land use intent

The area will be managed to protect the existing tourist industry. Expansion of existing developments will be considered where required, and where capability exists. The area is designated for mixed use where the prime uses will be commercial tourist development facilities and permanent residences.
