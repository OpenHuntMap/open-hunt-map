## Land area description

Area 18 includes two distinct areas of the District. The first area includes parts of Poncet and Plourde Townships, locally known as Plaunt's Cut, and is the site of 4 tourism lakes and part of the head water area for the West Sables River. The second area includes parts of Hotte and Ouellette Townships and encompasses several lakes.Both parts of area 18 experience and are capable of supporting a higher concentration of recreational use than the surrounding landscape. The capability for wood fibre production is also fairly high in these areas and large scale logging operations have occurred in the past.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

There will be two management priorities in area 18. They are the provision of a quality, road accessible recreational landscape, and the maintenance of a quality wood production area. Most other natural resource uses will also be permitted; in particular efforts will be made to maintain fish and wildlife population levels. Lake trout lakes will be protected from development and use pressures. The remote quality of existing tourism lakes will be maintained wherever possible.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Espanola District Guidelines (1983)

## Permitted uses — preface

In addition to provincial and regional policies, the following local policies will apply to the management of Natural lake trout lakes:­ no additional access will be permitted­ 120 metre Area of Concern will be applied­ forest management activities may be permitted within the Area of Concern subject to concerns for erosion/sedimentation and the protection of lake trout habitat.
