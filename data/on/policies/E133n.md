## Land area description

The McConnell Moraine is a seemingly random jumble of sand and gravel deposits that lie under the forestedhills of this area, is part of an interlobate moraine that extended south to Huntsville and northeast into Quebec. This spectacular collection of glacial features is part of the most extensive landscape of its kind in eastern Canada. This site of earth science significance is in site district 5E-6. The section is located south of Green Lake and abuts the northeast side of the Spring/Cut Lake Esker Conservation Reserve. Sure Catch Lake sustains one of the rare lacustrine brook trout populations..This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the 'Source of Direction' portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The protection of the earth science values will receive priority. Aggregate extraction will be controlled so as to not significantly affect the earth science values and cold water fisheries. Any proposed planning on activities adjacent to any conservation reserve will consider the management intent and direction of these protected areas. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Ontario's Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999).
