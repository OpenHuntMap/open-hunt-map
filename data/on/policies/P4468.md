Provincial season, licence, tag and firearm rules still apply, as do municipal discharge by-laws. Verify before hunting.

## Land area description

Located at Mono Centre and approximately 12km southeast of Primrose, Town of Mono, Dufferin County. This park is part of the Niagara Escarpment Parks System, and the Niagara Escarpment Biosphere Reserve. The park is accessible via the Bruce Trail.The park was regulated in 1985.

## Land use intent

This natural environment class park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act.
