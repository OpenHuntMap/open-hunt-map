## Land area description

Pipestone River Provincial Park is located northwest of the town of Pickle Lake in northwestern Ontario north. The park is 97, 375 hectares and the boundary generally encompasses a 200-metre strip on both sides the 315 kilometre waterway.The park contains representative features of ecoregions 2W and 3S. Significant glacial features include a fluted ground moraine, DeGeer moraines, Big Beaverhouse moraine and Glacial Lake Agassiz sands and silts. The area's boreal forests are home to moose, deer, black bear, wolf, marten, fox, lynx,and otter.The park overlays a portion of the traditional territories of Slate Falls, Cat Lake, North Caribou Lake, Kingfisher, Mishkeekogamang and Wunnumin Lake First Nations. Traditional activities on the land continue to be a central part of the lifestyle for residents of all these First Nations. Park management does not supercede any Aboriginal or treaty rights that may exist, or other legal obligations. In addition, the consideration and awarding of new resource-based tourism proposals in the park will reflect Ontario government policies and practices that are intended to promote Aboriginal self-reliance and economic development.There are no visitors' facilities. The park is accessible by float plane only. Wilderness and whitewater skills are requiredLocation: About 200 km north of Sioux Lookout. The park was regulated in 1989.

## Land use intent

This waterway class park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act, 2006.
