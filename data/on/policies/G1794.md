## Land area description

This area includes the organized Municipality of Chapleau and the airport. Most of Chapleau's 13.5 square kilometres, plus the additional 3.2 square kilometres at the airport site are privately owned. Half of the total land base of the town is not cleared or serviced for any sort of urban-type land uses. Land uses are governed by the municipality's official plan and zoning by-laws.

## Land use intent

The intent in this area is to continue to support municipal development. On those lands which are not occupied with urban-type uses, MNR will encourage a mix of resource oriented programs, particularly those which may enhance the urban environment (e.g. industrial and recreational). Urban and rural residential development will not be permitted adjacent to the airport. Land use development within this area will be guided by the official plan for the Municipality of Chapleau. Structural development in flood plain areas will be discouraged.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Chapleau District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
