## Land area description

McCallum Peninsula/Thistle Township Enhanced Management Area is made up of geographically separated areas managed as limited access areas. While not truly roadless areas, access within this enhanced management area is limited. This provides important benefits to tourist operations in the Marten River area and also allow extensive recreational experiences to be enjoyed in a limited access setting in most cases. Existing roads and trails have been left untended. Separated by the Temagami River Provincial Park (P139), these areas are located in the townships of McCollum and Thistle; townships located also in the West Nipissing Planning Area. This site also lies adjacent to Red Cedar Lake, a lake very important to the tourist industry. Forest management is also important. The entire area is unorganized.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Consistent with Enhanced Management remote access road policies continue to implement existing limited access policies in MNR North Bay District Land Use Guidelines. This area will be managed under the objective of maintaining the remote character provided by the areas of limited access. Providing existing road access is not enhanced, traditional recreational and resource uses (e.g. forest management) are permitted. Land Use Direction and resource management activities within this area are to be compatible with protecting recreational and tourism values as well as with protecting the recreational values of the proposed Temagami River Provincial Park. Special emphasis in management will be around the planning of roads.A portion of this area is subject to First Nation land claims negotiations, this Enhanced Management Area designation will not hinder or affect that negotiation process. These lands will not be re-opened for staking and will not be included in the forestry landbase until this matter is addressed. MNR is responsible for the management of Crown land and natural resources in unorganized townships.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Land Use Strategy (1999).

## Permitted uses — preface

Remote access enhanced management areas are intended to maintain the remote character of selected areas. Typically, these are relatively large areas which provide the public and tourism operators with high-quality remote recreational experiences including hunting, fishing, canoeing, and camping. Given the large size, remoteness, and relative absence of roads, these areas will play a significant role in protecting wilderness values outside the parks and protected areas system.
