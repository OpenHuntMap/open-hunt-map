## Land area description

Mikisew Provincial Park is represented by Middle Precambrian medisediments in the Ontario Gneiss Segment of the Grenville Geological Province. The topography of the park is that of a relatively flat, boulder-free sand plain which includes three relict shorelines and few Precambrian bedrock outcrops.The Park is based on the eastern edge of the Georgian Bay Forest Section of the Great Lakes - St. Lawrence Forest Region in Site District 5E-8. The western portion of the park is covered by early succession upland forest. Wetland areas occupy bedrock depressions with aquatic thicket swamps and beaver ponds.Mikisew Provincial Park was regulated as a provincial park in 1964.

## Land use intent

Mikisew Provincial Park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act. The boundary of the park is delineated by Ontario Regulation No. 161/64, Schedule 54.
