## Land area description

Peter's Woods Provincial Park is located along the eastern edge of the Oak Ridges Moraine, south of Rice Lake. The park protects older growth forest, tallgrass prairie and remnant oak savannah habitats. The savanna habitats are globally rare, and the site supports some of the best tallgrass prairie and oak savannas in all of Ontario. Peter’s Woods Provincial Park was established in 1976.

## Land use intent

Peter’s Woods will be managed as a nature reserve class park according to provincial park policy and approved park management plan (2009). Management of the park will protect its natural features.
