## Land area description

Kenny Forest Provincial Park is located 55 kilometres north of the City of North Bay in Kenny and Gladman Townships. This natural environment class park encompasses 2,200 hectares and is entirely within the Nipissing Crown Game Preserve. It is also located within the Temagami Anishanabai land claim area.Kenny Forest Provincial Park is a non-operating park and contains a diversity of wet and dry shoreline communities, interior upland forests with marshes, fens and peatlands, shoreline lowland forests and swamps.Kenny Forest Provincial Park was regulated as a provincial park in 1994.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Kenny Forest Provincial Park will be managed according to Provincial Park policy and Interim Management Statement (1996). The management of the park will be to provide recreation opportunities such as hiking, bird watching, water access camping, wildlife viewing and fishing while protecting the parks natural and cultural values.

## Permitted uses — preface

Management of this provincial park is carried out in accordance with Ontario Provincial Parks: Planning and Management Policies (1992), the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999) and the 1996 Interim Management Statement. The Interim Management Statement provides direction to ensure custodial management of park resources until such time as a park management planning process is conducted, which includes public review of proposals. At that time, significant decisions regarding resource stewardship, operations, development and permitted uses are made.
