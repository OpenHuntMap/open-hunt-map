## Land area description

This area is predominantly a contiguous parcel of land adjacent to the existing Pays Plat First Nation Reserve lands, along the northeastern shore of Lake Superior. Pays Plat First Nation has been engaged in the Addition to Reserve process under the Land and Larger Land Base initiative. Ontario’s contribution towards this initiative includes the disposition (transfer of administration and control) of Crown lands to Canada. A Holding Category General Use Area (GUA) is being used in Crown land use planning for the interim period prior to the transfer occurring. This holding category includes 1669 ha of land from G2688 The Interior GUA and 32 ha of land from E2233g Lake Superior Shoreline EMA.

## Land use intent

The area in the Holding Category General Use Area will be managed according to the pre-existing land use direction subject to the Final Agreement between Pays Plat First Nation and Her Majesty the Queen in Right of Canada and Her Majesty the Queen in Right of Ontario (April 2018), until such a time that the transfer of lands has been completed. The overarching intent of the area will be to secure protection of these lands from resource development. In order for these lands to be accepted by Canada and then transferred to the Pays Plat First Nation, these lands are to be free and clear of encumbrances and environmental liability. When the lands are transferred, this policy report no longer applies to the lands and will be removed from the Crown Land Use Policy Atlas.Pays Plat First Nation- Addition to Reserve General Use Area is located in an area in which one or more First Nations have asserted Aboriginal rights and title, including an unextinguished Aboriginal right to exclusive use and occupancy of the land. The First Nations’ claims are the subject of litigation and negotiations. Dispositions in Pays Plat First Nation- Addition to Reserve General Use Area may be subject to heightened Crown consultation and accommodation obligations.This may mean a greater degree of engagement with First Nation communities asserting rights and title prior to a decision regarding potential authorizations or dispositions of public lands or resources. For a map of areas affected by these claims, please go to the Notice of Caution on the Mining Lands Administration System (MLAS) at https://www.geologyontario.mndm.gov.on.ca/mines/lands/withreop/notices_e.html .

## Permitted uses — preface

The areas being re-designated to the Holding Category retain the permitted uses of the originating land use areas (ie G2688 The Interior GUA and E2233g Lake Superior Shoreline EMA), unless revised by the agreement, Final Agreement between Pays Plat First Nation and Her Majesty the Queen in Right of Canada and Her Majesty the Queen in Right of Ontario (April, 2018). This agreement must be referred to when land use or resource management decisions are proposed. Dispositions require careful consideration relative to the agreement.
