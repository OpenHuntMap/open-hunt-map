## Land area description

Kirkpatrick Lake South and Northeast Enhanced Management Area is located approximately 40 km north of Blind River in Albanel, Jackson, Kamichisitit, Le Caron, Nouvel, Poulin, Sayer, Slievert and Varley Townships. This Enhanced Management Area is surrounded by a variety of different land use designations. Little White River Provincial Park (P261) bisects the site, creating two distinct sections to the Enhanced Management Area: Kirkpatrick Lake South and Kirkpatrick Lake Northeast. Both sections of the site border Kirkpatrick Lake Northwest Enhanced Management Area (E240a). As well, Kirkpatrick Lake South surrounds Endikai Lake Perched Delta Enhanced Management Area (E249n). Kirkpatrick Lake South and Northeast Enhanced Management Area contains a number of high quality lake trout lakes which are managed through access restrictions and extended closed seasons. Some fly-in remote operations also exist which contribute to the high quality fishing experience. Access to the southern section of the Enhanced Management Area is restricted to snowmobiles, all terrain vehicles (ATVs), and non-motorized means of transportation. Motorized access is permitted in the northern section of the Enhanced Management Area; however, access to designated tourism lakes is prohibited. This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The intent of this area is to encourage remote and semi-remote tourism and recreation while providing for resource sector activities. This land use intent is consistent with the enhanced management area policies for recreation category enhanced management areas and the remote access policies contained in the MNR Blind River District Land Use Guidelines (1983). This includes the continued use of existing vehicular access controls on Crown land. This is an important resource sector (forestry, mining) area. Land use direction and resource management activities within this area will be compatible with protecting the natural and recreational values of the adjacent Little White River Provincial Park, Kirkpatrick Lake Northwest Enhanced Management Area and Endikai Lake Perched Delta Enhanced Management Area.

## Permitted uses — preface

Recreation enhanced management areas are areas of significant recreation value for activities such as angling, hunting, motorized and pedestrian trail use, and canoeing. This recreation enhanced management area has been identified to protect remote and semi-remote tourism and recreation values, while providing for resource sector activities. All activities need to be carried out in such a way as to maintain or enhance the remote and semi-remote recreation qualities. Any new uses or commercial activities associated with this Enhanced Management Area will be considered on a case-by-case basis and may be subject to the Class Environmental Assessment for MNR Resource Stewardship and Facility Development Projects (2003).
