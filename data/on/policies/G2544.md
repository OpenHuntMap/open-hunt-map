## Land area description

This area is located in the central portion of Dryden District. Contained within its boundaries are the Townships of Revell, Hyndman and parts of Hodgson, Bradshaw and Osaquan. The area is within Wabigoon Forest and the Dryden Forest. In addition, there are two tourist lodges, six outpost camps, one private recreation camp, three remote cottages and a number of private cottages within the area. Canada Land Inventory surveys indicate that this area contains some Class 2-4 agricultural land. Presently none of this land is under agricultural production. A band of moderate to high mineral potential stretches through the area. There are two heronies and three geological sensitive areas within the Bending Lake Area.Bending Lake Highway 622 traverses south from Highway 17 along the western edge of Fisheries Management Zone #21 (Ignace District Fisheries Management Plan) and the G2544 Bending Lake General Use Area. At it's closest point Highway 622 is within 600 metres of Bending Lake.The following are designated as tourism lakes: Bending, Osprey, Stormy, Kawashegamuk, Sandbar.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The purpose of this area will be to allow timber harvesting while maintaining commercial recreation facilities. Mining developments will also be encouraged. In order to protect the agricultural area, no disposition for permanent developments on Class 1 to 4 lands will occur. No future expansion of cottaging or tourism facilities will be allowed on Crown land.Bending Lake will continue to be managed with the intent of limiting road access. This will be accomplished through use limitations such as road restrictions or by artificial means as directed by the Ignace Fisheries Management Plan. This direction is also consistent with the definition of Semi-Remote Tourism in the Resource-Based Tourism Policy, Information Bulletin #1.Sensitive areas noted above will be recognized and protected in resource management plans.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Ignace District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
