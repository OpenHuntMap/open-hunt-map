## Land area description

This area comprises Kettle Lakes Provincial Park, a recreation class park. The park has a total area of 1,261 hectares and is situated 40 kilometres east of Timmins in German Township within the City of Timmins.Kettle Lakes Provincial Park was regulated as a provincial park in 1957.

## Land use intent

Kettle Lakes Provincial Park will be managed according to Provincial Park policy and Park Management Plan (1980).
