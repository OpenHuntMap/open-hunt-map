## Land area description

This area is accessible by a few gravel roads. Structural development includes cottages, hunt and fish camps, waste disposal site, commercial resorts, outpost camps and access points. The area lies within three separate licensed Management Units. There is no significant aggregate potential, but there is some high mineral potential. Crown land recreational use is moderate to high. The area contains 7 tourism lakes.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Public recreation and commercial tourism will be priorities in the area. Resource extraction will also continue to be important in non-shoreline areas.All activities will take into consideration the eagle nest on Arbeesee Lake and the heron rookery on Lake # 14 in Carter Township. In addition, resource management plans will contain guidelines to protect recreational values, tourism values and significant fish and wildlife habitat. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Gogama District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
