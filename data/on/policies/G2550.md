## Land area description

This area is located east of Highway 71 and includes Atikwa, Caviar and Kakagi Lakes. The area forms a major component of the Kenora Forest and is a significant recreational tourist area. There are presently 15 lodges and 3 outposts located in the zone. Road access into this area is restricted to resource management activities via three roads: Maybrun, Cameron, Tri-lake. The Crow Lake Indian Reserve No. 35-D is located in the area. Mineral potential in the vicinity of Atikwa Lake is considered to be relatively high and in the past, the Maybrun Mine which produced nickel and molybdenum was located in the area. The area forms part of Township of Sioux Narrows-Nestor Falls.The following are designated as tourism lakes: Atikwa, Cameron, Caviar, Denmark, Populus and Rowan.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.Water Management Areas have been removed from this Policy Report area as revisions, to more accurately reflect First Nation reserve lands affected by flooding.

## Land use intent

This area will be managed for resource extraction and commercial tourism in a manner which recognizes the importance of sport fishing and the lake trout environment. Crown land recreation is also important.The area will generally be one of low density development with an intent to preserve and protect the present remote character. In recognition of the particular significance of Atikwa, Kakagi and Rowan Lakes as recreational resources, any proposed resource activities directed towards these areas will be required to modify and adjust plans to ensure any perceived impacts are minimized. In addition, the unique and remote habitat represented by many small lakes in this area will be protected.
