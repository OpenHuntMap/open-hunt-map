## Land area description

This area encompasses five high quality agricultural areas. Found within the areas are two archaeological sites, one historical trading post site, 190 cottages, one youth camp and one tent and trailer park. The majority of the area is situated in a medium to high mineral potential zone. There are two official and seven unofficial access points in the areas. All except the agricultural pocket in Shaw Township are accessed via Highway 101. Approximately 70 percent of the active farms are found within the Township of Mountjoy.

## Land use intent

The priority in this area is the protection of agricultural lands and the continued development of agricultural activities and associated infrastructure.In co-operation with the Ministry of Agriculture and Food, MNR will encourage the use of Class 3 and 4 agricultural lands for agricultural purposes. A11 development in the area is subject to the official plan and by-laws of either the City of Timmins or Black River-Matheson. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Timmins District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
