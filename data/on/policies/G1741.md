## Land area description

This area includes five warm water lakes, (Saganash, Keenoa, Esmee, Shack and Pratt) located approximately 50 kilometres south of the Town of Kapuskasing. Access to the area is provided by the Chain of Lakes road and/or Swanson Road. At present, there is road access only to Keenoa Lake. Remote cottaging and low intensity uses such as hunting, fishing and camping are the major recreational activities within this area. Development includes remote cottages, recreation camps, commercial outpost camps, one access point on Keenoa Lake where camping is permitted, trailer park, one control dam and aggregate pits.

## Land use intent

The primary use in this area will be recreation. A mix of public recreation, cottaging and commercial tourism will be encouraged. Road access will not be developed to Saganash Lake. Forest management and mineral exploration will also be priorities outside of shoreline areas. Modified forest management will be practiced in shoreline areas to protect the recreational environment and significant fish and wildlife habitats. All activities will ensure protective measures for significant natural and cultural features.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Kapuskasing District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
