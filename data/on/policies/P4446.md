## Land area description

Frontenac Provincial Park was established in 1974. Located within the County of Frontenac, on the Frontenac Axis, the park has the rugged character of the Canadian Shield. It includes diverse and significant natural, cultural and recreational resources and is valued for its threshold wilderness experience. The habitat and species diversity of the park is considered exceptional within the ecodistrict and is home to a number of species at risk. The park is a popular destination for interior camping, canoeing and kayaking, hiking and fishing.

## Land use intent

This natural environment class park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act.
