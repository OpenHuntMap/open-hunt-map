## Land area description

This recommended conservation reserve addition will link the Grassy River Halliday Lake Forest and Lowlands Conservation Reserve to the Grassy River - Mond Lake Lowlands and Ferris Lake Uplands Provincial Park. These sites were identified for protection in 1999 because they contained natural, geological, cultural features unrepresented in the region, including: an Area of Natural and Scientific Interest (ANSI), shoreline fen areas, a variety of topography from lowland wetland sites to ridges and upland areas, balsam fir and red maple on lacustrine clay, forest communities on weakly broken aeolian sands, a large transverse dune complex (i.e. formed perpendicular to wind direction), osprey nesting sites, provincially significant wetlands, and a portion of the traditional Grassy River canoe route. This site may be accessed by Halliday Lake Road via the Sothman Kemp Road. Alternative access is possible by an existing canoe route.

## Land use intent

The intent is to regulate and manage this area as a conservation reserve.
