## Land area description

The land use intent is to manage a range of water-based recreational opportunities by protecting portages, trails, and the quality fishery. In addition, the intent is to promote the cultural heritage values of the river and to manage development and resource extraction with recognition of the cultural heritage values of the area.The concerns include the effects of Lower Notch Dam operation on fisheries and recreation as well as safety concerns for portages around dams. The management area objectives are to protect existing canoe routes and portages as well as maintain fisheries quality.The strategies include permitting public motorized access subject to road strategies and developing a water level regulation strategy with Ontario Power Generation to benefit fishery. In addition, strategies also include monitoring and identifying appropriate measures with dam operators regarding portage concerns. Further, viewscape management and Area of Concern planning will be applied for extractive activities in adjacent management areas.
