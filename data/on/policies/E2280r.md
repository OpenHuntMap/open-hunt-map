## Land area description

Lac des Mille Lacs is a significant recreational and tourism area in proximity to the city of Thunder Bay. It receives heavy use and is managed through a plan developed and maintained by a local citizen's committee. The Lac des Mille Lacs Planning area incorporates the two main water bodies of Lac des Mille Lacs and Cushing Lake, their islands and immediate shoreline area for 200 metres inland. This includes points of land or peninsulas in their entirety.

## Land use intent

Management of this area should continue through the Lac des Mille Lacs Management Plan, with emphasis on the conservation and enhancement of the lakes recreational and tourism resources. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Land Use Strategy (1999) and the Thunder Bay District Land Use Guideline (1983) and the specific policies of Lac des Mille Lacs Management Plan.

## Permitted uses — preface

Recreation enhanced management areas have been applied to areas with high recreation use of significant recreation values for activities such as angling, hunting, motorized and pedestrian trail use, and canoeing. The enhanced management area can be used for a wide range of recreation values and management intents, ranging from areas where relatively substantial recreation development is permitted, to areas providing low-density, high-quality recreation in a natural setting.Where the recreation enhanced management area has been identified to protect remote recreation values, all activities need to be carried out in such a way as to maintain or enhance the remote recreation qualities.
