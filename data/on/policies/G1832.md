## Land area description

The area consists of that portion of the Nat River located south of Bromley Lake and a portion of the Whitefish River and the McLeod Creek Tributary, including 120 metres inland from the standing treeline. There is no structural development in either area. Both areas are accessed via forest access roads from Highway 101. The wetland adjacent to the Nat and Whitefish Rivers are significant waterfowl nesting and staging areas, as well as excellent for wild rice production. Bromley Lake is the only identified tourism lake in this area.

## Land use intent

The primary use in this area will be the continued management of the wetlands. Resources management plans will contain guidelines for the protection of the wetland areas. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Timmins District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
