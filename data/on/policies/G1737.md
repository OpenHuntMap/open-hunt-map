## Land area description

This area represents the corridor of development along Highway 11. All major urban areas are within this policy area including the Town of Kapuskasing, the villages of Fauquier, Moonbeam, Val Rita, Harty, Opasatika and Mattice. Including outlying areas of rural residential development, the population of the area is approximately 17,947. Most of the area is private land and subject to municipal planning controls.Agricultural activity is an important land-use characterized by a variety of farms with dairy predominating followed by specialized beef farming.Commercial tourism facilities include hotels, motels and campgrounds to serve the travelling public along the highway. There are approximately 130 cottages in this area. The highway corridor provides access to two designated canoe routes. Other recreational features include winter trails (i.e. snowmobiling, cross country skiing).Part of this area is subject to the Range Management Policy in Support of Woodland Caribou Conservation and Recovery (2014). The Range Management Policy is accessible via the “Source of Direction.””

## Land use intent

The primary use in this area will be agriculture and compatible uses. Guidelines for this area are intended to encourage a viable farm community. Community development will be encouraged in urban areas. This Ministry will continue to encourage the appropriate use of hazard lands (i.e. flood plains).Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Kapuskasing District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
