## Land area description

The predominant representative factor within this life science site is broken ground moraine with fen and cut and burn which is less than 20 years old. The area was determined to be the most diverse in the Site District (3E-4) representing twenty different landform vegetation combinations.Pan Lake Fen Provincial Park was regulated as a provincial park on December 21, 2000.

## Land use intent

Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Ontario's Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999).
