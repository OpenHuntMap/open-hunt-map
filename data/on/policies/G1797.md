## Land area description

The general resource extraction and management area occupies about 80% of the District's total land base. It encompasses a significant portion of the District's high mining potential (e.g. the Swayze Greenstone Belt) and most of the areas where forest extraction activities are anticipated in the next 20 years. The area contains the majority of the District's roads and access facilities, along with commercial lodges, 79 commercial outpost camps, and 120 cottages. The primary outdoor recreation activities are fishing, hunting, canoeing and boating. A number of forest management companies operate in this area.The following, having both commercial tourist establishments and no existing road access, have been identified as tourism lakes: Cam, Biggs, Ridley, Paypeeshek, Rollo, Comox, Lower Turnbull, Bonar, Piano, Forearm, Mike, Deluge, Kyushk, Dumbell, Paul, Lincoln, Ossin, Sherlock, Komak, Shenango, Shawmere, Lemonine, Rice, Brett, Denyes, Cree, Swayze, Northpoint, Engineer, Gowagamak, Dyment, Sylvanite, Raney, Shiners and Moore. Northwind, Prairie Grass, Calf, Wassinka, Beak, Embarrass, West Abinette, Nineay, Shannon, Marionette, White Owl, Woman, Glade and Winnie Lake. The following waterbodies (located by UTM grid number) in these townships have also been identified as tourism lakes:Belford 0108, Bader 788338 & 794338, Lincoln 605495 Lincoln 627470 Lincoln 605425, Warren 708388, Lemoine 712500, Lougheed 709756, Rollo 710013, Eaton 706396, Beckett 28507, Patanaude 112516, Amundsen 35914. A group of four lakes in Ossin Township centred on 808733; the Ivanhoe River between UTM 510969 (Lackner Township and UTM 638059 (Raney Township) and the Paypeeshek River UTM 810843 and 886908 (Wadsworth Township); the Windermere River, between UTM 763207 (D’Avaugour Township); 765254 (Hornell Township) and Florence Lake in Grossman Township.Lakes with poor road access and existing commercial tourist camps, but still to be identified as tourism lakes, include: Oswald, Muskego and Rush, Burntwood Frechette, Laundry and a lake in Hardiman Township at UTM grid number 160207. In addition to the foregoing, new outpost camps and even fly-in lodges may be permitted where the fisheries resource can sustain them. Signature Sites are identified for their range of natural and recreational values and their potential to contribute to future recreation and tourism.

## Land use intent

The primary activity throughout much of this area will be an expansion of the forest, mining, and trapping sectors. This will be intermixed with locally dominant recreation and tourism concerns. Road access will be directed towards facilitating the priority land uses suggested for this area, while at the same time increasing recreational opportunities. All resource extraction/management plans will contain conditions to protect significant recreational values, historical and archaeological sites, and fisheries and wildlife habitat (e.g. heronries, eagle nests and an osprey nest.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Chapleau District Land Use Guidelines (1983)
