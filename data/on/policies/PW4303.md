## Land area description

Matchedash Bay Provincial Wildlife Area is located at the south-east end of Severn Sound on Georgian Bay in Tay Township, extending south to the Village of Coldwater in Severn Township.The Matchedash Bay Provincial Wildlife Area was established in 1981 in recognition of the significant wetlands in this area on Georgian Bay. The area is characterized by a great diversity of habitat types. To date, over 500 species of vascular plants have been identified, reflecting the natural diversity of its location at the interface between the St. Lawrence Lowlands and the Canadian Shield.Management of the area is support by the Marl-Tiny-Matchedash (M-T-M) Conservation Association. These Crown lands are located within the A Place to Grown: Growth Plan for the Greater Golden Horseshoe. These lands contribute to the Natural Heritage System identified under this provincial plan.

## Land use intent

The primary goal for the Matchedash Bay Provincial Wildlife Area is to manage wildlife habitat in the area and to perpetuate and increase waterfowl and other wetland dependent wildlife, while integrating other important resource interests.As described in the Matchedash Bay Provincial Wildlife Area Management Plan, 1991, the Provincial Wildlife Area is divided into three zones, natural, management and development, to guide management activities and separate differing uses. The zones differentiate areas which contain significant natural features and habitats from those which have been degraded and are of less significance and value as wildlife habitat or natural areas. The purpose of the zones is to ensure that natural areas and good quality habitats are not unduly impacted, while allowing for the management of areas which have been degraded and identifying access areas.

Commercial Activities and Land and Resource Management Activities listed as permitted may require authorization under the relevant legislation (e.g., Public Lands Act, Aggregate Resources Act, Fish and Wildlife Conservation Act, etc.).
