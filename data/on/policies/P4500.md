## Land area description

Timber Island Provincial Park was regulated in 1985. This small island in Lake Ontario is the least disturbed one within an archipelago that may have acted as a bridge for the migration of southern plants into Ontario about 8,000 years ago. Timber Island's flora present distinct southern affinities, and the island continues to be important for bird migration. There is no recreational use.

## Land use intent

This nature reserve class park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act.
