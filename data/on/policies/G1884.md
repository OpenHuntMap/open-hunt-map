## Land area description

This area is comprised of a complex of lakes and rivers in Raimbault and Nicholas Townships west of Mississagi Provincial Park. The area offers a semi-wilderness type of recreational experience with little road access being in place at this time.The area receives such recreational uses as angling, hiking, canoeing and camping; timber harvesting also occurs in the area. Present development includes commercial and private interests on Flack Lake.Unless otherwise delineated, the area boundaries are situated approximately 120 metres from the shoreline of the lakes and rivers along the perimeter.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The intent is to manage this area for extensive types of recreation, such as angling, canoeing, hiking and camping. Forest management will occur in the future.Disposition of Crown land will not be permitted; road developments for forestry purposes will be encouraged to locate away from high quality recreational areas.Significant wildlife populations (e.g. osprey nests, heronries) and known archaeological features will be protected from adverse developments.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Blind River District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
