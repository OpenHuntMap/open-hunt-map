## Land area description

The Nungesser Lake East Enhanced Management Area includes the eastern portions ofNungesser Lake and lands extending to the east of Pringle Lake. This area complements theprotection intent of the adjoining Pringle Lake Dedicated Protected Area and the Nungesser RiverCultural Landscape Waterway Dedicated Protected Area. The Nungesser and Pringle Lake areasexhibit earth science features of ‘fans’ or ‘outwash deltas’ associated with the Lac Seul Moraine,and are characterized by notable natural and cultural values. Numerous woodland caribou calvingislands are documented. First Nation people conduct various traditional activities for livelihoodand cultural purposes.Development in the area includes 2 main lodges on patent land, an outpost camp, boat caches,private recreation camps, and all or part of several mining claims. Management boundariesrelevant to the area include bear management areas, baitfish blocks, traplines, and a wildlifemanagement unit.

## Land use intent

The intent is to sustain the high quality fish and wildlife resources of Nungesser Lake andsurrounding area. The area is also dedicated to new opportunities in forestry, eco-cultural tourismand recreation while sustaining the cultural landscape, and allowing for mineral sectoropportunities. Strategic access planning will consider the needs of woodland caribou.

## Permitted uses — preface

Management will also be governed by the Land Use Strategy for the Whitefeather Forest andAdjacent Areas and MNR policies. All activities need to be carried out so that the fish and wildliferesources of this area are maintained or enhanceed.
