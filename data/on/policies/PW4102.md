## Hunting

Listed as a permitted use.

| Use | Permitted | Guidelines |
|---|---|---|
| Hunting | Yes | Hunting is permitted in accordance with the Fish and Wildlife Conservation Act and regulations. Special regulations apply to the Calton Swamp Hunting Area that permit hunting of migratory game birds and small game only. Hunting of migratory game birds is permitted on Mondays, Wednesdays, Thursdays or Saturdays between half an hour before sunrise and noon only. Small game may be hunted any day except Sunday, from November 1 to the last Saturday in February of the following year. Refer to Ontario Regulation 665/98 Part XVI - Special Hunting Areas for full details of regulations that apply or visit www.ontario.ca/hunting . Some municipalities have by-laws that apply to hunting including restricting the discharge of firearms. Check with the local municipal office for details. |

Provincial season, licence, tag and firearm rules still apply, as do municipal discharge by-laws. Verify before hunting.

## Land area description

Located southeast of the Town of Aylmer in Malahide Township, Elgin County, the Calton Swamp is a mix of wetlands, deciduous forest and a pine plantation. The Wildlife Management Area supports a diverse mix of species, including waterfowl, songbirds, wild turkey, ruffed grouse and pileated woodpeckers. Calton Swamp includes a Provincially Significant Wetland that provides waterfowl and marsh bird migratory and breeding habitats and has potential to support rare and declining species.Management of Calton Swamp is supported by the Catfish Creek Conservation Authority, Elgin Stewardship Council, and Ducks Unlimited Canada. The Catfish Creek Conservation Authority own 43 hectares of adjacent lands that are managed in association with the MNRF managed parcels. The area offers excellent opportunities for day use outdoor recreation.

## Land use intent

Calton Swamp will be managed to conserve the wetland and forest, to provide habitat for priority species and to support seasonal use including hunting, hiking, research, environmental education and nature appreciation. Activities and projects that protect and enhance wildlife and their habitat, as well as provide educational/research opportunities, will be considered. Unless there is a valid case that any other proposed activity is wildlife-oriented or compatible, no other activities will be permitted on the property.
