## Land area description

This area is located along the eastern boundary of the Timmins District. It includes the Townships of German, Stock, Bond, Sheraton, Michie, Timmins, Blackstock and Fasken and is accessed by the Gibson Lake road. It is a highly utilized recreation corridor, offering good quality fishing, hunting and cottaging. Moose Lake, Driftwood Creek and Crooked Creek contain high quality wild rice stands. As well, Moose Lake is excellent for waterfowl staging. This area lies entirely within one licenced forest Management Units. Moose Lake is the only identified tourism lake in this area.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The primary use in this area will be resource extraction in non-shoreline lands with emphasis on the continued management of the sport fisheries and existing recreational values in shoreline areas. Resource management plans will contain guidelines for the protection of significant recreation and fish and wildlife values. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Timmins District Land Use Guidelines (1983).

## Permitted uses — preface

In addition to provincial and regional policies, the following local policies will apply to the management of Natural lake trout lakes:- no additional access will be permitted­- 120 metre Area of Concern will be applied­- forest management activities may be permitted within the Area of Concern subject to concerns for erosion/sedimentation and the protection of lake trout habitat
