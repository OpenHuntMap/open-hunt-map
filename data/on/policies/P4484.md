## Land area description

One of the most popular and highly used parks in the provincial parks system, Sandbanks Provincial Park was established in 1970. Located on Lake Ontario, the park has two coastal baymouth dunes with the West Lake dune system being the largest in the world on freshwater; vegetation features of the dunes and associated panne wetlands are provincially significant, and are an important migratory stopover for shorebirds. Between the dunes, a limestone-based peninsula supporting mature woodlots and successional fields contains several significant prehistoric and historic sites. Visitors to Sandbanks Provincial Park's enjoy high-quality opportunities for swimming, sunbathing, and picnicking; boating; hiking the park's trails; and camping in a beautiful setting.

## Land use intent

This natural environment class park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act, 2006.
