## Land area description

This large area includes a majority of the Crown land base of the Espanola District. Rock outcrops are common; the soils are, generally, sandy; the vegetation is dominated by stands of jack pine, poplar and birch. The drainage patterns tend to be in a north-south direction. The major rivers are the Spanish, the Wakonassin and the Sables.Vehicle access into the area is by either the Massey Tote Road (Highway 553) or the West Branch, a forest access road that travels north of Webbwood.This area contains a large portion of the Spanish River Forest managed under a sustainable forest licence (SFL). The area has traditionally been a major source of wood fibre. Commercial tourism and extensive Crown land recreation are also common uses. There are significant wildlife habitat areas in this area including many trout lakes and rivers. There are four tourism lakes in the area: Comox, Deluge, Alces and Lake 21 lakes. The only major development in this area is in Hyman Township, Agnew Lake Mines (uranium ore) is located approximately 3 miles north of the east end of Agnew Lake.The major issue in this area revolves around the construction of roads - fly-in tourist operators, some recreationists, and some wildlife managers are opposed to major road network expansions, while other resource users view road development as necessary for proper resource management and use.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The primary purpose for this area shall be economic resource development, in particular logging and commercial tourism. However, most other natural resource uses are permitted and the maintenance of the existing environmental quality and acceptable levels of fish and wildlife populations will be a management goal. Lake trout lakes will be protected from development and use pressures. The remote quality of tourism lakes will be maintained wherever possible.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Espanola District Land Use Guidelines (1983).

## Permitted uses — preface

In addition to provincial and regional policies, the following local policies will apply to the management of Natural lake trout lakes:­ no additional access will be permitted­ 120 metre Area of Concern will be applied­ forest management activities may be permitted within the Area of Concern subject to concerns for erosion/sedimentation and the protection of lake trout habitat
