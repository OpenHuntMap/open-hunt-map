## Land area description

Alfred Bog Provincial Park protects about 90 % of a nationally significant ecological area located in southeastern Ontario. The bog is a provincially significant wetland and life science Area of Natural and Scientific Interest. The Alfred Bog is one of only three raised bogs in southern Ontario, and one of few such ecosystems found at this southern latitude. It is the largest remaining bog community in southern Ontario, and has been highly acclaimed for its classic peatland formations, representative bog communities and special features of conservation, scientific and educational interest.

## Land use intent

The intent is to regulate this recommended natural environment class park under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act.
