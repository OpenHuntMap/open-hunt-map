## Land area description

Mississagi Provincial Park is a destination natural environment park situated approximately 23 kilometres north of the Town of Elliot Lake. This 2,880 hectare park contains one campground, several hiking trails and offers excellent angling opportunities on a number of lakes. This park contains four lake trout lakes.Mississagi Provincial Park was regulated as a provincial park in 1973.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Mississagi Provincial Park will be managed according to Provincial Park policy.

At that time, significant decisions regarding resource stewardship, operations, development and permitted uses are made.
