## Land area description

Sioux Narrows Provincial Park is located 5 kilometres north of Sioux Narrows on the east side of Highway 71. The park is 130 hectares in size and was established on June 25, 1957.

## Land use intent

Sioux Narrows Provincial Park is classified as a recreation park. It provides camping as day-use opportunities and is operated through a partnership agreement with The Whitefish Bay First Nation.Management of this area is also governed by Ontario Parks Policy and the general policies contained in the Kenora District Land Use Guidelines (1983)
