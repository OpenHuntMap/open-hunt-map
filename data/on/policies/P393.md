## Land area description

The park contains one of the largest single concentrations of rock carvings, called petroglyphs, estimated to be between 500 and 1,000 years old and associated with the Algonkian speaking culture. The petroglyphs are significant on a provincial and national level. The park is located in Site District 5E-11.Petroglyphs Provincial Park comprising 1,555 hectares was regulated as a provincial park in 1976. An additional 88 hectares was regulated in 1993.

## Land use intent

The goal of this historical class park is to protect and preserve a unique and important archaeological resource in a natural setting, providing visitors the opportunity to view the petroglyphs on a day-use basis in a manner that will increase their understanding, appreciation and enjoyment of these rock carvings.
