## Land area description

Critical aggregate areas required for the protection of aggregate resources for future use have been identified in several locations as follows: - west of Thessalon on Highway 17; - Maple Ridge; - junction of Highways 546 and 639; and - several deposits south of the City of Elliot Lake.These areas contain significant deposits of high quality aggregate, in proximity to demand centres. The inactive portions of these sites often contain excellent timber resources.This area is located within the Great Lakes Heritage Coast Signature Site, one of 9 such areas featured in the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999). Signature Sites are identified for their range of natural and recreational values and their potential to contribute to future recreation and tourism.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The intent is to provide for aggregate extraction and to protect critical aggregate deposits. Other land uses, such as forestry and agriculture, may be permitted on exhausted aggregate sites.Requests for land disposition will be evaluated on a case by case basis, having regard to Ministry programs and the disposition policy contained in this document.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Blind River District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
