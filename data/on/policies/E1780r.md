## Land area description

This area is located within the Lady Evelyn River watershed. The values and uses of this area include a portion of the wilderness park's headwaters (Lady Evelyn River) and its aquatic ecosystems that are representative of the ecological Site Region. This management area is also part of the land base for forest management in MNR’s Kirkland Lake District.

## Land use intent

The land use intent is to protect park-related values of the northern headwaters of Lady Evelyn-Smoothwater Wilderness Provincial Park (LESWPP) while allowing for forest management activities.Concerns include the access to the wilderness park and new access within the management area, the water quality in the North Lady Evelyn River, and the impacts of forest management on park-related values (aesthetics, noise).The management area objectives are to ensure no mining related activities occur within the management area, to plan access development to complement Park Management Planning (e.g., use of Liskeard Lumber Road south of Kaa Lake), and to identify appropriate access management for resource and recreational use. Additional objectives are to prohibit new roads within 350-metres of park boundaries enhance recreation and tourism opportunities for car camping, hiking, and viewing.The strategies include developing road use strategies and access planning to address access issues and minimize conflicts. Additional strategies include applying special prescriptions developed for forest management and access to protect park-related values, implementing the prescription for forest management and access for the portion of the headwaters in Corkill Township in Kirkland Lake District, and applying viewscape management and area of concern planning.
