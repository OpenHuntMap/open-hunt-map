## Land area description

This area consists entirely of Sturgeon Lake and its surrounding shoreline. Existing activities include commercial tourism (10 lodges), commercial fishing, mining activities, Crown land recreation (4 access points) and some private cottaging.Sturgeon Lake is particularly important to the Ignace Area because of the concentration of tourism and the lake trout fishery. The St. Anthony historic mine site is located in this area as are two heronries and one pictograph site.Sturgeon Lake is designated as a tourism lake.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.Part of this area is subject to the Range Management Policy in Support of Woodland Caribou Conservation and Recovery (2014). The Range Management Policy is accessible via the “Source of Direction.””

## Land use intent

It is recommended that the lake trout resources of Sturgeon Lake be protected. Expansion of commercial tourism facilities will be considered, within biological limits.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Ignace District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
