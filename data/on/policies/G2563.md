## Land area description

This area is located west of Calm Lake and is bounded on the north by Highway 11 and the south shore of the Seine River and on the south by the Pipestone River. The area is crossed by a hydro line corridor and is bounded on the north by a rail line.Present use is centered primarily on resource extraction. It is a low intensity general recreation area. The area is known for its high deer population and contains some of the best deer hunting opportunities in the District. Loss of deer habitat and a reduction in the population as result of logging is a concern.Harnett Lake is designated as a tourism lake.Water Management Areas have been removed from this Policy Report area as revisions, to more accurately reflect First Nation reserve lands affected by flooding.

## Land use intent

The area is designated for resource extraction and the maintenance of the high deer population. Recreational opportunities in the area are primarily centered on the deer population. General recreational levels will be maintained. Access roads will be constructed for resource extraction purposes. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Atikokan District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
