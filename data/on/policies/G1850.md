## Land area description

Round Lake is accessed by Highways 11, 112 and 564 and local roads board roads. The area is not municipally organized. The land is almost entirely patented land. Cottaging and rural residential uses are the main activities. There are commercial lodges on the lake in addition to over 100 cottage lots. There is no Crown maintained access to water. Local residents have concerns regarding the water quality and water levels of Round Lake.

## Land use intent

The intent is to provide a natural resource base for existing recreational development. In this area, the MNR main management concern is the warm water fishery. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Kirkland Lake District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
