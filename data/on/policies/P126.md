## Land area description

This waterway, in Ecodistrict 5E-10 protects the Grant's Creek Marsh provincially significant wetland andwaterway associated with Algonquin Park. The area offers a variety of recreational opportunities including anAlgonquin Park canoe route.The forest surrounding the creek is a mixed upland forest dominated by white and red pine and poplar. The creek winds through a number of cold water trout lakes and includes a number of small waterfalls and a scenic waterslide closer to the Ottawa River.

## Land use intent
