## Land area description

The Matabitchuan River Conservation Reserve is located within the Matabitchuan River watershed located south of the town of Haileybury. The values and uses within the reserve include representative old growth red and white pine stands which have been aged up to 130 years. The reserve also contains in excess of 27 forest communities and a number of disturbed areas containing shrubs, grasses, and predominately grassy meadows. This area experiences the moderating effect of Lake Timiskaming which provides a favourable climate for flora. The Ottawa-Temiskaming Highland Trail system passes through the Matabitchuan River Conservation Reserve and provides recreational and natural heritage appreciation opportunities. The Matabitchuan River Conservation Reserve was regulated on February 21, 1997.

## Land use intent

The intent for the Matabitchuan River Conservation Reserve is to protect a representative old growth pine area which is accessible to the public. The conservation reserve will also provide natural and cultural heritage appreciation opportunities in a natural setting without resource extraction. Land use management concern includes the need for natural disturbances to create suitable conditions for pine regeneration. Natural processes will be allowed to occur in the protected area; however, programs may be developed to control forest insects and diseases where there is a concern that significant value may be compromised. Fire suppression activities will be considered a high priority and will continue until such time as policy directions change.Other land use strategies include identifying appropriate natural heritage appreciation opportunities (e.g.,, trails).
