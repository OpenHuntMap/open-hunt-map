## Land area description

This land use area is comprised of two areas immediately adjacent to reserve lands where the Mattagami First Nation community is located on Mattagami Lake.

## Land use intent

The area is being considered for transfer of the administration and control from Ontario to Canada for addition to reserve purposes for Mattagami First Nation. This may be a lengthy process, and the province will maintain management responsibility of the area in the interim. The land use intent of this area is to prevent activities or land uses that may impact Canada’s acceptance of the lands to be set apart as reserve.Should the transfer be determined to be proceeding, existing provincial interests on the lands will be discontinued or transferred to federal permits, where appropriate. Should the transfer be determined not to be proceeding, an amendment will be sought to remove the holding category from the lands and return them to their original land use designation: General Use Area G1813 - Predominant Cottaging Area.

## Permitted uses — preface

Management decisions must prevent activities or land uses that may impact Canada’s acceptance of the lands to be set apart as reserve. New approvals in this area will generally not be granted. Some maintenance of existing infrastructure may be permitted. Prior to any approval, it must be determined that the activity or land use would not impact Canada’s acceptance of the lands to be set apart as reserve. Confirmation from Canada may be warranted.
