## Land area description

Area D is located in the geographic Townships of Marmora and Huntington. It is approximately 40,000 hectares in size and is almost entirely private land. The primary land uses are agriculture, sand and gravel extraction, hunting, fishing and private land forestry.The most significant features in Area D are numerous high quality aggregate deposits and eight high potential mineral development areas.The main problem in this area is the loss of significant aggregate resources to other uses which preclude extraction.

## Land use intent

Ministry program emphasis will be private land forestry, mineral exploration, and administration of sand and gravel extraction. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Tweed District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
