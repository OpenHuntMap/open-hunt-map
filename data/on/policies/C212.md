## Land area description

This conservation reserve is located in Ecodistrict 5E-4. It contains rugged and rolling country covered mainlyby hardwood forests. Poplar and white birch stands grow on the deep ground moraine of the site, as well asold cedar stands, open and treed wetlands.

## Land use intent

The intent is to manage this area as a conservation reserve.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Land Use Strategy (1999).
