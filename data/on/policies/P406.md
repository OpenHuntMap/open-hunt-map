## Land area description

Westmeath Provincial Park is located on a large bend in the Ottawa River east of Pembroke in Site District 5E-12. The park is situated in the ancient Ottawa-Bonnechere rift valley. This valley is composed of Precambrian bedrock overlain by younger Middle Ordovician limestone and is covered by relatively deep post glacial lacustrine sands, alluvial silts sands, clays and graves. Extensive wet lowland forest communities, dominated by silver maple and black ash occur at the northern and southern ends of Bellows Bay. The park was regulated in 1985.

## Land use intent

Westmeath Provincial Park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act. The boundary of the park is delineated by Ontario Regulation No. 493/85, Schedule 213.
