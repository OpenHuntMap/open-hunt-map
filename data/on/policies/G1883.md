## Land area description

This area includes portions of the upper Little White River Watersheds north of Mississagi Provincial Park. Major lakes in the area include Mount Lake, McElrea Lake, Sister Lake and Little Sister Lake.This is a popular recreational area for tourists, boaters, anglers, hunters, canoeists and hikers. Existing development includes summer cottages, residences, commercial lodges and outpost camps. Forest management is occurring in several portions of the area.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The intent of the area is to provide recreational opportunities on the lakes, rivers and trail systems and to encourage forestry and other resource activity within other portions of the area. Disposition of Crown land will generally be discouraged.Significant wildlife populations (e.g. osprey nests, heronries) and known archaeological features will be protected from adverse developments.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Blind River District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
