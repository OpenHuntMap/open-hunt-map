## Land area description

This area, south and north of the Town of Smooth Rock Falls, contains the Mattagami River, and comprises 120 metres of shoreline on each side. Road access to the river is available south of Smooth Rock Falls by timber access roads and at Jacksonboro where there is a federal government access point. The area includes a considerable amount of patent land around the Town of Smooth Rock Falls, most of the rivers shoreline in the Townships of Mabee, Dargavel and Aubin and a small leased area around the Lower Sturgeon Dam.Structural developments in the area include approximately 15 cottages, 2 hydro-electric generating stations and one gravel pit. This river is a provincial canoe route. The river has good recreation capability for both intensive (e.g. cottaging) and extensive (e.g. canoeing) use. A water power lease and Licence of Occupation on the river provide flooding rights to 231.7 metres above sea level. Forest management is carried out under a sustainable forest licence (SFL). The river may be needed in the future, to provide cottage lots to the residents of the Smooth Rock Falls area. Yellow Falls, south of the town and Grand Rapids in Hogg Township have been identified as potential hydraulic power generation sites.This area is subject to the Range Management Policy in Support of Woodland Caribou Conservation and Recovery (2014). The Range Management Policy is accessible via the “Source of Direction.”

## Land use intent

The primary use of this area will be recreation. This will take the form of public recreation, cottaging and commercial tourism. Activities proposed in these areas will be in accordance with these municipal planning controls, where they apply. Hydro-electric power generation is also a priority in this area. Structural development will not be permitted in areas subject to shoreline erosion or flooding. The area of rapids immediately below Sturgeon Falls supports a substantial spawning and post spawning yellow pickerel population during the spring. This habitat will be protected from deterioration and/or destruction by adjacent uses. In addition, there is an Indian burial ground in this area on patented land in Mabee Township that may require some protection from future development. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Cochrane District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
