## Land area description

Located at the junction of French, Butler and Phelps Townships this enhanced management area in site district 5E-6 is of earth science significance. It contains a provincially significant delta complex which includes a variety of features such as an esker and numerous kettles. A variety of forest and wetland habitats - the latter being in several undisturbed bogs - are also found in this area, including some mature sugar maple stands. This site is an important water recharge and discharge area and a source of cold water. It is situated in the headwaters of streams that have supported a fish hatchery and at least two significant populations of brook trout. This site overlays a locally significant life science area known as Balsam Creek Esker, located in the southeast corner of the enhanced management area. It contains locally significant features in rare species, ice-contact formations, seepage springs related to kettle holes and mature Sugar Maple deciduous forest. The entire area is unorganized.

## Land use intent

Earth science and locally significant life science natural heritage values will be protected through more detailed future planning, providing for traditional Crown land uses such as forest management, controlled aggregate extraction, hunting, fur harvesting, and mineral exploration and development.A clean, assured water supply for the existing fish hatchery and potential expansion of the facility is to be considered in all land management decisions. Aggregate extraction is not permitted within the reservation area of the North Bay Fish Hatchery. Wildlife habitat on Crown land will be managed at the existing overall quantity and quality.MNR is responsible for the management of Crown land and natural resources in unorganized townships.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Land Use Strategy (1999) and the North Bay District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
