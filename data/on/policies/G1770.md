## Land area description

The general resource extraction and management area occupies about 15,000 square kilometers. It encompasses areas of significant mining potential (e.g. The Swayze Greenstone Belt) and most of the areas where forest extraction activities are anticipated in the future. The area contains several roads and access facilities, along with 6 commercial lodges, 79 commercial outpost camps, and 120 cottages. The primary outdoor recreation activities are fishing, hunting, canoeing and boating.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The primary activity throughout much of this area will be an expansion of the forest, mining, and trapping sectors. This will be intermixed with locally dominant recreation and tourism concerns. Road access will be directed towards facilitating the priority land uses suggested for this area, while at the same time increasing recreational opportunities. All resource extraction/management plans will contain conditions to protect significant recreational values, historical and archaeological sites, and fisheries and wildlife habitat.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the District Land Use Guidelines for Chapleau, Sault Ste. Marie, Sudbury, Timmins and Hearst (1983).
