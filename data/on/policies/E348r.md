## Land area description

This area is located within the Temagami River watershed. The values and uses of the area include considerable amount of maple, yellow birch, and red oak and the Gull Lake Dam and flume.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Inland Ontario Lakes Designated for Lake Trout Management (2006) accessible in the Source of Direction portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The land use intent is to maintain the remote character of the area and the quality fishing opportunities while allowing forestry and mining related activities.Concerns include the potential to develop motorized access to Lake Temagami, Gull Lake, and Skunk Lake without access control and the use of portages as ATV trails.The management area objectives are to manage the forest for old growth characteristics, to allow for resource extraction, to restrict public motorized access, to retain the tolerant hardwood forest conditions as high value wildlife areas, and to ensure access to this management area from Lake Temagami by ATV is restricted to fuelwood permit-holders. An objective is also to retain tolerant hardwood forest conditions as high value wildlife areas.The strategies include planning access and developing road use strategies to restrict public motorized access and minimize conflicts, developing a strategy to address ATV use (i.e., fuelwood strategy for Lake Temagami), and applying viewscape and Area of Concern planning to extractive activities.

## Permitted uses — preface

Land use and resource management activities for this area are also governed by the policies of the Temagami Land Use Plan (TLUP) (1997). The policies from the TLUP are integrated into this area report. Refer to the TLUP for background information and additional details.
