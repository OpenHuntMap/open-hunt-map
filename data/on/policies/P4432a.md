## Land area description

Bronte Creek Provincial Park is located within the Regional Municipality of Halton on the Burlington-Oakville municipal boundary, immediately north of the Queen Elizabeth Way/Highway 403. The 682 hectare (ha) recreational class park was regulated in 1975. In 2004, the Minister of Natural Resources announced the intent to add provincially owned lands located north of Bronte Creek Provincial Park, totalling approximately 68 hectares, to the park. These lands protect provincially significant life science features and are a critical link in connecting the park to the Niagara Escarpment. MNR has also acquired an additional four hectares of land adjacent to the park which contains provincially significant life science features. MNR is proposing to add these 72 ha of acquired land to the regulated park.

## Land use intent

The recommended Bronte Creek Provincial Park Addition will be regulated under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act and managed as a recreational class park according to provincial park policy. Management of the park will protect its natural features.

## Permitted uses — preface

Management of this recommended park addition will be consistent with the Bronte Creek Provincial Park Management Plan (1998). Additional management direction is also provided in Ontario Provincial Parks: Planning and Management Policies (1992).
