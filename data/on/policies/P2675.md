## Land area description

Shesheeb Bay Provincial Park (275 hectares) is comprised of four parts along the shoreline of Shesheeb Bay, Otter Cove and Otter Island of the Black Bay Peninsula. Shesheeb Bay was regulated in 1985 as a Provincial Nature Reserve to protect earth and life science features.This area is located within the Great Lakes Heritage Coast Signature Site, one of 9 such areas featured in the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999). Signature Sites are identified for their range of natural and recreational values and their potential to contribute to future recreation and tourism.

## Land use intent

The intent is to manage this park as a nature reserve class provincial park, in accordance with MNR and Ontario Parks Policy.
