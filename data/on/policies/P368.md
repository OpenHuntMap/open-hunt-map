## Land area description

Bigwind Lake Provincial Park is situated in Site District 5E-8. The Park is covered by late succession (100-130 year old) deciduous mixed forest over rugged landscape. Wetland vegetation occupies a major portion of park and includes bog, thicket swamp and occasional black spruce dominated kettle bogs.Earth science features include representation of gneiss's of the Opeongo Domain of the Algonquin Terrain, Central Gneiss Belt.The park was regulated in 1985.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Bigwind Lake Provincial Park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act. The boundary is delineated by the following plan: Ontario Regulation No. 279/85, Schedule 184.
