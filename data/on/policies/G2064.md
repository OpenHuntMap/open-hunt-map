## Land area description

This is an area of shallow soils and extensive bedrock outcrops with some drainage north to Lake Nipissing, but most tends east to the French River. There are some private lands, and agriculture occurs in Loudon and Falconer Townships, but most of the land base is Crown. The entire area is organized. A traditional deer wintering area occurs in Bertram Township. Timber production from Crown land is a major industrial use.Portions of the West Bay Area abut P110, French River Additions, Provincial Park Addition, E163N, the Latchford Enhanced Management Area and the French River Provincial Park.

## Land use intent

Resource production and fish and wildlife management are the major uses of the area and these will continue. Recreation, tourism and other present intensive uses of the land will be encouraged. The existing overall quantity and quality of wildlife habitat will be maintained. Important waterfowl concentration areas will be given special status and consideration. Deer winter habitat will be protected. Several sites, where preservation is the main theme of management (e.g. heronries, wetlands, etc.) are found in this area. These and other such sites, which may be found in the future, will be protected during resource management operations and will be identified as values requiring protection in municipal planning.Any proposed planning on activities adjacent to any park will consider the management intent and direction of these areas. MNR is responsible for the management of Crown land and natural resources in organized townships.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in appropriate District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
