## Land area description

Port Bruce Provincial Park, is located along the north shore Lake Erie, off County Road 73 (Imperial Road), approximately 15 kilometres south of the town of Aylmer, in the Township of Malahide, County of Elgin. The park is popular for its day use opportunities associated with the beach and adjacent picnic area. The former Department of Highways (now Ministry of Transportation) administered the park as a highway picnic area until 1958 when it was transferred to the then Department of Lands and Forests (now Ministry of Natural Resources). The property boundary was regulated under the Provincial Parks Act in 1974 and amended in 1975 and 1980. Following a public consultation process, the Port Bruce Provincial Park Interim Management Statement was approved in 1999.

## Land use intent

This recreational class park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act.
