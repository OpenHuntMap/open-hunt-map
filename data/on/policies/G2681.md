## Land area description

This area includes Stirling Township and the northwest corner of Lyon Township. Existing activities are mixed: agriculture, residential, cottaging, transportation, recreation, resource extraction and hunting. The area is part of the Lakehead Forest and has the CNR, CPR, Highway 11-17, gas pipeline and hydro corridors. The town of Hurkett is also in this area.Timber harvesting and peat exploration and extraction are issues which will have to be addressed in the future management of this area.This area is located within the Great Lakes Heritage Coast Signature Site, one of 9 such areas featured in the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999). Signature Sites are identified for their range of natural and recreational values and their potential to contribute to future recreation and tourism.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

A variety of land uses, including general recreation, residential development, agriculture and resource extraction will continue. Other ongoing activities will be permitted. The water supply of the Dorion Fish Culture station will be protected from adverse development. Crown land cottaging will not be encouraged in this area. All development and uses within the Municipality of Dorion will conform to its approved zoning by-law.The identified aquatic habitat of Hurkett Cove will be protected from disturbance.Stirling Township General Use Area is located in an area in which one or more First Nations have asserted Aboriginal rights and title, including an unextinguished Aboriginal right to exclusive use and occupancy of the land. The First Nations’ claims are the subject of litigation and negotiations. Dispositions in Stirling Township General Use Area may be subject to heightened Crown consultation and accommodation obligations.This may mean a greater degree of engagement with First Nation communities asserting rights and title prior to a decision regarding potential authorizations or dispositions of public lands or resources. For a map of areas affected by these claims, please go to the Notice of Caution on the Mining Lands Administration System (MLAS) at https://www.geologyontario.mndm.gov.on.ca/mines/lands/withreop/notices_e.html .
