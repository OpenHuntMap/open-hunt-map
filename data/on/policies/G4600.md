## Land area description

This policy report applies to various Crown land areas designated as General Use Areas managed by the Ministry of Natural Resources and Forestry’s (MNRF) Kemptville District, including the beds of lakes and rivers under Crown ownership.Kemptville District covers 15,219 square kilometres, comprising a large area located between the St. Lawrence and Ottawa Rivers. The district covers the most easterly part of Ontario bordering the province of Quebec and the USA. The area supports a population of over one million people, the majority of which live within the largest centres that include Ottawa - the nation’s capital city - Brockville and Cornwall. Despite the presence of large numbers of people in an intensively managed agricultural and rural/urban setting, significant natural landscapes and features persist.The majority of land in the district is in private ownership and the Crown lands managed by Kemptville District are interspersed throughout the area. The Crown lands include a range of property sizes and diverse habitats. Many parcels are adjacent to lands owned by municipalities and conservation authorities, and form part of large public natural areas. The diversity of habitats means that there is a wide variety of fish and wildlife species found in the District. Portions of the District are part of a UNESCO World Biosphere Reserve, the Frontenac Arch.

## Land use intent

These Crown lands, designated as General Use Areas, provide opportunities for a range of outdoor recreation and resource development activities. MNRF supports and promotes the sustainable use and management of natural resources on Crown lands for the benefit of future generations. Crown lands are to be used responsibly to help protect the natural environment, and with respect for other users of Crown land. These Crown lands are generally unmaintained, support a variety of users and should be used with an understanding and acceptance of the associated risks.

## Permitted uses — preface

MNRF will consider the Land Use Intent and Management Direction outlined in this policy report when reviewing applications for permitted activities that require licences, leases, permits, or other forms of approval. Commercial Activities and Land and Resource Management Activities listed as permitted may require authorization under the relevant legislation (e.g., Public Lands Act, Aggregate Resources Act, Fish and Wildlife Conservation Act, etc.). The review of individual applications involves the consideration of a variety of factors and requirements on an application and site-specific basis, in addition to land use policy.Individuals seeking to carry out any of the permitted activities listed below should contact the local MNRF district office to find out if an authorization is required.Crown lands are managed in a manner consistent with the Constitutional protection afforded to existing Aboriginal and treaty rights.Certain Crown land parcels within part or all of this land use area are subject to the Algonquin Land Claim Agreement-in-Principle (AIP). These lands are described in the AIP as Proposed Settlement Lands, Areas of Algonquin Interest and recommended additions to the protected area system (provincial parks). Until such time as a Final Agreement is reached, current management direction or permitted uses in the policy report are the applicable direction. However, permitted uses - such as certain dispositions - noted in this policy report may be further restricted due to the ongoing tri-partite negotiation of the Algonquin Land Claim. Links to additional information can be found in the Source of Direction section at the end of this policy report.
