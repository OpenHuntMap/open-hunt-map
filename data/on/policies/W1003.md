## Land area description

This wilderness area comprises a small parcel of land located on the west end of Eighteen Mile Island on the French River. It contains representative and special features of natural heritage significance.

## Land use intent

Wilderness areas are areas that are regulated under the Wilderness Areas Act. The Act states that these areas are set aside “…as a wilderness area for the preservation of the area as nearly as may be in its natural state in which research and educational activities may be carried on, for the protection of the flora and fauna, for the improvement of the area, having regard to its historical, aesthetic, scientific or recreational value…” …” . Most commercial activities are not permitted, and any land and resource management activity or recreation activity must take into consideration the intent of the Wilderness Areas ActConsistent with the Guide for Crown Land Use Planning, this Wilderness Area will be assessed in the future on whether there are natural or recreational values that warrant further protection (i.e. through the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act).
