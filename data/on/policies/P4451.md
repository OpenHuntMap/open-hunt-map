## Hunting

Not addressed in this policy.

Provincial season, licence, tag and firearm rules still apply, as do municipal discharge by-laws. Verify before hunting.

## Land area description

Located approximately 5km northwest of Tiverton, Municipality of Kincardine, Bruce County.The park was regulated in 1967.

## Land use intent

This cultural heritage class park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act.
