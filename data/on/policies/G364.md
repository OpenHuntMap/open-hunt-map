## Land area description

This area is primarily Crown land. There is some marginal agricultural land (pasture) in Rama Township. The area is mostly forested with rough topography, many small lakes and swamps, and is the beginning of the Precambrian shield.This area is located within the Great Lakes Heritage Coast Signature Site, one of 9 such areas featured in the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999). Signature Sites are identified for their range of natural and recreational values and their potential to contribute to future recreation and tourism.

## Land use intent

The emphasis for resource management will be on forestry, fish and wildlife and recreation. The policies governing the resource use of this area will generally be identified in resource management plans such as the Crown Lands Forest Management plan. The Huronia District Crown Land Management plan provides the specific resource managemet policies for this area and is compatible with and follows the general policies of the Huronia District Land Use Guidelines, and provincial policies and procedures for Crown lands administration.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Huronia District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
