## Land area description

This area is located within the Matabitchuan River/Temagami River watershed. The values and uses of this area include the representative old growth white and red pine forest as well as the significant stands of white birch and cedar located on the western shore of Cassels Lake. Also of value are the community partnership in the management area along with the hiking trails and scenic lookout adjacent to the town of Temagami.

## Land use intent

The land use intent of this area is to protect cultural and natural heritage values and recreational activities (old growth and hiking trails). In addition, the intent is to identify natural, cultural heritage appreciation, and recreational opportunities in an area that are easily accessible from the town of Temagami. The concerns include the potential for conflicting uses of trails (motorized - snowmobiling use only and non-motorized) and the need for natural disturbances to create suitable conditions for pine regeneration.The objectives of this management area are to maintain backcountry recreation opportunities close to urban area and to apply a strategy to minimize mining impacts on natural heritage and recreation values.The strategies include minimizing the impacts of mineral exploration and ensuring exploration sites are restored upon completion of exploration. Access for mineral exploration will no longer be via the Whitebear access trail from Highway 11.

## Permitted uses — preface

Land use and resource management activities for this area are also governed by the policies of the Temagami Land Use Plan (TLUP) (1997). The policies from the TLUP are integrated into this area report. Refer to the TLUP for background information and additional details.
