## Land area description

This area is comprised of Crothers and part of McBride Townships. The area is not developed and access is somewhat limited. Hunting, fishing, timber extraction and mineral exploration are the only activities presently occurring here. This area lies within two separate licenced forestry Management Units.

## Land use intent

The primary use in this area will be resource extraction with Crown land recreation as priority in shoreline areas. Resource management plans will contain guide lines for the protection of significant recreation areas and fish and wildlife habitat. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Timmins District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
