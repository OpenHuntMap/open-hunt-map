## Land area description

Menzel Centennial Provincial Park, established in 1997 on lands acquired to celebrate the 1993 Ontario Provincial Parks Centennial, protects a significant portion of one of the largest open and treed fen wetland habitats in eastern Ontario. The park also protects alvar habitat, drumlin-like islands and a pristine lake, which are accessible to visitors via a short boardwalk.

## Land use intent

The land use intent of Menzel Centennial Provincial Park is to protect a variety of ecosystems - fen and swamp wetlands, alvars, woodlands and fields - and the communities they support, while stimulating visitor appreciation for the park’s natural values. Menzel Centennial is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act, and will be managed as a nature reserve class park according to provincial park policy and the Menzel Centennial Interim Management Statement (1994).
