## Land area description

This area is located within the Marten River watershed. The values and uses of this area include the Nipissing Crown Game Preserve where hunting and trapping are not permitted, the potential for wildlife viewing, and the existing and heritage portages.

## Land use intent

The intent of this area is to provide remote angling and recreational opportunities in the Nipissing Crown Game Preserve, while also allowing forest management and mining related activities. The intent is also to maintain the remote characteristic of lakes with fly-in tourism.The concerns include the use of portages as ATV trails (conflicts with non-motorized recreation) and the access to remote tourism lakes by motorized vehicles (e.g., ATV, truck).The objectives of this management area include protecting existing canoe routes and retaining potential for new routes, restricting public motorized access where resource extraction occurs, and retaining existing remoteness for tourism.The strategies of this area include managing access by using a road strategy with restrictions to public motorized road access, coordinating access planning with the area south of the Boyce Lake Management Area to maintain remoteness, and applying viewscape and Area of Concern planning for extractive activities. A strategy was developed to prohibit further use of existing portage trails by ATVs within this management area as part of the Crown Land Recreation Plan Temagami Integrated Planning process.

## Permitted uses — preface

Land use and resource management activities for this area are also governed by the policies of the Temagami Land Use Plan (TLUP) and the Crown Land Recreation Plan Temagami Integrated Planning. The policies from the TLUP are integrated into this area report. Refer to the TLUP for background information and additional details.
