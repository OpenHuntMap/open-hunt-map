## Land area description

High quality recreational landscape that surrounds Lac Aux Sable and Big Trout Lake, considered a provincially significant coldwater lake. The area includes Sable - Bark Lake Canoe Route which is in the Mississagi River Provincial Park Additions. Presently designated for low density limited road access recreational use. Area also encompasses four high quality sportfishing lakes, several private recreation camps, outpost camps and main base lodge. Traditional uses include commercial bear hunting, moose hunting, snowmobiling and sportfishing. Important area for forestry. Road access is managed on a restricted basis.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Manage this important remote lake tourism, recreation and resource sector (forestry) area consistent with the enhanced management area (recreation) policies and the existing remote access policies of the MNR Espanola District Land Use Guidelines. It is the intention of MNR to manage this area for low density, limited road access, recreational uses such as canoeing, tent camping, nature photography, hunting, snowmobiling and fishing. Most other natural resource uses will be permitted, however, the ability of the landscape to fulfill the primary intent must not be impaired. Road access to this area will be closely regulated, for example network density will be light, roads may be of a limited duration and lesser quality. Public recreational use of the permitted road system may be restricted or prohibited. Further planning will detail individual zones within this overall area to provide for the needs of remote tourism, remote recreation and resource extraction.Land use direction and resource management activities within this area to be compatible with protecting the recreational values of Mississagi River Provincial Park and its proposed additions.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Land Use Strategy (1999) and the Espanola District Land Use Guidelines (1983).

## Permitted uses — preface

Recreation enhanced management area have been applied to areas with high recreational use of significant recreation values for activities such as angling, hunting, motorized and pedestrian trail use, and canoeing. The enhanced management area can be used for a wide range of recreation values and management intents, ranging from areas where relatively substantial recreation development is permitted, to areas providing low-density, high-quality recreation in a natural setting.Where the recreation enhanced management area has been identified to protect remote recreation values, all activities need to be carried out in such a way as to maintain or enhance the remote recreation qualities.In addition to provincial and regional policies, the following local policies will apply to the management of Natural lake trout lakes:­ no additional access will be permitted­ 120 metre Area of Concern will be applied­ forest management activities may be permitted within the Area of Concern subject to concerns for erosion/sedimentation and the protection of lake trout habitat
