## Land area description

Finlayson Point Provincial Park is a recreation class park located one-kilometre south of the town of Temagami. The park is situated on a small peninsula on the northeast arm of Lake Temagami and is the gateway to explore the lake's many arms, bays, coves, and inlets. The park is adjacent to the White Bear Forest Conservation Reserve containing one of the area's most accessible stands of old growth forest. A network of trails offers opportunities to explore this ecosystem which includes red and white pine stands ranging from 150 to 350 years old. A hiking trail links the park to nearby Caribou Mountain which overlooks the town of Temagami. Visitors can also climb the rebuilt fire tower for a view of the park, Lake Temagami, and the surrounding forest Finlayson Point provides a variety of recreational opportunities including camping, swimming, boating, fishing, and canoeing. The park also provides access to the backcountry parks in the area.Finlayson Point Provincial Park was regulated as a provincial park in 1963.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Inland Ontario Lakes Designated for Lake Trout Management (2006), accessible in the Source of Direction portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Finlayson Point Provincial Park will be managed to provide recreation opportunities while protecting the natural and cultural values within the park.

## Permitted uses — preface

Management of this provincial park is carried out in accordance with Ontario Provincial Parks: Planning and Management Policies (1992), and the Finlayson Point Provincial Park Management Plan (1986). The permitted uses associated with provincial parks are found in approved management direction, which are available on the Ontario Parks website.
