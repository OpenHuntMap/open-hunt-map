## Land area description

Murphys Point is located on the north shore of Big Rideau Lake in a lake-dotted, rolling, rocky countryside typical of the Frontenac Axis. The park contains representative biological and geological features of Site District 5E-11. The park has a mosaic of vegetation types ranging from forest to old field, which have arisen in response to settlement activities, topography and imperfect drainage.Cultural and geological features are related in the park. The availability of the Rideau Canal as a commercial waterway was a factor in the establishment of former small scale mining in the park. Abandoned mines provide an excellent geological and historical interpretive opportunity.Most of Murphys Point Provincial Park is located beyond the Ontario's Living Legacy planning area. The total area of this park is 1241 hectares.Murphys Point Provincial Park was regulated as a provincial park in 1967.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Murphys Point Provincial Park was first opened to the public in 1979. It is classified as a natural environment park in recognition of its biological, geological and cultural features representative of the Frontenac Axis, as well as the provincially significant black rat snake and significant plant associations. In addition, the park provides a range of high quality recreation opportunities in a natural setting and encourages participation by users from the two linear recreation systems, the Rideau Trail and the Rideau Canal, that traverse the park area.
