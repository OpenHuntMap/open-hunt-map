## Land area description

This area is located west of Highway 144 and is accessed by gravel roads commencing at this highway. Structural development includes commercial resort, outpost camps, access points and aggregate pit. This area also contains part of the Tatachikapika River canoe route. This area lies within two separate licenced Management Units. The southern half possesses medium mineral potential while the northern section contains significant aggregate potential. Crown land recreational use is moderate to high. Tatachikapika Lake, Lake #3 (Stethem Township) are the identified tourism lakes in this area.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Resource extraction and recreation will be of equal importance in this area. Recreation will be given the priority in shoreline areas. In non-shoreline areas, resource extraction will have priority.Road access will be managed to facilitate the overall land use intent. Resource management plans will contain guidelines to protect significant recreational areas, historical and archaeological sites, and fish and wildlife habitat. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Gogama District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
