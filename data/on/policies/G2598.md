## Land area description

This area includes the City of Kenora as well as the unorganized townships of Umbach, Pellat, Redditt, Pettypiece, Haycock and Kirkup. This is the area in which most residential and industrial development will occur. The area is well accessed and demonstrates a relatively high degree of residential development. There are presently 40 main base lodges located in the area.The following are designated as tourism lakes: Armstrong, Black Sturgeon, Blindfold, Corn, Culloden, Longbow, Lulu and the Winnipeg River.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.Water Management Areas have been removed from this Policy Report area as revisions, to more accurately reflect First Nation reserve lands affected by flooding.

## Land use intent

Within this area, MNR will attempt to ensure through the plan input and review process that opportunities for the achievement of Ministry objectives are maintained. All resource activities will be required to recognize the residential-recreation role of the area and attempt to reduce and minimize potential conflicts. Within organized areas, Ministry program objectives will be developed in concert with existing and future official plans.
