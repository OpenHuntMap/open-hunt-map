## Land area description

Areas of natural and scientific interest (ANSIs) are areas of land and water containing natural landscapes or features which have been identified as having values related to protection, natural heritage appreciation, scientific study or education. ANSIs encourage the protection of additional areas not regulated as provincial parks to provide a focus for both the public and private sectors to contribute to the protection of Ontario's natural heritage.This area is located in the vicinity of Big Chute at the lower reaches of the Severn River. The landform and vegetation features here are both representative of the interior Gneissic Rockland in the side district, and are notable in the presence of a concentration of Provincially and Regionally significant plant species.

## Land use intent

To ensure that land uses and activities which occur provide for the protection of the life science features identified for the area.Management of this area is governed by the policies contained in the Huronia District Land Use Guidelines (1983), and the Huronia District Crown Land Management Plan (1987).
