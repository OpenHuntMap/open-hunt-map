## Land area description

This area, regulated as a nature reserve class Provincial Park in 1985, straddles the Pratt/Spohn Township line. The area once formed part of the bed of glacial Lake Agassiz and is representative of a clay plain with some morainal features. It contains a single small lake and is presently inaccessible by road. One private land inholding is located in the area.The park provides representation of the swamp, bog and fen communities within Landscape Unit 31 (Rainy River Clay Plain). Owners of private inholdings will be encouraged to manage their properties in a manner which complements the surrounding park and its objectives and policies.

## Land use intent

The intent is to manage this park as a nature reserve class provincial park, in accordance with MNR and Ontario Parks Policy.
