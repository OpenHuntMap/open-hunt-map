## Land area description

Located in the Haliburton Highlands, the Leslie M. Frost Natural Resource Centre was established by MNR in 1974 as a resource management education centre. Its primary goal is to foster a wider public understanding and appreciation of Ontario's natural resources, their management and use. A plan for the Frost Centre was approved in 1980.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

In addition to the broad program objectives of MNR (land and water management, resource products, and outdoor recreation), three additional and distinctive objectives have been established for the Frost Centre. These objectives relate to education and demonstration; integrated resource management; and innovative and experimental resource management. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Integrated Plan for Land Use and Resource Development (1980) and the general policies of the Minden and Bracebridge District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
