## Land area description

The 321 hectare Komoka Provincial Park is regulated under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act (2006) and located in the County of Middlesex, straddling the municipal boundaries of the Township of Middlesex Centre and the City of London. The park is administered by the Park Superintendent at Port Burwell Provincial Park, reporting to the Southwest Zone office in London.The park, located in Ecodistrict 7E-6, contains provincially significant representation of a terraced, forested river corridor and its associated vegetation types including black oak, black walnut and sugar maple/black maple deciduous forests in addition to water stargrass marsh and significant prairie communities. The park protects large portions of the provincially significant Komoka Provincial Park Earth Science and Komoka Park Reserve and Adjacent Lands Life Science Areas of Natural and Scientific Interest and is home to a number of species at risk. The park offers excellent opportunities for self interpretative natural heritage appreciation. Komoka Provincial Park was first regulated in 1989. The park boundary was amended in 2017 to include two parcels of provincially acquired land.

## Land use intent

Komoka Provincial Park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Ac, 2006. The current boundary of the park is described in Ontario Regulation No. 316/07, Table 1.Komoka Provincial Park is regulated as a natural environment class park to protect outstanding recreational landscapes, representative ecosystems and provincially significant elements of Ontario’s natural and cultural heritage and to provide high quality recreational and educational experiences.

## Permitted uses — preface

Management of the park is guided by the Komoka Provincial Park Management Plan (2010). Additional management direction is provided in Ontario Provincial Parks: Planning and Management Policies (1992).The permitted uses associated with provincial parks are found in approved management direction, which are available at Ontario.ca provincial park management direction.
