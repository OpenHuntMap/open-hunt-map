## Land area description

This area covers the Township of Sioux Narrows and includes Berry Lake, Dogpaw Lake, Snake Bay and Lobstick Bay. This is the recreational centre for the middle Lake of the Woods area and presently supports 28 main base lodges: There is access throughout the area for lodges and cottage subdivisions. The Whitefish Bay Indian Reserve No. 32-A, 33-A and 34-A are located in this area. Although there are areas of relatively high mineral potential, there has been no mining activity in the area to date.Portions of the Lake of the Woods Islands Conservation Reserve are located in this area.Berry Lake is a designated tourism lake.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.Water Management Areas have been removed from this Policy Report area as revisions, to more accurately reflect First Nation reserve lands affected by flooding.

## Land use intent

The area will be managed to maintain existing tourism development. Expansion of existing tourist facilities where resource capability exists will be considered. Land uses in the area which potentially conflict with recreational tourism will be reviewed in order to minimize impacts.
