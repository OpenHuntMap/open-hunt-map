## Land area description

This area includes the land and water base in a small portion of the eastern part of Beckwith Township.The area is used extensively for mineral aggregate extraction, agriculture and forestry. Special features include:- high potential mineral aggregate deposits.

## Land use intent

Areas of high agricultural capability and existing agricultural uses will be recognized and ministry programs will complement them.MNR will encourage the sequential use of specific areas, for example, rehabilitating abandoned pits and quarries for agricultural or forestry uses.Discussion:The aggregate resources within this area make an important contribution to the district target for mineral resources. The management of these resources will receive particular attention to provide both current and future demands.Forestry activities throughout the area will be undertaken on a demand basis from private landowners.Specific strategies:- identify mineral resources to municipalities, and other agencies and encourage land use policies and practices that do not preclude future extraction operations; - carry out private land forestry activities as part of a pits and quarries rehabilitation program. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Carleton Place District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
