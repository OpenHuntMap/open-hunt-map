## Land area description

Holland Landing Prairie Provincial Nature Reserve is located within the Town of East Gwillimbury, Regional Municipality of York. The park is located seven kilometres southeast of Bradford and 53 km from Highway 401 north of Toronto. Located on the deep sand and silt deposits of the former Algonquin Lake Plain, this nature reserve protects one of the few remaining remnant patches of tallgrass prairie in Ontario. The park was regulated in 1994.

## Land use intent

This nature reserve class park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act.
