## Land area description

This area is comprised of that part of Lake of the Woods south of the Aulneau Peninsula. There is a relatively small amount of development in this part of the lake. However, 7 lodges, 2 outposts and 5 Indian Reserves are located in this area and several more located on the mainland near Morson make extensive use of this portion of the Lake. The mainland shoreline on the eastern border of this area is administered by Fort Frances District. This area forms part of Lake of the Woods Township.Portions of the Lake of the Woods Islands Conservation Reserve are located in this area.Water Management Areas have been removed from this Policy Report area as revisions, to more accurately reflect First Nation reserve lands affected by flooding.

## Land use intent

This area will be managed as a very low density development area. The area supports an important part of the local tourist industry and management guidelines will be designed to ensure that this is not compromised. The overall intent is to keep the area relatively undisturbed and therefore, no disposition of Crown land will be considered.
