## Land area description

Goose Island lies in Rainy Lake approximately 12 km northeast of Fort Frances.Vegetation of Goose Island is typical of the transition area between the Great Lakes St. Lawrence and Boreal Forests, and includes Red Pine, White Pine, Red and Sugar Maple and Northern Pin Oak. Bedrock terrain best describes the geology of the site, with very little glacial deposits. The shoreline is primarily bedrock with some boulder/cobble areas

## Land use intent

The north part of the island (75.45 ha) is owned by Nature Conservancy Canada (NCC). In accordance with the legal lease agreement signed by NCC and MNR, the park will be regulated and managed as a nature reserve class park.
