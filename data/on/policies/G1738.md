## Land area description

This shoreline area encompasses the portion of Remi Lake not included within the provincial park. The developed area, Remi Peninsula, Lefebvre's Peninsula, Ouellette's Bay and the Air Base Road area are all included in this policy area. Remi Lake is a large, accessible lake located close to the Town of Kapuskasing and offers a wide range of recreation activity. Cottaging is a primary activity (approximately 640 cottages). Where roads are open to year-round traffic many cottages are being converted to permanent residents. Remi Lake also supports trailer parks, youth camps, municipal day-use areas, marina, sailing club, tourist outfitter and private sea plane base. It is also important for boating and fishing opportunities. This Ministry maintains one access point in the area and a regulating dam at the north end of the lake.

## Land use intent

The primary use within the area will continue to be a combination of cottaging public recreation and commercial tourism.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Kapuskasing District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
