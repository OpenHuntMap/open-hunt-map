## Land area description

Ouimet Canyon Provincial Park is located northwest of Dorion. Ouimet Canyon was regulated as a nature reserve class Provincial Park in 1972 to protect a steep-walled canyon and arctic-alpine disjunct plants.The park provides representation of a portion of the diabase-influenced landscape of the Thunder Bay-Nipigon area.

## Land use intent

The intent is to manage this park as a nature reserve class provincial park, in accordance with MNR and Ontario Parks Policy.
