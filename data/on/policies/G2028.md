## Land area description

This area represents the North Channel of Lake Huron west of Darch, Innes and Clapperton Islands and the area of Lake Huron around Cockburn and south and east of Manitoulin Island. The area does not include any of the mainland or Manitoulin Island. The major existing uses of this area are commercial fishing and commercial transportation; secondary activities include recreation along the near shore waters, especially in the large bays and around some of the islands close to Manitoulin. Fish production is the major, local natural resource capability.Coastal portions of this area are located within the Great Lakes Heritage Coast Signature Site, one of 9 such areas featured in the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999). Signature Sites are identified for their range of natural and recreational values and their potential to contribute to future recreation and tourism.

## Land use intent

MNR will provide sustainable fisheries for commercial fishery and angling. Emphasis will be directed to rehabilitate Lake Huron fishery to both commercial and sport fisheries.Nesting areas for cormorants, herons, gulls and eagles will continue to receive site specific protection from MNR. Islands and lands under the water have been withdrawn from staking.
