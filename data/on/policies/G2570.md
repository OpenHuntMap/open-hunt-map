## Land area description

The Mercutio area is located northeast of the Town of Atikokan. It is bounded on the north and west by the Seine River and on the south by Crooked Pine Lake.Existing uses include resource extraction (logging), and general recreation. Presently there are cottages and outpost camps in the area. A natural gas pipeline corridor is found along the western side of the area.This area has a high moose population and is attractive to hunters. The protection of the high moose population and the utilization of the area's timber resources are concerns which will have to be addressed in the management of this area.The following are designated as tourism lakes: Byers, Lodge, Mercutio, Elbow, Longross, Union and Old Man Lakes.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The area is designated for resource extraction and general recreation with the intention of maintaining the moose population.Areas of Concern will be applied to protect recreational and tourism development. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Atikokan District Land Use Guidelines (1983).

## Permitted uses — preface

In addition to provincial and regional policies, the following local policies will apply to the management of Natural lake trout lakes:- lakes shall have a 120 metre Area of Concern- timber harvesting will not be permitted within 120 metres
