## Land area description

Silent Lake is located in Site District 5E-11, in the lower Canadian Shield. The forest here is a mix of deciduous and coniferous trees, with birch, maple, hemlock and white pine being the most common species. A wide variety of wildflowers grows throughout the park, including a number of rare species. Otter, mink and beaver live in the marshy areas near the shoreline of the lake and deer can often be seen on higher ground.Silent Lake Provincial Park was regulated as a provincial park in 1977.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

This natural environment class provincial park was established to protect provincially significant earth and life science features and to provide recreational opportunities. A 169 ha addition to the park identified through the Ontario's Living Legacy Land Use Strategy was regulated in 2000.Silent Lake Provincial Park provides a variety of high quality, low intensity natural environment recreation experiences on a year-round basis, compatible with the protection of the park's provincially significant earth and life features.
