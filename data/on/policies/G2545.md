## Land area description

Located in the southeast portion of Dryden District, this area includes most of the Township of Ignace, and parts of Osaquan, Ilsley and Hodgson townships. Further to the south, this area borders the Turtle River-White Otter Lake Provincial Park. Also, there are five pictographs and/or historical sites here. The area is important for recreational purposes.The focal point is Agimak Lake, located almost entirely within Ignace township. Agimak Lake is noted for private cottaging and tourism developments and other mixed uses. There are a total of three tourist lodges, four remote cottages, and five outpost camps located in this area.The following are designated as tourism lakes: Agimak, NaMaygoos, Kinmoapiku, Raleigh, Spruce, Unnamed Lake WE67-10.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

No expansion of tourism will be allowed on Crown land. Cottaging will be considered on Agimak Lake. The nine sensitive areas noted above will be recognized and protected in resource management plans.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Ignace District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
