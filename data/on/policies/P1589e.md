## Land area description

Greenwater is an existing natural environment class park. It is located in the Great Claybelt, north of Highway 11 and 34 kilometres northwest of the town of Cochrane. The park encompasses an area of 5,350 hectares. The park is situated on a 61 metre high esker ridge which was formed less than 10,000 years ago by glacial fluvial actions.Greenwater Provincial Park was regulated as a provincial park in 1957.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.This area is subject to the Range Management Policy in Support of Woodland Caribou Conservation and Recovery (2014). The Range Management Policy is accessible via the “Source of Direction.”

## Land use intent

Greenwater Provincial Park will be managed according to the Provincial Park policy and Park Management Plan (1979). The park will provide recreational opportunities while protecting the significant natural and cultural values of the park.
