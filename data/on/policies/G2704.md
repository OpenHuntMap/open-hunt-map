## Land area description

The land use area includes all Crown land portions of Flatland Island and Pie Island.

## Land use intent

Pie Island and Flatland Island in Lake Superior have been selected for transfer to the federal government as part of the Fort William First Nation land claim settlement. The future intent of these islands is Indian Reserve. Until the transfer occurs, Ontario will carry out management of these islands consistent with this future intent.
