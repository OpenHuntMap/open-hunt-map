## Land area description

The primary uses occurring in this area are tourism, cottaging, fishing and hunting with forestry and mineral exploration occurring as secondary uses. The decline of the fisheries in some of the major lakes has had a detrimental effect on the local tourist industry. This area has large areas of forested Crown land and both a substantial number of warmwater lakes as well as coldwater lakes.Special features include:- the Ore Chimney Mine and the Marble Lake Stromatolites which are Areas of Natural and Scientific Interest (ANSI)- significant deer winter concentration areas in geographic township of Palmerston- high potential mineral development area in geographic township of Palmerston.- three significant waterfowl areas; Mud Lake, Dead Creek and a marsh in the geographic Township of TudorProblems and issues include a local concern over uranium exploration and local opposition to the commercial harvest of wild rice in Mud Lake (1983).This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Major emphasis will be given to enhancing outdoor recreation and tourism opportunities throughout Area B. Resource extraction such as forestry and sand and gravel operations will be permitted provided they do not unduly conflict with recreational uses. In areas where mineral resources potential has been identified exploration and development will be a primary use. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Tweed District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
