## Hunting

Not addressed in this policy.

Provincial season, licence, tag and firearm rules still apply, as do municipal discharge by-laws. Verify before hunting.

## Land area description

The addition of 1950 hectares of Wolf Lake Old Growth Forest Reserve F175 and 135 hectares of Chiniguchi Waterway Forest Reserve are recommended for addition to Chiniguchi Provincial Park. The addition of 2085 hectares of Forest Reserve will promote the protection of one of the largest remaining contiguous old growth red pine forests in Ontario. The addition will enhance life science representation in Ecodistrict 4E-4.

## Land use intent

The intent is to regulate the recommended additions under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act, 2006 as part of Chiniguchi Provincial Park.

## Permitted uses — preface

Management of this provincial park is carried out in accordance with Ontario Provincial Parks: Planning and Management Policies (1992), the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999) and the ChiniguchiWaterway Provincial Park Interim Management Statement (2007).

## Addendum

Management of this provincial park is carried out under the provisions of the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act and within the context of provincial park policy. Note: MNR will consider the Land Use Intent and Management Direction outlined in this policy report when reviewing applications for permitted activities that require licences, leases, permits, or other forms of approval. The review of individual applications involves the consideration of a variety of factors and requirements on a site-specific basis in addition to land use policy.
