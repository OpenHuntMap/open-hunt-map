## Land area description

Shallow River Provincial Nature Reserve is located in Wilkie Township, approximately 55 kilometres southeast of Cochrane. This tiny nature reserve is protected for its geological features associated with ancient Lake Wisconsin. Geologists will be most interested in a well-preserved, dual sequence of carved clay. It shows the Barlow-Ojibway Formation below, and the entire Frederickhouse and Connaught Formation above. Varied clay consists of alternating light and dark layers of sediment deposited annually by the lake. Carved clay's stripes can be counted to reveal the age of the deposit, similar to the rings of a tree.There are no park facilities.Shallow River Provincial Nature Reserve was regulated as a provincial park in 1985.

## Land use intent

Shallow River Provincial Nature Reserve will be managed according to Provincial Park policy and Interim Management Statement (1985). Management of the park will protect its significant geological features.
