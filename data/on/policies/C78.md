## Land area description

The upland mixed forest in this area provides representation of eleven combinations of vegetation and landforms, including spruce forests growing on rolling sandy hills and bare bedrock, as well as balsam fir, hemlock and white birch forests on low bedrock hills. This conservation reserve is in Site District 5E-8.The area is used for a variety of traditional uses, including hunting.Big Deer Lake was regulated as a conservation reserve on August 10, 2000.

## Land use intent

Consistent with the provincial policies for the management of Conservation Reserves, no new roads for resource extraction or private use will be permitted into or through this Conservation Reserve. Consistent with the traditional management of Crown lands in this area (as identified in the 1983 MNR Parry Sound District Land Use Guidelines) and consistent with the surrounding Enhanced Management Area E304a, public access for recreation purposes is limited to the access routes (roads and/or trails), and means of access used in 1983.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Land Use Strategy (1999).
