## Land area description

This area is located east of the English River and north of Meandering Lake. The area is largely cut over and accessed as a result of past timber harvesting . There are no outpost camps or lodges located in this area.

## Land use intent

The primary use of this area will be for forestry purposes. Tending of existing plantations and regenerated areas will be continued. Other uses will be continued, however, no new resource allocations will be considered.
