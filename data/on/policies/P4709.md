## Land area description

Serpent Mounds Provincial Park was established in 1957 and is located along Rice Lake. In 1983, Harris Island was added to the park by regulation. The mainland, or Roach Point, protects provincially significant burial mounds; since 1983 it has no longer been leased to Ministry of Natural Resources and has not operated as a provincial park. Harris Island (79 ha) protects natural and cultural heritage values.

## Land use intent

The intent is to deregulate the mainland of the park (i.e. lands that are not in ownership of the Crown). This cultural heritage class park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act.
