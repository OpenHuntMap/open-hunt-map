## Land area description

Grundy Lake Provincial Park is situated in the Great Lakes - St. Lawrence Forest, 65 kilometres south of Sudbury, in the northern portion of Ecodistrict 5E-7. Vegetation in the park represents a variety of communities including wetlands, upland forests, rock barrens and aquatic environments. The park‘s vegetation and landforms including rolling rockland with intervening flat deposits of sand with sugar maple, yellow birch stands and older balsam fir stands on low hills with sandy soils and also white pine dominated uplands. The park is represented by both Middle and Late Precambrian rocks.A variety of recreational activities occur in the park, including camping, canoeing, hunting, trapping and snowmobiling; a trap cabin is located in the area. The area is also used by First Nations for a variety of traditional activities.Grundy Lake Provincial Park is a natural environment class park and was originally regulated in 1959. An addition was made to the park in 2003 as a result of Ontario’s Living Legacy.

## Land use intent

Grundy Lake Provincial Park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act. Ontario Regulation No. 238/03, Schedule 2, delineates the boundary of the park.
