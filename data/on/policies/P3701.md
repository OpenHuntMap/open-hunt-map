## Land area description

The park lies on the transition zone between the boreal and sub-arctic forest. Fawn River Provincial Park extends both east and west of Big Trout Lake. The east side provides a 60 km canoe route of outstanding quality. The west side, which is 40 km, is slow-moving water with no obvious changes in elevation. It is very challenging due to several rock gardens. The park is accessible by float plane only. Wilderness and whitewater skills are requiredThe park contains representative features of eco-region 2W, as well as recreational opportunities.First Nation communities from Big Trout Lake and Angling Lake actively use the park for traditional natural resource harvesting activities. There are no facilities, but visitors may enjoy backcountry camping and canoeing. Several Aboriginal trapping and fishing camps are found here. Location: About 450 kilometres north of Sioux LookoutThe park was first regulated in 1989. The boundary was amended in 1998 and 2001.

## Land use intent

This waterway class park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act, 2006. All protected areas within the Far North of Ontario are subject to the provisions of the Far North Act 2010
