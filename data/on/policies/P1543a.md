## Hunting

Not addressed in this policy.

Provincial season, licence, tag and firearm rules still apply, as do municipal discharge by-laws. Verify before hunting.

## Land area description

Missinaibi Provincial Park protects a provincially significant waterway, over 500 kilometres long, with many natural and cultural heritage values. Missinaibi Lake, over 40 kilometres long, provides car camping facilities at the Barclay Bay campground, as well as boating and canoeing opportunities. Backcountry opportunities for canoeing and angling are found in Missinaibi and Little Missinaibi Lakes and along the entire river. The approved Missinaibi Provincial Park Management Plan (2004) committed to adding lands to the park. They include: 1) Adding or extending the park boundary to 200m on either side of the river in Ericson, Puskuta, Byng, Cromlech, Abbott, Rykert, Magladery, Orkney, Staunton, Devitt, Eilber, and Scovil Townships, four of which were acquired to ensure continuity of the park along the entire length of the river (Rykert Orkney, Magladery, and Staunton). 2) Adding significant lands, including biological, landform and historical sites, around Brunswick Lake in Ericson, Byng, and Cromlech Townships 3) Bringing the regulated area around Glassy Falls access zone, Staunton Township, out to 400 metres from the river. This boundary amendment does not include additions intended for the Far North (downriver as of Mahoney Township) that were outlined in the management plan.

## Land use intent

The intent is to regulate these areas as part of Missinaibi Provincial Park.

## Permitted uses — preface

Management of this recommended provincial park is carried out in accordance with the Missinabi Provincial Park Management Plan (2004) and interim protection measures. Uses currently permitted may continue unless park values are threatened. Changes in management direction will be determined through future management planning, including consultation.
