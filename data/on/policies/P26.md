## Land area description

This is a large scenic and semi-remote area in ecodistrict 5E-11, with high quality natural and recreational values. With the exception of some cottaging, much of thepark is undisturbed.The landscape is dominated by hemlock, sugar maple, red oak, white pine and red maple forests growing on thin soiled, bedrock hills and rugged ground moraine. Portions of the area contain rock barrens with scrubby growth and young forests. The rugged bedrock landscape contains numerous small lakes and wetlands, the shorelines of which contain rich wetlands with uncommon eastern and southern plant species. Thepark has high recreational values and contains a number of well-used canoe routes and snowmobile trails.Many of the lakes within the Kawartha Highlands Signature Site Park have a long-established community of cottages and year-round residences on private lands. Some Crown lands within the Kawartha Highlands are currently leased for private purposes, including hunt camps. There are also some mining lands. All of the Crown lands have been used for outdoor recreation for many years.Kawartha Highlands Signature Site Park was regulated on April 21, 2005This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The intent is to operate the Kawartha Highlands Signature Site Park as a natural environment class provincial park under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act and manage the park under the provisions of both the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act and Kawartha Highlands Signature Site Park Act, 2003. The Kawartha Highlands Signature Site Park Act, 2003 outlines site-specific policy and operational direction for the Kawartha Highlands Signature Site. The Act states that its purpose is to ensure: i) that the protection of the ecological integrity of the Kawartha Highlands Signature Site Park is recognized as the overriding priority in the management and administration of the park, so as to preserve, protect and enhance the natural composition and abundance of native species, biological communities and ecological processes in the park;ii) that the policies governing the park, including its management, will protect the park’s natural and cultural values, maintain its traditional uses and provide the opportunity for recreational activities that are compatible with the natural heritage values and semi-wilderness character of the park;iii) that the park will be managed so as to permit continued access to and enjoyment of private property and of Crown land that is subject to a land use permit, licence of occupation or lease under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act where that private property or Crown land is surrounded by park lands or abuts park lands; andiv) that decisions with respect to the development and any major revision of the management plan for the park are made with prior public consultation.

## Permitted uses — preface

Management for this provincial park will be carried out in accordance with direction provided by the Kawartha Highlands Signature Site Park Act, 2003. This Act applies to the full signature site, which includes the new protected area and the previously existing Kawartha Highlands Provincial Park. The Kawartha Highlands Signature Site Parkis also subject to the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act and associated regulations, however wherever the provisions of the Kawartha Highlands Signature Site Park Act, 2003 and the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act are in conflict, the provisions in the Kawartha Highlands Signature Site Park Act, 2003 prevail. The Kawartha Highlands Signature Site Charter provides some supplementary direction and clarification. Where a use is possible (“Yes” or “Maybe” on the permitted uses table), the long-term management direction will be determined through planning. Preparation of a management plan will be initiated within one calendar year of the regulation of the Kawartha Highlands Signature Site under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act.A Management Advisory Board will be established to advise the Minister of Natural Resources on the planning and management of the park. In particular, the Board will provide advice on:i) the identification of roads or trails that are to be approved as pre-existing roads and trails for the purposes of the Kawartha Highlands Signature Site Park Act, 2003;ii) the preparation of the management plan for the park;iii) advertising and marketing with respect to the park;iv) park fees; andv) matters relating to the long-term sustainability of the park Public consultation will be a key aspect of the ongoing planning and management of the park.
