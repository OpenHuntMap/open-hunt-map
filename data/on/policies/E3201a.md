## Land area description

The Remote Whitefeather Enhanced Management Area (EMA) includes several areas of theWhitefeather Forest described as Wavell Lake, Zellar Lake, and an area north of Zellar Lake. Theareas are characterized by gently rolling landscape features covered by coniferous boreal forest.Development is sparse, consisting of five outpost camps in total. The areas are significant toPikangikum First Nation’s cultural landscape, with natural and cultural values throughout. Culturallandscape waterways including the Berens and Throat Rivers course through, or provide naturalboundaries for these areas. There are customary winter and summer travel routes connecting thecommunity of Pikangikum to traditional territories. There is the potential for interest in a roadcorridor to access northern First Nations. Sections of Provincially Significant Mineral Potential arepresent, however there are currently no mining claims. Woodland caribou are found throughoutthe Whitefeather Forest; there are caribou calving islands and travel routes in the EMA areas.First Nation people conduct various traditional activities on this land for livelihood and culturalpurposes.

## Land use intent

The primary intent for the Remote Whitefeather Enhanced Management Area is to contribute to anew forestry opportunity while supporting remoteness objectives by implementing a temporaryaccess road policy. Forestry activities will be determined through an environmental assessmentprocess and subsequent forest management planning . Activities associated with the mineralsector, eco-cultural tourism, and non-timber forest products will be encouraged.No permanent road access will be created. Temporary access into the area will be carefullyplanned to prevent access into sensitive areas and to consider the values of the area that supportwoodland caribou populations.
