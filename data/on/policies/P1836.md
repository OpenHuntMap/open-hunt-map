## Land area description

Pushkin Hills Provincial Nature Reserve is located approximately 100 kilometres northeast of the Town of Kirkland Lake in Ben Nevis Township.What distinguishes this small, hilly nature reserve is a very good section of volcanic rock, part of what is termed the Blake River Group. Made of cooled lava, the pumice rocks exposed here are well preserved despite their age.There are no park facilities.Pushkin Hills Provincial Nature Reserve was regulated as a provincial park in 1985.

## Land use intent

Pushkin Hills Provincial Park will be managed according to Provincial Park policy and Park Management Plan (1988). Pushkin Hills Provincial Nature Reserve will protect the provincially significant bedrock geology, which contains outstanding examples of pumice lava. The management of the park will protect its natural features while providing opportunities for research.
