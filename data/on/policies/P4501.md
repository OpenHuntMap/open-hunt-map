## Land area description

DuPont Provincial Park is located along the St. Lawrence River east of the village of Morrisburg. It contains mature deciduous forests representative of the historical forests of the area, and the deciduous swamp found on the property supports one of the largest heronries in south eastern Ontario. DuPont is rich in species diversity, and contains a number of provincially rare species. The park also protects part of Hoasic Creek, one of the last remaining natural creeks in the area.Dupont Provincial Park was regulated as a provincial park in 2011.

## Land use intent

Dupont Provincial Park will be managed as a nature reserve class park according to provincial park policy and the Dupont Provincial Park Management Plan (2009).

## Permitted uses — preface

The permitted uses associated with Dupont Provincial Park are found in the approved park management plan.. MNR intends to protect the natural and cultural features of DuPont Provincial Park and to maintain and develop opportunities for heritage appreciation for the residents of Ontario and visitors to the Province.The permitted uses associated with provincial parks are found in approved management direction, which are available on the Ontario Parks website.
