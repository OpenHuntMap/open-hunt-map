## Land area description

Minnitaki Kames Provincial Park is located south of Minnitaki in Ignace Area. Minnitaki Kames was regulated as a nature reserve class park in 1989 to protect the a group of five large kame features. These features represent the largest kame deposits in northwestern Ontario with added significance for the numerous raised shoreline features found on their slopes.The park provides representation of Kame formations related to glacial Lake Agassiz with excellent potential for representing landform-vegetation features which are not widespread in the site region.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The intent is to manage this park as a nature reserve class provincial park, in accordance with MNR and Ontario Parks Policy.
