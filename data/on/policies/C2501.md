## Hunting

Not addressed in this policy.

Provincial season, licence, tag and firearm rules still apply, as do municipal discharge by-laws. Verify before hunting.

## Land area description

The waters adjacent to Sable Islands Provincial Nature Reserve in the southern portion of Lake of the Woods provide continuity of protection from the mainland to the offshore islands. Important fisheries and wildlife habitat occur in the area.

## Land use intent

The Lake of the Woods Waters Conservation Reserve was regulated on June 20, 2006.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Land Use Strategy (1999).
