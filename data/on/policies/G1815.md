## Land area description

The area is accessed by Highway 66W, Highway 560 and forest access roads, notably the Englehart Management Unit Roads (North and South). The area is sparsely settled. Small portions of the organized Township of James and the Improvement District of Matachewan are in the area.Forestry is the major activity. The Englehart Crown Management Unit and parts of four other forest management units are located in this area. This area supplies the partial wood requirements for five sawmills. Some high mineral potential exists. Commercial blueberry harvesting occurs in the area. The area includes parts of two canoe routes(Watabeag River and Englehart River), about 80 cottages, one access point, the Swastika Tree Nursery, the Hill's Lake, and Provincial Fish Hatchery.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The intent of this area is to provide primarily for resource extraction activities, particularly forestry. Other extractive activities and general recreation activities such as cottaging and canoeing are secondary uses.Where applicable, development will be encouraged to conform to municipal land use policies and controls.All development proposals will consider the possible effects on the water supply and quality for the Hills Lake Fish Hatchery.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Kirkland Lake District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
