## Land area description

The 239 hectare Port Burwell Provincial Park is regulated under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act (2006) and located in the County of Elgin within the Township of Bayham. The park, located in Ecodistrict 7E-2, contains significant elements of the Norfolk Sand Plain, the Lake Erie Bluff, beach meadows and dune succession. The park offers excellent opportunities for car and group camping, day use recreation opportunities, including a 2.5km long beach, and interpretive programming from July 1st to Labour Day. Port Burwell Provincial Park was first regulated in 1971. The park boundary was amended in 2017 to correct a boundary error to exclude municipally owned lands and to remove roadways that provide access to private properties. These roadways were transferred to the municipality.

## Land use intent

Port Burwell Provincial Park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Ac, 2006. The current boundary of the park is described in Ontario Regulation No. 316/07, Table 1.Port Burwell Provincial Park is regulated as a recreational class park to provide a wide variety of compatible outdoor recreation opportunities in attractive natural surroundings.
