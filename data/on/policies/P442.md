## Land area description

Strawberry Island Provincial Park (Nature Reserve Class) is located off the northeast side of Manitoulin Island, and is only accessible by water. It lies approximately two kilometres (by water) east of Little Current and 120 kilometres west of Sudbury. Little Current is accessible from Highway 6 south of Espanola, south of Highway 17.Strawberry Island contains significant natural heritage areas, including provincially significant wetland and provincially significant alvar communities. Because it is only accessible by boat, and was previously private land, it has very low levels of visitation and use. There are three small parcels of land on the island adjacent to the park: two private parcels and one piece of federal land (lighthouse).Strawberry Island was acquired by the Nature Conservancy of Canada (NCC) on April 30, 2002, and transferred to the Crown in 2008. Strawberry Island Provincial Park was regulated on March 20, 2014 under the Provincial Parks and Conservation reserves Act, 2006.

## Land use intent

Strawberry Island will be managed as a nature reserve class provincial park to protect provincially significant, special and representative natural heritage values of the Manitoulin Island Eco-district. Scientific research is encouraged.

## Permitted uses — preface

Management of this provincial park will be carried out in accordance with Ontario Provincial Parks: Planning and Management Policies,1992, A Class Environmental Assessment for Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves, 2005, and the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act, 2006. This direction will ensure custodial management of park resources, until such time as a park management planning process is conducted, including public and Aboriginal involvement. At that time, significant decisions regarding resource stewardship, operations, development and permitted uses will be made.The permitted uses associated with provincial parks are found in approved management direction, which are available on the Ontario Parks website.
