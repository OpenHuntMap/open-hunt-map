## Land area description

The scenic, rugged, rocky terrain and open stretches of water and small craft harbours in this area attract recreationists in the pursuit of motor boating, sailing, hiking, angling, hunting and viewing opportunities. Much of the shoreline is included in Killarney Coast and Islands Provincial Park. Conflicts between sport and commercial fishermen have arisen primarily in the vicinity of the waters open to commercial fishing.This area is located within the Killarney Signature Site and the Great Lakes Heritage Coast Signature Site, one of 9 such areas featured in the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999). Signature Sites are identified for their range of natural and recreational values and their potential to contribute to future recreation and tourism.

## Land use intent

Public recreation will continue to be the primary use. Private recreation and commercial fishing activities are important secondary uses. Commercial aggregate, timber extraction and development, as well as further road access will be prohibited. The mining rights to the lands under the waters of the Great Lakes were withdrawn from staking under an Order-in-Council in 1912 and the islands in Georgian Bay and the French River Mouth area were withdrawn under the Mining Act in 1926. In those areas not affected by the 1912 and 1926 withdrawals of the mining rights to the lands under the waters of the Great Lakes and the islands in Georgian Bay, including the French River mouth area, on-shore mining developments may expand underground, outward under the Great Lakes, subject to existing international agreements. In the remainder of the area, generally no further Crown land disposition will be permitted. Green Island, in Georgian Bay, will continue to be recognized as an important heronry. The island will remain under Crown ownership.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Sudbury District Guidelines (1983).
