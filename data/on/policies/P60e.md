## Land area description

Bonnechere Provincial Park is located within the Grenville Province of the Precambrian Shield. The topography of the park is attributed to the last ice age and glacial Lake Wisconsin, which covered the present day Round and Golden Lakes. The park is mostly flat with the exception of an old beach ridge, a remnant of the glacial lake.The park is located in Site District 5E-10 and is within the Middle Ottawa section of the Great Lakes – St. Lawrence Forest Region. Two forest communities dominate Bonnechere Park. White Pine is found on the well drained delta sands and the community of red maple, basswood and bur oak is found in the cool, wet lowlands of the Bonnechere River.Bonnechere Provincial Park was regulated as a provincial park in 1971.

## Land use intent

Bonnechere Provincial Park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act. The boundary of the park is delineated by Ontario Regulation No. 179/94, Schedule 92.
