## Land area description

Duncan Escarpment Provincial Nature Reserve is situated on the Niagara Escarpment in the Town of Blue Mountains. The park is part of the Niagara Escarpment parks System and the Niagara Biosphere Reserve. The park is located 16 km south of Thornbury and approximately 170 km north of Toronto. The park was previously named Duncan Crevice Caves Provincial Nature Reserve.Due to seasonal extremes in temperature and the forces of gravity, large blocks of rock have broken away from the main face of the Niagara Escarpment. The gradual shifting downwards of these huge blocks has produced the crevice caves of the Collingwood-Blue Mountain area, including this park. The park's range of microclimates supports interesting cliff face vegetation communities. Duncan Escarpment Park was regulated in 1985

## Land use intent

This nature reserve class park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act.
