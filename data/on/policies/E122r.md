## Land area description

This area supports high recreation and tourism values and was formerly part of the McConnellLakes Recreation Area. A commercial main base camp is located on Troutbait Lake. An outpostcamp on Emerald Lake (within C134- God's Lake Old Growth White Pine Forest) is locatedsouthwest of Troutbait Lake.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the 'Source of Direction' portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Manage this area to protect high quality recreation and tourism values, and for its high qualityforest values and other resource values.More specifically, Troutbait Lake Area is to be managed for commercial and day-use interestsonly. Limited resource extraction may occur within the area. The importance of Troutbait Lake tothe local tourism industry is recognized.

## Permitted uses — preface

Recreation enhanced management areas have been applied to areas with high recreational use of significant recreation values for activities such as angling, hunting, motorized and pedestrian trail use, and canoeing. The enhanced management area can be used for a wide rang of recreation values and management intents, ranging form areas where relatively substantial recreation development is permitted, to areas providing low-density, high-quality recreation in a natural setting.Where the Recreation enhanced management area has been identified to protect remote recreation values, all activities need to be carried out in such a way as to maintain or enhance the remote recreation qualities.
