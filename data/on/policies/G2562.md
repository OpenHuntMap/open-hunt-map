## Land area description

The area is located on either side of Highway 11 east and west of the Town of Atikokan.The area is heavily developed and present uses include general recreation and servicing, tourism and resource extraction. There are over 400 cottages and more than 20 commercial tourist lodges, most of which are along or near Highway 11. The area can be described as a cottaging and tourism corridor. Part of the Highway 11 area lies in the Crossroute Forest and part within the Sapawe Forest. Several hydro line corridors and the corridors for Highways 11, 623 and 632 are found in this area.A hydro generating station and dam is located at the west end of Calm Lake. Power from this facility is supplied to Fort Frances. A private gravel airstrip is located just north of Crystal Lake. A sawmill is located within the area at Sapawe. A number of rural residences are located along Highway 11, at Sapawe, Kawene and south of Eva Lake.A protected use area requested by Quetico Centre is a concern as is the environmental effect on the area by the Atikokan Generating Station. An increase in the use of cottages year round and conversion to permanent residences concerns the Township. A significant loss of productive land occurred in the area from the construction of several hydro lines, a railway, Highway 11 and many access roads.Windigoostigwan Lake is designated as a tourism lake.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The area is designated for cottaging, public recreation, commercial tourist development and resource extraction. All recreational and tourism developments will be protected by the application of Areas of Concern. Quetico Centre will be protected by the application of policies and guidelines noted below. The watershed of Quetico Provincial Park is an Area of Concern. Resource management and land use activities in the watershed will be carried out in consideration of the protection of values within the park. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Atikokan District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
