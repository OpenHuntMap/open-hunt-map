## Land area description

Dividing Lake Provincial Nature Reserve is located in the eastern part of Livingstone Township in the County of Haliburton. Algonquin Provincial Park bounds it to the east. The site is in Site District 5E-9.The Nature Reserve was created to protect one of the last remaining stands of primary growth forest in Ontario, and protects giant white pines up to 490 years in age. The park lies in an area of ground moraine landform and has a rugged landscape of granite rockland with low cliffs.The park was regulated in 1985.

## Land use intent

Dividing Lake Provincial Nature Reserve is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act. The boundary of the park is delineated by a metes and bounds description. Ontario Regulation No. 85/01, Schedule 171.
