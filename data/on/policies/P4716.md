## Land area description

Carden Alvar Provincial Park is an area of high ecological diversity. There are 16 ecosites and 30 ecotypes recorded in Carden Alvar Provincial Park. The ecosites can be classified into four broad groups: alvar, cultural, forest, and wetland ecosites. The alvar ecosites are among the site’s most significant natural heritage features because of their global and provincial rarity. The park, located in Ecodistrict 6E-9 (Madoc), supports a number of significant flora and fauna. The park is situated on the flat to undulating plains of Middle Ordovician limestone of the Simcoe Group (Trenton and Black River), Bobcaygeon Formation. Carden Alvar Provincial Park is composed of three properties: Cameron Ranch, Windmill Ranch, and the Katherine McGuaig MacDonald Nature Sanctuary. The Cameron Ranch and Windmill Ranch properties were purchased by the Nature Conservancy of Canada (NCC) in 2003 and 2006, respectively, with the support of local partners, the Couchiching Conservancy, and Ontario Parks. The Katherine McGuaig MacDonald Nature Sanctuary was donated to the NCC in 1998 by the MacDonald Family and is named in honour of the donor’s grandmother.

## Land use intent

The intent is to manage this property as a natural environment class provincial park and protect its high ecological diversity including the globally rare alvar habitat.
