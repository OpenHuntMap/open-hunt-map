## Land area description

This area is located within the Matabitchuan River/Temagami River watershed. The values and uses of this area include extensive tree plantations, rural residential and tourism operations near Highway 11, a well-used accessible angling and hunting area as well as the existing and heritage portages.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Inland Ontario Lakes Designated for Lake Trout Management (2006) accessible in the Source of Direction portion of this policy report.NOTE: Part or all of this Management Area contains lands set aside pending resolution of the Temagami area aboriginal land claim. These lands will not be re-opened for staking and will not be included in the forestry land base.

## Land use intent

The land use intent is to provide opportunities for forestry, mining related activities, and aggregates as well as to maintain motorized and non-motorized recreation activities. The intent is also to rehabilitate lake trout populations and enhance wildlife habitat.The concerns of this area include the aesthetics for tourism, cottaging, residential, and recreation as well as the use of portages as ATV trails (conflicts with non-motorized recreation). The management area objectives include maintaining resource extraction with planned public motorized access and existing canoe routes as well as retaining potential for new routes. The objectives also include protecting self-sustaining lake trout populations in Net, Kanichee, Chambers lakes as well as determining potential for new fishery in Lenore Lake through a Class Environmental Assessment.The strategies include applying viewscape management and Area of Concern planning for extractive activities. A strategy was developed to prohibit further use of existing portage trails by ATVs within this management area as part of the Crown Land Recreation Plan Temagami Integrated Planning process.

Land use and resource management activities for this area are also governed by the policies of the Temagami Land Use Plan (TLUP) and the Crown Land Recreation Plan Temagami Integrated Planning. The policies from the TLUP are integrated into this area report. Refer to the TLUP for background information and additional details.
