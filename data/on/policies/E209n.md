## Land area description

This area of earth science significance in site district 4E-3 is an esker and delta landscape that marks the interaction between the continental glaciers and glacial lake Algonquin. The conifer stands here are used as later winter cover for moose, the pockets of wetlands in the depressions are also important moose aquatic feeding areas, and cold-water streams emerge from the site.

## Land use intent

This is an important recreational and resource sector (forestry, mining) area. Forest management and aggregate extraction permitted (including controlled expansion of existing pits), provided that they are consistent with the protection of earth science natural heritage values.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Land Use Strategy (1999) and the Espanola District Land Use Guidelines (1983).

## Permitted uses — preface

Natural heritage enhanced management area are intended to protect areas with significant natural values, while allowing a range of resource activities.
