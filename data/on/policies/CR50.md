## Land area description

The Pinetorch Lake Conservation Reserve is located west of the town of Temagami within the Obabika River/Yorston River watershed. The values and uses within the reserve include a significant backcountry canoe route linking two conservation reserves and a low-use area providing challenging wilderness recreation opportunities. There are also many scenic cliffs and old growth forests within the reserve.The Pinetorch Lake Conservation Reserve was regulated on June 25, 2004.

## Land use intent

The intent of the Pinetorch Lake Conservation Reserve is to protect a significant wilderness canoe route where resource extraction activities do not occur. Land use concerns include the use of portages as ATV trails and the access provided to a portion of Landers Lake for forest management. The management area objectives are to restrict public motorized use, to maintain wilderness experiences for backcountry recreation users, and to allow natural processes to occur. The land use strategies are to allow a tertiary road crossing of the Pinetorch Lake Conservation Reserve, if necessary, to access a portion of Landers Lake (southwest corner) which is to be rehabilitated when no longer required. The strategy also includes developing a strategy to address potential ATV use within the reserve.
