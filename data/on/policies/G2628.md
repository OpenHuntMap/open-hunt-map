## Land area description

This area has traditionally been popular for Crown land recreation such as fishing, hunting and camping. Forestry operations have occurred in the past and there is potential for additional harvesting and mining activity.There are several known eagle nesting sites.Existing development levels are sparse but there is potential for cottage development on eight lakes in the area.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The watershed of Quetico Provincial Park is an Area of Concern. Resource management and land use activities in the watershed will be carried out in consideration of the protection of values within the park. In this area public and private recreation and resource production will have equal emphasis.Operating guidelines for resource extraction activities will consider other resource values, and recreational land uses as well as economic extraction factors. As demand warrants cottages may be provided by lake development plans for Weikwabinonaw, McGrath, Hoof, Blunder, Marks, Chambers and Swallow.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Thunder Bay District Land Use Guideline (1983).
