## Land area description

This area contains mixed stands of coniferous and deciduous trees. Topography is gently rollingin the southeast portion. This weakly broken plain gives way to moderately broken uplands in thecentral and northern portions. The dominant glacial feature is the McConnell Lake moraine.Drainage tends mainly eastward to the Ottawa River. Natural springs occur in the northwestcorner of McAuslan Township. Big Webb, Little Web, Opimika and Otter (brook trout lakes) aswell as one significant brook trout creek (Opimika) provide fishing opportunity for the area.Shallow warm water lakes provide a variety of angling opportunities.The McLaren Bay Area is densely developed with several cottages and an access point. The entire area is unorganized. This area abuts Jocko River and Ottawa River provincial parks and God's Lake Old Growth Conservation Reserve.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the 'Source of Direction' portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The Land Use Intent is primarily resource extraction with recreational activities (e.g. hunting, fishing, wilderness camping) as secondary use. Resource management direction should protect the natural springs occurring in the northeast corner of McAuslan Township. Otter, Big Webb and Little Web Lakes will be managed as low use brook trout fisheries. Any proposed planning on activities adjacent to any provincial park or conservation reserve will consider the management intent and direction of these protected areas.MNR is responsible for the management of Crown land and natural resources in unorganized townships.
