## Land area description

This area follows the English River designated canoe route from the hamlet of English River on Highway 17 to the east boundary of Upper English River Conservation Reserve. Recreational facilities presently in the area are; two tourist lodges, several private cottages and a private recreation camp. The area is important for local and non-resident hunting and fishing.The following are designated as tourism lakes: SelwynPart of this area is subject to the Range Management Policy in Support of Woodland Caribou Conservation and Recovery (2014). The Range Management Policy is accessible via the “Source of Direction.””

## Land use intent

The primary purpose of this area is to provide non-wilderness recreation opportunities. Resource management plans will recognize and protect the recreational values in the area. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Ignace District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
