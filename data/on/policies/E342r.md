## Land area description

This area is within the Wabi River/Montreal River/Blanche River watershed. The values and uses of this area include Lepha Lake which is the only low use natural lake trout lake in the north-eastern portion of the planning area.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Inland Ontario Lakes Designated for Lake Trout Management (2006) accessible in the Source of Direction portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The land use intent is to permit forestry and mining related activities while restricting new public motorized access in order to maintain the area's trail-based recreational opportunities (ATV and non-motorized uses such as dog sledding).The concerns include the potential for conflicts among resource extraction operations and motorized and non-motorized recreational uses.The management area objectives are to retain backcountry recreational opportunities where available, to restrict public motorized access with resource extraction, and to permit identified ATV use, but not allow the access area to expand or be upgraded to public truck/car access.The strategies include planning access and developing road use strategies to restrict public motorized access outside of existing ATV areas while MNR is to facilitate discussions between motorized and non-motorized users to identify appropriate areas for non-motorized winter use and to develop appropriate management strategies.. The strategy for the area also includes developing a fisheries management strategy for Lepha Lake.

## Permitted uses — preface
