## Land area description

Extending south from the outer limits of the Bustard Islands, this area includes the waters of Georgian Bay within the Sudbury District. These waters are important for commercial and recreational travel and fishing. Commercial fishermen from Parry Sound and Killarney fish these waters for whitefish, perch, chub, menominee and walleye. Commercial vessels and deep water recreational craft traverse these waters. The recreational boaters are primarily in search of the open stretches of water, spectacular scenery, and the excellent harbours scattered throughout the adjacent 30,000 Islands, French River Mouth - Georgian Bay shoreline, Killarney area, and the waters of the North Channel of Lake Huron. This area is adjacent to French River Provincial Park.This area is located within the Great Lakes Heritage Coast Signature Site, one of 9 such areas featured in the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999). Signature Sites are identified for their range of natural and recreational values and their potential to contribute to future recreation and tourism.

## Land use intent

Public recreation activities, including boating and sport fishing, and commercial fishing will continue to be the primary uses of these off-shore waters.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Sudbury District Guidelines (1983).
