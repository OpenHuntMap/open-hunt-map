## Land area description

The Aulneau Peninsula is located in the centre of Lake of the Woods and is largely undeveloped. There are presently 2 outpost camps on the peninsula. Access is limited to water and air. There are 5 Indian Reserves on the peninsula, none of which are inhabited year round.The peninsula contains archaeological sites, wildlife habitat and old growth forest communities. A wildlife management plan is under development.Water Management Areas have been removed from this Policy Report area as revisions, to more accurately reflect First Nation reserve lands affected by flooding.

## Land use intent

The Fish and Wildlife category has been used for areas that are managed for the maintenance and enhancement of fish and wildlife habitat and populations, while allowing for the multiple use of other natural resources. The criteria for selection of these areas include:-areas of known or potential fish or wildlife habitat for species that require special management practices;-areas where forestry practices can be applied to enhance or maintain special habitat requirements; or,-areas where enhanced hunting and fishing opportunities may be provided through specific management and land use practices. One or more of these criteria may apply to a specific Recreation Enhanced Management Area.The Aulneau Peninsula Enhanced Management Area has been designated as a special wildlife management area. All activities will be conducted so as to maintain and enhance habitat and maximize primitive hunting and viewing opportunities. In addition, the wilderness character of the centre core of the peninsula will be maintained; the Aulneau Interior Conservation Reserve is located in part of this area.Outpost camps will be phased out and patented land will be acquired over the long term. The area is to be accessed only via winter roads. Low intensity public recreation will be emphasized and the existing remoteness of the area will be maintained.Wildlife habitat will be supported by forest management activities. Resource management will be directed by the Aulneau Peninsula Wildlife Management Plan. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Land Use Strategy (1999)
