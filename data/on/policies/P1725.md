## Land area description

Hicks-Oke Bog Provincial Nature Reserve is located in Hicks and Oke Townships, approximately 72 kilometres northwest of Timmins and in the vicinity of the Groundhog River. Hicks-Oke Bog Provincial Nature Reserve is a large peatland area which contains significant organic deposits. Features include valley bogs, graminoid bogs, treed bogs and shrub bogs.There are no park facilities.Hicks-Oke Bog Provincial Nature Reserve was regulated as a provincial park in 1994.This area is subject to the Range Management Policy in Support of Woodland Caribou Conservation and Recovery (2014). The Range Management Policy is accessible via the “Source of Direction.”

## Land use intent

Hicks-Oke Bog Provincial Nature Reserve will be managed according to Provincial Park policy. Management of the park will protect its natural features.
