## Land area description

This policy report applies to the Holland Marsh Provincial Wildlife Area located north of Bradford, on the west bank of the Holland River at the tip of the southern arm of Lake Simcoe. It is situated in the Township of Bradford West Gwillimbury, Simcoe County. The property was acquired in the 1950s and 1960s to protect habitat for wildlife and provide opportunities for resource-based recreation including waterfowl hunting.The area is identified as a Provincially Significant Life Science Area of Natural and Scientific Interest. It contains a Provincially Significant Wetland and is comprised of marsh, swamp, fen and bog. The area provides opportunities for wildlife viewing and waterfowl hunting.The Provincial Wildlife Area is located within the boundaries of the Lake Simcoe Protection Plan, Greenbelt Plan and the A Place to Grow: Growth Plan for the Greater Golden Horseshoe. The lands contribute to the Natural Heritage System identified under these provincial plans.

## Land use intent

This Provincial Wildlife Area is managed specifically for wildlife and to provide opportunities for a variety of outdoor recreation activities, particularly hunting and wildlife viewing. The Crown land area will be managed in an ecologically sustainable way to ensure that natural resources will be available for future generations. The Crown land area is to be used responsibly to help protect the natural environment, and with respect for other users. Crown lands are generally unmaintained, support a variety of uses and should be used with an understanding and acceptance of the associated risks.
