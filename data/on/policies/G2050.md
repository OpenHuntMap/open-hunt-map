## Land area description

This area is accessed by a number of roads, the Canadian National Railway line and two Federal Government docks at Boland's Bay and Portage Bay, as well as numerous water access points, all of which have influenced the pattern of land uses around the Lake. Land use activities, including mineral exploration and development in the south-eastern and northeastern areas, have increased in the last few years. Aggregate extraction has also occurred. The area is predominantly held under private ownership.A number of small, scattered, private parcels, including some islands in West Bay and Portage Bay, are used primarily for summer cottages and commercial tourist establishments. A few mining operations occur in Scadding, Rathbun and Norman Townships.Thin soils, flood hazard and wave damage along the shoreline place constraints on seasonal residential development and garbage and sewage disposal. Garbage, water pollution and sand bluff erosion problems on the north shore of the Lake have also been identified.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Public and private recreational uses and forestry will continue to dominate the land uses in this area. Within that area referred to as Industrial Mineral Extraction Area in the official plan for the Greater City of Sudbury, other uses, including urban, rural residential, forestry, wildlife management, etc. which do not preclude future extraction, may also be permitted. Where conflicts occur between recreation and resource extraction activities, the impact on recreation values will be minimized, wherever possible.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Sudbury District Guidelines (1983).
