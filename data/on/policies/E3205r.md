## Land area description

The Pine Ridge Enhanced Management Area surrounds the north shore of an unnamed lake, 25km northwest of Red Lake. The southern shore of the unnamed lake is within the Ontario’s LivingLegacy Land Use Planning Area. All-weather road access to the southwest shore of this lake isprovided by the Pine Ridge Road; it is primarily used for recreation purposes.The unnamed lake has potential to provide high quality recreational opportunities for angling,boating, recreation lodging (e.g. camping, backcountry recreation). In addition there are beachescapable of supporting family beach activities. Management boundaries relevant to the areainclude a baitfish block, a trapline, and a wildlife management unit.

## Land use intent

The intent is to provide new opportunities in recreation while sustaining high quality fish andwildlife resources. The area will support the appreciation of the landscape by visitors. Activitiesassociated with the mineral sector are permitted.The recreation enhanced management area category is applied to this area to encouragerecreational use of land and resource values that support activities such as angling, hunting,motorized and pedestrian trail use, and canoeing. To achieve the area’s intent, further additionalplanning may be recommended to consider activities, facilities, and access interests. A cooperativeapproach will be taken with interested parties.
