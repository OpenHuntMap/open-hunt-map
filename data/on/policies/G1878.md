## Land area description

Urban areas are generally located along the Highway 17 corridor east of Sault Ste. Marie and also at Elliot Lake. This area includes the built up portions of established municipalities and other urban developments which are situated outside of municipal boundaries. These include the following communities: Nestorville, Thessalon, Little Rapids, Iron Bridge, Blind River, Algoma Mills, Spragge, Serpent River and Elliot Lake.This area is located within the Great Lakes Heritage Coast Signature Site, one of 9 such areas featured in the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999). Signature Sites are identified for their range of natural and recreational values and their potential to contribute to future recreation and tourism.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Land uses within this area are generally restricted to those related to residential, industrial, commercial and other uses which are associated with urban development. Land uses are usually regulated through municipal official plans and controlled through zoning by-laws in organized areas. Land uses in unorganized areas may be controlled through zoning orders administered by the Ministry of Municipal Affairs and Housing.Generally, Crown lands within urban areas will be disposed of only for those uses which are compatible with the existing urban development. Provincial and municipal land disposition policies will provide additional guidance in this regard.With few exceptions, urban areas have not been considered in the evaluation of the achievability of Ministry objectives and targets.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Blind River District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
