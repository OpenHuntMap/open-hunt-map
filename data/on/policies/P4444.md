Not addressed in this policy.

Provincial season, licence, tag and firearm rules still apply, as do municipal discharge by-laws. Verify before hunting.

## Land area description

Fitzroy Provincial Park was established in 1963. Located along the Ottawa River, this park protects river shoreline, cultural heritage features and mixed forest. Recreational values include camping, hiking, boating, swimming and fishing along the Carp and Ottawa Rivers.

## Land use intent

This recreational class park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act.
