## Land area description

Area A is located in the northern portion of the Mazinaw Area which is 75 percent Crown Land. Approximately 60 percent of this area is under Order-In-Council Crown timber licences and forestry is the dominant land use. However, provincial park recreation, hunting, fishing, back-country travel and cottaging also are significant uses. Special features include:- significant deer winter concentration areas in the geographic Townships of Ashby, Denbigh, Miller and North and South Canonto- one significant waterfowl area, Robinson Marsh.- Fortune-Schooner Lake system which is regionally significant in terms of its scenic values- Bon Echo Provincial Park, a natural environment park, which contains the largest concentration of Indian rock paintings found in Ontario.Due to the significant number of deer concentration areas and trout lakes and streams and the intensive forest management which occurs in this area, there is potential for conflict between forestry and fish and wildlife programs.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

This is a significant wood production area in the district. MNR's program emphasis will be Crown land forest management. However, in the important wildlife resource areas forest activity will be modified, restricted or, if necessary, prohibited.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Tweed District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
