## Land area description

Frederick House Lake Provincial Nature Reserve encompasses 13 hectares on the northwest shore of Fredrick House Lake. Located within the park is an internationally recognized stratigraphic type section for the Connaught varved clays of the Barlow-Ojibwa formation.Accessible by water only, the park has no visitor facilities.Frederick House Lake Provincial Nature Reserve was regulated as a provincial park in 1985.

## Land use intent

Fredrick House Lake Provincial Park will be managed according to Provincial Park policy and Interim Management Statement (1985). The park will protect natural and cultural values.

## Permitted uses — preface

Management of this provincial park is carried out in accordance with Ontario Provincial Parks: Planning and Management Policies (1992), the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999) and the 1985 Interim Management Statement. The Interim Management Statement provides direction to ensure custodial management of park resources until such time as a park management planning process is conducted, which includes public review of proposals. At that time, significant decisions regarding resource stewardship, operations, development and permitted uses are made.
