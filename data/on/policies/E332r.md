## Land area description

This area is located within the Lake Timiskaming/Matabitchuan River watershed. The values and uses of this area include the Beaver Mountain lookout, potential for backcountry recreation opportunities, Cooper Lake cold-water fishery, and the mature white and red pine stands. The Ottawa-Temiskaming Highland Trail also passes through this management area and provides significant recreational and natural heritage appreciation opportunities.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Inland Ontario Lakes Designated for Lake Trout Management (2006) accessible in the Source of Direction portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The land use intent of this area is to manage an isolated rugged area to provide remote non-motorized backcountry recreational opportunities (e.g. hiking trails) and to provide opportunities for forestry and mining related activities.The concerns include the aesthetics management for Lake Timiskaming and the use of portages as ATV trails (conflicts with non-motorized recreation).The objectives of this management area include limiting public motorized recreational access to identified ATV areas and retaining potential for remote non-motorized trail opportunities. Additional objectives include protecting existing tourism values such as viewscapes, trails, and tourism facilities as well as managing Crown land forests to retain old growth characteristics.The strategies include mitigating impacts on backcountry recreation and remote tourism by not permitting public motorized road use maintain the potential for an appropriate shoreline hiking trail corridor through the identification of appropriate trail corridor and associated AOC. Additionally, strategies include managing for old growth characteristics when harvesting white and red pine, applying viewscape and Area of Concern planning for extractive activities, and developing road use strategies to restrict public motorized access and minimize conflicts. A strategy was developed to prohibit further use of existing portage trails by ATVs within this management area as part of the Crown Land Recreation Plan Temagami Integrated Planning process.
