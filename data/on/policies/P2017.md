## Land area description

Chutes Provincial Park is located north of Highway 553, adjacent to the Town of Massey. The park consists of wetlands, rock lands, floodplains and terraces.The rapids and picturesque waterfalls of the Aux Sables River are the highlights of this recreation park. The region's historic importance as a logging center is still seen in the composition of the sand along the beach.Chutes Provincial Park was regulated as a provincial park in 1970.

## Land use intent

Chutes Provincial Park will be managed according to Provincial Park policy and Park Management Plan (1988). The park will provide day-use and camping opportunities while protecting the parks natural, cultural and recreational values.
