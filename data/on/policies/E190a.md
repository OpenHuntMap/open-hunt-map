## Land area description

An expansive area that surrounds Onaping Lake and Friday / Scotia Lake Conservation Reserve. It has high remote tourism, recreational, and resource sector (forestry, mining) value. There are a number of tourism lakes with fly-in outpost camps as well as cottages and commercial tourism operations on Onaping Lake.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Manage this area to protect high quality recreational and remote tourism values, while providing for activities. Management emphasis will ensure that the remote quality of the lakes is retained in order to maintain a high quality of fishery. Existing authorized access including road, trail and off-trail use (e.g. all terrain vehicle) will be permitted to continue. New roads and trails will be carefully planned and managed to ensure the protection of high quality fishery, recreation and tourism resource values, including Friday and Scotia lakes and Onaping Lake conservation reserves and Halfway Lake Provincial Park. The land use intent is to protect the aforementioned values by ensuring that no new road, trail or all-terrain vehicle (ATV) route is developed to remote tourism lakes.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Land Use Strategy (1999) and the Sudbury District Land Use Guidelines (1983).

## Permitted uses — preface

Remote access enhanced management area are intended to maintain the remote character of selected areas. Typically, these are relatively large areas which provide the public and tourism operators with high-quality remote recreational experiences including hunting, fishing, canoeing, and camping. Given the large size, remoteness, and relative absence of roads, these areas will play a significant role in protecting wilderness values outside the parks and protected areas system.In addition to provincial and regional policies, the following local policies will apply to the management of Natural lake trout lakes:­ no additional access will be permitted­ 120 metre Area of Concern will be applied­ forest management activities may be permitted within the Area of Concern subject to concerns for erosion/sedimentation and the protection of lake trout habitat.
