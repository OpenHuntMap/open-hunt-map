## Land area description

Twenty kilometres from the salt waters of James Bay, Tidewater consists of four islands in the Moose River estuary. Water taxis take visitors to Charles Island where sub-Arctic flora flourish and seals and belugas sometimes play. This is in the heart of traditional Cree territory and the site of the first Hudson's Bay Company trading post at Moose Factory.The park was regulated in 1970.

## Land use intent

This natural environment class park is designated by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act, 2006. All protected areas within the Far North of Ontario are subject to the provisions of the Far North Act 2010
