## Land area description

This area is located mainly south of the Montreal River. Two townships and portions of three others are privately owned by a company known as Michipicoten Forest Resources. These lands are presently used primarily for timber harvesting. Mineral exploration activity and public recreation also occur. The unusual split of natural resources ownership/administration between Michipicoten Forest Resources and the Crown provides a distinctive identity to these lands. Mineral rights, surface and subsurface and all aspects of their administration are controlled entirely by this company, except where the company has sold patented mining claims to private individuals. Michipicoten Forest Resources administers its own claim staking procedures and controls the disposition of mineral rights. Cottage lots are leased on its lands. Two dams on the Montreal River regulate water levels to stabilize hydroelectric power generation by the Great Lakes Power Corporation.The Crown also controls forest fire management through the enforcement of the Forest Fires Prevention Act.Portions of this area are located within the Great Lakes Heritage Coast Signature Site, one of 9 such areas featured in the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999). Signature Sites are identified for their range of natural and recreational values and their potential to contribute to future recreation and tourism.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The primary activities in the area will be forest management, mineral exploration, mining, hydroelectric power generation, tourism and Crown land recreation. On-shore mining developments may expand underground, outward under the Great Lakes, subject to existing international agreements.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Sault Ste. Marie District Land Use Guidelines (1983) and the Wawa District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
