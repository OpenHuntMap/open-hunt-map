## Land area description

The land and water along Highway 588 from the rural area west to Northern Light Lake has been a traditional focus for cottaging, sport fishing, hunting, tourism and Crown land recreation. Development in the area remains sparse but nodes of lodges and cottages do exist on lakes touched by the highway.Resource extraction has primarily been forestry and wild rice harvesting. Mineral exploration has been limited but the potential does exist.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The primary role of this area is to contribute to private, public and commercial recreation needs. Resource extraction will continue but will recognize the area's recreational and aesthetic benefits. The watershed of Quetico Provincial Park is an Area of Concern. Resource management and land use activities in the watershed will be carried out in consideration of the protection of values within the park. While Northern Light Lake will be managed according to the Area of Concern policy to protect the lake trout resource, resource management within the skyline of the lake will consider recreational values and aesthetics and maintenance of the red and white pine stands which contribute to this lake's character. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Thunder Bay District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
