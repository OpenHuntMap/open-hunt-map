## Land area description

This area consists of 120 metres on each side of the southern section of the Mattagami River known as Kenogamissi Lake and the entire northern section of the Mattagami River north of the Sandy Falls Hydro generating station. The Mattagami River is a canoe route. All of the cottaging in this area occurs on Lake Kenogamissi. Along with the numerous cottages on Lake Kenogamissi is one commercial lodge located on the north end in Thornloe Township. There are several historical sites of some importance in the area, including an Indian burial site, an archaeological site and an historic trappers cabin. All are located on Kenogamissi Lake. On the south end of the lake a commercial fishing licence is in effect for the harvesting of white fish. At present, no development has taken place on the northern section of the Mattagami River.Future management of this area will focus on the development of future cottaging opportunities on the northern section of the Mattagami River.Part of this area is subject to the Range Management Policy in Support of Woodland Caribou Conservation and Recovery (2014). The Range Management Policy is accessible via the “Source of Direction.””

## Land use intent

The primary use of this area will be public recreation, cottaging and commercial tourism. Resource management plans will contain guidelines for the protection of recreational and tourism values, historical and archaeological sites and fish and wildlife habitat. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Timmins District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
