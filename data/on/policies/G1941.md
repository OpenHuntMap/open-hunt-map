## Land area description

This is an area of moderately broken rock knob terrain, with complex, sometimes steep slopes drained by the Tomiko River and a number of creeks southwesterly to the Sturgeon River. Soils are generally shallow with exposed bedrock, with the exception of some outwash plain areas. There are few aggregate resources. The area contains a mixture of organized and unorganized areas. Private land holdings are not extensive. Timber production from Crown land is an important industrial use.

## Land use intent

Resource production, fish and wildlife management, recreation and cottaging are existing accepted uses. The area will continue to provide resource products and recreational opportunities. Little Tomiko, Rock Island and Tomiko-Chebobomog Lakes are included in Lake Management plans and will be managed in accordance with the detailed policies of those plans.The existing overall quantity and quality of wildlife habitat will be maintained. Important aquatic feeding and winter concentration areas for moose and waterfowl concentration areas will be given special status and consideration. Several sites, where protection is the main theme of management (e.g. heronries, cold water fisheries habitat, etc.) are found in this area. These and other such sites, which may be found in the future, will be protected during resource management operations and will be identified as values requiring protection in municipal planning.Important to the recreational aspect are the lakes and rivers associated with trout habitat.MNR is responsible for the management of Crown land and natural resources in organized and unorganized townships.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in North Bay District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
