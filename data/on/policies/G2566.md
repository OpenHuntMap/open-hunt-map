## Land area description

This area consists of the Corporation of the Township of Atikokan and includes two surveyed townships, Freeborn and Schwenger, and a similar sized area to the south. It is well accessed by provincial highways and resource access roads. In addition, this area is part of the Crossroute Forest.Existing uses include urban and rural residential, some cottaging, tourism and public recreation. There is also considerable resource extraction and a limited amount of small scale agriculture carried out. The Atikokan Generating Station and the Atikokan Municipal Airport are located in this area. The township is crossed by several hydro line corridors, a natural gas pipeline, and a railway.Major issues include the need to develop opportunities for economic growth for the community while ensuring sustainable resources. Jackfish, Lerome and Plateau lakes have been identified for development opportunities to stimulate economic benefits to the Township of Atikokan. This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The primary use will be the continued servicing of the municipal township with emphasis on resource extraction (forestry and mining, including aggregate), industrial development and tourism and recreational activities.Areas of Concern will be applied to recreational, residential and tourism developments as required. Road access will be managed to facilitate the overall land use intent.All Crown land development and uses will attempt to conform to the Official Plan and associated zoning by-laws of the Township. MNR will continue a close working liaison with the Township regarding all activities in the area.Lerome Lake, Plateau Lake and Jackfish Lake are identified as having potential for the disposition of Crown land for cottaging or other development. The Atikokan River flows through the Township and is prone to flooding. The flood plain will be identified and incorporated in the Official Plan for the town as a hazard area. Supplementary by-laws will control development and land use within the identified flood plain. The watershed of Quetico Provincial Park is an Area of Concern. Resource management and land use activities in the watershed will be carried out in consideration of the protection of values within the park.
