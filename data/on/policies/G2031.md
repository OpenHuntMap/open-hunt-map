## Land area description

This area includes part of the LaCloche Mountains that parallel the shores of Bay of Islands and McGregor Bay. The LaCloche Mountains, mainly composed of a white quartzite rock, rise quite abruptly from the Lake Huron waters and provide a startling visual contrast. This area borders the La Cloche Ridge conservation reserve. The existing use of this area is mostly recreation - cottaging (along the south edge), hiking, canoeing and hunting; however, trapping also occurs. The Sables, Spanish Rivers Official Plan identifies this area as a sensitive site.This area is located within the Great Lakes Heritage Coast Signature Site and the Killarney Signature Site, two of 9… Most of this area is within the GLHC SS, however a small portion of this area is within F219 which will be added to P187 (Killarney addition).

## Land use intent

MNR will direct management efforts in this area towards maintaining a prime recreational landscape that compliments the Bay of Islands and McGregor Bay areas. Most other resource programs will be permitted.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Espanola District Guidelines (1983).
