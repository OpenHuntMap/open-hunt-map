## Land area description

This area, located north of Highway 11 is an area of high resource production and recreation potential. Cottaging, fishing, hunting, tourism outpost camps, camping, forestry and mineral exploration are the present uses in the area.Development has been sparse but there is potential for increased levels of use. The area is crossed by several corridors; Ontario Hydro transmission lines, CN rail and Highway 11. No specific problems have been identified in this area, however, the continuing issue of the blending of resource extraction and recreation exists.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The watershed of Quetico Provincial Park is an Area of Concern. Resource management and land use activities in the watershed will be carried out in consideration of the protection of values within the park. Resource extraction and recreation uses will be of equal importance in this area. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Thunder Bay District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
