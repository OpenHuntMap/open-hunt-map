## Land area description

Charleston Lake Provincial Park was established in 1975. Located on the Frontenac Axis, the park has the rugged character of the Canadian Shield. The park includes lands along the eastern and western shores of the lake. It contains diverse and significant natural, cultural and recreational resources. The park is a popular destination for camping, boating, hiking and fishing. Since the park was established, two additions have been made. The first addition was regulated in 1997 to include the Blue Mountain area of the park and many Crown islands. The second addition to the park was regulated in 2014 to expand protection of the Blue Mountain and Tallow Rock Bay areas of the park, as well as to incorporate a portage route to Red Horse Lake and a former junior ranger cabin. The third addition was regulated in 2017 and expanded protection to include a large area of land along the north shore of Red Horse Lake. An additional parcel was also added to the Blue Mountain area.

## Land use intent

This natural environment class park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act, 2006.
