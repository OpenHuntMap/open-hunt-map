## Land area description

This area includes the developments associated with the operation of former uranium mines in the Elliot Lake area. Abandoned mines, tailings ponds, water diversions, aggregate extraction sites and road systems are present.Poor water quality throughout the portion of the Serpent River watershed in this area limits recreational use, but many of the smaller lakes unaffected by past tailings discharges are utilized by local anglers.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

MNR will recognize containment and maintenance of post mining activities in this area. Fisheries management programs are being undertaken to enhance the angling quality on some of the smaller lakes in the area, through the development and/or maintenance of self sustaining or put-delay-and-take fisheries.Land disposition will generally be restricted to approved mining and municipal uses.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Blind River District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
