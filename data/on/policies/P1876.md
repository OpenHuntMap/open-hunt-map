## Land area description

This Nature Reserve park encompasses 2,395 hectares, protecting an extensive sandy “bird’s foot” delta at the mouth of the Mississagi River where it meets the North Channel of Lake Huron near the Town of Blind River. The park also includes a chain of offshore islands known as the French Islands. A number of cultural heritage values are also found in the park.This area is located within the Great Lakes Heritage Coast Signature Site, one of 9 such areas featured in the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999). Signature Sites are identified for their range of natural and recreational values and their potential to contribute to future recreation and tourism.Mississagi Delta Provincial Nature Reserve was regulated as a provincial park in 1985.

## Land use intent

Mississagi Delta Provincial Park will be managed according to Provincial Park policy and Park Management Plan (1998). Natural and cultural heritage values will be protected. Use and development in the park will be restricted to trails, signs and low-intensity recreational activities.
