## Land area description

Labyrinth Lake, which is located on the Ontario-Quebec border, is north of Virginiatown and Highway 66E. The area is not municipally organized. There are about 20 cottage lots currently on the Ontario side of the lake. Most of the land in this area is patented.

## Land use intent

The intent is to provide for water based recreation activities. The priority in shoreline areas will be cottaging and public recreation. The management of the warm water fishery is this Ministry's main management concern in this area. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Kirkland Lake District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
