## Land area description

This area contains 247 hectares, on the south side of Highway 101 in the northwestern part of Miskokomon Township. It has been designated to protect its regionally significant geological feature, a series of large potholes, active and abandoned in the bed of the Kinniwabi River. The potholes developed initially when the area was part of a large glacial meltwater drainage system.The area is presently used mostly by local residents for passive recreation activities.Potholes Provincial Nature Reserve was regulated as a provincial park in 1985.

## Land use intent

Potholes Provincial Park will be managed according to Provincial Park policy and Interim Management Strategy (1986). It has been regulated as a Nature Reserve to protect its significant geological feature while providing day-use opportunities.
