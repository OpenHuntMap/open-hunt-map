## Land area description

Oastler Lake Provincial Park is situated in the Parry Sound Metavolcanic and Ontario Gneiss Segment of the Grenville Province of the Precambrian Shield. The park exhibits features typical of the Shield such as rock outcrops and shallow soils covered in northern hardwoods.Oastler Lake Provincial Park was regulated as a provincial park in 1967.

## Land use intent

Oastler Provincial Park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act. The boundary of the park is delineated by Ontario Regulation No. 695/70, Schedule 63.
