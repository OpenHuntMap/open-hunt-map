## Hunting

Not addressed in this policy.

Provincial season, licence, tag and firearm rules still apply, as do municipal discharge by-laws. Verify before hunting.

## Land area description

Major built up areas of cities, towns and villages and areas set out in approved official plans comprise this area. Included are: Astorville, Balsam Creek, Bonfield, Cache Bay, Callander, Corbell, Field, Lavigne, Mattawa, North Bay, Powassan, Redbridge, River Valley, Sturgeon Falls, Trout Creek, Verner.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

These areas have not been considered in the evaluation of achievability of Ministry objectives and targets.

## Permitted uses — preface

Existing and proposed activities will be in accordance with approved official plans and zoning by-laws and government policies.
