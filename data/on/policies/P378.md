## Land area description

Oxtongue River – Ragged Falls Provincial Park is located in Central Ontario in Site District 5E-9 near the southwest corner of Algonquin Provincial Park. The park lies within the Grenville Province of the Canadian Shield. The river flows through an ancient spillway, which drained meltwaters from the Algonquin Highlands into glacial Lake Algonquin. The river drains southwestward from Algonquin Park, cascading over several waterfalls and rapids.Sugar maple forests dominate the undulating upland areas. Hemlock occupies the thinner soils and bare steep slopes. Thicket communities and peatlands dominate the low-lying areas of the river.This park was regulated in 1985. Park additions, including a 5.4 hectare private donation and a 125 hectare addition of the Crown land Oxtongue River Bog Forest identified through the “Keep It Wild” program were added to the park in 1995 (Management Plan Amendment No. 1995-001). The additions increased the area of the park protected under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act from 382 hectares to 507 hectares.

## Land use intent

Oxtongue River – Ragged Falls is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act. The boundary of the park is delineated by Ontario Regulation No. 768/94, Schedule 176.
