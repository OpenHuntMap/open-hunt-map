## Land area description

Misery Bay Provincial Park (Nature Reserve Class) is located on the west end of Manitoulin Island, approximately 40 kilometres (by road) west of Gore Bay and 180 kilometres west of Sudbury. The park is accessed by road from Highway 540 and Highway 6 south of Espanola, south of Highway 17.Misery Bay Provincial Park contains significant natural heritage areas including a provincially significant wetland and regionally significant alvar communities.Misery Bay Provincial Park was regulated in 1989. A 251 ha addition to the park was regulated in 2014.

## Land use intent

The land use intent is to manage Misery Bay Provincial Park as a nature reserve class provincial park to protect provincially significant, special and representative natural heritage values of the Manitoulin Island Eco-district. Scientific research is encouraged.

## Permitted uses — preface

Management of this provincial park will be carried out in accordance with the Misery Bay Provincial Park Management Plan (1996), Ontario Provincial Parks: Planning and Management Policies,1992, A Class Environmental Assessment for Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves, 2005, and the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act, 2006.The permitted uses associated with provincial parks are found in approved management direction, which are available on the Ontario Parks website.
