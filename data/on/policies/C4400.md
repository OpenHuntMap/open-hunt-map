## Land area description

This policy report applies to the C4400 Recommended Conservation Reserve, located in Peterborough District. The area encompasses: - Point Petre Provincial Wildlife Area, including Charwell Point - Ostrander Point Crown Land Block- the adjacent bed of Lake Ontario, to a distance of 30 m from the shoreline, except at Charwell Point where the distance is increased to 200m.The C4400 Recommended Conservation Reserve is located 15 kilometres south of Picton, on the shores of Lake Ontario in southern Prince Edward County. This area can be accessed for day use activities, such as, hiking, picnicking, swimming, wildlife viewing, and hunting. The C4400 Recommended Conservation Reserve supports a wide variety of wildlife habitats including open meadow, forest, ponds, vernal pools, wet meadows, lakeshore marshes, dry scraped limestone, treed alvar, alvar meadows, beach ridges and limestone cliffs. The undeveloped Lake Ontario shoreline consists of pebble beaches, limestone ledges, and unique gravel bars. The area is a significant migratory bird stopover and bird watching area, as well as a global monarch butterfly sanctuary, and provides habitat for several species at risk and rare alvar communities. Many of the habitats found on these lands contribute to the protection of under-represented or rare ecosystems in the ecodistrict.Unexploded explosive ordnance (UXO) can be found in the water, on land and on the shorelines of the C4400 Recommended Conservation Reserve. See www.canada.ca/en/department-national-defence/services/uxo/uxo-locations/practicing-uxo-safety-prince-edward-county.html for information on practicing UXO safety in Prince Edward County.

## Land use intent

This recommended conservation reserve designation establishes the land use intent to regulate these areas as a Conservation Reserve under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act, 2006. The area will be managed specifically to enable permanent protection, promote non-industrial resource use and to provide opportunities for a variety of compatible outdoor recreation activities, particularly hunting and wildlife viewing.

## Permitted uses — preface

Management policies for this recommended conservation reserve are temporary and apply only until such a time that the lands are regulated under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act, 2006. Upon regulation, the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act, 2006, associated regulations and policies apply, and this policy report ceases to direct the management of the lands.
