## Land area description

Sandbar Lake Provincial Park is located north of Ignace. Sandbar was established in 1970 and is regulated as a natural environment class park to protect earth and life science features and to provide opportunities for day-use and car-camping.The park provides representation of earth and life science features for Site Districts 3W-2 and 4S-3.

## Land use intent

The intent is to manage this park as a natural environment class provincial park, in accordance with MNR and Ontario Parks Policy.
