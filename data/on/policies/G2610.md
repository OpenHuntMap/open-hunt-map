## Land area description

This area covers Whitefish Bay on Lake of the Woods. The emphasis presently is on sport fishing and outdoor recreation. The bay is a significant lake trout habitat and presently supports four main lodges. Portions of the Lake of the Woods Islands Conservation Reserve are located within this area.A rollerway has been established between Whitefish Bay and Sabaskong Bay and the channel closed to prevent eutrophication of Whitefish Bay by the waters of Sabaskong Bay.The area forms part of the Township of Sioux Narrows-Nestor Falls.

## Land use intent

This area will be managed to protect the lake trout environment. No new dispositions of Crown land will be considered and increased development on private land will be discouraged.
