## Land area description

The Wainfleet Bog Conservation Reserve is located 8 kilometers northwest of the urban centre of Port Colborne, within the Township of Wainfleet and the City of Port Colborne. The area lies adjacent to and immediately east of Highway 58, just north of the Onondaga Escarpment. The Conservation Reserve is part of a much larger protected area that is owned and managed by the Niagara Peninsula Conservation Authority (NPCA) and the Nature Conservancy of Canada (NCC). The Reserve together with the larger complex is the largest peatland area remaining within the Carolinian Region of Ontario. The Area of Natural and Scientific Interest (ANSI) portion of the Reserve provides habitat for approximately 283 species of vascular plants. A total of 52 species of birds have been observed within the Reserve or in the immediate vicinity, of which 30 species are believed to be breeding. The site is also habitat for three provincially significant species of reptiles, the Eastern Massassauga Rattlesnake, Spotted Turtle and Black Rat Snake, as well as five provincially significant birds, the Yellow-Breasted Chat, Least Bittern, Tufted Titmouse, Short-eared Owl and White-eyed Vireo.
