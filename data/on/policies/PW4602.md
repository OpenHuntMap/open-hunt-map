## Land area description

The Hoople Creek Provincial Wildlife Area is located in the Township of South Stormont, northeast of Ingleside Ontario on the St. Lawrence River.The Crown land area includes Provincially Significant Wetland, areas of marsh, species at risk habitat, mature forests and fields. The land is suitable for deer and small game hunting, and supports waterfowl hunting, education and nature appreciation activities. The area provides for migratory game bird hunting opportunities on a controlled blind allocation system.

## Land use intent

This Provincial Wildlife Area is managed specifically for wildlife and to provide opportunities for a variety of outdoor recreation activities, particularly hunting and wildlife viewing. It is to be kept open for public purposes, providing waterfowl habitat, hunting, public viewing, education and interpretive opportunities. The Crown land area will be managed in an ecologically sustainable way to ensure that natural resources will be available for future generations. The Crown land area is to be used responsibly to help protect the natural environment, and with respect for other users. Crown lands are generally unmaintained and should be used with an understanding and acceptance of the associated risks.
