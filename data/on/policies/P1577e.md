## Land area description

Dana-Jowsey Lakes Provincial Park is located approximately 30 kilometres west of Timmins, in Ecodistrict 3E-5. This park consists of lacustrine fine sandy till uplands dominated by all age classes of black spruce with some old growth (101+). The park also contains weakly broken deep lacustrine organic clay plain dominated by moderate to old age black spruce. The topography of the site is flat. Crawford River drains the western portion of the site and Lundberg and Opishing Lakes form the major aquatic habitats for the eastern portion and boundary. Old cedar is concentrated along Opishing Lake. Extensive shoreline fens exist along Crawford Creek.This park provides recreational opportunities such as fishing, swimming, boating and hiking. There are no visitor facilities.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.Dana-Jowsey Lakes Provincial Park is a recreation class park and was originally regulated in 1989. An addition was made to the park in 2005 as a result of Ontario’s Living Legacy.

## Land use intent

Dana-Jowsey Lakes Provincial Park protects natural, cultural and recreational values of the park. Some values include sandy beaches, a cold water lake trout fishery and the scattered red and white pine around Dana Lake.Dana-Jowsey Lakes will be managed, as a non-operating park, according to Provincial Park policy and the approved Park Management Plan.
