## Land area description

The area's main features are Highway 11 and two large warm water lakes, Sesekinika Lake and Kenogami Lake. None of the area is within organized municipalities.The area is used for highway commercial development, rural residences, cottaging (about 400 lots) and commercial tourism. There are areas of good quality aggregate in the area. Mineral potential is mainly medium with some high. Some small areas are forecast for timber harvesting. There is a seed orchard located in Grenfell Township.

## Land use intent

The intent of this area is to provide for the existing mix of land uses. The retention of the recreation environment will have priority in shoreline areas. Resource extraction and highway commercial expansion are secondary uses. Development on hazard land areas particularly flood prone areas will be discouraged. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Kirkland Lake District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
