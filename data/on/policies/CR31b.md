## Land area description

The Indian Bay South Conservation Reserve is located within the Montreal River watershed west of New Liskeard. The values and uses within the reserve include representative white pine old growth stands which are nearly 200 years of age, a variety of mature and old growth mixed-wood stands, and a number of wetland communities associated with the Montreal River. The reserve is encompassed by the Lepha Lake Ice Contact Delta which is a locally significant feature. The conservation reserve also provides opportunities for scientific and educational pursuits as well as a number of outdoor recreation and nature appreciation activities.The Indian Bay South Conservation Reserve was regulated as a conservation reserve on July 4, 1997.

## Land use intent

The intent of the Indian Bay South Conservation Reserve is to protect representative natural heritage values (such as the old growth white pine stand) and to provide opportunities for research, low impact recreation, and natural heritage appreciation and interpretation. Land use concerns include the potential impacts of mining related activities in the surrounding management area and the need for natural disturbances to create suitable conditions for pine regeneration. The management area objectives are to allow natural processes to occur and to ensure that mining-related activities in adjacent management areas do not impact the conservation reserve.
