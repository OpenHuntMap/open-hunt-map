## Land area description

This area includes the Highway 101 corridor from Hoyle to the west side of the urban core of the City of Timmins. Found in the area is a mixture of commercial, industrial, urban residential and rural residential uses. The areas population is approximately 45,000. Also found within the area are the major operating mines in the Porcupine area. The urban centres of Timmins, Schumacher, South Porcupine and Porcupine are all located within the area along the Highway 101 corridor. Planning control in the area include the City of Timmins Official Plan and Zoning By-law, as well as the Mattagami Region Conservation Authorities Flood and Fill Line Regulations.

## Land use intent

The primary activity in this area will be the continued development of the Timmins-Porcupine urban area.All development and uses should conform to the official plan and zoning by-law of the City of Timmins. Structural development will be discouraged on land subject to flooding, erosion or subsidence. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Timmins District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
