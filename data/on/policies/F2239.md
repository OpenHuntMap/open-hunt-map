## Land area description

This forest reserve is surrounded by Ruby Lake Provincial Park. It contains a substantial deposit of marble, some of which has been extracted through previous quarrying operations.There remains significant opportunity for economic development via the extraction of the remaining marble resource in the forest reserve.Activities occurring within the forest reserve include: recreational hiking, skiing, snowshoeing, hunting, use of ATVs and snowmobiles, and commercial fur harvesting.There are no trails within the forest reserve; recreational travel occurs either on roads or, where permitted by the policies in this policy report, off-trail.

## Land use intent

Forest reserves are areas where protection of natural heritage and special landscapes is a priority, but some resource use can take place with appropriate considerations. The longtermintent for this forest reserve is that it will eventually become part of Ruby Lake Provincial Park. The short-term intent of this forest reserve is to provide economic benefit to Red Rock Indian Band through the extraction of the marble resource.Protection of the coastal ecosystem and its natural, cultural, recreational and scenic attributes are secondary land use goals. These goals will be considered in the review ofproposals for land uses and activities in the forest reserve.The forest reserve lands have been withdrawn from staking under the Mining Act, and all commercial activities except aggregate (marble) extraction by Red Rock Indian Band,commercial fur harvesting and energy transmission corridors are prohibited. Aggregate extraction operations and eventual site rehabilitation will be carried out in a manner thatprovides for compatibility with the future intent of adding the lands to Ruby Lake Provincial Park.Land and resource management activities and infrastructure are only allowed where they are demonstrated to be necessary for the extraction of the marble resource and compatible with the management direction and objectives for the surrounding Ruby Lake Provincial Park.Recreation activities and facilities are permitted only where they are compatible with marble extraction operations and the management direction and objectives for thesurrounding Ruby Lake Provincial Park.The forest reserve lands will be added to Ruby Lake Provincial Park after the opportunity to develop the marble deposit has been exercised by Red Rock Indian Band or the Band no longer demonstrates an active interest in developing the deposit.Ruby Lake Forest Reserve is located in an area in which one or more First Nations have asserted Aboriginal rights and title, including an unextinguished Aboriginal right to exclusive use and occupancy of the land. The First Nations’ claims are the subject of litigation and negotiations. Dispositions in Ruby Lake Forest Reserve may be subject to heightened Crown consultation and accommodation obligations.This may mean a greater degree of engagement with First Nation communities asserting rights and title prior to a decision regarding potential authorizations or dispositions of public lands or resources.For a map of areas affected by these claims, please go to the Notice of Caution on the Mining Lands Administration System (MLAS)at https://www.geologyontario.mndm.gov.on.ca/mines/lands/withreop/notices_e.html .
