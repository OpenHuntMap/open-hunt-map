## Land area description

This area is located within the Obabika River watershed. The values and uses of this area include the significant low use backcountry canoe route, the alternate linkage of the Pinetorch wilderness canoe route with Obabika River Waterway Park, and the maintenance of park-related values.

## Land use intent

The land use intent is to provide an alternative low use canoe route, allowing mining related activities while maintaining recreation and park-related values.Concerns include the desire to minimize crossings, ensure special management of resource extraction activities, and restrict motorized activities to conserve the backcountry setting while also mitigating the impacts of resource management activities (e.g., noise, aesthetics, access).The management area objectives are to maintain the backcountry recreation experience through the use of appropriate resource management prescriptions.The strategies include having a 200-metre Area of Concern on either side of water bodies with no forestry activities. Another strategy is to ensure that mineral exploration will be undertaken with considerations to protect recreational values with no mining above ground (further development of prescription required).

## Permitted uses — preface

Land use and resource management activities for this area are also governed by the policies of the Temagami Land Use Plan (TLUP) (1997). The policies from the TLUP are integrated into this area report. Refer to the TLUP for background information and additional details.
