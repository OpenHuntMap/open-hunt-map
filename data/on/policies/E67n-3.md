## Land area description

This enhanced management area has been established to protect the headwaters of Algonquin Provincial Park. This area contains high value timber resources and a variety of recreational landscapes. The entire area is unorganized.

## Land use intent

This Enhanced Management Area has been designated to protect the water quality of rivers flowing into Algonquin Provincial Park. Most land use activities and resource management practices permitted within the General Use Area are permitted within this Enhanced Management area, as these activities and practices do not generally have an adverse impact on water quality. It is not the intent of this policy to manage these lands as potential future additions to Algonquin Provincial Park.All land use activities and resource management practices must be conducted in a manner that protects the water quality of these watersheds. In particular:- with the implementation of the forest management planning process and applicable forest management planning guidelines and practices, forest management is compatible with the protection of water quality;- existing and improved guidelines for access road planning and construction and aggregate extraction will be implemented to protect water quality;- mineral exploration and development are permitted within this designation. However, it is recognized that controls and monitoring will need to be applied to mineral exploration and development activities within this Enhanced Management Area.Watershed boundaries for this Enhanced Management Area will be refined through more precise mapping. Due to the scale of mapping in this Strategy, only major watershed boundaries have been mapped. Further planning will identify watershed boundaries for lower order watercourses flowing into the Park and will apply the above provisions.In addition to managing these areas through the above provisions, these areas will also be managed for the provision of backcountry recreation and tourism opportunities.This is a sparsely developed agricultural area and the primary uses will be resource production and wildlife management. Recreation and tourism are acceptable uses. The Algonquin Park Area of Concern is an area of no finite distance surrounding the park boundary where development and management activities are identified and reviewed to ensure park values are not impacted. Resource and recreational activities outside the park will be reviewed in conjunction with the Area of Concern Guidelines contained within the Algonquin Park Management Plan when possible. Significant aggregate resources occur in Boulter, Papineau and Cameron Townships and these should remain accessible for future use when possible. Several sites (heronries, cold water habitat, etc.) are found in this area. These and other such sites, which may be found in the future, will be protected during resource management operations and will be identified as values requiring protection in municipal planning. MNR is responsible for the management of Crown land and natural resources in unorganized townships.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Land Use Strategy (1999), the North Bay District Land Use Guidelines (1983), and the Algonquin Park Management Plan.
