## Land area description

Killbear Provincial Park is located on the southern edge of the Canadian Shield within the Great Lakes- St. Lawrence Forest Region in Site District 5E-7. Upland maple-beech communities with scattered stands of pine dominate the park's vegetation. Black spruce and cedar occupy the poorly drained sites. The terrain consists of shallow to deep sandy till deposits, with large areas of exposed, gneissic bedrock.This area is located within the Great Lakes Heritage Coast Signature Site, one of 9 such areas featured in the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999). Signature Sites are identified for their range of natural and recreational values and their potential to contribute to future recreation and tourism.Killbear Provincial Park was regulated as a provincial park in 1971.

## Land use intent

Killbear Provincial Park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act. The boundary of the park is delineated by Ontario Regulation No. 354/72, Schedule 97.
