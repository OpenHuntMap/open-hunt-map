## Land area description

Located in the Madawaska Highlands, the nature reserve protects mature tolerant hardwood forest, including old hemlock stands, as well as plant communities growing on marble outcrops in Site 5E-11. Few people visit the reserve because of its inaccessibility; however, access roads are nearing the park boundaries.Matawatchan Provincial Nature Reserve was regulated as a provincial park in 1968.

## Land use intent

This nature reserve was established to protect regionally significant life science values.Management will be in accordance with the Matawatchan Provincial Nature Reserve Interim Management Statement (2002).

## Permitted uses — preface

Management of this provincial park is carried out in accordance with Ontario Provincial Parks: Planning and Management Policies (1992), the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999) and the Interim Management Statement. The Interim Management Statement provides direction to ensure custodial management of park resources until such time as a park management planning process is conducted, which includes public review of proposals. At that time, significant decisions regarding resource stewardship, operations, development and permitted uses are made. The permitted uses associated with provincial parks are found in approved management direction, which are available on the Ontario Parks website.
