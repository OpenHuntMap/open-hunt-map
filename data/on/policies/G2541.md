## Land area description

Located in the northeast section of Dryden District, this area is transected by the Brightsands River. The area is characterized by mature forests and small, scattered sections of moderate to high mineral potential. There is only one historic site in this area; a pictograph south of Blackbird Lake. There is one private recreation camp on Arrow Lake.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.This area is subject to the Range Management Policy in Support of Woodland Caribou Conservation and Recovery (2014). The Range Management Policy is accessible via the “Source of Direction.”

## Land use intent

The intent of this area is to encourage timber harvesting. Commercial fishing on Wapikaimaski Lake will be continued. The Blackbird Lake pictograph will be recognized and protected as a sensitive area in resource management plans.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Ignace District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
