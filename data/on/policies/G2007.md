## Land area description

This area is located within the Matabitchuan River/Marten River watershed. The values and uses of this area include large tree plantations, a large road-accessible recreation area especially used for angling and hunting as well as existing and heritage portages.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Inland Ontario Lakes Designated for Lake Trout Management (2006) accessible in the Source of Direction portion of this policy report..

## Land use intent

The land use intent is to allow forestry and mining related activities as well as a range of recreational opportunities within a road-accessible setting while protecting remote tourism outposts in adjacent management areas from improved road access. In addition, the intent is to protect existing canoe routes and heritage portages.The concerns of this area include the unorganized camping at access points. The management area objectives are to permit public motorized access subject to road strategies (some areas may be managed to continue to provide lower level of access (e.g., ATV access) and ensure access to Four Bass Lake from the south will remain 4-wheel drive/ATV only. In addition, the objective is to protect existing canoe routes, retain potential for new routes, and to protect existing tourism values (aesthetics, portages, campsites, trails, etc.).The strategies include developing road use strategies to manage public motorized access, identifying appropriate sites for road-side camping, managing for old growth characteristics when harvesting white and red pine as well as applying viewscape management and Area of Concern planning for extractive activities.
