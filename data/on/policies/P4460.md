## Hunting

Not addressed in this policy.

Provincial season, licence, tag and firearm rules still apply, as do municipal discharge by-laws. Verify before hunting.

## Land area description

Located approximately 2km east of Lion's Head and 3km north of Barrow Bay on Georgian Bay, Municipality of Northern Bruce Peninsula, Bruce County. The park is part of the Niagara Escarpment Parks System and the Niagara Escarpment Biosphere Reserve. The park is accessible via the Bruce Trail.The park was regulated in 1985.

## Land use intent

This nature reserve class park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act.
