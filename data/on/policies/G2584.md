## Hunting

Not addressed in this policy.

Provincial season, licence, tag and firearm rules still apply, as do municipal discharge by-laws. Verify before hunting.

## Land area description

An area of 1025 hectares on the east shore of Lake of the Woods south of the community of Morson. Formerly a portion of Lake of the Woods Provincial Park, now classified as Federal Crown Lands.

## Land use intent

Administration and control of the lands within this area will be transferred from Ontario to Canada to constitute a reserve for the use and benefit of the Assabaska First Nations of Mishkosiimiiniiziibing (Big Grassy First Nation) and the Ojibways of Onegaming First Nations.The First Nations will be encouraged to manage the area in accordance with the general policies contained in Fort Frances District Land Use Guidelines (1983).All uses at the discretion of the First Nations subject to the following guidelines.The final settlement agreement of the Assabaska Shoreline issue commits the Assabaska First Nations to offer park services on these lands for so long as it is economically viable and addresses the following concerns associated with the portion of the Little Grassy River and the portion of the bed of Lake of the Woods included in the settlement:-the public right of navigation-the public's desire to continue to use these waters for sport fishing and other recreational activities.-the rights of the owners of lands abutting the Little Grassy River to access the Little Grassy River for the purpose of allowing use and enjoyment of their land.-the public's need to utilize lands included in the settlement to by-pass unsafe ice conditions between Lake of the Woods and the Little Grassy River by snowmobile

## Addendum

This area will be managed by the Government of Canada and First Nations subject to the terms of the land claim settlement.
