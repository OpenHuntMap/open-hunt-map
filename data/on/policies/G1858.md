## Land area description

Located to the east of Highway 11, the area is accessed by secondary highways (Highways 624, 650 and 564). The Corporation of the Township of Larder Lake has municipal control over the Townships of Skead and Hearst and the Township of McGarry is also an organized municipality. The majority of the area is Crown land. The area is sparsely settled but does include the community of Boston Creek. The predominant land use is resource extraction. Mineral potential is medium to high. The Adam's Mine is located in this area. Forestry is a major activity in the area. Most of the over 80 cottages in the area are located on the shoreline of St. Anthony Lake and Raven Lake. Five hunt camp sites and one downhill skiing area are located in this area. The area includes a wetland wildlife marsh at Grassy Lake in McElroy Township and two earth science sites in Skead Township.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The general intent of this area is to provide an adequate land base for resource extraction activities. Recreation is a secondary use and will include such activities as cottaging and Crown land recreation. Land uses in Skead, Hearst and McGarry Townships will be in accordance with municipal controls for those areas. Urban Development allowed. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Kirkland Lake District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
