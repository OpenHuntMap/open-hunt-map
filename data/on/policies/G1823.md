## Land area description

The Kamiskotia Complex is a broad band traversing north of the city of Timmins. It is easily accessed by Highway 655, 576, 101 and numerous resource extraction roads. Present uses include rural residences, mineral exploration, the Timmins Airport, the Kamiskotia Ski Hill, cottaging and aggregate extraction. This area lies within two separate licenced forest Management Units. Bromley Lake is the only identified tourism lake in this area.Part of this area is subject to the Range Management Policy in Support of Woodland Caribou Conservation and Recovery (2014). The Range Management Policy is accessible via the “Source of Direction.””

## Land use intent

The priority in this area will be resource extraction with a minimum of shoreline disturbance. Recreation and the protection of fish and wildlife habitats will be priorities adjacent to Kamiskotia, Ice Chest, Bromley, Harriet, and Sewell Lakes and the Kamiskotia River. Resource management plans will contain guidelines to protect significant recreation and fish and wildlife habitat. Any development near the Timmins airport must be compatible with the City of Timmins Official Plan and zoning by-law as well as adjacent airport use. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Timmins District Land Use Guidelines (1983)
