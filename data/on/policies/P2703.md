## Land use intent

This provincial waterway class park encompasses about 80% of the mainland shore of Lake Temagami. The park will be managed to maintain Lake Temagami as a significant recreation and tourism lake, a unique cultural heritage landscape , and a high value natural lake trout fishery. Maintenance of the old pine component of the historic skyline reserve for aesthetic beauty, is also important. The new park does not include islands in Lake Temagami or the bed of the lake.Concerns: Pine regeneration in the Skyline Reserve Competition for campsites (i.e. houseboats, boats, canoes) Use of portages as ATV trails Unplanned access and road-side camping Management of recreational activities and usesManagement Objective: Prevent new road access to Lake Temagami Retain authorized road access point at Baie Jeanne No mainland development Maintain existing canoe routes and retain potential for new routes, campsites and trails Retain viewscape and maintain high quality fishery Restrict access from Lake Temagami by ATV; restrict to fuelwood permit-holders onlyStrategies: In concert with the Temagami Aboriginal community, the Municipality of Temagami and local stakeholder groups, develop a park management plan within 4 years of the execution of the final settlement agreement of the Temagami Land Claim. to include policy direction for Lake Temagami Provincial Park as per Ontario Provincial Parks Planning and Management Policies (OMNR, 1992) and the following strategies: Foster partnerships to develop and implement strategies, and resolve issues (i.e. vegetation management strategy, access management, etc.) - the strategies will be added to the Land Use Plan Address access issues with appropriate processes (e.g. Class EA); integrate Lake Temagami access points into future access management and visitor distribution system for the Temagami Recreational Area and area parks. Develop strategy to address ATV use in Management Area 39 (fuelwood strategy for Lake Temagami). Strategies to address concerns will be developed during the park management planning process.Management of this area is governed by the policies of the Temagami Land Use Plan (1997). The Management Area policies from the Temagami Plan are provided in this area report. On matters pertaining to the Temagami Comprehensive Planning Area, direct reference should be made to the Temagami Land Use Plan.
