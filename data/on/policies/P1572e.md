## Land area description

Biscotasi Lake Provincial Park is located approximately 100 kilometres northwest of Sudbury, in Ecodistricts 3E-5 and 4E-3. A variety of different landform vegetation associations are represented within this park. The dominant landform vegetation type is weakly broken ground moraine with mixed coniferous forest, mixed deciduous forest, and sparse forest.Biscotasi Lake is the starting point for three canoe routes: Spanish River, Mississagi River and the Sakatawi. Backcountry camping is also available. Biscotasi Lake Provincial Park is a natural environment park and was originally regulated in 1989. An addition was made to the park in 2001 as a result of Ontario’s Living Legacy.

## Land use intent

Biscotasi Lake Provincial Park will be managed according to provincial park policy and interim management statement (2002). Use and classification will be reviewed through park management planning. The interim management statement indicates that the park and the park addition (P1572) will be managed as a natural environment class park.Ontario's Living Legacy addition to Biscotasi Lake Provincial Park (P1572) is identified as a proposed natural environment class park.

## Permitted uses — preface

Management of this provincial park is carried out in accordance with Ontario Provincial Parks: Planning and Management Policies (1992), the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999) and the Interim Management Statement, (2002). The Interim Management Statement provides direction to ensure custodial management of park resources until such time as a park management planning process is conducted, which includes public review of proposals. At that time, significant decisions regarding resource stewardship, operations, development and permitted uses are made.
