## Land area description

This area includes the area east of the English River and includes the Wabigoon River and Anishinabi Lake. Existing activities include resource extraction (forestry), commercial tourism development (lodges and outposts), cottaging, and Crown Land recreation. The area is part of the Whisky Jack Forest. The area is relatively road accessible with a small number of fly-in tourism lakes.The following are designated as tourism lakes: Aerobus, Anishinabi, Bridge, Clay, Devlin, Portal, Sup, Toole and Wine.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The main activity in the area will be resource extraction, commercial tourism and low intensity recreation. Road access will be managed to serve tourism, Crown land recreation and resource harvesting and extraction. Road location and management will ensure that the remoteness of tourism lakes will not be significantly compromised. The area also includes the Cedar River Watershed Management Plan. Additional access points may be developed in this area which will facilitate a low intensity Crown land recreation experience. All activities in this area will take into consideration the known sensitive area features of a pictograph, a white spruce production area, a grave site and the numerous eagle nests.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Dryden District Land Use Guidelines (1983)
