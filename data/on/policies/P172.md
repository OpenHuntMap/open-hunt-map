## Land area description

The Daisy Lake site, in Ecodistrict 5E-4 is part of a larger landscape that has been disturbed by more than100 years of land use and pollution related to nickel smelting. The intention is to leave this site to respond onits own, without some of the restoration efforts that have effectively "greened" large areas in the Sudburyregion. A long term research study based out of Laurentian University is monitoring ecological changes.Pockets of acid-tolerant bog vegetation survived the landscape level disturbances while young whitebirch, grasses and sedges have revegetated deforested areas; mosses and lichen are recolonizingsome of the acid-etched bedrock. Common loon have returned to aquatic lake habitats after years ofbeing absent.

## Land use intent

Daisy Lake Uplands Provincial Park will be managed as a nature reserve class provincial park.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999).
