## Land area description

This is generally an area of rugged high relief in the north, decreasing to moderately broken terrain in the south with a thin soil mantle. Along the Sturgeon and Temagami Rivers are some areas of wide alluvial plains and there is an extensive moraine outwash complex in Janes Township. The area contains a mixture of organized and unorganized areas. The Village of River Valley at the south end of this area is the largest community. Timber production from Crown land is an important industrial use.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

This is a resource production area providing industrial wood. Mineral potential is high and mining opportunities should not be obscured by other development. Wildlife management, recreation and tourism are important accepted uses. Several lake trout lakes attracting tourists and anglers require special consideration in planning decisions.Wildlife habitat will be maintained in the existing overall quantity and quality. Important aquatic feeding areas and winter concentration areas for moose and waterfowl concentration areas will be given special status and consideration. Several sites, where protection is the main theme of management, (e.g. heronries, cold water fisheries habitat, etc.) are found in this area. These and other such sites, which may be found in the future, will be protected during resource management operations and will be identified as values requiring protection in municipal planning.MNR is responsible for the management of Crown land and natural resources in organized and unorganized townships.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in North Bay District Land Use Guidelines (1983).

## Permitted uses — preface

In addition to provincial and regional policies, the following local policies will apply to the management of Natural lake trout lakes:­ limiting access­ 120 metre Area of Concern will be applied­ forest management activities may be permitted within the Area of Concern subject to concerns for erosion/sedimentation and the protection of lake trout habitat.
