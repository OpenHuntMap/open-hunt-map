## Land area description

The Massasauga Provincial Park is located in the Township of the Archipelago and the Township of Seguin in the District of Parry Sound in Ecodistrict 5E-7. The park includes many small bedrock islands with windswept white pines that are typical of island biogeography of eastern Georgian Bay. The park is provincially significant for its representation of the Parry Sound Greenstone Belt geology. The topography of the park is a rugged undulating surface of impermeable bedrock. Vegetation associations range from windswept oak and pine barrens to sphagnum bogs, fens and swamps and beaver meadows with hardwood forests in uplands on deeper soils.This area is located within the Great Lakes Heritage Coast as described in Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999). The Massasauga Provincial Park is known for a range of natural and recreational values. This park was regulated in 1989. In 1996 and 2018 a number of land and water parcels were regulated increasing the size of the park from 11796 hectares to 13264 hectares.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The Massasauga Provincial Park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act, 2006. The boundary of the park is delineated by Ontario Regulation No. 296/96, Schedule 242.
