## Hunting

Not addressed in this policy.

Provincial season, licence, tag and firearm rules still apply, as do municipal discharge by-laws. Verify before hunting.

## Land area description

Quackenbush Provincial Park was regulated on December 31, 1985. This non-operating park protects the former site of the closest-known Iroquoian Village to the Canadian Shield. There is no recreational use.

## Land use intent

This cultural heritage class park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act.

The permitted uses associated with provincial parks are found in approved management direction, which are available on the Ontario Parks website.
