## Land area description

Access to this area is provided by forest access roads from Matheson in the north and Highway 66 in the south. Dominant features are Watabeag Lake, a number of small cold water and warm water lakes and extensive sand, gravel and reforested areas. Structural developments include a hydro control dam, four official access points, and over 150 cottages. The area also includes a boy scout campground and gravel pits. Mineral potential is generally low although there are marl deposits in the area. The main forestry activity will be the maintenance of regeneration areas although some harvesting may occur. Commercial harvesting of blueberries occurs in the area. Alexander Lake is a sanctuary for aurora trout. Water level control on Watabeag Lake may conflict with fisheries habitat protection or recreational uses of the lake.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The intent of this area is to provide for a combination of public recreation, cottaging and commercial tourism. Water storage for hydroelectric power generation is also a significant priority. In non-shoreline areas, maintenance of forest plantations will be a priority. Extractive activities will continue as a secondary use. The heron nesting site in northeast Terry Township will be given consideration for protection during the review of any plans or development proposals. On Watabeag Lake, water storage hydroelectric power generation is a priority on land below the 1059 contour flooding level.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Kirkland Lake District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
