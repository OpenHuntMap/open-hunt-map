## Land area description

Indian Point Provincial Park is situated on Balsam Lake, part of the Trent-Severn Waterway in the picturesque Kawarthas in Bexley Township. The park is located 35 km north of Lindsay and 60 km east of Orillia. The park features one of the longest undeveloped shorelines in the Kawartha Lakes region. Consisting of a low, limestone escarpment, this lake shore property is an alvar. Indian Point Provincial Park was regulated in 1989.

## Land use intent

This natural environment class park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act.
