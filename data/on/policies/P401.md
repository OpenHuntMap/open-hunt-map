## Land area description

Carson Lake Provincial Park is forested by stands of poplar, white pine and red pine. The park is situated within the Ontario segment of the Grenville structural provinces. The rocks here are of Middle Precambrian age. The park is situated in Site District 5E-11.Carson Lake Provincial Park was regulated as a provincial park in 1971.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Carson Lake Provincial Park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act. The boundary of the park is delineated by Ontario Regulation No. 382/01. The boundary was previously delineated by Ontario Regulation No. 49/92, Schedule 93.

## Permitted uses — preface

Management of this provincial park is carried out in accordance with Ontario Provincial Parks: Planning and Management Policies (1992), the Ontario's Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999) and the Carson Lake Interim Management Statement (2007). The Interim Management Statement provides direction to ensure custodial management of park resources until such time as a park management planning process is conducted, which includes public review of proposals. At that time, significant decisions regarding resource stewardship, operations, development and permitted uses are made. Where a use may be permitted (Maybe or Yes on the table), the long-term management direction will be determined through planning. Such uses that are existing may continue in the interim, unless park values are threatened.
