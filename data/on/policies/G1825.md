## Land area description

The Muskasenda Complex is situated south-east of Timmins. Parts of the Redstone and Mountjoy River canoe routes cross the area. The area contains many cottages, and several access points. Several good moose wintering areas along with waterfowl production and staging sites are located within the boundaries. A heron rookery is situated on Telluride Lake in Beemer Township. This area lies within two separate licenced Management Units. The following are identified as Tourism lakes: Upper and Lower Scott Lakes, Lower Michegama Lake, Upper Michegama Lake, McArthur Lake, Forks Lake, Lake #35 (Musgrove Township) and Peterlong Lake.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The primary use in this area will be a combination of Crown land recreation, cottaging and commercial tourism in shoreline area while resource extraction will have priority in non-shoreline areas. Resource management plans will contain guidelines for the protection of significant recreation and tourism values, historical and archaeological sites and fish and wildlife habitats. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Timmins District Land Use Guidelines (1983).

## Permitted uses — preface

In addition to provincial and regional policies, the following local policies will apply to the management of Natural lake trout lakes:­ no additional access will be permitted­ 120 metre Area of Concern will be applied­ forest management activities may be permitted within the Area of Concern subject to concerns for erosion/sedimentation and the protection of lake trout habitat.
