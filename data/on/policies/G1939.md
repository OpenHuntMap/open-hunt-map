## Land area description

This is an area of moderately broken relief overlain by shallow glacial till, interspersed with rock outcrops. Some rural residential uses occur. The area contains a mixture of organized and unorganized areas. There are very few private land holdings. Timber production from Crown land is an important industrial use.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Resource production, wildlife management, commercial tourism, recreation, cottaging, and rural residential are existing accepted uses. Ministry programs in the area will be designed to encourage tourism through management of fish and wildlife and other resources in a multiple use sustained yield manner. Lands adjacent to Baie Jeanne on Lake Temagami will be administered in a manner conforming to the Temagami Land Use Plan. Several sites, where protection is the main theme of management (e.g. osprey nests, cold water fisheries habitat, etc.) are found in this area. These and other such sites, which may be found in the future, will be protected during resource management operations and will be identified as values requiring protection in municipal planning. MNR is responsible for the management of Crown land and natural resources in organized and unorganized townships. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in North Bay District Land Use Guidelines (1983) and the Temagami Land Use Plan (1997).
