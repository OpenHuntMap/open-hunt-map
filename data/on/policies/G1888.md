## Land area description

This area comprises a portion of the upper Thessalon River watershed and includes the following lakes: Horner Lake, Conacher Lake, Primeau Lake, Carpenter Lake, Darragh Lake, Sheldon Lake and adjoining waterways. The management area includes the above mentioned lakes and their backshores a distance of approximately 120 metres from the shoreline unless otherwise delineated.This popular recreational area is highly utilized for angling, hunting, camping, day-use and tourism purposes.Primary users of the area are from Sault Ste. Marie and area.Existing developments include cottages, outpost camps and lodges.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The intent is to manage the area for recreational purposes, both public and private (tourism). Recreational features will be protected from conflicting uses. Crown land will be disposed of only for those uses which complement the recreational use of the area.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Blind River District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
