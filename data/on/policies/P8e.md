## Land area description

The park is located on the southern edge of the Canadian Shield 15 kilometres north of the village of Cloyne in Site District 5E-11. Bon Echo Provincial Park contributes to the representation of several themes. It contains the largest concentration of Indian rock paintings (pictographs) found in Ontario. It also protects a large and representative sample of the southern Canadian Shield.Bon Echo Provincial Park was regulated as a provincial park in 1971.

## Land use intent

This natural environment class park protects significant natural and cultural heritage features. Three additions to the park were proposed through the Ontario's Living Legacy Land Use Strategy.Management will be in accordance with the Bon Echo Provincial Park Management Plan (1991) and as amended in 1998 and 2001.
