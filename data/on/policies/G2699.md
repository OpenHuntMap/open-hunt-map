## Land area description

The Shebandowan Lake planning area includes the lake, its islands, and the immediate shoreline for 300 metres inland. This includes points of entry and peninsulas in their entirety.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

In view of the traditional recreational uses and developments, the proximity of Thunder Bay and the degree of access,this area's main role will be to meet recreational needs. A lake management plan has been prepared to reduce land use impacts and guide the use of the lake for power generation, fishing, boating and recreation. The plan has the following management objectives- maintain the health of Shebandowan Lake- protect the cold water ecosystem in the middle and upper basins, as well as the water quality of the entirelake by striving for a reduction of nutrient inputs from human sources- protect the littoralsl and shoreline vegetation and groundwater sources- maintain healthy and diverse forest ecosystemsCottaging use is expected to expand. Private land development should meet most cottage demands. Resourceextraction activities will also be modified to protect the tourism, recreation and aesthetic values and reduce conflicts with other landowners and land uses.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Thunder Bay District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
