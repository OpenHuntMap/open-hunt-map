## Land area description

The Darling Area of Natural and Scientific Interest protects examples of colder than normal marble based wetlands and small lakes which support uncommon calcium-loving plants and aquatic vegetation. Little Green and Minnow Lake contain uncommon shoreline plants including the Bird's eye primrose (Primula mistassinica). Other rare plants in the area include the regionally rare hoary vervaine (Verbena stricta). The mature shade-tolerant forests surrounding these lakes are dominated by sugar maple, yellow birch, red oak, basswood, butternut and beech.The Area of Natural and Scientific Interest contains private land along a large portion of the shoreline areas with mostly cottages developed. Recreational uses include hunting, trapping, boating and fishing. The area also contains a significant mineral deposit and there is an existing mining lease covering a large area in which the Darling Area of Natural and Scientific Interest is included. Significant exploratory work and investment has occurred in the area on the lease. Some forest management has occurred on the Crown land forests just outside the Area of Natural and Scientific Interest.

## Land use intent

Five provincially significant life science Areas of Natural and Scientific Interest (Area of Natural and Scientific Interest) were identified in the Madawaska Highlands Land Use Plan. They will be managed consistent with the Area Specific Management Strategies, and the policies and general intent of the land use plan, to protect their significant natural heritage values while allowing compatible uses. In general, resource activities are permitted in resource Management Zones of the Area of Natural and Scientific Interests, while in Core Protection Zones, forestry, mining and/or development of new roads is not permitted (except Darling permits sub-surface mining). Traditional uses such as hunting, trapping, hiking and camping are permitted in Area of Natural and Scientific Interests but off-road vehicles are restricted to designated trails (trappers excluded).
