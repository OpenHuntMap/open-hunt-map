## Land area description

This area of earth science significance in site district 4E-3 is a provincially significant landscape of moraine and delta features related to the Cartier Moraine. The feature shows evidence of the interaction between melting glacial ice and accumulating surface waters that formed a lake in this area near the end of the last glaciation. The coarse deposits resist significant erosion and, despite being logged in the past, the feature reveals intact landforms with good integrity. The site includes a moraine (a distinct bouldery ridge), a delta, a series of terraces in front of the moraine and fluvial channels (dry beds of ancient streams). These features are all well-developed and exposed. This area is important for resource sector (forestry, mining) values.

## Land use intent

The land use intent for this area is to protect the natural heritage earth science value of provincial significance. This is an important recreational and resource sector (forestry, mining) area. Forest management is permitted, provided that it is consistent with the protection of earth science natural heritage values. Aggregate extraction is not permitted within this area, due to the provincial significance of this moraine and delta landscape.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Land Use Strategy (1999) and the Sudbury District Land Use Guidelines (1983).

Natural heritage enhanced management area are intended to protect areas with significant natural values, while allowing a range of resources.
