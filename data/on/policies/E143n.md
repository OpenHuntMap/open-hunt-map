## Land area description

Located in the east central part of site district 5E-6, this area which abuts Jocko River Waterway Park, contains an important significant earth science feature known as the Little Jocko Ice Contact Delta. This kettled esker delta provides a fairly undisturbed representation of glacial fluvial and/or lacustrine land forms, with a variety of deposits including sandy outwash, organic, alluvial and eolian. This delta has been partially eroded by the river which distributed materials down stream to create a system of beaches and dunes. This delta partially overlaps with a wetland of diverse wetland types; its lowland type is very sensitive given the highly compressible nature of peat soils. The entire area is unorganized.

## Land use intent

The intent of this enhanced management area is to protect the natural heritage features contained within the site. Important in any planning decision will be the protection of the provincially significant earth science feature and wetland values as well as regionally significant life science values. Of particular consideration in planning is the sensitive nature of the sand dunes and peat soils and the flooding hazards of the alluvial plain areas.Any proposed planning on activities adjacent to any provincial park will consider the management intent and direction of these protected areas. MNR is responsible for the management of Crown land and natural resources in unorganized townships.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Land Use Strategy (1999) and the North Bay District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
