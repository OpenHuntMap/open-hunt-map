## Land area description

Nestled within the arms of the McConnell Lakes Road and Cut Lake Branch Road, the Spring/CutLake Esker Conservation Reserve is bordered to the east by the McAuslan/Wyse Township line.This conservation reserve is 782 hectares in size and is approximately 75 kilometres northeast ofthe city of North Bay. Almost all of this conservation reserve falls within the northern core area ofa highly significant landform complex known as the McConnell Interlobate Moraine. Due to thelarge geographic expanse and assemblage of glacial materials, this landform complex has gainedconsiderable recognition in eastern Canada. Large areas of this moraine have beenrecommended as provincially significant on previous occasions for their life science values. BothSpring and Cut Lakes are two deep, clear, long, cold lakes occupying kettle troughs andseparated by an esker ridge. This esker ridge is pitted with many small dry to wet kettle holes tothe north, but to the south, the ridge weakens and hummocky end moraines are interrupted bytow rock knobs. The esker ridge supports a portion of a historical life science site known as theSpring Lake White Birch Stand. A dry 60-70 year old white birch dominated deciduous forestgrows here. To the north of the esker, one of the vegetation associations located in a glacialkettle depression is considered rare or unusual to the site district. Here dominants ofchokecherry, sedge and Hepatica grow in a graminoid low shrub open meadow. This dry site issusceptible to fire. Spring/Cut Lake Esker was regulated as a conservation reserve on December7, 2002.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Conservation Reserve Management to recognize the highly significant land form complex knownas McConnell Interlobate Moraine. Land use intent should also focus on protecting sensitive andrare vegetation successions. Management direction should protect the lake trout population ofCut Lake and other cold water habitat. Consideration may be given to specific recreationalcomponents of the McConnell Lakes Plan during planning as long as they are in keeping with theintent of Spring/Cut Lake Esker Conservation Reserve Management Direction. Management ofthis area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Land Use Strategy (1999).
