## Land area description

Cavern Lake Provincial Park is located north of Dorion. Cavern Lake was regulated as a nature reserve class provincial park in 1975 to protect to protect bat hibernaculum, a portion of a steep-walled canyon and arctic-alpine disjunct plants.The park provides representation of a representative portion of the diabase-influenced landscape of the Thunder Bay-Nipigon area, as well as protection for one of few bat hibernacula in northern Ontario.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The intent is to manage this park as a nature reserve class provincial park, in accordance with MNR and ontario Parks Policy.
