## Land area description

The Kirkwood Delta Apex Enhanced Management Area is located approximately 10 km northwest of the Town of Thessalon in Rose and Bridgland Townships. An number of well developed road systems provide easy access to this site. The Emerson Lake Road and the Kirkwood plantation roads both bisect the Enhanced Management Area, while the Bridgland Road forms part of the site's western boundary. This site encompasses a portion of an extensive deltaic sand plain, referred to as the Kirkwood Delta, which immediately underlies parts of Rose, Bridgland and Kirkwood Townships and the Town of Thessalon. This Delta marks the point where a "river" (the Mississagi Meltwater Channel) entered a prehistoric lake.. As lake levels dropped towards those of today, the river gouged deep travel routes into the deposits of the delta's apex. The entire Kirkwood Delta is considered to be provincially significant due to its large size. This area of earth science significance is located in Ecodistrict 5E-1.

## Land use intent

The land use intent of this area is to protect the earth science features found in Kirkwood Delta Apex Enhanced Management Area, while allowing for a range of resource activities.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Ontario's Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999) and the Blind River District Land Use Guidelines (1983).

## Permitted uses — preface

Natural heritage enhanced management areas are intended to protect areas with significant natural values, while allowing for a range of resource activities. Those uses or management activities not listed in the following table are governed by the existing policy for natural heritage enhanced management areas. Any new uses or commercial activities associated with this enhanced management area will be considered on a case-by-case basis and may be subject to the Class Environmental Assessment for MNR Resource Stewardship and Facility Development Projects (2003).
