## Land area description

This area includes the waters of the Lac Ste. Therese, Hanlan, Wolverine and Pivabiska Lakes and shoreline areas 120 metres in width measured from the normal high water mark of these waterbodies. There are approximately 140 cottages within this area. There are also a number of permanent residences on Lac Ste. Therese and on Pivabiska Lake. This area's close proximity to the Town of Hearst makes it a popular and important source of public recreation opportunities, with activities such as angling, swimming, boating and water skiing taking place.Part of this area is subject to the Range Management Policy in Support of Woodland Caribou Conservation and Recovery (2014). The Range Management Policy is accessible via the “Source of Direction.””

## Land use intent

Cottaging and public recreation will continue to be the major uses within this area. The intent of this area is to provide a suitable recreational environment for cottaging, boating, fishing and other recreational opportunities.The 120 metre shoreline areas around the lakes in this chain will continue to be managed primarily for recreation. Additional cottaging development will only be permitted as prescribed in the approved lake development plans governing these areas.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Hearst District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
