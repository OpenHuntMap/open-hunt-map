## Land area description

Driftwood Provincial Park is located 20 kilometres northwest of Deep River and encompasses 422 hectares.This recreation class park contains glacial remnants such as sand and gravel, diabase dike, ice-push ridge and a well-pronounced esker. The park landscape has also been shaped by flooding as a result of opening Des Joachims hydroelectric dam, and much farmland and a small lake were submerged. A fire swept the forest in the southern part of the park in 1975. The most significant geological featyre is a relict dune ridge that rises almost 30 metres.The park provides recreation opportunities such as swimming, hiking, boating, canoeing and fishing. Driftwood offers the opportunity to explore the Ottawa River Valley and is located in the vicinity of the Dumoine River.Driftwood Provincial Park was regulated as a provincial park in 1963.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Driftwood Provincial Park will be managed according to Provincial Park policy and the approved Park Management plan. The management of the park will provide recreation opportunities while protecting the natural and cultural values.
