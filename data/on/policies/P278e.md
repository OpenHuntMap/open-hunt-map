## Land area description

“Pancake Bay Provincial Park, located on the shores of Lake Superior and accessible from the Trans.Canada Highway (#17), provides travelers and residents of the region with excellent day-use and camping opportunities. The 3.5 kilometre sandy beach is one of the longest on Lake Superior’s north shore, and is popular with park visitors. Hiking trails access lookouts which provide views of Lake Superior and surrounding landscapes.”This area is located within the Great Lakes Heritage Coast Signature Site, one of 9 such areas featured in the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999). Signature Sites are identified for their range of natural and recreational values and their potential to contribute to future recreation and tourism.Pancake Bay Provincial Park was regulated as a provincial park in 1968.

## Land use intent

Pancake Bay Provincial Park will be managed according to Provincial Park policy and Park Management Plan (1988). As a recreation class park, Pancake Bay provides a variety of outdoor recreation opportunities to park visitors, contributing to the region’s tourism economy. However, the park also protects significant landscapes, such as its sand beach and raised glacial beaches, and life science values such as provincially rare plants. Management of the park will protect all these values.
