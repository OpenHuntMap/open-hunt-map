## Land area description

The 146 metre Petawawa Terrace is a large sand ridge that was once the shoreline of the Ottawa River. The landform is cloaked in a middle aged jack pine, red pine and white pine forest as well as red pine plantation. It is the best example of this type of landscape in Site District 5E-10. It overlooks a flat area of flooded deciduous swamp, shrubby wetlands and old fields, and a natural stretch of Ottawa River shoreline. Natural springs can be found at the base of the terrace.

## Land use intent

Petawawa Terrace is regulated as a provincial park.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Ontario's Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999).
