## Land area description

Silver Lake Provincial Park has a landscape of rocky outcrops and swampy lowlands, characteristic of the southern Precambriam Shield. The park is situated in Site 5E-11. It is located at the east end of Silver Lake, a lake managed for trout. The main attraction is the park's natural sand beach.Silver Lake Provincial Park was regulated as a provincial park in 1958.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Silver Lake Provincial Park was established to provide recreational opportunities such as camping and swimming.
