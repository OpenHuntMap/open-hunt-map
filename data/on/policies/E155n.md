## Land area description

Two areas within Sisk Township (organized) make up the Marten River Kames Enhanced Management Area. This area of earth science significance is site district 4E-4 contains a significant intact kame field, the kame hills, massive unsorted piles of sand, gravel and boulders area dissected by eskers and former stream beds. One of the areas abuts Marten River Provincial Park.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The intent of this Natural Heritage Enhanced Management Area is to protect the significant earth science value, while allowing for compatible forest management activity. Aggregate extraction is not permitted within this area due to the significance and sensitivity of the earth science values. Special prescriptions to direct resource management will be developed as part of ongoing planning and must be consistent with the protection of this earth science natural heritage value.Any proposed planning on activities adjacent to any provincial park will consider the management intent and direction of these protected areas. MNR is responsible for the management of Crown land and natural resources in organized townships.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in North Bay District Land Use Guidelines, (1983).

## Permitted uses — preface

Natural heritage enhanced management areas are intended to protect areas with significant natural values while allowing a range of resource activities.
