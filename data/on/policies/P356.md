## Land area description

Sharbot Lake Provincial Park has a diverse landscape of sandy beaches, lush meadowland, wooded ridges and rocky terrain. The park is situated near the southern edge of the Precambrian Shield. Rugged and sparse rock ridges rise abruptly above the lakeshore which has been worn down by ancient glaciers. A deciduous forest of maple, oak and birch as well as some butternut and hickory trees adds beauty and richness to the landscape.The park encompasses a picturesque area of the Canadian Shield and provides a variety of experiences and challenges for the park user. The park fronts on Black Lake and Sharbot Lake. Black Lake is small, quiet and sheltered while Sharbot Lake is large, deep and often windy. Views to both Sharbot Lake and Black Lake are afforded by the high ridge between them. The ridge area contains life and earth science features of local significance in Site District 5E-11.Sharbot Lake Provincial Park was regulated as a provincial park in 1958.

## Land use intent

This recreation class provincial park established to provide opportunities to enjoy recreational activities on land or water and to relax in a quiet natural setting. As stated in the Sharbot Lake Provincial Park Management Plan, approximately 14 ha of Crown land east of the ridge between Sharbot Lake and Black Lake will be added to the park.
