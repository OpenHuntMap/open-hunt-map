## Land area description

Presqu’ile Provincial Park is located along the shores of Lake Ontario in the County of Northumberland, near the Town of Brighton. Established in 1922, it protects a tombolo (barrier beach linking a former island with the mainland), which includes a tremendous diversity of features including sand beach, dune, meadow and marsh. The park includes diverse and significant cultural and recreational resources. It is a popular destination for camping, birdwatching, hiking, and biking. An amendment to the Presqu’ile Provincial Park boundary was regulated on June 9, 2008 to reflect direction in the 2000 park management plan. The amended regulation increased the park boundary by 45 hectares, much of which is within the water boundary extending into the bed of the lake. All of the added areas are Crown land.

## Land use intent

This natural environment class park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act.

## Permitted uses — preface
