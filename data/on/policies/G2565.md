## Land area description

The area consists of the developed portion of the Township of Atikokan, and is the urban, industrial and service centre for the Atikokan Area. There are little or no Crown lands in this area.Development is controlled by an Official Plan and zoning by-laws.The town is accessed by provincial highways and resource access roads and is crossed by several hydro line corridors, a railway and a natural gas pipeline corridor.

## Land use intent

The area will continue as a townsite.MNR will maintain liaison with the Atikokan township to ensure that Ministry concerns are addressed in their Official Plan and zoning by-laws and to encourage industrial, residential and tourist oriented development in this area.The Atikokan River flows through the Townsite and is prone to flooding. The flood plain will be identified and incorporated in the Official Plan for the town. Supplementary by-laws will control development and land use within the identified flood plain. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Atikokan District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
