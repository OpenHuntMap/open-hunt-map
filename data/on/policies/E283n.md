## Land area description

Achigan Lake Enhanced Management Area is located approximately 40 km northeast of the City of Sault Ste Marie in Marne and Sheilds Townships. The area includes old growth white pine interspersed with a tolerant hardwood forest wihin a relativly roadless recreational area. This Enhanced Management Area was developed by local stakeholders and the forest industry to protect old growth white pine and yellow birch, water quality, recreation and remote road access values in the area surrounding Achigan Lake.Access to Achigan Lake Enhanced Management Area is primarily achieved by the Algoma Central Railway (ACR), which bisects the site. Milepost 43 on lower Achigan Lake is the most commonly used ACR stop. Access to the periphery of the site is also provided by a number of unmaintained tertiary roads and a mountain bike trail, previously maintained by Searchmont Resort.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The Land Use Intent of Achigan Lake Enhanced Management Area is to protect the sustainability of old growth white pine and yellow birch, water quality, recreation and remote road access values.Key aspects of the Land Use Intent of this area were established through the approved recommendations of the Marne Townshop Working Group in 1996. As a result of these recommendations, forest managment has been deferred until April 1, 2023. Additionally, the MNR will protect the integrity of the area and generally not issue dispositions in the Enhanced Management Area until 2023.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Ontario's Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999) and the Sault Ste. Marie District Land Use Guidelines (1983).

## Permitted uses — preface

As a result of a study conducted by the Marne Township Working Group, a motion was presented to the Algoma Forest Local Citizens Committee in 1996, which included the following:1.Timber management operations in the Marne Township study area are to be deferred for a period of at least 28 years (i.e. year 2023), while providing the forest industry with a contingency area. 2.Approval of the motion will not constitute a land use decision; these types of decisions will be dealt with through forthcoming planning initiatives, such as the Ecological Lake Use Planning process, the Roadless/Wilderness Policy development, etc. (Note: Area subsequently designated as an enhanced management area in 1999; see below). 3.For the interim, the MNR will not approve any new land-related tenure in the study area (e.g. Land Use Permit for trails), until such time as one of the above mentioned planning initiatives include the study area. 4.Existing uses, including tourism and recreation, can continue to take place in the study area at the current level, however; exclusive use will not apply. This motion was endorsed by the Algoma Forest Local Citizen's Committee and approved by the Sault Ste. Marie District Manager on May 22, 1996. Three years later, the area surrounding Achigan Lake in Marne and Shields Townships was formally approved as a natural heritage class enhanced management area, as part of the Ontario's Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999); a comprehensive land use planning initiative, as referenced in points two and three above. The intent of the original 1996 agreement is reflected in this policy report for Achigan Lake Enhanced Management Area. Natural heritage enhanced management areas are intended to protect areas with significant natural values, while allowing for a range of resource activities.
