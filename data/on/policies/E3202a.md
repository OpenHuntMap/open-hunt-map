## Land area description

The North Pipestone Bay – McIntosh Enhanced Management Area is located within the ValhallaAdjacent Area of the Whitefeather Forest and Adjacent Areas Land Use Strategy (2006). Thearea abuts the northern boundary of the Pipestone Bay – McIntosh Enhanced Management Area,ID number E2359a which is defined in the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999).Development in the area includes two remote outpost camps, and four commercial boat cashes.Bear management areas, baitfish blocks, traplines, and a wildlife management unit are also usedto manage the area. Surveys have documented high winter use by woodland caribou

## Land use intent

The primary intent for the North Pipestone Bay – McIntosh Enhanced Management Area is tocontribute to a new forestry opportunity while supporting remoteness objectives by implementinga temporary access road policy. Forestry activities will be determined through an environmentalassessment process and subsequent forest management planning . Activities associated withthe mineral sector, eco-cultural tourism, and non-timber forest products will be encouraged.No permanent road access will be created. Temporary access into the area will be carefullyplanned to avoid undesireable impacts on nearby park and tourism values, to prevent access intosensitve areas and to consider the values of the area that support woodland caribou populations.Specific measures to address the conservation of woodland caribou population currently usingwinter habitat within the North Pipestone Bay – McIntosh Enhanced Management Area will beidentified during forest management planning.These areas will complement the protection of wilderness values by the parks and protectedareas system and provide high-quality remote recreational experiences including hunting, fishing,canoeing, and camping.

## Permitted uses — preface

Management will also be governed by the Land Use Strategy for the Whitefeather Forest andAdjacent Areas and MNR policies. This direction complements land use direction provided forthe adjacent Pipestone Bay – McIntosh EMA.
