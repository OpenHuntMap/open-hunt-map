## Land area description

This area includes the Canadian portion of Lake Superior from Pointe Louise to Montreal River, excluding Goulais Bay, Inner and Outer Batchawana Bay, Sand Bay and the waters of Lake Superior bounded by the coastline and a line from Sinclair Island to Theano Point. It excludes that part of General Use Area G1903 located in Lake Superior off of Gros Cap, as well as a 1.6 kilometre setback into Lake Superior associated with Lake Superior Provincial Park and park additions. The present land use activities occurring in Lake Superior include resource production (commercial fishing, aggregate extraction) and recreation.Coastal portions of this area are located within the Great Lakes Heritage Coast Signature Site, one of 9 such areas featured in the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999). Signature Sites are identified for their range of natural and recreational values and their potential to contribute to future recreation and tourism.

## Land use intent

The general land use intent for this area is resource production, commercial fishing and aggregate extraction. Fish spawning areas along the shoreline will be protected from detrimental uses. Recreational uses will be permitted in most shoreline areas. The District will dispose of Crown water lots for public or private use subject to official plans and associated zoning by-laws. Major disposition is also subject to the Ministry of the Environment's review which may result in the requirement of an environmental assessment. Disposition which would result in direct or indirect negative impact on any Ministry program will not be permitted. The shoreline areas, protected bays and natural harbours offer attractive recreational boating opportunities (motor boats, yachts, sailboats, canoes). Some of these areas are well known and have been publicized in various publications and consequently are relatively extensively used by cottage owners and tourists. In order to maintain the availability of such opportunities, MNR will retain for public use, at least the present area, on Crown islands and Crown shorelines of the Great Lakes; however, no future development for recreation is planned at present. Islands and lands under the waters of Lakes Superior and Huron have been withdrawn from staking. All activities in the area will take into consideration the known spawning areas. The specific location for each can be found in a compendium at the District office.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Sault Ste. Marie District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
