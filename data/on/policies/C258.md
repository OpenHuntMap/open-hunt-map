## Land area description

Thessalon River Delta/Rock Lake Red Oak Conservation Reserve demonstrates a relatively high degree of diversity for its size. It contains unique geological and ecological features, including the best representative example of moderately broken shallow sandy till uplands on bedrock in Ecodistrict 5E-1. Exposed bedrock ridges, oriented in a northwest to southeast direction, are interspersed with small wetlands. Red oak and sugar maple communities dominate most of the site's ridges, with eastern white pine growing throughout the site, and pockets of eastern white cedar in moist bedrock valleys. Growing at the northern extent of its range, supported by rocky uplands, the red oak in this conservation reserve is considered to be provincially significant. Although not entirely captured within the conservation reserve, extensive wetland areas around Indian Lake and at the mouth of the Thessalon River are important waterfowl staging areas, providing habitat for a variety of ducks, and other migratory birds. Old Mill Beach at the site's southern extent provides public boat access onto Rock Lake.Thessalon River Delta/Rock Lake Red Oak Conservation Reserve was regulated on April 6, 2001. It is regulated under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act.A 34 ha addition encompassing a large portion of the provincially significant Thessalon River Delta was donated to MNR and regulated as part of the conservation reserve in 2014.LOCATION:This protected area is located approximately 16 kilometres (km) north of the Town of Bruce Mines, in Aberdeen Township. Part of the conservation reserve’s southern boundary is defined by the north shores of Rock Lake and Indian Lake, and the remainder of the site’s boundary is defined by lot lines of adjacent patent properties. This site is surrounded by the Deer Yarding Areas (G1901), and Waterfowl Areas (G1902) General Use areas.

## Land use intent

To protect the natural heritage values on these public lands while permitting compatible land use activities.

## Permitted uses — preface

The Thessalon River Delta/Rock Lake Red Oak Conservation Reserve Statement of Conservation Interest (SCI) was approved on May 30, 2003 and should be consulted for detailed information on the site's management. Those uses and management activities not listed in the SCI are governed by existing Conservation Reserves Policy of the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act, and by the general policies contained in the Ontario's Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999).
