## Land area description

Kaiashk Provincial Park (780 hectares) is located near the Gull River north west of the city of Thunder Bay.Kaiashk Provincial Park was regulated in 1989 and is classified as Nature Reserve to protect significant earth science features.This area is subject to the Range Management Policy in Support of Woodland Caribou Conservation and Recovery (2014). The Range Management Policy is accessible via the “Source of Direction.”

## Land use intent

The intent is to manage this park as a nature reserve class provincial park, in accordance with MNR and Ontario Parks Policy.

## Permitted uses — preface

Management of this provincial park is carried out in accordance with Ontario Provincial Parks: Planning and Management Policies (1992), the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999) and the Interim Management Statement, where available. At that time, significant decisions regarding resource stewardship, operations, development and permitted uses are made.
