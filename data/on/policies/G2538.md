## Land area description

Located northeast of the City of Dryden this area includes Gullwing Lake and Bluett Lake. Activities occurring in this area include timber harvesting, tourist lodge development, cottaging, commercial fishing, sport fishing and hunting. The area is known for its very high moose population, one of the highest in the District and is a popular hunting area for Dryden residents. This area forms part of the Wabigoon Forest. Issues which will have to be addressed in the future management of this area include: timber harvesting, maintaining a high moose population and access to the interior of the area.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The primary use of this area will be for resource extraction (timber harvesting and mineral exploration/extraction) however, it will occur in such a manner that Gullwing Lake will not be jeopardized and the moose population will remain at its current high level. The placement and management of access roads during timber harvesting and mineral exploration will be the key to ensuring that these fish and wildlife values are maintained. Restrictions on hunting in the area may also be necessary immediately following an area being cut in order to ensure that the moose population is not unduly impacted. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Dryden District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
