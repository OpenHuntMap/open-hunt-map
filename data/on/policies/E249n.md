## Land area description

The Endikai Lake Perched Delta Enhanced Management Area is located in the headwater area of the Little White River, approximately 37 km northwest of the City of Elliot Lake in Le Caron and Albanel Townships. Access to this site is difficult due to the rugged nature of the terrain. There are no roads within the Enhanced Management Area and the only known existing trail is an unmaintained portage trail which connects Black Lake, located just east of the Enhanced Management Area, to Endakai Lake, located a short distance south of the area. The north, west and east boundaries of Endikai Lake Perched Delta Enhanced Management Area abut Kirkpatrick Lake South and Northeast Enhanced Management Area (E237r); whereas, the area's southern boundary abuts Little White River Provincial Park (P261). Endikai Lake Perched Delta is an area of earth science significance and is located in Ecodistrict 4E-3. It contains an ancient delta that was deposited at a point where the receding glacier was in contact with glacial Lake Algonquin. The delta was subsequently abandoned when the lake level dropped, and now it remains ”perched“ in the hills. Kettles are found on the upper surface of this landform. Due to the exceptional quality and ease of interpretation of these earth science features, this site is considered to be provincially significant.

## Land use intent

The land use intent of this area is to protect the earth science features found in Endikai Lake Perched Delta Enhanced Management Area, while allowing for a range of resource activities.Land use direction and resource management activities within this area will be compatible with protecting the natural and recreational values of the Little White River Provincial Park.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Ontario's Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999) and the Blind River District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
