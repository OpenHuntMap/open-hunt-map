## Land area description

Esker Lakes Provincial Park is a 3,237 hectare natural environment class park, located 37 kilometres northeast of the Town of Kirkland Lake. Esker Lakes contain a rich diversity of significant geological and vegetative features.Twenty-nine kettle lakes, one of the largest eskers in Ontario, sand dunes, and other post-glacial formations are found through the park.Esker Lakes Provincial Park was regulated as a provincial park in 1957.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Esker Lakes Provincial Park will be managed according to Provincial Park policy and Park Management Plan (1988). The park will protect significant geological and vegetative features while providing recreation opportunities.
