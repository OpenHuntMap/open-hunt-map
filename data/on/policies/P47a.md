## Hunting

Not addressed in this policy.

Provincial season, licence, tag and firearm rules still apply, as do municipal discharge by-laws. Verify before hunting.

## Land area description

The Ministry of Natural Resources and Forestry (MNRF) purchased two parcels (20 ha and 40 ha) of land from private ownership in 2004 for the purpose of adding them to Burnt Lands Provincial Park. The addition of these lands to the park will provide increased protection for park values, including open alvar meadows and a mixture of relatively undisturbed upland and lowland mixed and coniferous forests. The proposed addition will help to consolidate the park boundary and increase the amount of interior core protected area, which contributes to ecological function.

## Land use intent

The intent is to regulate the recommended additions under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act, 2006 as part of Burnt Lands Provincial Park.

## Permitted uses — preface

Management of the recommended additions will be carried out in accordance with the Burnt Lands Interim Management Statement (2001), Ontario Provincial Parks: Planning and Management Policies (1992), and Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999).

## Addendum

Note: MNRF will consider the Land Use Intent and Management Direction outlined in this policy report when reviewing applications for permitted activities that require licences, leases, permits, or other forms of approval. The review of individual applications involves the consideration of a variety of factors and requirements on a site-specific basis in addition to land use policy.
