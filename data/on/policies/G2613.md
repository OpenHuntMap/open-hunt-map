## Land area description

Shoal Lake is covered under the Shoal Lake Restricted Area Order. As a result of past harvest and little available area, there is very little logging and the main activities include cottaging and commercial fishing. The Indian communities of Shoal Lake Bands 39 and 40 are located on the northwest corner of the lake. The population of these communities is approximately 400. The area is one of significant mineral potential. Additionally, Shoal Lake supplies drinking water to the City of Winnipeg, therefore, any proposed development must be considered with due regard for water quality.A watershed management plan is currently (2001) being prepared for the Shoal Lake watershed.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.Water Management Areas have been removed from this Policy Report area as revisions, to more accurately reflect First Nation reserve lands affected by flooding.

## Land use intent

This area will be one of limited development. Future development must adhere to the existing Development Guidelines for the Restricted Area Order presently in place. The primary role of the area will be for extensive recreation use considering the lake as the source of water supply for the City of Winnipeg. Resource extraction activities and future developments will be reviewed prior to approval and recommended for application under the Environmental Assessment Act to minimize the risk of inadvertent degradation of water quality. MNR supports in principle the need for further study to accurately determine the particular sensitivity and development capacity of Shoal Lake.
