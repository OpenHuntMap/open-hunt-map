## Land area description

Balsam lake Provincial Park is located on the Trent-Severn Waterway in the picturesque Kawarthas in Bexley Township. The park is approximately 45 km northwest of the Town of Lindsay and approximately 142 km north of Toronto.Surficial features of the Wisconsin found in the park include an esker and a drumlin. An esker kame complex supports a rolling and partially wooded landscape.Balsam Lake Park was regulated in 1968.

## Land use intent

This recreational class park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act.
