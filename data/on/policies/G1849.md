## Land area description

Long Lake is made up of previously named Kushog Lake, Kinogami Lake and Robillard Lake. The area adjoins the Englehart River Provincial Park. The major access road in the area is Highway 560. The area is not municipally organized. Recreation is a major activity in the area. Five commercial tourism facilities, 40 cottages, two access points and a portion of the Englehart River canoe route are located in the area. There are existing farm operations within and adjacent to the area. Parts of the area are forecast for timber harvesting. Mineral potential is low. There are some wild rice beds in Kushog Lake.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The intent for this area is to provide for existing recreation opportunities and some additional cottaging. Recreation will be the priority. Agriculture and resource extraction will continue in non-shoreline areas. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Kirkland Lake District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
