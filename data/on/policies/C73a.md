## Land area description

The Louck Lake provincially significant wetland in Site District 5E-8 is an excellent example of the flat glacial deposits that snake north to south across the area west of Algonquin Park. The flat lands and organic deposits are on an ancient sand plain associated with glacial Lake Algonquin. This landform type is very limited in the Site District. Louck Lake is a shallow waterbody with extensive open water, floating and submergent aquatic vegetation. The shorelines are dominated by low shrub marshes and shrub fens, ringed by lowland conifer swamps on peat. This area is located within the Great Lakes Heritage Coast Signature Site, one of 9 such areas featured in the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999). Signature Sites are identified for their range of natural and recreational values and their potential to contribute to future recreation and tourism. The Louck Lake Wetland Conservation Reserve Addition was recommended for protection in order to facilitate the resolution of a Court case involving an unauthorized occupation within the Louck Lake Wetland Conservation Reserve. The Ministry of Natural Resources reviewed various resolution options associated with an unauthorized cabin constructed on Lot 4, Con. 7, Laurier Township. It was determined that the most environmentally sensitive resolution would be to allow the illegally built cabin to remain where it is and facilitate a land exchange between the owner of the building and the Crown, resulting in the acquisition with the intent to regulate the Louck Lake Wetland Conservation Reserve Addition. Approximately 24 hectares of this 40 hectare addition is within the Louck Lake provincially significant wetland. Louck Lake Wetland was regulated as a conservation reserve on May 8, 2002.

## Land use intent

MNR proposes to regulate this 40 ha within the Louck Lake Wetland Conservation Reserve in order to facilitate a land exchange. Once this land is transferred, this area will be managed under the same policy regime as C73 Louck Lake Wetland Conservation Reserve. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Land Use Strategy (1999).

## Addendum

Management of this conservation reserve is carried out under the provisions of the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act and within the context of conservation reserve policy. Note: MNR will consider the Land Use Intent and Management Direction outlined in this policy report when reviewing applications for permitted activities that require licences, leases, permits, or other forms of approval. The review of individual applications involves the consideration of a variety of factors and requirements on a site-specific basis in addition to land use policy.
