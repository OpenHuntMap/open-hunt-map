## Land area description

The South Timiskaming Shoreline is located within the Lake Timiskaming watershed. The values and uses within the reserve include the backcountry hiking trail along a scenic portion of Lake Timiskaming and the scenic values along the Ottawa-Timiskaming Waterway, including a rugged shoreline of cobble beaches, cliffs which rise from the water’s edge to 90 meters in some places, and a mixture of coniferous and deciduous forest stands. The Ottawa-Temiskaming Highland Trail system passes through the conservation reserve and provides recreational and natural heritage appreciation opportunities. The South Timiskaming Shoreline Conservation Reserve was regulated on June 25, 2004.The South Timiskaming Shoreline Conservation Reserve contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Inland Ontario Lakes Designated for Lake Trout Management (2006) accessible in the Source of Direction portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The South Timiskaming Shoreline Conservation Reserve will protect a shoreline corridor along Lake Timiskaming for further development of a low-intensity backcountry hiking trail and will protect the aesthetics of the shoreline for recreational users of the Ottawa-Timiskaming waterway. Land management concerns include retaining trail potential and aesthetics adjacent to the shoreline of Lake Timiskaming. Management area objectives are to ensure that no roads or infrastructure other than primitive hut-to-hut facilities are developed and to allow natural processes to occur.

## Permitted uses — preface

Those uses and management activities not listed in the following table are governed by existing conservation reserve policy, the Temagami Land Use Plan, and the South Timiskaming Shoreline Conservation Reserve Statement of Conservation Interest (2005). Any new uses and commercial activities associated with conservation reserves will be considered on a case-by-case basis and will be required to pass a test of compatibility to be acceptable.
