## Land area description

This area extends along the southeastern border of the Dryden District. Included are parts of Grummett, Cathcart, McNevin, Corman and Chartrand Townships. The White Otter designated canoe route passes through this area along the Scotch River. This area is also noted for the cottaging opportunities it provides. Crown subdivisions exist on Lower Scotch, Savoy and Encamp Lakes; remote cottages on Kathleen, Phyllis, and Gulliver Lakes; and two private recreation camps on Nora Lake.Nora and Savoy Lakes are designated as tourism lakes.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The primary activities in this area will be existing cottaging and timber harvesting. No additional cottaging will be considered. Existing tourism and cottaging in the area will be permitted to continue. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Ignace District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
