## Land area description

This area encompasses the shoreline (120 metres) around seven waterbodies. They include the following adjoining lakes, Rufus, Penelton, Opasatika, Usnac and Inlet. Rice Lake and Little Rice Lake are also included in this policy area. The area is located approximately 100 kilometres southwest of the Town of Kapuskasing. Access is provided by the Fergus Road and the Cargill-Bourinot Roads. This complex of lakes is an important area for public recreation. Activities include fishing, hunting, boating and camping. Commercial tourism and cottaging are also major activities. Development is concentrated on Rufus Lake and includes a commercial lodge and marina, an official access point, remote cottages and a Ministry fire tower cabin. Development elsewhere in the lake complex is limited and includes a company guest camp, one remote cottage and a trapper's cabin. A concrete weir north of Rufus Lake controls water levels.

## Land use intent

This lake complex will continue to be a warm water fisheries priority and will be managed for this objective. Cottaging and commercial tourism are also priorities that will be managed consistent with fisheries objectives. Tourism lakes will be managed to protect their remote quality. Rice Lake is designated as a tourism lake.Modified forest management will be practiced in shoreline areas to protect significant natural and cultural features and the recreational environment. All activities will ensure protective measures for a bald eagle's nest, numerous pickerel spawning areas and a waterfowl staging area. Further mechanisms for protecting two regionally significant historical sites relating to the fur trade will be sought.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Kapuskasing District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
