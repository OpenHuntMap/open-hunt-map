## Land area description

This area is located within the Montreal River/Lady Evelyn River watershed. The values and uses in this area include old white and red pine stands, a mixture of stocked cold-water fisheries, and acidification research lakes. The management area borders on Montreal River and includes the low use backcountry recreation area of Lady Evelyn River between the Montreal River and Lady Evelyn Lake that is only accessible by boat. This area also includes existing and heritage portages.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Inland Ontario Lakes Designated for Lake Trout Management (2006) accessible in the Source of Direction portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The land use intent is to provide opportunities for forestry and mining related activities, to maintain backcountry recreation and remote tourism values, and to maintain and enhance old growth pine characteristics.Concerns include maintaining the water-only access to Lady Evelyn Lake and the protection of the aesthetic values of the lake and river, the mining related activities on the shores of Lady Evelyn Lake, the maintenance of the areas remote character (e.g., water access, limited development), and the use of portages as ATV trails (conflicts with non-motorized recreation).The management area objectives are to manage for old pine characteristics, to rehabilitate acidified lake trout Lakes (Gullrock, Kitt, and Kittson lakes), to maintain remote angling and tourism values (e.g., Lady Evelyn Lake), and to maintain existing canoe routes and retain potential for new routes.The strategies include applying road use strategies to restrict public motorized access and minimize conflicts, and managing for old growth characteristics when harvesting white and red pine through carefully planned and monitored forestry activities. In addition, strategies include developing prescriptions for forestry and mining that maintain lake aesthetics/recreational values on Lady Evelyn Lake, and applying viewscape and Area of Concern planning for extractive activities. A strategy was developed to prohibit further use of existing portage trails by ATVs within this management area as part of the Crown Land Recreation Plan Temagami Integrated Planning process.
