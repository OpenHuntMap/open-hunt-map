## Land area description

This site consists of moderately broken outwash deposit showing a fair amount of relief, dominated by even aged stands of white birch, spruce, jackpine, and poplar/aspen. The site is drained by Wildgoose River and contains three lakes. Wetlands are diverse here with tamarack and cedar swamps and some some fens and bogs.

## Permitted uses — preface

Management of this provincial park is carried out in accordance with Ontario Provincial Parks: Planning and Management Policies (OMNR 1992), the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (OMNR 1999) and an Interim Management Statement, when available. The Interim Management Statement will provide direction to ensure custodial management of park resources until such time as a park management planning process is conducted, which includes public and Aboriginal review of proposals. At that time, significant decisions regarding resource stewardship, operations, development and permitted uses will be made.
