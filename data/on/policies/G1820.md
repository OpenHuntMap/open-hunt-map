## Land area description

The Deloro Grassy complex is located south of the urban areas of Timmins, Schumacher and South Porcupine. Approximately 50 percent of the area falls within the municipal jurisdiction of the City of Timmins and is subject to the existing official plan and zoning by-law. There is a small amount of residential development in the north portion of the area. Extensive mineral exploration and extraction has occurred in Tisdale and Deloro Townships. The complex lies within 2 forest Management Units. Three canoe routes traverse the area, the Grassy, Mountjoy and the Redstone. There are numerous unofficial access points in the area. Several good moose wintering and waterfowl production staging areas are located on the Grassy River. An historical site is located in Cody Township and was the site of an early half way house on the Porcupine Trail. The area also has one outpost camp situated on Upper Michegama Lake.

## Land use intent

The priority in this area will be resource extraction commercial tourism, and the maintenance of the Grassy River, Mountjoy River and Redstone River canoe routes. MNR will also investigate further mechanisms for protecting the regionally significant cultural resources of Hills Landing. Resource management plans will contain guidelines for the protection of significant recreation, historic and fish and wildlife values. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Timmins District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
