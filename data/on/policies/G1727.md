## Land area description

This area encompasses the developed portion of the Town of Hearst which is presently covered by an official plan and a comprehensive zoning by-law. The major industry in the area is forestry which employs a large percentage of the town's population of over 5,000. The area's single industry economy lacks the stability supplied by diversification.

## Land use intent

The primary intent of the area will be to encourage the orderly development of the Town of Hearst and to provide a focus for the development of urban residential, industrial and service facilities. MNR will conduct its resource management programs in accordance with the official plan and zoning by-law which are approved for the area. MNR will assist the Ministry of Municipal Affairs and Housing where required in the review and amendment of municipal plans and by-laws which would influence the use, enjoyment, conservation or preservation of the natural resources in this and adjacent areas. Structural development will not be permitted in areas subject to flooding, erosion or land subsidence (hazard lands).Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Hearst District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
