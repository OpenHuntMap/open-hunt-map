## Land area description

This important caribou travel corridor links Lake Nipigon and Onaman Lake along the southern boundary of the Onaman River, and has significance related to its location near the southern limit of caribou range. Extensive forest operations and associated access occurs in this corridor, as well as recreational angling and hunting. Other activities include mineral exploration, cottaging, tourism and trapping.Onaman Lake and Northwind Lake are designated as tourism lakes.Road access to Onaman Lake is an issue to be addressed in this area.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.This area is subject to the Range Management Policy in Support of Woodland Caribou Conservation and Recovery (2014). The Range Management Policy is accessible via the “Source of Direction.”

## Land use intent

Forestry activities will be planned to protect caribou habitat and enhance travel routes from Lake Nipigon to Onaman Lake. The extent of existing and future access in the Enhanced Management Area will be examined to determine where permanent access is required for forestry and transportation, where access is desirable for recreation and tourism activities (e.g. hunting and angling) and where access should be removed or allowed to naturally deteriorate, to address caribou habitat concerns. Landscape level planning will be integrated with local FMP planning to ensure landscape linkages between Onaman Lake and Lake Nipigon are maintained.Other activities including mineral exploration, cottaging, tourism and trapping will continue. Special protection will be given to Onaman lake in recognition of the important role it plays in the District's fishery program.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Nipigon District Land Use Guidelines (1983).Humboldt - Onaman Corridor Enhanced Management Area is located in an area in which one or more First Nations have asserted Aboriginal rights and title, including an unextinguished Aboriginal right to exclusive use and occupancy of the land. The First Nations’ claims are the subject of litigation and negotiations. Dispositions in Humboldt - Onaman Corridor Enhanced Management Area may be subject to heightened Crown consultation and accommodation obligations.This may mean a greater degree of engagement with First Nation communities asserting rights and title prior to a decision regarding potential authorizations or dispositions of public lands or resources. For a map of areas affected by these claims, please go to the Notice of Caution on the Mining Lands Administration System (MLAS) at https://www.geologyontario.mndm.gov.on.ca/mines/lands/withreop/notices_e.html .

## Addendum

Northwind Lake and Onaman Lake are designated as a Tourism Lakes. In addition to provincial and regional policies, the Onaman Lake Fisheries Management Plan (1998-2008) will apply to the management of Onaman Lake. When feasible, permanent forest access roads will be located a minimum of 1.6 kilometres from these lakes.
