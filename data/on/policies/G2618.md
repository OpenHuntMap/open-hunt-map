## Land area description

This area includes much of the bed of Lake Nipigon and a small portion of the shoreline within Windigo Bay Provincial Park. Several parcels of private land occur on the mainland and islands at the south east end of the lake, some of which are used for cottaging. Excluded from the area are all existing and proposed provincial parks and conservation reserves (including the aquatic portions of the Lake Nipigon Conservation Reserve (Humbolt Bay, South Bay, McIntyre Bay, West Bay) and the aquatic portion of the Recommended Black Sturgeon River Provincial Park Addition (Black Sturgeon Bay) and Kopka River Provincial Park (Wabinosh Bay)Present uses of the area include Crown land recreation, tourism, cottaging, mineral exploration, trapping, commercial fishing and sport fishing. An aboriginal healing lodge is located on a small portion of Crown land on the north shore of Lake Nipigon.Lake Nipigon provides habitat for a wide array of species at risk and other important wildlife and fisheries. Included are woodland caribou, white pelicans, bald eagles, and the Lake Nipigon Brook Trout.This area is located within the Lake Nipigon Basin Signature Site, one of 9 such areas featured in the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999). Signature Sites are identified for their range of natural and recreational values and their potential to contribute to future recreation and tourism.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.This area is subject to the Range Management Policy in Support of Woodland Caribou Conservation and Recovery (2014). The Range Management Policy is accessible via the “Source of Direction.”

## Land use intent

This area will be managed to ensure the continued health of the Lake Nipigon ecosystem. While allowing for a diversity of uses. Management activities on Crown land will recognize that flooding rights are reserved on Lake Nipigon to contour elevation 260.6 metres for hydroelectric purposes. Management strategies will be implemented to address any sources of water pollution, prevent the introduction of exotics and minimize impacts related to hydro development Aqua-culture will not be permitted on Lake Nipigon. The Lake Nipigon Basin Ecological Land Use and Resource Management Strategy governs the management of this area.. Identified historic sites, and special fish, wildlife and bird habitat will be protected.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Nipigon District Land Use Guidelines (1983).Lake Nipigon General Use Area is located in an area in which one or more First Nations have asserted Aboriginal rights and title, including an unextinguished Aboriginal right to exclusive use and occupancy of the land. The First Nations’ claims are the subject of litigation and negotiations. Dispositions in Lake Nipigon General Use Area may be subject to heightened Crown consultation and accommodation obligations.This may mean a greater degree of engagement with First Nation communities asserting rights and title prior to a decision regarding potential authorizations or dispositions of public lands or resources. For a map of areas affected by these claims, please go to the Notice of Caution on the Mining Lands Administration System (MLAS)at https://www.geologyontario.mndm.gov.on.ca/mines/lands/withreop/notices_e.html .
