## Land area description

Makobe-Grays River Provincial Parks is a waterway class park that follows the Makobe-Grays River northward from the northern boundary of Lady Evelyn-Smoothwater Wilderness Park to the community of Elk Lake. The park boundary extends 200-metres from the river's edge along both shores. Along its 28-kilometre length are numerous small waterfalls and scenic ravines. In spring high water, numerous rapids can be Class I and II white water.Makobe-Grays River Provincial Park was regulated as a provincial park in 1985.

## Land use intent

Makobe-Grays River Provincial Park provides protection for a remote, seasonal whitewater river canoe route which is accessible from Lady Evelyn-Smoothwater Park while also protecting the park’s natural features.

## Permitted uses — preface
