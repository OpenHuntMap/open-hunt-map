## Land area description

The North Yorston Conservation Reserve is located west of the town of Latchford within the Yorston River/Lady Evelyn River/Sturgeon River watersheds. The conservation reserve includes part of the headwaters for the Sturgeon, Yorston, Pilgrim, and Lady Evelyn Rivers. Other values and uses within the reserve include the old growth white pine stands, scenic cliffs, and the good potential for backcountry hiking trails. The reserve also contains four canoe routes, numerous campsites, and fifteen portages.The North Yorston Conservation Reserve was regulated on June 25, 2004.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Inland Ontario Lakes Designated for Lake Trout Management (2006) accessible in the Source of Direction portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The intent of the North Yorston Conservation Reserve is to protect the park-related values of the Lady Evelyn-Smoothwater Wilderness Park by maintaining ecosystem integrity and offering a remote recreational area for canoeing, hiking, and other low-impact backcountry recreation activities. The land use concerns in this area include access to Land Use Permits. The management area objectives are to ensure motorized activities do not occur within the reserve, allow natural forest processes to complement adjacent park management, and to consider possible remote non-motorized recreational trail development in the future.
