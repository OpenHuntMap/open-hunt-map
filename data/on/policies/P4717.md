## Land area description

Brockville Long Swamp Fen Provincial Park consists of four parcels with a total area of 174 ha, located in the municipal Township of Elizabethtown-Kitley. Acquisition of the Brockville Long Swamp Fenproperties occurred between 1994 and 2005 and was made possible through the Ontario Parks Legacy 2000 program, a partnership between Ontario Parks, Ministry of Natural Resources and Forestry (MNRF) and the Nature Conservancy of Canada.Brockville Long Swamp Fen is located in Ecodistrict 6E-11 and is situated at the eastern edge of the Frontenac Axis portion of the Canadian Shield. Brockville Long Swamp Fen contains a diverse wetland complex of open and treed fen, mixed swamp, and emergent marsh. The site supports a number of rare species and provides some representation of mixed forest on till plain. The fen is a provincially significant wetland and has been defined by MNRF as a Life Science Area of Natural and Scientific Interest (ANSI).Brockville Long Swamp Fen Provincial Park protects one of the largest fen wetlands in southern Ontario.

## Land use intent

This nature reserve class park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act, 2006 (PPCRA).
