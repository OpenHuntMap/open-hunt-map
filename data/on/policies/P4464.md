## Land area description

Mara Provincial Park is located on the shores of Lake Simcoe in Rama Township, 6 km south of Orillia. For over 4 000 years First Nations used the narrows to harvest fish with weirs. Today, the area is still renowned for bass, muskie and trout fishing. The main attraction of the park is the wide, sandy beach, one of Lake Simcoe's best.Mara Provincial Park was regulated in 1971.

## Land use intent

This recreational class park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act.
