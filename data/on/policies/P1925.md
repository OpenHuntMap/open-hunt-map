## Land area description

Samuel de Champlain Provincial Park is located off Highway 17, 50 kilometres east of the City of North Bay. The park lies in the valley of the historic Mattawa River that is known as a historic travel route for aboriginal people, explorers and voyageurs. The outstanding landform feature within the park is a segment of the Mattawa River Fault, a 600-million year old break in the earth’s crust. The park provides attractions and recreation opportunities such as the nationally significant canoe route, a voyageur exhibit, cross-country skiing, hiking, camping and swimming. The park also includes a learning center that provides education programming and research about ecosystems and sustainable management in the Mattawa valley and district.Samuel de Champlain Provincial Park was regulated as a provincial park in 1967.

## Land use intent

Samuel de Champlain Provincial Park will be managed according to Provincial Park policy and Park Management Plan (1990). The park’s natural, cultural and recreational values will be protected while providing day-use and camping opportunities associated with the natural environment.
