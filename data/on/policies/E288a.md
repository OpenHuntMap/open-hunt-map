## Land area description

An important recreational and forestry area with the main feature being Mozhabong Lake Conservation Reserve to the east. This Enhanced Management Area, which surrounds the conservation reserve is relatively remote, but contains some road accessible portions. The forest access road is located to the north and is managed on a restricted access basis. This area is managed under a sustainable forest Licence (SFL).This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Manage this important recreation and resource sector (forestry, mining) area consistent with the enhanced management area (remote access) policies and with current MNR land management practices and MNR Espanola District land use planning documents and resource management plans. Use of the existing road access to the south end of Mozhabong Lake will continue.Land use direction and resource management activities within this area to be compatible with protecting the recreational values of the recommended Mohzabong Lake Conservation Reserve.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Land Use Strategy (1999) and the Espanola District Land Use Guidelines (1983).

## Permitted uses — preface

Remote access enhanced management area are intended to maintain the remote character of selected areas. Typically, these are relatively large areas which provide the public and tourism operators with high-quality remote recreational experiences including hunting, fishing, canoeing, and camping. Given the large size, remoteness, and relative absence of roads, these areas will play a significant role in protecting wilderness values outside the parks and protected areas system.In addition to provincial and regional policies, the following local policies will apply to the management of Natural lake trout lakes:­ no additional access will be permitted­ 120 metre Area of Concern will be applied­ forest management activities may be permitted within the Area of Concern subject to concerns for erosion/sedimentation and the protection of lake trout habitat.
