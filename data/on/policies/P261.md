## Land area description

The park includes a approximately 100 kilometres stretch of the Little White River, which reaches from 'Blue Lake' headwater area (in Eco District 4E-3) southwest to the Mississagi River (EcoDistrict 5E-1). The river's meanders, rapids and gravel flats pass through forests and shoreline habitats such as wetlands, 'oxbow' sloughs and floodplain swamps of silver maple, black ash and white elm. The river also has natural connections with several natural heritage areas, including the Endekai Perched Delta enhanced management area and Mississagi River Provincial Park. The 'Blue Lakes' portion includes self sustaining populations of Lake Trout, Brook Trout and Rainbow Trout. The Raven Lake natural heritage area which is included in the park contains bedrock cliff faces, upland rock barrens, deep gorges, ravines, convoluted linear wetlands and significant stands of white pine growing on the glacier-scoured hills.The park area includes a number of commercial tourism and snowmobile trail facilities.Little White River Provincial Park was regulated as a provincial park on December 7, 2002.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The park boundary includes the skyline area north of Two Camp Creek. The Raven Lake area may be considered as a natural environment park in combination with the Wakomata portion of the Mississagi River during future planning.This area is a provincial park, waterway class.In accordance with commitment #20 of the Ontario Forest Accord, one road crossing of the park for forestry purposes may be permitted where alternatives that do not cross the park are determined to be unacceptable through future forest management planning.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Ontario's Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999).

## Permitted uses — preface

Management of this provincial park is carried out in accordance with Ontario Provincial Parks: Planning and Management Policies (1992), the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999) and the Interim Management Statement, where available. The Interim Management Statement provides direction to ensure custodial management of park resources until such time as a park management planning process is conducted, which includes public review of proposals. At that time, significant decisions regarding resource stewardship, operations, development and permitted uses are made.
