## Land area description

This area is located in the southeastern portion of Dryden District and is well accessed by Highways 17 and 599 and other resource access roads. Included in this area are large blocks of patent land - Abitibi Block No. 6 and parts of Abitibi Block No. 7. Recreational uses of the area include one tourist lodge, one campground, one outpost camp, two private recreation camps, and a cottage sub-division. Sensitive areas are few. There is one historical quarry at Bonheur.The following are designated as tourism lakes: Raven, Notman, XE6929 Unnamed Lake North of Tri-Lake, Baltic, Running Deer, Ruxton.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.Part of this area is subject to the Range Management Policy in Support of Woodland Caribou Conservation and Recovery (2014). The Range Management Policy is accessible via the “Source of Direction.””

## Land use intent

The intent of this area is to encourage timber and peat extraction in harmony with Crown land recreation and commercial tourism. Existing mining operations will be maintained and mineral exploration will be encouraged. Some additional tourism recreation opportunities will be considered in this area. The sensitive areas noted above will be recognized and protected in resource management plans.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Ignace District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
