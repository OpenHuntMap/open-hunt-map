## Land area description

This area includes four warm water lakes, (Pearce, Owlet, Ghost and Bovril), located approximately 35 kilometres north of the Town of Kapuskasing via the Fred Flatt Road. At present, access to the area is available to the public only during the winter months. Present activity in the area relates primarily to the forest industry. Harvesting and regeneration activities are ongoing. Owing to a lack of public access, recreational activities have primarily been limited to winter angling. Development includes one remote cottage.This area is subject to the Range Management Policy in Support of Woodland Caribou Conservation and Recovery (2014). The Range Management Policy is accessible via the “Source of Direction.”

## Land use intent

The primary use in this area will be cottaging and public recreation. Extraction activities (i.e. forestry, mining) are priorities outside of shoreline areas. Resource management plans will contain guidelines for the protection of significant features (i.e. fish and wildlife habitat and recreational features.)Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Kapuskasing District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
