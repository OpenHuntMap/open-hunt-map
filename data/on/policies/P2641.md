## Land area description

Kakabeka Falls Provincial Park (500 hectares) is located 32 kilometres west of Thunder Bay. The park was established in 1957 and regulated in 1967. It is regulated under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act. Kakabeka Falls is classified as a natural environment park.Kakabeka Falls Provincial Park provides day use and camping opportunities for local residents and tourists. The park also protects geologic features such as Kakabeka Falls, and stromatolite microfossils.

## Land use intent

The intent is to manage this park as a natural environment class provincial park, in accordance with MNR and Ontario Parks Policy.
