## Land area description

This area is located within the Lake Timiskaming watershed. The values and uses of this area include the agricultural and rural residential lands as well as a number of cultural heritage sites related to mining, logging, fur trading, and settlement. The aesthetics of the Lake Timiskaming shoreline and the Tri-Town Ski Village (downhill and cross country) are also considered of value and use in the area. The Ottawa-Temiskaming Highland Trail system passes through this management area and provides significant recreational and natural heritage appreciation opportunities.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Inland Ontario Lakes Designated for Lake Trout Management (2006) accessible in the Source of Direction portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The land use intent is to provide for forestry and mining related activities while recognizing the unique heritage of the area and protecting the tourism values associated with Lake Timiskaming. The intent is also to provide for the full range of land based recreation by protecting trails and promoting heritage appreciation. The concerns of this area include the aesthetics related to tourism and recreation values (e.g., boating, trails, Highway 567). The management area objectives are to maintain potential for lookouts and hiking trails, potential to develop heritage attraction at Mission Point, and to recognize and retain values associated with tourism. The strategy for this area includes developing road use strategies to manage public motorized access.

## Permitted uses — preface

Land use and resource management activities for this area are also governed by the policies of the Temagami Land Use Plan (TLUP) (1997). The policies from the TLUP are integrated into this area report. Refer to the TLUP for background information and additional details.
