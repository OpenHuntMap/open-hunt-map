## Land area description

Ivanhoe Lake Provincial Park is located on Highway 101, 11 kilometres southwest of Foleyet and 114 kilometres southwest of Timmins, in Ecodistrict 3E-5. Distinct stands of old white cedar are found along the low lying north shore of Ivanhoe Lake on clay soil substrate. On the north shore, the topography rises gently away from the lake and contains a predominate deciduous forest of poplar, birch with some spruce and balsam fir. On the south shore of the lake, cedar is found adjacent to the wetlands. The slope is steeper on the south shore and is covered by a mixed forest. The park landscape is a mix of glacial features: kames, kettle lakes and eskers. The park's campground, beaches and trails are a local recreation destination. Ivanhoe Lake is popular for motor boating, swimming and canoeing. This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.Ivanhoe Lake Provincial Park is a natural environment class park and was originally regulated in 1957. An addition was made to the park in 2005 as a result of Ontario’s Living Legacy.

## Land use intent

Ivanhoe Lake Provincial Park will be managed according to Provincial Park policy management and the Ivanhoe Lake Plan (1986). Ivanhoe Lake Provincial Park protects significant geomorphologic and ecological features, and provides a range of recreational opportunities.
