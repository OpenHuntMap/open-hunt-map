## Land area description

This area encompasses 641 hectares on the north shore of Lake Wanapitei. It contains a lowland swamp forest and jack pine rocklands which border the mouth of Parkin Creek and the adjacent Lake Wanapitei shoreline. The area has high mineral potential. A gold bearing structure occurs on the land and may extend under the north shore of Lake Wanapitei.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Mining, timber management, commercial fishing and public recreation will be the primary uses. Operating guidelines will be applied to resource extraction activities to ensure the protection of fish and wildlife habitat and recreational values.Close monitoring of resource extraction and recreational activities will take place to ensure appropriate mitigation measures are in place.
