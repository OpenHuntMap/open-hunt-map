## Land area description

This area is located to the east of Rocky Island Lake and includes the Mississagi River upstream to the District boundary. The park continues through the Espanola Area into Chapleau District to Biscotasing on the Canadian Pacific Railway.This popular canoe route also offers angling and hunting opportunities throughout. The isolated nature of the park is one of the main reasons for its popularity.Mississagi River Provincial Park was regulated as a provincial park in 1974.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Mississagi River Provincial Park will be managed according to provincial parks policy. The intent is to continue managing the area as a waterway park. No land uses will be permitted which may jeopardize existing park values.
