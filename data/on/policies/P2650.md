## Land area description

Pigeon River Provincial Park (953 hectares) is situated along the Pigeon River where the river enters Lake Superior. The Park provides earth and life science feature representation as well as day use opportunities. The area is located within the Great Lakes Heritage Coast Signature Site, one of 9 such areas featured in the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999). Signature Sites are identified for their range of natural and recreational values. The original 5 hectare park was established in 1957 and regulated in 1960 as Middle Falls Park; the remainder was regulated in 1989 and classified as a Natural Environment park. The park name was changed to Pigeon River Provincial Park from Middle Falls in 1998.Five hectares of patent land adjacent to the Pigeon River was acquired in 2000 and added to the park boundary in 2018. This piece of land is one of the last links in the protected river frontage along the Pigeon River and Canada/USA border waters, from Quetico Provincial Park to Lake Superior.

## Land use intent

The area is regulated as a natural environment class provincial park under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act, 2006 and, in accordance with Ministry of Natural Resources and Forestry and Ontario Parks policies.

## Permitted uses — preface

The permitted uses associated with provincial parks are found in approved management direction which are available on the Ontario Parks website. Management of this provincial park will be consistent with Ontario Provincial Parks: Planning and Management Policies (1992), the Ontario's Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999) and the Pigeon River Provincial Park Management Plan (1993). The Park Management Plan provides direction regarding resource stewardship, operations, development and permitted uses. Future development as a day use picnic area may be considered through park management planning.The permitted uses associated with provincial parks are found in approved management direction, which are available at https://www.ontario.ca/page/provincial-park-management-direction .

Management of this provincial park is carried out under the provisions of the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act, 2006 and within the context of provincial park policy.
