## Land area description

This conservation reserve, in Site District 5E-8 contains old hemlock on hilly terrain with sandy soils, with some flatter sandy deposits as well. There are three combinations of vegetation and landforms within the entire area. The site is within the Loring deer yard, with the old hemlock providing winter protection for the deer.The area is used for a variety of traditional uses, including hunting.Raganooter Lake was regulated as a conservation reserve on April 6, 2001.

## Land use intent

Consistent with the provincial policies for the management of Conservation Reserves, no new roads for resource extraction or private use will be permitted into or through this Conservation Reserve. Consistent with the traditional management of Crown lands in this area (as identified in the 1983 MNR Parry Sound District Land Use Guidelines) and consistent with the surrounding Enhanced Management Area E304a, in the Ferrie Township part of the CR, public access for recreation purposes is limited to the access routes, and means of access (roads and/or trails) used in 1983. New facilities (e.g. trails) which could enhance all-weather access are discouraged in the East Mills Twp side of the site. The bridge over the Pickerel River will be removed to limit access to this area. Further details on these access policies, including geographic areas of policy coverage, are provided in the approved Statement of Conservation Interest for this Conservation Reserve.Vegetation management (deer habitat) is provided for and addressed in Statement of Conservation Interest.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Land Use Strategy (1999).
