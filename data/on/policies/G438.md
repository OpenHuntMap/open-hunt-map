## Land area description

This area is located on the south shore of Twelve Mile Bay on Georgian Bay and is comprised of lands adjacent to Moose Deer Point First Nation.

## Land use intent

This area has been selected for transfer to the federal government as part of an addition to reserve request by Moose Deer Point First Nation. The future land use intent for this area is Indian Reserve. Management of this area will ensure protection of these lands for this future land use intent. The management direction table below reflects the original permitted activities for the area (i.e. Multiple Resource Management - G359); however, activities that were deemed not compatible with the intended future land use intent were changed.
