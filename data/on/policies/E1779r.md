## Land area description

This area is located within the Montreal River / Sturgeon River watershed. The values and uses of the area include the two portions adjacent to the north-western corner of Lady Evelyn-Smoothwater Wilderness Park, and the park-related canoe route and canoe access to the park associated with the Montreal River / Lady Dufferin Lake.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Inland Ontario Lakes Designated for Lake Trout Management (2006) accessible in the Source of Direction portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The land use intent is to control public motorized access to the boundary of Lady Evelyn-Smoothwater Provincial Park, and to protect park-related values while allowing forestry activities and mining related activities.Concerns include the snowmobile route that runs south from Lady Dufferin Lake into the wilderness park which is incompatible with park policy, and the public motorized access to the park boundary which is undesirable.The management area objectives are to ensure no new motorized public access within the management area are developed to prevent roads within 350-metres of park boundaries, and to permit seasonal forestry operations to mitigate impacts of noise, etc.The strategies include planning access and developing road use strategies to restrict public motorized vehicle use and minimize conflicts, and applying viewscape and area of concern planning to extractive activities.
