## Land area description

This area is located within the Marten River/Temagami River watershed. The values and uses of this area include the Nipissing Crown Game Preserve where hunting and trapping are not permitted as well as the motorized access to lakes with limited access by tourist operations. In addition, backcountry ski trails and the existing and heritage portages are of value.

## Land use intent

The land use intent is to provide for mining related, forest management along with a range of recreational opportunities in the Nipissing Crown Game Preserve. Additionally, the intent is to manage the existing canoe routes and retain potential routes.The concerns of the area include the potential for upgraded roads to provide better access to remote lakes resulting in greater angling pressure. The management area objectives are to manage existing canoe routes and to retain potential for new routes. In addition, the objective is to ensure access is not enhanced to remote and limited-access lakes.The strategies include applying viewscape and Area of Concern planning for extractive activities and developing road use strategies to maintain lakes which are remote or have limited access for tourism.
