## Land area description

This area includes lands within Sault Ste. Marie District, lying east of the Serpent River and its enhanced management area (E222r), and south of Pecors Lake. Across the district boundary, this general use area coincides with Sudbury District's general use area Serpent River West (G2061).The majority of the Serpent River system is now within the Serpent River Enhanced Management Area (E222r) although a portion of the system, in McCarthy Lake, remains in this general use area. The Serpent River water quality is steadily improving as a result of uranium mine closures and decommissioning activities in the Elliot Lake area.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The intent is to manage the area for extractive operations such as forestry and mining, and to support tourism activities.Requests for land disposition will be evaluated on a case by case basis, having regard to Ministry programs and the disposition policy contained in this document.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Blind River District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
