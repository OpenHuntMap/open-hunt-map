## Land area description

Area F is approximately 82,500 hectares in size and is largely privately owned land with strong emphasis on agricultural uses.Among the special features of the area are the deer winter concentration areas in the geographic Township of Hungerford and Sheffield. The geographic Townships Huntingdon and Hungerford contain high quality sand and gravel deposits.Problems and issues in this area include loss of aggregate resources to non-compatible uses and conflict between existing sand and gravel operations and nearby residential land uses.

## Land use intent

The major ministry concerns will be the identification and protection of sand and gravel resources for local use and private land forestry.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Tweed District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
