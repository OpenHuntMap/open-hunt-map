## Land area description

This area is situated in and adjacent to the Township of Rutherfored and George Island. In addition to the commercial fishing port development at the town of Killarney, commercial recreation establishments and seasonal recreation development occur throughout this area. Mineral and aggregate extraction occur in the form of a small scale surface pit and quarry operation on Badgeley Point and a silica quarry on Badgeley Island. Recreation, mineral exploration and development, commercial tourism and commercial fishing activities will continue to be permitted. All other uses such as hunting, trapping, sport fishing and seasonal residential uses should neither preclude future resource extraction activities, nor the viability of the commercial tourism industry.This area is located within the Killarney Signature Site and the Great Lakes Heritage Coast Signature Site, two of 9 such areas featured in the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999). Signature Sites are identified for their range of natural and recreational values and their potential to contribute to future recreation and tourism. This general use area includes part of the Killarney additions which would be part of the Killarney Signature Site but the some waters of Georgian Bay are within the GLHC Signature Site and is now also part of the French River Addition (P110).

## Land use intent

The mining rights to the lands under the Great Lakes were withdrawn from staking in 1912 and the islands in Georgian Bay were withdrawn under the Mining Act in 1926. In those few areas not withdrawn from staking (e.g. Badgeley Island) onshore mining developments may expand under ground, outward under the Great Lakes, subject to existing international agreements. In the remainder of the area.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Sudbury District Guidelines (1983).
