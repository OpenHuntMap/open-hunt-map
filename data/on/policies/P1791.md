## Land area description

The Shoals Provincial Park, straddling Highway 101 west of Chapleau, contains significant post-glacial landscapes and wetlands. A campground on Little Wawa Lake and a number of interior campsites provide a range of recreation opportunities, including car camping and backcountry canoeing.The Shoals Provincial Park was regulated as a provincial park in 1970.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The Shoals Provincial Park will be managed according to Provincial Park policy and Interim Management Statement (1999). The Shoals Provincial Park will provide a variety of recreational opportunities while protecting significant earth and life science values. The Shoals will be managed as a natural environment class park.
