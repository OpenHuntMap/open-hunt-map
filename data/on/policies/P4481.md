## Land area description

Rideau River Provincial Park was established in 1963 to offer recreational facilities in an attractive setting along the Rideau Waterway. Located 40 km south of Ottawa, and close to several historic villages and towns, the park provides swimming, boating, beach, fishing, and camping opportunities for visitors.

## Land use intent

This recreational class park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act.
