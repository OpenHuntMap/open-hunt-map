## Land area description

Kirkwood Delta Margin Enhanced Management Area is located approximately 6 km northeast of the Town of Thessalon in Kirkwood Township. The Enhanced Management Area can be easily accessed from Tower Road, which runs west off of Hwy 129, and from the Kirkwood plantation roads, a number of which bisect the site. Kirkwood Delta Margin Enhanced Management Area encompasses a portion of an extensive deltaic sand plain, referred to as the Kirkwood Delta, which immediately underlies parts of Rose, Bridgland and Kirkwood Townships and the Town of Thessalon. This Enhanced Management Area protects part of the edge, also known as the margin, of the ancient Kirkwood Delta. The margin was formed by historic lake level changes that are now indicated by sand and gravel deposits. Some of the sands, exposed to the winds near the shore, have been blown into dunes that now support forests. Due to its large size, the entire Kirkwood Delta is considered to be provincially significant. This area of earth science significant is located in Ecodistrict 5E-1.

## Land use intent

The land use intent of this area is to protect the earth science features found in Kirkwood Delta Margin Enhanced Management Area, while allowing for a range of resource activities.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Ontario's Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999) and the Blind River District Land Use Guidelines (1983).

## Permitted uses — preface

Natural heritage enhanced management areas are intended to protect areas with significant natural values, while allowing for a range of resource activities. Those uses or management activities not listed in the following table are governed by the existing policy for natural heritage enhanced management areas. Any new uses or commercial activities associated with this enhanced management area will be considered on a case-by-case basis and may be subject to the Class Environmental Assessment for MNR Resource Stewardship and Facility Development Projects (2003).
