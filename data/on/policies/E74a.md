## Land area description

This enhanced management area was managed as a limited access area. East Mills encompasses parts of the Loring Deer Yard, some recreational trails, hunt camps and tourist operations along its periphery. It is an important area for commercial tourism and recreational use in a remote setting. Forest operations are important and help to enhance wildlife habitat. The entire area is unorganized.West boundary of enhance management area abuts up to Charter Lake, a cottaging lake. The area abuts Raganooter Lake Conservation Reserve.

## Land use intent

Continue to implement existing limited access area policies in MNR North Bay District Land Use Guidelines, while managing this multiple use land base for forest management, wildlife habitat, backcountry recreation and commercial tourism.The intent is to maintain the recreational experiences currently enjoyed at these locations. Existing traditional uses such as hiking, camping, canoeing, wildlife viewing, hunting, angling, trapping, etc., are encouraged. Resource production may occur provided current levels of accessibility within the areas area not enhanced. This may require the consideration of options such as winter only operations, winter roads, etc.Any proposed planning on activities adjacent to any conservation reserve will consider the management intent and direction of these protected areas. MNR is responsible for the management of Crown land and natural resources in unorganized townships.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Land Use Strategy (1999).

## Permitted uses — preface

Remote access enhanced management areas are intended to maintain the remote character of selected areas. Typically, these are relatively large areas which provide the public and tourism operators with high-quality remote recreational experiences including hunting, fishing, canoeing, and camping. Given the large size, remoteness, and relative absence of roads, these areas will play a significant role in protecting wilderness values outside the parks and protected areas system.
