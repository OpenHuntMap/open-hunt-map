## Land area description

This area is located within the Temagami River watershed. The values and uses of this area include the high quality remote fisheries with limited local and tourism use, the significant number of boat caches, the access via portages only, and the backcountry canoeing area with important linking routes to Lake Temagami, Cross Lake, and Jumping Caribou Lake. Of values are also the no development (e.g., cottages, LUPs, etc.) areas and the existing and heritage canoe routes.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Inland Ontario Lakes Designated for Lake Trout Management (2006) accessible in the Source of Direction portion of this policy report.NOTE: Part or all of this Management Area contains lands set aside pending resolution of the Temagami area aboriginal land claim. These lands will not be re-opened for staking and will not be included in the forestry land base.

## Land use intent

The land use intent is to maintain the management area as a significant recreational area not accessible by road, to provide opportunities for forestry and mining related activities, and to maintain backcountry recreation and remote tourism opportunities. Concerns include access control (maintain Wasaksina as a remote lake), boat cache problems (e.g., location at portage, numbers of boats), and the use of portages as ATV trails (conflicts with non-motorized recreation).The management area objectives are to ensure there is no public road access to lakes, to maintain the self-sustaining high quality lake trout and walleye fishery on Wasaksina Lake, to maintain the remote tourism values, and to maintain the existing canoe routes and the potential for new routes. Identified ATV use will continue, but will not expand or be upgraded to public truck access. Boat caches will be managed and undeveloped lakes (e.g., cottages, LUPs, etc.) will be maintained.The strategies include applying viewscape and Area of Concern planning for extractive activities, identifying appropriate boat cache management methods, and planning access and developing road use strategies to restrict public motorized access and minimize conflicts. A strategy was developed to prohibit further use of existing portage trails by ATVs within this management area as part of the Crown Land Recreation Plan Temagami Integrated Planning process.

Land use and resource management activities for this area are also governed by the policies of the Temagami Land Use Plan (TLUP) and the Crown Land Recreation Plan Temagami Integrated Planning. The policies from the TLUP are integrated into this area report. Refer to the TLUP for background information and additional details.
