## Land area description

Clear Creek Forest is a 393 hectare property, located in the Municipality of Chatham-Kent (former geographic township of Orford), straddling the Talbot Trail (Chatham-Kent Road 3, formerly Talbot Road) at Clearville, approximately four kilometres west of the Elgin County municipal boundary. As part of the Ontario Parks Legacy 2000 partnership, Ontario Parks and the Nature Conservancy of Canada (NCC) worked together to secure this significant natural area to be regulated as a nature reserve class provincial park. The NCC began land securement for Clear Creek Forest in 2000. To date, three additional, adjacent parcels have also been acquired. The NCC currently retains title to three of the parcels while title to the fourth has been registered to MNR. The possibility exists that Clear Creek Forest may be expanded through additional land securement.Clear Creek Forest lies within Ecodistricts 7E-1 and 7E-2. The site is also situated within the Carolinian Life Zone, a zone representing the extreme southwest region of Ontario where the eastern deciduous forest of North America has its most northern limits. It is within this zone that the highest frequencies of rare and endangered floral and faunal species in Canada are found. Clear Creek Forest is comprised of primary hardwood forest communities, secondary upland forests, cultural meadows, cultural thicket and cropped fields. The site is the largest woodland component of the provincially significant Kent-Elgin Shoreline Life Science Area of Natural and Scientific Interest (ANSI).

## Land use intent

As a condition of a 99-year lease agreement endorsed by Ontario Parks and NCC in 2001, Ontario Parks is committed to regulating these lands under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act as a nature reserve class provincial park. Approved park planning policy and the lease agreement between the NCC and Ontario Parks, also requires that a park management plan be developed for Clear Creek Forest to determine policy for the protection and management of the site.

## Permitted uses — preface

Management of Clear Creek Forest will be in accordance with the Clear Creek Forest Park Management Plan (2012). Additional management direction is provided in Ontario Provincial Parks: Planning and Management Policies (1992).The permitted uses associated with provincial parks are found in approved management direction, which are available on the Ontario Parks website.
