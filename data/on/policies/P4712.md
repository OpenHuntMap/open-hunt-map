## Land area description

Cedar Creek is a 70 ha nature reserve class provincial park, located in the Town of Essex, County of Essex west of Arner Townline Road (County Road 23) at Arner, approximately seven km east of Harrow and west of Kingsville. As part of the Ontario Parks Legacy partnership, Ontario Parks and the Nature Conservancy of Canada (NCC) worked together to secure thissignificant natural area. Cedar Creek Provincial Park was regulated in 2014.Cedar Creek lies within Ecodistrict 7E-1 and the Carolinian Life Zone, which represents the extreme southwest region of Ontario where the eastern deciduous forest of North America has its most northern limits. It is within this zone that the highest frequencies of rare and endangered floral and faunal species in Canada are found. Cedar Creek protects almost 10% of the provincially significant Cedar Creek Life Science Area of Natural and Scientific Interest (ANSI).

## Land use intent

As a condition of a 99-year landholding agreement endorsed by both parties in 2006, Ontario Parks is committed to managing Cedar Creek as a nature reserve class provincial park.

## Permitted uses — preface

Approved park planning policy and the lease agreement between the NCC and Ontario Parks requires that park management direction be developed for Cedar Creek to determine policy for the protection and management of the site. The park management planning process was initiated in 2006, and is ongoing. The park will also be managed according to provincial park policy and the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act, 2006.The permitted uses associated with provincial parks are found in approved management direction, which are available on the Ontario Parks website.
