## Land area description

Located about 140 kilometres east of Marten River, this area of earth science significance in site district 5E-6 contains an abandoned delta fed by an esker complex. The delta formed in an ancient glacial lake and is now perched high in the hills. Kettles, large depressions in the gravelly deposits that once held massive blocks of ice that now hold boggy wetlands, are also found within this area. The entire area is in unorganized.

## Land use intent

Forest management is permitted, provided that it is consistent with the protection of earth science natural heritage values, particularly the sensitive areas of the delta. Aggregate extraction will be controlled, so as to not significantly affect earth science natural heritage values.Prescriptions to direct resource management will be developed as part of ongoing planning. The intent is also to maintain landform integrity. Any proposed planning on activities adjacent to any provincial park or conservation reserve will consider the management intent and direction of these protected areas. MNR is responsible for the management of Crown land and natural resources in organized townships.

## Permitted uses — preface

Remote access enhanced management areas are intended to maintain the remote character of selected areas. Typically, these are relatively large areas which provide the public and tourism operators with high-quality remote recreational experiences including hunting, fishing, canoeing, and camping. Given the large size, remoteness, and relative absence of roads, these areas will play a significant role in protecting wilderness values outside the parks and protected areas system.
