## Land area description

This area is located within the Temagami River watershed. The values and uses in the area include high value tourism and recreation Cross Lake, the high quality natural lake trout/walleye lake, the hiking trail potential between Cross Lake and Lake Temagami, and the existing and heritage portages.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Inland Ontario Lakes Designated for Lake Trout Management (2006) accessible in the Source of Direction portion of this policy report.NOTE: Part or all of this Management Area contains lands set aside pending resolution of the Temagami area aboriginal land claim. These lands will not be re-opened for staking and will not be included in the forestry land base.

## Land use intent

The land use intent is to maintain Cross Lake as a significant recreation and tourism lake by providing a variety of high quality boating, canoeing, and angling activities. The intent is also to provide opportunities for forestry and mining related activities and to maintain the backcountry recreation and remote tourism values in the area.The concerns include the competition for campsites (e.g., houseboats, boats, and canoes), the development on Cross Lake which detracts from the remote recreation experience, the use of portages as ATV trails, and the increased angling pressureThe management area objectives are to provide access to Cross Lake, to retain the remote recreation experience in the management area outside of Cross Lake, and to maintain the existing canoe routes and the potential for new routes.The strategies include monitoring Cross Lake fisheries and developing management strategies where appropriate. Strategies also include applying planned access and road use strategies to restrict public motorized access and applying viewscape and Area of Concern planning to extractive activities. An appropriate motorized access point to Cross Lake has been identified on the western shore through a Class Environmental Assessment process. A strategy was also developed to prohibit further use of existing portage trails by ATVs within this management area as part of the Crown Land Recreation Plan Temagami Integrated Planning process.
