## Land area description

This unorganized community is located southeast of Highway 144 and contains a mixture of residential use (approximately 681 residents), cottaging, related service industries, 2 waste disposal sites, 2 access points, 2 resorts, 3 hotels/motels and 1 tent and trailer park. The Gogama area ground water is contaminated with nitrates and is a major concern.

## Land use intent

Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Gogama District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
