## Land area description

This area consists of a water network which offers large sand beaches, several excellent canoe routes, remote lakes, and good fishing and hunting to recreationalists. These uses have been the primary focus of this area in the past, and its development has been limited to a few outpost camps scattered throughout the area.The following are designated as Tourism Lakes: Rude, Mountairy, Hilltop, Grid Iron, Sassafras, Gosling.This area is subject to the Range Management Policy in Support of Woodland Caribou Conservation and Recovery (2014). The Range Management Policy is accessible via the “Source of Direction.”

## Land use intent

This area will serve both a recreation and resource production role. This area will contribute to the recreational needs of the public and the tourism industry. The canoe routes and sand beaches will be managed for Crown land recreation.Resource extraction operations will be permitted but will recognize the recreational role of this area. An outline of the ongoing activities which are recommended for continuation is given in the Land Use Activity section. It should be realized that in some instances further refinement may be required.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Thunder Bay District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
