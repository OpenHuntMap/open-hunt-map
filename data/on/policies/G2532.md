## Land area description

This area is located on the southeast edge of the District. Timber harvesting, mineral exploration, commercial tourism, commercial fishing and low intensity public recreation are the main uses occurring in this area. The area is a part of the Wabigoon Forest, Crossroute Forest and the Dryden Forest. Access in the area is limited. The Manitou Highway (Highway 502) passes through the area.The following are designated as Tourism Lakes: Upper Manitou, Sasakwei, Boyer, Walmsley, Washeibemaga.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The major problem to be addressed in the future management of this area is increased road access to lake trout lakes.The primary purpose of this area will be to maintain the existing lake trout fishery in areas of timber harvesting and mineral exploration activity. The location and management of roads will be one of the main tools used to ensure that fishing pressure on lake trout lakes does not exceed capability. Highway commercial development will not be permitted within 1.6 kilometres of the Manitou Highway (Highway 502). All activities in this area will take into consideration the known sensitive areas, which include a cemetery, two eagle nests and a heron rookery.Management of these area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Dryden District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
