## Land area description

This area in site district 5E-5 has been identified to protect life science features. The site is dominated by a gently rolling bedrock landform forested by white birch, spruce, fir, red pine and black ash. Large black ash is an interesting feature, with slow growing small stands sometimes reaching over one hundred years of age. The entire Enhanced Management Area is in an organized township.

## Land use intent

Further resource management planning will provide for protection of life science representation values, while permitting management of red pine plantations and recognizing the existing silvicultural investment along roaded areas.MNR is responsible for the management of Crown land and natural resources in organized townships.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Land Use Strategy (1999) an the appropriate District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
