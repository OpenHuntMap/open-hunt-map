## Land area description

The Orono Forest Crown Lands consist of historic plantations, fields, and naturally forested valley lands situated within the Wilmot Creek watershed immediately west of the Village of Orono in the Municipality of Clarington.The Orono Forest Crown lands are part of the Orono Provincial Forest Station that operated from 1922 to 1996.The property has significant natural heritage resources, particularly relating to aquatic resources, forest resources and landscape connectivity. The area includes an extensive network of footpaths and trails. Educational groups use these lands for classroom-based activities, co-op student training, outdoor educational activities, interpretative programs and leisure-time skills. Management of the area is supported by the Orono Crown Land Trust.The Orono Crown Forest Lands are located within the A Place to Grow: Growth Plan for the Greater Golden Horseshoe and the Greenbelt Plan. These lands contribute to the Natural Heritage System identified under the Greenbelt Plan and A Place to Grow.

## Land use intent

The Orono Forest Crown Lands will be managed on a sustainable ecosystem basis to ensure long-term protection and enhancement of the area’s natural resource and cultural values, and to provide educational and recreation opportunities and resource products for the continuous social and economic benefits to the people of Ontario.Objectives for the Orono Forest Crown Lands include:· To sustain and enhance the Orono Forest Crown Lands under the principles of ecosystem management by protecting and conserving the valuable soil, forest, vegetation, wildlife, and aquatic resources as well as their biological foundations to ensure long-term benefits to the public, the environment, and the economy of Ontario.· To protect the natural heritage and biological features of provincial and local significance.· To provide for a wide range of outdoor recreational opportunities that will increase the awareness of the ecological relationships among vegetation, wildlife and human activity on the site.· To support and facilitate partnerships with citizens’ groups, municipalities, public agencies, non-government organizations and school boards to share in the management of these lands in an ecologically sustainable way.· To ensure that the Orono Forest Crown Lands continue to offer a safe environment for the people of Orono and the general public to enjoy recreational and educational pursuits.· To address property security and maintenance.

## Permitted uses — preface

MNRF will consider the Land Use Intent and Management Direction outlined in this policy report when reviewing applications for permitted activities that require licences, leases, permits, or other forms of approval. Commercial Activities and Land and Resource Management Activities listed as permitted may require authorization under the relevant legislation (e.g., Public Lands Act, Aggregate Resources Act, Fish and Wildlife Conservation Act, etc.). The review of individual applications involves the consideration of a variety of factors and requirements on an application and site-specific basis, in addition to land use policy.Individuals seeking to carry out any of the permitted activities listed below should contact the local MNRF district office to find out if an authorization is required.Crown lands are managed in a manner consistent with the Constitutional protection afforded to existing Aboriginal and treaty rights.
