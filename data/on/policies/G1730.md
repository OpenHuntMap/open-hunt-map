## Land area description

This area includes portions of the six townships along Highways 11 and 583 which comprise the traditional concentrations of farming. The area encompasses lands already committed to agriculture and previously cleared agricultural land (Canada Land Inventory soil classes 3 & 4). This area is part of the Hearst Municipal Planning Area with land uses governed by the Hearst Official Plan and a Minister's Zoning Order under the Planning Act. The corporation of the Townships of Eilber and Devitt also has a secondary plan and an approved zoning by-law controlling land use. There is a mix of land uses within this policy area. Uses range from residential development to resource extraction activities such as forestry and aggregate removal. Along with development near the Town of Hearst, such as Val Cote, Hallebourg, Jogues and Coppell form nodes of agricultural use.

## Land use intent

The primary use within this area will be agriculture. The intent in this area is to promote and encourage the agricultural use of better quality, i.e. cleared Class 3 and 4 (CLI) agricultural soils in accordance with the Food Land Criteria. MNR in co-operation with the Ministry of Agriculture and Food and local municipalities will encourage these lands to be used for agricultural production.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Hearst District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
