## Land area description

This area encompasses two parcels of Crown land situated in the Ramsey Lake drainage basin. The larger area is located west of the East Arm of Ramsey Lake and includes the west half of Joffre Township. The smaller area is located in the southeast corner of Kelso Township abutting Ramsey Lake. Ramsey Lake is located within Mississagi River Provincial Park and Addition.

## Land use intent

This mixed use Area will be managed as a semi-remote area. Resource management plans will contain guidelines to protect significant recreational and tourism values, heritage resources, fisheries values and wildlife habitat. This area will be managed in a manner complementary to the adjacent Mississagi River Provincial Park and Addition.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Chapleau District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
