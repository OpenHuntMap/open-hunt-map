## Land area description

Kap-Kig-Iwan Provincial Park encompasses 328 hectares and is a natural environment class park. The park is located about five kilometres west of Highway 11 near the Town of Englehart.The main feature of this park is the rushing Englehart River, with a waterfall, several series of whitewater rapids, ragged rock outcrops and deep ravines.The park sits on the northern edge of the Little Clay Belt, one of the richest farming areas in northern Ontario. Although the park is in the boreal forest, its aspen, birch and red and white pine grow alongside white elm and black ash, species of trees more usually found in the Great Lakes-St. Lawrence lowlands.Kap-Kig-Iwan Provincial Park was regulated as a provincial park in 1957.

## Land use intent

Kap-Kig-Iwan Provincial Park will be managed according to Provincial Park policy and Park Management Plan (1984). The park will protect significant natural and cultural values. As of 2002, the park will provide day use recreation opportunities only.
