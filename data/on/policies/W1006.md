## Land area description

This Wilderness Area comprises all of Lot20, ConcessionVII in Hilton Township, on St. Joseph Island. The lot is bounded on its north side by Highway 548; along its west, south and most of its east sides, it is bounded by private land. The area was harvested in 1971 in order to release quality hardwood stems; white pine and hemlock were retained to provide shelter and seed. The site is now dominated by a young mixed stand including hard maple and beech. There has been some additional planting of hemlock and white spruce.

## Land use intent

Wilderness areas are areas that are regulated under the Wilderness Areas Act. The Act states that these areas are set aside “…as a wilderness area for the preservation of the area as nearly as may be in its natural state in which research and educational activities may be carried on, for the protection of the flora and fauna, for the improvement of the area, having regard to its historical, aesthetic, scientific or recreational value…”.Most commercial activities are not permitted, and any land and resource management activity or recreation activity must take into consideration the intent of the Wilderness Areas ActConsistent with the Guide for Crown Land Use Planning, this Wilderness Area will be assessed in the future on whether there are natural or recreational values that warrant further protection (i.e. through the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act). The Hilton Township Wilderness Area was identified as a location in which to implement a number of forest management prescriptions designed to enhance wildlife habitat as well as overall forest diversity and sustainability.”
