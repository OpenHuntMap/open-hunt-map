## Land area description

The Burwash Lake area is known for its lake trout fishing and contains some of the most important lake trout lakes in the Area. This area is located south-east of Gogama and is accessible primarily by a gravel road known locally as the ''Welcome Lake Road''. Structural development includes cottages, hunt and fish camps, outpost camp and a transmission line. The area lies between two separate licenced Management Units. Crown land recreation use is moderate.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The maintenance of the existing lake trout fishery will be the fisheries management priority. Resource management plans will contain guidelines for the protection of specific recreational values and significant fish and wildlife habitat. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Land Use Strategy (1999)

## Permitted uses — preface

In addition to provincial and regional policies, the following local policies will apply to the management of Natural lake trout lakes:­ no additional access will be permitted­ 120 metre Area of Concern will be applied­ forest management activities may be permitted within the Area of Concern subject to concerns for erosion/sedimentation and the protection of lake trout habitat
