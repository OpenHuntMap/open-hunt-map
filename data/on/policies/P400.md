## Land area description

Bonnechere River Provincial Park runs from the southern boundary of Algonquin Provincial Park following the watercourse of the Bonnechere River through to Bonnechere Provincial Park on Round Lake. The park is situated in Site District 5E-10. The Park protects good representative examples of floodplain forests, including stands of silver maple, red maple and bur oak. The park is situated on extensive Outwash and Lacustrine landform.The park was regulated in 1989.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Bonnechere River Provincial Park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act. The boundary of the park is delineated by Ontario Regulation No. 256/89, Schedule 218.
