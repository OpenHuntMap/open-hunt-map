## Land area description

Crotch Lake is popular with campers, boaters, anglers and supports a number of tourism establishments. This scenic lake is surrounded by rugged terrain and boasts a number of significant wildlife species. There is seasonal public access on the south and north side of the lake, and private access on the northeast and south end of the lake. There is relatively little access in other areas. Crotch Lake has significant water level draw down in the fall and winter.

## Land use intent

Maintain the remote access characteristics of this area, while also managing for forest management, other resource uses and high quality backcountry recreation, including allowing for management of the campsites and portages along the Mazinaw - Mississippi canoe route. Remote access management of this Crown land block will not preclude existing/future private road or water access to landlocked patent land parcels.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Land Use Strategy (1999).

## Permitted uses — preface

Remote access enhanced management areas are intended to maintain the remote character of selected areas. Typically, these are relatively large areas which provide the public and tourism operators with high-quality remote recreational experiences including hinting, fishing, canoeing, and camping. Given the large size, remoteness, and relative absence of roads, these areas will play a significant role in protecting wilderness values outside the parks and protected areas system.
