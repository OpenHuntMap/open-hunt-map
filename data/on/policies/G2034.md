## Land area description

This area is located immediately north of the Highway No 17, Spanish River corridor (area 3). It is bordered on the north by the north sides of Lehman, Gerow, Boon, Gough and part of Shakespeare Townships. Forest management is a major existing use managed under a Sustainable Forest Licence (SFL). Highway No 553 (the Massey Tote) runs north-south through the area.There are two lake trout lakes in this area; Snapshot Lake and East Bull Lake. There are two tourism lakes; Pawson Lake and Complin Lake.Significant water quality problems exist in the Serpent River system. There is considerable public concern in the Town of Massey for the maintenance of high water quality in the Sables River, the town's source of drinking water.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Ministry programs in this area will be primarily aimed at supporting such commercial and industrial activities as tourism, mining and forestry. Lake trout lakes will be protected from development and use pressure. The remote quality of tourism lakes will be given consideration in Ministry programs. MNR will continue to provide site specific management practices to protect features such as great blue heron nesting areas and scenic attractions such as Cameron and Meareau Falls.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Espanola District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
