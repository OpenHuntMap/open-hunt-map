## Land area description

This recommended conservation reserve is located along the eastern boundary of the Swan Lake Conservation Reserve (C114). This recommended addition has similar characteristics to the Swan Lake Conservation Reserve. Adjacent features include: bedrock landforms supporting meadows, alder swales, treed wetlands and stands of hemlock, red maple and white cedar, among others.

## Land use intent

The Swan Lake Conservation Reserve protects stands of red maple and hemlock mixed with white cedar, poplar and white birch. The area encompasses part of the Portage Lake winter deeryard. Vegetation management (deer habitat) to be addressed in Statement of Conservation Interest. Values include life science, earth science, cultural and recreational/aesthetic with an emphasis on the life sciences. To protect these values and to be consistent with the provincial policies for the management of Conservation Reserves and the traditional management of Crown lands in this area there is limited access development permitted into or through this Conservation Reserve. Conservation reserves protect natural heritage values on public lands, while permitting compatible land use activities. Swan Lake Conservation Reserve is surrounded by two Enhanced Management Areas (EMAs). The EMA designation was established in order to provide more detailed land use direction in areas of special features or values. Preserving the area in its pristine state may also be beneficial as a benchmark in future studies. The intent is to regulate this addition as part of the Swan Lake Conservation Reserve.

## Permitted uses — preface

Management of this conservation reserve is carried out in accordance with the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act 2006, Conservation Reserve Policy, Ontario's Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999), and this amendment (2008). Future management direction (Statement of Conservation Interest) will elaborate on permitted uses. Any new uses, and commercial activities associated with conservation reserves, will be considered on a case by case basis, and must pass a test of compatibility to be acceptable. ## Addendum

Management of this conservation reserve is carried out within the context of Conservation Reserve policy as amended by the policies for new conservation reserves outlined in the Ontario's Living Legacy Land Use Strategy and the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act, 2006. Note: MNR will consider the Land Use Intent and Management Direction outlined in this policy report when reviewing applications for permitted activities that require licences, leases, permits, or other forms of approval. The review of individual applications involves the consideration of a variety of factors and requirements on a site-specific basis in addition to land use policy.
