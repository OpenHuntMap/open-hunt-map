## Land area description

This area is comprised of a portion of the upper Serpent River watershed which surrounds the built-up portion of the City of Elliot Lake, downstream to Esten Lake. This is a popular day-use area which is expected to receive additional use in the future.Apart from the City of Elliot Lake, the majority of this area is undeveloped Crown land.Included in the area are Elliot and Esten Lakes. The management area encompasses these lakes and backshore areas a distance of approximately 120 metres from the shoreline.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The intent is to provide passive (e.g. non-structural) recreational uses for local residents and tourists. Day-use, angling, boating and camping will be emphasized.Shoreline development will be regulated to permit a continuation and expansion of these uses.Crown land will be disposed of only for those uses which are complementary to the proposed recreational intent for the area, and which are permitted under the official plan and zoning by-law.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Blind River District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
