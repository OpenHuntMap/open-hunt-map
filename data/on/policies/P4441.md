## Land area description

Emily Provincial Park was established in 1957. Situated along the east shore of the Pigeon River near the city of Peterborough, this park protects natural river shoreline and wetland areas. It attracts recreational users for camping, picnicking, swimming, fishing and boating along the Trent Severn Waterway.

## Land use intent

This recreational class park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act.
