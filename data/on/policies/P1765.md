## Land area description

Obatanga Provincial Park is located on Hwy. 17, 56 kilometres west of Wawa and 37 kilometres east of White River. This natural environment class park encompasses 9,409 hectares. The park lakes which provide canoeing and wildlife viewing opportunities are on the headwaters of the Dog River which flows into Lake Superior. The park is used as a staging area for canoe trips down the Dog (University) River. The park landscape varies from flat sand plains to rugged bedrock hills.Obatanga Provincial Park was regulated as a provincial park in 1967.

## Land use intent

Obatanga Provincial Park will be managed according to Provincial Park policy and the Park Management Plan (1988). The park will provide recreation opportunities while protecting the parks natural values.
