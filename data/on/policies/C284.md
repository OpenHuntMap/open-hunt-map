## Land area description

Wabos North Conservation Reserve, found within Ecodistrict 5E-13 (formerly classified as Site District 4E-2), captures spectacular, diverse topography featuring rock faces, cliffs, and rugged landscape, with steep-sloped morainal deposits, and lacustrine deposits where the topography is level. Dense stands of mature sugar maple and yellow birch mixedwoods dominate the site, and several of these communities have been classified as old-growth. Stands of white and black spruce, balsam fir, poplar and eastern white cedar occur throughout the conservation reserve in various combinations, usually in ravines, and alongshorelines of small lakes and streams (including portions of Wabos and Achigan creeks) within the conservation reserve. A unique poplar dominated ridge in the west, and a moose aquatic feeding area, associated with the south shore of an unnamed lake in the north, add to this site’s diversity.The history of lands within and surrounding this conservation reserve is linked strongly to the Algoma Central Railway, which forms most of the site’s western boundary.Wabos North Conservation Reserve was regulated on December 21, 2000. It is regulated under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act.LOCATION:This protected area is situated approximately 40 kilometres (km) northeast of the City of Sault Ste. Marie, and 7 km northwest of the community of Searchmont, in Shields Township. The site’s boundaries are defined, in part, by the Algoma Central Railway, small creeks (including a tributary of Wabos Creek in the north), and lot lines of adjacent patent properties. Wabos South Conservation Reserve (C286) is less than 1 km south, and the Achigan Lake Area Enhanced Management Area (E283n) is less than 5 km northwest of this site. Additionally, this conservation reserve is surrounded by the Agriculture (G1905), and Batchawana (G1908) General Use areas.

## Land use intent

To protect the natural heritage values on these public lands while permitting compatible land use activities.

## Permitted uses — preface

The Wabos North Conservation Reserve Statement of Conservation Interest (SCI) was approved on May 30, 2003 and should beconsulted for detailed information on the site's management. Those uses and management activities not listed in the followingtable are governed by existing Conservation Reserves Policy and the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act, and by the general policiescontained in the Ontario's Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999). Any new uses and commercial activities will be consideredon a case by case basis and must pass a Test of Compatibility, and comply with requirements under the Class EnvironmentalAssessment for Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves (2004), as well as other applicable Ministry of Natural Resources'(MNR) policies and procedures, to be acceptable. Compatibility is normally determined through a planning process.The following tables aim to present current information. However, with the passage of new legislation, updates to provincial policy and the development of protected areas management direction documents, certain content elements may be out of date.
