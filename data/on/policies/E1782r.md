## Land area description

This area is located within the Makobe River watershed. The values and uses of this area include the adjacent Makobe River, the quality backcountry canoe route with good seasonal white-water in a remote setting, the headwaters area for Makobe-Grays Provincial Park, and two adjacent parks with no current road access.

## Land use intent

The land use intent is to provide opportunities for forestry and mining-related activities with an emphasis on maintaining viewscapes and aquatic ecosystem integrity. An additional intent is to manage for park-related values in both Lady Evelyn-Smoothwater Wilderness Park and Makobe-Grays Waterway Park.Concerns include the maintenance of water quality and quantity standards during resource extractive activities and the maintenance of park-related viewscapes.The management area objectives are to allow resource extractive activities to occur with special Area of Concern conditions, to ensure headwater conservation and viewscape management, to restrict public motorized access, and to prohibit roads within 350-meters of park boundaries.The strategies include planning access and developing road use strategies which restrict public motorized access and minimize conflicts, applying viewscape management, and developing Area of Concern guidelines to address water quality and quantity concerns.

## Permitted uses — preface

Land use and resource management activities for this area are also governed by the policies of the Temagami Land Use Plan (TLUP) (1997). The policies from the TLUP are integrated into this area report. Refer to the TLUP for background information and additional details.
