## Land area description

Old growth forests are rare natural features in the Ottawa Valley. The Boom Creek site is a significant natural old growth stand of red and white pine in site district 5E-10. More than half of the site is made up of pine stands in the 150 to 160 year old range, while the remainder is 120 to 130 years of age. This old pine grows over areas of rock outcrop, a situation that sets these old growth red and white pine stands apart from other similar sites in the area.Boom Creek Old Growth Forest was regulated as a conservation reserve on May 21, 2003.Site is bordered on the east by Rankin Creek and on the west by Boom Creek both of which are brook trout streams of local significance.

## Land use intent

Conservation reserve management to recognize importance of the old growth ecosystem and brook trout habitat. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Land Use Strategy (1999).
