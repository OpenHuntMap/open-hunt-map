## Land area description

Lake Nipissing and its bays, inlets and islands together with a strip of land 200 metres in-land from the normal high water mark form an extensive water based recreational and resource production area. Commercial fishing for sturgeon and whitefish by commercial fishermen at Sturgeon Falls is a well established use on Lake Nipissing. There are intensive cottage developments from North Bay to the South Bay and along several large bays in the western arm of the lake. Municipal planning controls exist. The area contains a mixture of organized and unorganized areas. Much of the shoreline is in private ownership.The area abuts several protected areas including: French River Park Addition, Mashkinonje Park Addition, South Bay, Manitou Islands, and West Sandy Island provincial parks; and Callander Bay Wetland, Cache Bay Wetland and Fish Bay Conservation Reserves.

## Land use intent

Existing acceptable uses are management for fish, game, waterfowl and furbearers, resource production including commercial fishing, and extensive and intensive recreation. These will be encouraged to continue. Other private land uses such as urban, industrial, commercial, cottaging, and rural residential, will not generally be encouraged to expand onto Crown lands. Wetland habitats are extremely important and these will be inventoried, classified and given appropriate status and consideration. Several sites, where protection is the main theme of management, (e.g. heronries, archaeological sites, pickerel spawning beds, etc.) are found in this area. These and other such sites, which may be found in the future, will be protected during resource management operations and will be identified as values requiring protection in municipal planning. Any proposed planning on activities adjacent to any provincial park or conservation reserve will consider the management intent an direction of these protected areas. MNR is responsible for the management of Crown land and natural resources in organized and unorganized townships.
