## Land area description

Located in the Great Lakes-St Lawrence Forest Site Region between Algonquin Park and Georgian Bay, the conservation reserve is large, relatively undisturbed series of bedrock barrens interspersed with wetlands and treed uplands. The site is approximately 1,906 hectares in size, and is situated in the District Municipality of Muskoka, in Township of Muskoka Lakes and the Town of Gravenhurst, and is comprised of parts of the geographic townships of Muskoka Lakes and the Town of Gravenhurst, and is comprised of parts of the geographic townships of Muskoka and Wood. The reserve is south of Lakes Muskoka, Rosseau and Joseph.Torrance Barrens was regulated as a conservation reserve on July 4, 1997.

## Land use intent

To manage this area as a Conservation Reserve under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act, to ensure that identified natural heritage values are protected.Management of this area is also governed by the policies contained in the Torrance Barrens Conservation Reserve (Dark Sky Reserve) Statement of Conservation Interest (August 1999).

Those uses and management activities not listed in the following table are governed by existing conservation reserve policy. Any new uses, and commercial activities associated with conservation reserves, will be considered on a case by case basis, and they must pass a test of compatibility to be acceptable. Compatibility is normally determined through a planning process.The following tables aim to present current information.
