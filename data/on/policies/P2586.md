## Land area description

This area, regulated as a recreation class provincial park in 1960, occupies 147 hectares on the northeast shore of Caliper Lake. Dominated by clayey sand tills, the park contains representative mature red and white pine.Caliper Lake Park provides day use and car camping opportunities for residents of Ontario and tourists.

## Land use intent

Caliper Lake Park will continue to serve its present role over the planning period. Facilities will be expanded within the park as demands dictate and funding permits.The intent is to manage this park as a recreation class provincial park, in accordance with MNR and Ontario Parks Policy.
