## Land area description

White Lake Peatlands Nature Reserve is located at the north end of White Lake in Atikameg Township on the west side of the Shabotik River.This park consists of wet organic terrain developed on glacial fluvial outwash sand deposits that extend to the northeast along the Shabotik River valley. This peatland is bounded by rock knob uplands to the north and surrounded by old cut-over on the mineral soiled sites at its periphery.There are no park facilities.White Lake Peatlands Provincial Nature Reserve was regulated as a provincial park in 1997.

## Land use intent

White Lake Peatlands Provincial Nature Reserve will be managed according to Provincial Park policy.Management of the park will be to protect its significant features.
