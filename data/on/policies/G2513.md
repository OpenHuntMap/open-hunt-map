## Land area description

This area is located east of the South Pakwash Road. It includes that part of the English River system east of Separation Lake, including Lount, Indian and Ball Lakes. The area forms part of the Whiskey Jack Forest. Grassy Narrows Indian Reserve # 21 is located within the area.The following are designated as tourism lakes: Ball, Big Fox, Brown Bear, Campfire, Conifer, Grassy Narrows, Indian, Lount, Separation, Solitary, Sumach and Unnamed (2).This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.Part of this area is subject to the Range Management Policy in Support of Woodland Caribou Conservation and Recovery (2014). The Range Management Policy is accessible via the “Source of Direction.””

## Land use intent

The primary resource use in this area will be timber harvesting, but tourism and recreation will also be important activities. The group of lake trout lakes in the vicinity of Lennan Lake will receive specific consideration and protection. Some potential for expansion of sport fishing may be available in this area.
