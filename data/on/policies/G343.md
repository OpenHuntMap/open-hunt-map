## Land area description

This is an area of general resource use located in the Minden Area. It has a relatively low capability for timber production.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

To provide and encourage provision of multiple management of resources. Management of the timber resource will not be an important component of this area. Standard management practices, combined with the concept of sequential use, will enable MNR to take full advantage of these natural resources.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Minden District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
