## Land area description

Extensive recreational experiences that are enjoyed in a limited access setting, currently being managed as a remote access area. This area also encompasses stocked and native brook trout streams and lakes, hunt camps and several recreational trails. The area provides opportunities for remote hunting and angling. Mining and forest resources are also important values.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Manage this important recreational and resource sector (forestry, mining) area consistent with the enhanced management area (remote access) policies and the limited access area policies of the MNR Espanola District Land Use Guidelines and local resource management plans. Traditional uses such as hiking, camping, hunting, angling, wildlife viewing etc. will be encouraged. Timber extraction, mineral exploration and aggregate extraction will be permitted provided that long term, all season or year round public access is not impaired. Lake trout lakes will be managed to protect lake trout populations and habitat. Minor boundary adjustments may be made to this area through further planning exercises.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Land Use Strategy (1999) and the Espanola District Land Use Guidelines (1983).

## Permitted uses — preface

Remote access enhanced management area are intended to maintain the remote character of selected areas. Typically, these are relatively large areas which provide the public and tourism operators with high-quality remote recreational experiences including hunting, fishing, canoeing, and camping. Given the large size, remoteness, and relative absence of roads, these areas will play a significant role in protecting wilderness values outside the parks and protected areas system.In addition to provincial and regional policies, the following local policies will apply to the management of Natural lake trout lakes:­ no additional access will be permitted­ 120 metre Area of Concern will be applied­ forest management activities may be permitted within the Area of Concern subject to concerns for erosion/sedimentation and the protection of lake trout habitat.
