## Land area description

This area consists of Sand Lake, Gun Lake and the north end of the Winnipeg River. The area forms part of the Kenora Forest. Access is generally well developed by Highway 596, the English River road, and several secondary resource access and cottaging roads. There are 12 main base lodges located in this area. The Islington Band Indian Reserve # 29 is located at the confluence of the Winnipeg and English Rivers, and a portion of the Band's Traditional Land Use Area is located within this area.A portion of the area includes the Wabaseemoong Traditional Land Use Area. Consultation will be undertaken with the First Nation Respecting Crown Land Disposition and other resource proposals, consistent with the 1983 Ontario Islington Agreement.The following are designated as tourism lakes: Alexander, Gun, Roughrock, Tetu, the Winnipeg River and Unnamed (3).This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.Water Management Areas have been removed from this Policy Report area as revisions, to more accurately reflect First Nation reserve lands affected by flooding.

## Land use intent

The priority use in this area will be outdoor recreation in recognition of the importance of tourism to Minaki and the local economy. Roads and timber harvesting will recognize the potential conflicts with existing tourist operations. There is little or no potential remaining for expansion of recreational development based on sport fishing. Prior to any development proposed within two km. of the Manitoba border being approved, discussions will be held with the Manitoba Government to avoid potential conflicts and minimize any negative impacts. In addition, no management action will be taken on boundary waters without advisement and discussion with the Manitoba Government.Management of the area is also governed by the Kenora Forest Management Plan and the Ontario Islington Band Agreement (1983).
