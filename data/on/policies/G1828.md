## Land area description

This area encompasses five of the highest quality and road accessible aggregate areas in the Timmins District. Highway 101,144 and 655 access the areas. Four of the five areas lie within the municipal jurisdiction of the City of Timmins. Only the McKeown Township complex does not come under municipal control. This area lies within two separate licensed forest Management Units. Frederick house Lake is the only identified tourism lake in this area.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.Part of this area is subject to the Range Management Policy in Support of Woodland Caribou Conservation and Recovery (2014). The Range Management Policy is accessible via the “Source of Direction.””

## Land use intent

The primary use in this area is to ensure an adequate supply of aggregate is available in the Timmins District within economical hauling distances of areas of consumption. Proven critical aggregate deposits will be protected by discouraging non-compatible uses in these areas, specifically structural development. The impacts of extraction in shoreline areas will be minimized. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Timmins District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
