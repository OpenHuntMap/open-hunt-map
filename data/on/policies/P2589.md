## Land area description

This area, regulated as a natural environment class Provincial Park in 1985, is comprised of Sandpoint Island in Rainy Lake. There are two patented mining claims on the island, which is withdrawn from staking. The area experiences moderate hunting pressure for deer and there is one registered trapping license.The park provides representation of small scale and special earth and life science features such as cedar swamps, tombola beaches and bedrock features. It also has the potential to provide day use and tent camping opportunities on a water accessible basis. Owners of private inholdings will be encouraged to manage their properties in a manner which complements the surrounding park and its objectives and policies.

## Land use intent

The intent is to manage this park as a natural environment class provincial park, in accordance with MNR and Ontario Parks Policy.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Fort Frances District Land Use Guidelines (1983)
