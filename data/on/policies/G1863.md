## Land area description

This area is located within the Makobe River watershed. This area is presently characterized by few lakes with limited access and is within the Kirkland Lake District.

## Land use intent

The intent is to provide opportunities for forestry and mining related activities. There are no known concerns within this area. The management area objectives include allowing resource extraction and providing new accessible recreation opportunities (e.g., hunting).The strategies of this area include planning road locations through the Timiskaming forest management planning process and applying viewscape and area of concern planning to extractive activities.

## Permitted uses — preface

Land use and resource management activities for this area are also governed by the policies of the Temagami Land Use Plan (TLUP) (1997). The policies from the TLUP are integrated into this area report. Refer to the TLUP for background information and additional details.
