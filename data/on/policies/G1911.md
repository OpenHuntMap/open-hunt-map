## Land area description

This area includes the St. Marys River from Pointe Louise through the St. Marys Rapids, down to Lake George and St. Joseph Channel to the boundary of Johnson and Plummer Additional Townships then easterly along this boundary line to the intersection with a north-south line immediately east of Milford Island then southerly along this line to the International Boundary. The water quality of this area varies from Lake Superior down through the City of Sault Ste. Marie to Lake Huron. The existence of local industry on the river and the municipal sewage treatment plant contribute to the varying quality of water. The land uses presently occurring in the area include sport fishing, boating, commercial navigation, cottaging, power development, commercial fishing, municipal and industrial water intake, and sewage disposal facilities.Coastal portions of this area are located within the Great Lakes Heritage Coast Signature Site, one of 9 such areas featured in the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999). Signature Sites are identified for their range of natural and recreational values and their potential to contribute to future recreation and tourism.

## Land use intent

The general land use intent for this area will be public recreation. Commercial fishing will be permitted within areas east of St. Joseph Island as defined by Ontario Commercial Fishing Licences. All other land use activities will be permitted providing they do not seriously conflict with Ministry programs and policies. Two hydro-electric power facilities exist on the St. Marys River. Provision for their continued use will be made. All activities in the area will take into consideration known spawning areas, nesting sites and historical sites. The specific location for each can be found in a compendium at the District office. MNR will protect and/or encourage protection of the critical fisheries habitat identified when considering any development proposals in this area. The St. Marys Rapids have been identified as a significant fisheries value. The District and the Upper Great Lakes Management Unit will object and/or recommend mitigating measures to development that will have a negative impact on the rapids area. MNR will protect and encourage the protection of important waterfowl habit identified in the area. The District will dispose of Crown water lots for public or private use subject to municipal official plans and associated zoning by-laws. Major disposition is also subject to the Ministry of the Environment's review, which may result in the requirement of an environmental assessment. Disposition which would result in direct or indirect negative impacts on any Ministry program will be discouraged. The District will retain for public use, at least the present area, on Crown islands and Crown shorelines of the Great Lakes. Islands and lands under the water of this area have been withdrawn from staking. Development proposals and dispositions, including water lots, on the Great Lakes shoreline will be reviewed for conformity with the District’s Shoreline Management Plan.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Sault Ste. Marie District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
