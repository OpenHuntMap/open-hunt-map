## Land area description

The Grew River and associated chain of lakes between Pakashkan and Stonehouse Lakes is one of the few remaining areas in the area with limited access and good tourism potential. Present uses include tourism outpost camps, canoeing, Crown land camping, trapping and forest harvesting. The prime issue for this area will be the maintenance of the tourism potential.The following are designated as Tourism Lakes: Dora, Loganberry, Kearns and Stonehouse.Part of this area is subject to the Range Management Policy in Support of Woodland Caribou Conservation and Recovery (2014). The Range Management Policy is accessible via the “Source of Direction.””

## Land use intent

Primary recreational activities will consist of cottaging on Pakashkan Lake, Crown land recreation such as fishing and canoeing on the Grew River and tourism on the chain of lakes in the area. Cottaging may be provided in the future if demand warrants, on Pakashkan Lake. Resource extraction will continue in the area, recognizing the tourism and recreational values in the area. An outline of the ongoing activities which are recommended for continuation is given in the Land Use Activity section. It should be realized that in some instances further refinement may be required. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Thunder Bay District Land Use Guidelines (1983)
