## Land area description

This area is primarily used for a variety of recreational activities; boating, fishing, camping, cottaging and canoeing. The adjoining Lady Evelyn Lake is one of the most productive pickerel fisheries in the area. There are several commercial lodges dependent on the lake and surrounding area. High concentrations of moose and winter feeding areas are found within the area. Moderate to high mineral potential is characteristic of the area. The lake is a dam controlled hydro storage reservoir with a 5 metre winter draw down.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The primary activity in this area will be the provision of recreational opportunities. Discreet resource production, fish and wildlife management, commercial tourism, recreation and cottaging will be continuing uses. Ministry programs will encourage the continuation of a variety of recreational uses. The production of resources will be promoted. Ontario Hydro's control of water levels for power production will continue. All activities will take into consideration the presence of wildlife, geological and cultural values. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Temagami District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
