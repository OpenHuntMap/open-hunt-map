## Land area description

Craigleith Provincial Park is small operating park located at the base of Collingwood's Blue Mountains on the Georgian Bay shoreline. The park is approximately 30 km west of Wasaga Beach tourist area and 160 km north of Toronto. The park sits on fractured flat plates of shale containing invertebrate fossils 455 million years old. Craigleith Provincial Park was regulated in 1967.

## Land use intent

This recreational class park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act.
