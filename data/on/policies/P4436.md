## Land area description

Darlington Provincial Park was established in 1959. Located along the north shore of Lake Ontario near Oshawa, this park protects provincially significant wetland habitat in McLaughlin Bay, lake shoreline, the outlet of Robinson Creek and cultural heritage features. The park’s recreational values include a sand beach and hiking trails. It is popular for camping and day use, including picnicking, walking, boating and beach activities.

## Land use intent

This recreational class park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act.
