## Land area description

This area is located east of Highway 144 and is accessed primarily by the ''Grassy Road''. Structural development includes a waste disposal site, access points and a transmission line. This area also contains part of the Grassy River canoe route. The area lies within two separate licenced Management Units. Most of the area contains high mineral potential. Crown land recreation potential and use is also high. Blair Lake, Mond Lake, and Nursery Lake are the identified tourism lakes in this area.

## Land use intent

Crown land recreation will be a priority use in this area. In addition, resource management plans will contain guidelines to protection recreational values, historical and archaeological sites and significant fish and wildlife habitat. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Gogama District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
