## Land area description

This area is located within the Makobe River watershed. The values and uses of this area include portions of the headwaters of a wilderness park and its aquatic ecosystems that are representative of the ecological Site Region along with the high mineral potential for low grade copper, cobalt and silver. The area forms part of the land base for forest management in the Kirkland Lake District.

## Land use intent

The land use intent is to protect park-related values of the Makobe River Headwaters that flow into Lady Evelyn-Smoothwater Provincial Park while allowing for forest and mining-related activities.Concerns include public motorized access from this management area into the adjacent wilderness park, water quality of the headwaters flowing into the wilderness park, and the impacts of resource extraction on park-related values.The management area objectives are to ensure that forest management and mining related activities follow prescriptions which protect water quality and park-related values, to restrict motorized public access to the wilderness park, and to prohibit roads within 350-metres of park boundaries.The strategies include applying special prescriptions for forest management and mining to protect park-related values, and planning access and developing road use strategies which restrict public motorized access to the wilderness park.
