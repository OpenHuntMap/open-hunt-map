## Land area description

This large urban oriented lake offers good recreational opportunities for the people of Thunder Bay.Present uses include cottaging, Crown land camping, fishing, hunting, forestry, aggregate production and mineral exploration. The levels of development are moderate on the south and southeast shoreline and sparse on the rest of the lake.Dog Lake is managed as a reservoir for hydroelectric facilities at Silver Falls and Kakabeka Falls. There are numerous historic and archaeological sites along the shore of the lake and several sensitive wetlands and nest sites identified in the northern portion of Block Creek and Creighton Bay. A wetland area surrounding Block Creek is separated from the main body of the land use area by the Dog River Conservation Reserve.

## Land use intent

The primary role of this area is to serve the near urban recreational needs of the District. An effort will be made to provide additional cottaging through a lake development plan. Private lands will also provide additional recreation development; cottages or lodges. The known historic and archaeological sites and the sensitive fish and wildlife resources will be protected within the operating guidelines and development proposals of all land uses.Resource extraction activities will continue recognizing the historic, fish and wildlife and recreational values of Dog Lake. A lake management plan will be written to coordinate the private recreation, tourism, Crown land recreation and hydro uses of Dog Lake.This area has identified one (1) candidate park recommended for park status. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Thunder Bay District Land Use Guideline (1983)
