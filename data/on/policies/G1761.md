## Land area description

This area is characterized by rolling topography with north to south trending eskers. Year round access is primarily limited to air transportation. Present development includes commercial outpost camps, part of a private power line and a few remote cottage sites. Forest management occurs under a sustainable forest licence.This area is subject to the Range Management Policy in Support of Woodland Caribou Conservation and Recovery (2014). The Range Management Policy is accessible via the “Source of Direction.”

## Land use intent

The land use priority in this area is directed toward the protection and limited expansion of the commercial tourist industry (i.e. outpost camps). Management of the area will be compatible with the adjacent North of the French River Conservation Reserve.This area overlaps with the Straggler Lakes Area of the approved Cochrane District Remote (Wilderness) Tourism Strategy. Management of the overlapped area will be subject to the approved Cochrane District Remote (Wilderness) Tourism Strategy (1997). Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Cochrane District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
