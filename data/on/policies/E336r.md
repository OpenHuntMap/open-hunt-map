## Land area description

This area is located within the Lake Timiskaming/Montreal River watershed. The values and uses of this area include the unorganized trail system and hunting with limited access.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Inland Ontario Lakes Designated for Lake Trout Management (2006) accessible in the Source of Direction portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The land use intent is to permit forestry and mining related activities, maintain hardwood forest habitat for a variety of wildlife species, and to re-establish pine where appropriate. The intent is also to retain the areas trail-based backcountry recreation. A concern is retaining the backcountry setting.The objectives of this management area include maintaining non-motorized recreational values, allowing for continued use of ATV trails where identified, and promoting pine regeneration on appropriate sites.The strategies include developing road use strategies to restrict public motorized access and minimize conflicts, managing for pine to be identified in forest management planning, and applying viewscape management and Area of Concern planning for extractive activities.

## Permitted uses — preface

Land use and resource management activities for this area are also governed by the policies of the Temagami Land Use Plan (TLUP) (1997). The policies from the TLUP are integrated into this area report. Refer to the TLUP for background information and additional details.
