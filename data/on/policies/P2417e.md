## Land area description

This area, regulated as a nature reserve class Provincial Park in 1985, is comprises of the Sable Islands in Lake of the Woods and a portion of the adjacent mainland. The islands are presently used as a day-use recreational area by anglers who fish the adjacent waters. Bait fishing is conducted in the shallow waters adjacent to the island. The park serves as a highly productive staging and breeding area for waterfowl. The area is presently inaccessible by road. Two private land inholdings are located within the area. Other features include beach dunes, wetlands and rare plants. The park provides representation of a provincially significant barrier bar and sand dune complex, as well as a representative marsh area in Landscape Unit 31 (Rainy River Clay Plain). Owners of private inholdings will be encouraged to manage their properties in a manner which complements the surrounding park and its objectives.The Sable Island Waters Conservation Reserve abuts this park, on the bed of Lake of the Woods.

## Land use intent

The intent is to managed this park as a nature reserve class provincial park, in accordance with MNR and Ontario Parks Policy.
