## Land area description

This is a major commercial tourism and recreational area. There are numerous commercial lodges, with tourist use occurring on Wakomata Lake, Chub Lake, Jobammageeshig Lake, Little Pickerel Lake, Cumming Lake and Tunnel Lake. More than 100 cottages are situated on the above lakes and Axe Lake. One commercial outpost camp is located on Damn Lake.Recreational use is facilitated by the operation of public access points.Unless otherwise identified, the management area includes the above noted lakes and water-ways and their backshore areas a distance of approximately 120 metres from the shoreline.Tunnel Lake is a hydro reservoir for hydro generating purposes. Two generating stations are situated on the lake.The following are designated as Tourism Lakes: Wakomata, Chub, Jobammageeshig, Little Pickerel, Cumming, Tunnel, Axe and Damn.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The intent is to manage the area to primarily benefit the commercial tourism and cottaging sectors. Day-use will also be encouraged.Requests for land disposition will be evaluated on a case-by-case basis, having regard to Ministry programs and the disposition policy contained in this document. Protection of existing recreational developments will be of prime importance when evaluating any requests for disposition.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Blind River District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
