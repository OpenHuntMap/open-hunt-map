## Land area description

Springwater Provincial Park is located in Simcoe County nine kilometres northwest of Barrie. The original park was expanded in 2008 from 47 ha to 193 ha with the inclusion of the former Midhurst Tree Nursery. Natural springs enabled reforestation of this area, once a wasteland due to logging. The park supports day use activities and includes wildlife display areas.Springwater Provincial Park was regulated in 1958.

## Land use intent

This recreational class park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act.
