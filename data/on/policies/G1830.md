## Land area description

The area consists of the Night Hawk Lake watershed unit south of Highway 101 and the Night Hawk River watershed unit. Within the area are many cottages, marinas, a campground and several areas of mineral exploration. The area lies entirely within a single forestry licenced Management Unit. Mineral extraction has taken place on the North Peninsula and exploration activities have been intense in and around the northern and southern portion of the lake. Night Hawk Lake is the only identified tourism lake in this area.

## Land use intent

This area will be primarily managed for recreation including quality sport fishing and water-fowl hunting. Mineral exploration/extraction and forest harvesting will be priority uses in non-shoreline areas. Development in the area will avoid the wild rice stands, pickerel spawning beds, heron rookeries, waterfowl staging areas, and significant moose habitat. No structural development will be permitted in recognized hazard land areas. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Timmins District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
