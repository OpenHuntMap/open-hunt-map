## Land area description

This area consists of the block of land which straddles Highway 502 immediately north of Rainy Lake. A considerable portion of the area has undergone timber harvesting, and, as a consequence, is reasonably well accessed by forest roads. This area is comprised exclusively of Crown land. This area has been used primarily for timber production, hunting, angling, cottaging and Crown land recreation. Angling pressure has been particularly high on the Cuttle/Mainville chain of lakes. The potential for metallic minerals is moderate to low. This area lies within the Crossroute Forest. Issues which will be taken into account in future management include the impact of heavy angling pressure, and deer habitat management required to achieve and maintain the populations which the area is capable of sustaining.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The role of this area will be to provide a mix of resource extraction and recreational benefits. Major uses of the area will be hunting, angling, cottaging and timber extraction. Areas of Concern will be managed on a priority basis to protect recreational activities and potentials. Resource access will be managed to achieve resource production and recreational benefits. Due consideration will be given to the protection of the Indian pictographs at Cuttle Lake. The Province does not have jurisdiction over the Redgut Bay Indian Reserve. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Fort Frances District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
