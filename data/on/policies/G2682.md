## Land area description

This area covers the Township of Nipigon and has within its boundaries the municipalities of Nipigon and Red Rock and the Red Rock Indian Reserve. Present activities include residential, commercial, industrial, Crown land recreation and resource extraction. The area is part of the Lakehead Forest and has Highways 11 and 17, the Trans-Canada Gas Pipeline, the CNR, the CPR and a hydro corridor. No major issues have been identified for this area.This area is located within the Lake Nipigon Basin Signature Site, one of 9 such areas featured in the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999). Signature Sites are identified for their range of natural and recreational values and their potential to contribute to future recreation and tourism.

## Land use intent

The primary use of this area will continue to be urban. All development and uses will conform to approved official plans and zoning by-laws. Resource extraction activities such as forestry, aggregate removal and mineral extraction are acceptable. Road access will be managed to facilitate the overall land use intent. Forestry operations and other development within 300 metres of the Nipigon River and Lake Superior will be subject to special planning considerations to ensure that fishery and scenic attributes of the area are maintained. The identified archaeological site will be respected.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Nipigon District Land Use Guidelines (1983).Nipigon/Red Rock General Use Area is located in an area in which one or more First Nations have asserted Aboriginal rights and title, including an unextinguished Aboriginal right to exclusive use and occupancy of the land. The First Nations’ claims are the subject of litigation and negotiations. Dispositions in Nipigon/Red Rock General Use Area may be subject to heightened Crown consultation and accommodation obligations.This may mean a greater degree of engagement with First Nation communities asserting rights and title prior to a decision regarding potential authorizations or dispositions of public lands or resources. For a map of areas affected by these claims, please go to the Notice of Caution on the Mining Lands Administration System (MLAS) at https://www.geologyontario.mndm.gov.on.ca/mines/lands/withreop/notices_e.html .
