## Land area description

Wolf Island Provincial Park was regulated in 1985. A transitional area where Paleozoic limestone outcroppings rub shoulders with much older Precambrian bedrock, the island is blanketed by swamp forest and thickets, mixed and deciduous forest, and open rockland forest of scattered trees and shrubs. Crescent-shaped gouges and striations, carved by glaciers during the late Wisconsinian era, are visible in the bedrock. This park does not offer facilities for visitors. There are several interior campsites that are accessible only by boat.

## Land use intent

This natural environment class park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act.
