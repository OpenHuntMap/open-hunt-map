## Land area description

Area E stretches from the geographic Township of Grimsthorpe to the geographic Township of Hinchinbrooke and is approximately 98,000 hectares in size of which 37 percent is Crown land.The primary activities are fur harvesting, mineral exploration, sand and gravel operations, agriculture and hunting and fishing.The special features in this area include Lingham Lake, the most significant bass fishery in the area, and several deer winter concentration areas located in the geographic Township of Sheffield and Hinchinbrooke.Potential for conflict exists between deer management and residential and recreational development.

## Land use intent

The area is recognized as being important for commercial fur harvesting. In addition, MNR will support mineral exploration, and hunting and fishing activities. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Tweed District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
