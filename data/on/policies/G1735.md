## Land area description

This area includes a corridor, 120 metres wide along both shorelines of the Kapuskasing, and Mattagami Rivers. Small portions of the Mattagami are found in other policy areas. Only the Kapuskasing River from the Fred Flatt bridge north of Highway Number 11 is included in this area. Public recreation is the major activity in this area. These rivers are designated provincial canoe routes and support other low intensity activities such as hunting, fishing, boating and camping. The Mattagami traverse several areas of high and medium mineral potential. In the north, the Mattagami travels through areas having both medium and high potential (lignite, kaolinite, fireclay and silica sand). Mineral exploration is ongoing in this area. Aggregate deposits are found at various locations along the corridor. A small segment of the Kapuskasing River dissects a high quality aggregate deposit in Teetzel and Pearce Township. Forest harvesting, regeneration and associated road construction are ongoing adjacent to much of this policy area.This area is subject to the Range Management Policy in Support of Woodland Caribou Conservation and Recovery (2014). The Range Management Policy is accessible via the “Source of Direction.”

## Land use intent

The primary use in this area will be recreation. This will take the form of public recreation, cottaging and commercial tourism. Areas for cottage lot development, outpost camps and access facilities will be examined. Water storage for hydro electric power generation is also a significant priority. Structural development will be discouraged on hazard lands (i.e. flood plains). Modified forest management will be practiced to maintain recreational objectives. All activities will ensure protective measures for significant natural and cultural features.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Kapuskasing District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
