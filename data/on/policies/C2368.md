## Land area description

The Campfire River Conservation Reserve is located approximately 73 kilometres north of the City of Kenora, adjacent to the Pakwash Road.The site contains representative landform and vegetation types, including burns, open wetlands and mixed conifer forests on broken ground moraine and lacustrine deposits. A spawning area and archaeological site are located in the area.Campfire River Conservation Reserve was regulated on May 21, 2003.Part of this area is subject to the Range Management Policy in Support of Woodland Caribou Conservation and Recovery (2014). The Range Management Policy is accessible via the “Source of Direction.””

## Land use intent

Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Land Use Strategy (1999).

## Permitted uses — preface

Those uses and management activities not listed in the following table are governed by the existing conservation reserve Policy and the Campfire River Conservation Reserve Statement of Conservation Interest (July 2001). Any new uses, and commercial activities associated with conservation reserves, will be considered on a case by case basis, and they must pass a test of compatibility to be acceptable. Compatibility is normally determined through a planning process.The following tables aim to present current information. However, with the passage of new legislation, updates to provincial policy and the development of protected areas management direction documents, certain content elements may be out of date.
