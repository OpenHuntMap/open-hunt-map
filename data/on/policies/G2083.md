## Land area description

The Two Peaks Area is one of four tourism areas identified in the Approved Cochrane District Remote (Wilderness) Tourism Strategy (RWS). This area is a major remote tourism area for wilderness recreation (e.g. fly-in fishing, hunting, nature appreciation). It is also an important resource area for forestry and mining. Public recreational uses include fishing, hunting, ATV's, and snowmobiling. Forest management is expanding northwards for future harvesting areas. The increasing road access to this remote area has caused significant concerns from the tourist outfitters.This portion of the Two Peaks Area is largely inaccessible. This area includes osprey nests and heron colonies, native brook trout, and scattered caribou populations.This area is subject to the Range Management Policy in Support of Woodland Caribou Conservation and Recovery (2014). The Range Management Policy is accessible via the “Source of Direction.”

## Land use intent

The management priority in this area is to protect the remote quality of the tourism lakes. The intent is to increase the protection of remote tourism lakes in the Two Peaks area by applying enhanced forest harvesting techniques, improved notice of mining exploration and by restricting new public access in and around these lakes, including closure of winter fishing.Maintenance of significant fish and wildlife populations and habitat will also be a priority in the area.Identified tourism lakes will be managed in accordance with the remote tourism strategy, and resource uses, including harvesting and public recreation, will be permitted subject to conditions to protect tourism lakes.Management of these areas may include seasonal restrictions, selective harvesting or outright protection. Structural development on and near these features will be discouraged. Proposals for these areas will be dealt with on a case-by-case basis.Management of this area is governed by the general policies contained in Cochrane District Land Use Guidelines (1983) and the Approved Cochrane District Remote (Wilderness) Tourism Strategy (1997).

## Permitted uses — preface

Land use policies from the RWS for those identified tourism lakes in the Two Peaks area is reflected in this policy report under the sections for commercial timber harvest, commercial tourism, road maintenance and development, road use (public), and sport fishing. For clarity, these RWS policies have been intentionally marked with an asterisk (*)Additional management direction for these lakes (which is more prescriptive in nature) is contained in the RWS and must also be followed. It is the intent to capture these prescriptions in appropriate forest management plans and or fisheries management strategies.
