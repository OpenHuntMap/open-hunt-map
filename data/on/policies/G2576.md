## Land area description

This area lies to the north and west of Pipestone Lake and the Manitou chain of lakes. Major existing uses include fly-in tourism, forestry and trapping. The area is located within the Crossroute Forest. Substantial portions of the area possess high or moderate mineral potential. Overall, the area is sparsely developed. Issues which will be taken into account in future management include the need to protect fly-in tourism operations from the impact of increased road access and forestry operations, and the need to protect the lake trout fishery.The following are designated as tourism lakes: Upper Lawrence, Frypan, Lawrence, Rowan, North Penassi, Harris, Upper Frypan, Penassi, Katamiagamak, Priam, Cleftrock, Hill, Kamini, Ajax, Hector, Brooks lakes.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The role of the area will be to provide increased levels of resource production while protecting the fly-in tourism industry. Considerable road construction and timber harvest occurs within this area. Road user controls may be used to protect remote tourist facilities from increases in road accessibility. The potential for Lawrence and Katamiagamak Lakes to support commercial fisheries is addressed in the fisheries management plan for the District. Also the opportunity exists to increase commercial fur production. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Fort Frances District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
