## Land area description

Existing activities include mineral exploration and development, timber management, trapping, commercial fishing, aggregate extraction and commercial lodge and outpost camp developments. The fish and wildlife resources and the rugged terrain have attracted fishermen and hunters to this area for many years. There are 5 tourism lakes in this area.Fisheries resources and the moose populations in some areas have been subjected to over-harvesting, attributed in part, to the relative ease of access provided by roads constructed for logging and mining purposes. This accessibility is regarded as being in conflict with the desire of recreationists and tourist operators to maintain the remote character of portions of the area and a natural environment recreation experience.Forest management is carried out under two sustainable forest licences (SFLs).A portion of this area is located within the Spanish River Signature Site.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Resource extraction will continue to be the primary use with fish and wildlife management and public recreation the important secondary uses. Lake trout lakes and other fish and wildlife populations will be protected from development and use pressures. The remote character of portions of the area and tourism lakes will be maintained wherever possible. Additional remote tourism development will be considered together with limited public recreation opportunities. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Sudbury District Land Use Guidelines (1983).

## Permitted uses — preface

In addition to provincial and regional policies, the following local policies will apply to the management of Natural lake trout lakes:­ no additional access will be permitted­ 120 metre Area of Concern will be applied­ forest management activities may be permitted within the Area of Concern subject to concerns for erosion/sedimentation and the protection of lake trout habitat.
