## Land area description

This portion of the Ottawa River extends south from Parkman Township to the Ontario Power Generation dam at Rolfton in the south. It flows through Site Districts 5E-6, 5E-5 and 5E-10. The area contains numerous recreational and scenic values. Stands of white pine, a symbol of the areas rich logging history will be protected in this corridor. The park will also provide great value as a component of the proposed Ottawa to New Liskeard recreational boating route.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The intent is to regulate this area as a provincial park.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Ontario's Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999).In accordance with commitment #20 of the Ontario Forest Accord, one new crossing of this recommended park may be permitted to provide access to harvestable timber that would otherwise be uneconomical to harvest. The crossing would be located at Block 7, at High Falls. Subject to the future boundary refinement of this provincial park, this crossing may in fact be situated outside the regulated park.

## Permitted uses — preface

Management of this provincial park is carried out in accordance with Ontario Provincial Parks: Planning and Management Policies (1992), the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999) and the Interim Management Statement, where available. The Interim Management Statement provides direction to ensure custodial management of park resources until such time as a park management planning process is conducted, which includes public review of proposals. At that time, significant decisions regarding resource stewardship, operations, development and permitted uses are made. Where a use may be permitted (Maybe or Yes on the table), the long-term management direction will be determined through planning.
