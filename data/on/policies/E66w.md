## Land area description

The Joly-Paxton Township Special Fisheries Management Area is an isolated area in Joly and Paxton Townships containing numerous small coldwater lakes important for long term fisheries research and management.

## Land use intent

Maintain existing Crown land management direction in MNR Bracebridge District Land Use Guidelines and Fisheries Management Plan for protecting this high quality coldwater fisheries resource. Management strategies within this area will ensure the preservation of native fish populations and the maintenance of high quality brook trout fisheries. District Land Use Guidelines direction includes identification of an area of concern around all permanent waterbodies in which timber harvesting will generally only occur in the winter under a selective harvest system, and roads and landings are generally not permitted.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Land Use Strategy (1999) and the Bracebridge District Land Use Guidelines (1983).

## Permitted uses — preface

The Fish and Wildlife category has been used for areas that are managed for the maintenance and enhancement of fish and wildlife habitat and populations, while allowing for the multiple use of other natural resources. The criteria for selection of these areas include:- areas of known or potential fish or wildlife habitat for species that require special management practices;- areas where forestry practices can be applied to enhanced or maintain special habitat requirements; or,- areas where enhanced hunting and fishing opportunities may be provided through specific management and land use practices.As is detailed in Bracebridge District Fisheries Management strategies that are specific to this area will be implemented.
