## Land area description

A significant recreational area containing a concentration of quality coldwater lakes, warm and coldwater ecosystems including headwater lakes and ponds and rugged and scenic topography. Area offers both road accessible and remote recreational opportunities. Whiskey Lake, Folson Lake and Wiggly Lake are important to the local tourism industry and an outpost camp and main base lodge exist in the area. Traditional uses include snowmobiling, sportfishing, canoeing, hunting, baitfishing, trapping and cottaging. Forestry and mining are important and new access is restricted in part of the area.Poor water quality throughout the portion of the Serpent River watershed in this area limits recreational use, but many of the smaller lakes unaffected by past tailings discharges are utilized by local anglers.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Manage this important remote lake tourism, recreation and resource sector (forestry, mining) area consistent with the enhanced management area (remote access) policies and the existing remote access policies of the MNR Blind River District and Espanola District Land Use Guidelines, including consideration of the continued use of existing vehicular access controls on Crown land. Further planning may detail individual zones within this overall area to provide for the needs of remote tourism, remote recreation and resource extraction, as well as the need to address the management of the area's important and sensitive coldwater fisheries.Traditional uses such as hiking, climbing, canoeing, hunting, angling, trapping, wildlife viewing etc. will be encouraged. Timber extraction, mineral exploration, mining and aggregate extraction activities will be permitted provided that long term, all season or year round public access is not improved.Land use direction and resources management activities within this area will be compatible with protecting the recreational values of the Glen Crombie Conservation Reserve (C227). Due to the complexity of the land use pattern throughout the area, new proposals for development will require a detailed evaluation on a case-by-case basis to ensure compatibility with surrounding uses.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Land Use Strategy (1999), the Blind River District Land Use Guidelines (1983) and Espanola District Land Use Guidelines (1983).

## Permitted uses — preface

Remote access enhanced management areas are intended to maintain the remote character of selected areas. Typically, these are relatively large areas which provide the public and tourism operators with high-quality remote recreational experiences including hunting, fishing, canoeing, and camping. Given the large size, remoteness, and relative absence of roads, these areas will play a significant role in protecting wilderness values outside the parks and protected areas system.In addition to provincial and regional policies, the following local policies will apply to the management of Natural lake trout lakes:­ no additional access will be permitted­ 120 metre Area of Concern will be applied­ forest management activities may be permitted within the Area of Concern subject to concerns for erosion/sedimentation and the protection of lake trout habitat.
