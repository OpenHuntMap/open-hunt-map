## Land area description

Aubrey Falls Provincial Park provides opportunities for boating, canoeing, hiking, angling and a number of other recreation activities. Aubrey Falls, an impressive cataract on the Mississagi River, is accessible by hiking trail from Highway 129. The park also contains water control structures related to hydro-electric generation.Aubrey Falls Provincial Park was regulated as a provincial park in 1985.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Aubrey Falls Provincial Park will be managed according to Provincial Park policy. The park will continue to provide recreational opportunities as a non-operating park. The hydro-electric facilities will remain in the park.Park Management Planning may provide additional direction in the future.
