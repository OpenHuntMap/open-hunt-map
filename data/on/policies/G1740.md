## Land area description

This area includes the shoreline (120 metres) of the Opasatika River north of Highway Number 11 up to and including Allan and Zadi Lakes. The Opasatika River, which presently provides the only access to these lakes is located approximately 30 kilometres west of the Town of Kapuskasing. Public and private recreation are the primary activities in this area. Development includes an access point at Highway 11, remote cottages, private summer cottage lots and trapper's cabins. The Zadi Lake Dam, belonging to Ontario Hydro, regulates water levels in this area.Part of this area is subject to the Range Management Policy in Support of Woodland Caribou Conservation and Recovery (2014). The Range Management Policy is accessible via the “Source of Direction.””

## Land use intent

Public recreation will be the major priority in this area. The intent is to provide a suitable recreational environment for existing remote cottaging, fishing and other recreational opportunities. This 120 metre shoreline area will continue to be managed primarily for recreation objectives. All activities will ensure protective measures for significant natural and cultural features. Known sites include an historic graveyard on Allan Lake.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Kapuskasing District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
