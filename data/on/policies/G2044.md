## Land area description

This area is south of the City of Greater Sudbury and includes the southern reaches of the Wanapitei River. Lands are primarily held under Crown ownership. Forest management (i.e. harvesting an renewal) is an important activity that supplies local wood-using mills. Mineral aggregate extraction has occurred along the Highway 637 corridor.Ontario Hydro operates the McVittie generating station on the Wanapitei River in the southwest corner of Secord Township. In conjunction with the Stinson and Coniston generating stations further upstream, Ontario Hydro provides hydro-electric power services for residential uses and mining industry needs in the Sudbury area. The sport fishery is dominated by warm water fish species including pickerel, pike and bass. Several moose wintering areas have been identified throughout the area and wildlife management efforts will continue to monitor the deer, moose and elk populations in an attempt to increase population and harvest opportunities. Fisheries resources and the moose populations in some areas have been subjected to overharvesting. This situation has been partly attributed to the relative ease of access provided by roads initially constructed for logging and mining purposes. This increased level of accessibility is also in conflict with the desire of some recreationists and tourist operators to maintain a natural environment recreation experience.A portion of this area is located within the Great Lakes Heritage Coast Signature.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Fish and wildlife management, in harmony with forest management, will dominate land use activities, in an attempt to ensure that fish and wildlife resources and associated recreation and tourism opportunities will be available in this portion of the District in the future. Any development which would reduce cover and browse will, therefore, not be encouraged on Crown land and not supported on private land. Hydro-electric power generation in Secord Township at the McVittie site will continue to be a recognized land use activity on that segment of the Wanapitei River. Resource management will be complementary to the adjacent Killarney Provincial Park and French River Provincial Park.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Sudbury District Guidelines (1983).

## Permitted uses — preface

In addition to provincial and regional policies, the following local policies will apply to the management of Natural lake trout lakes:­ no additional access will be permitted­ 120 metre Area of Concern will be applied­ forest management activities may be permitted within the Area of Concern subject to concerns for erosion/sedimentation and the protection of lake trout habitat.
