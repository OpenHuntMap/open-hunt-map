## Land area description

This Holding Category/General Use Area holds Crown lands being transferred to a designated nominee of Whitesand First Nation as determined in the agreement between Her Majesty the Queen (HMQ) in Right of Ontario (as represented by the Minister of Natural Resources and Minister of Aboriginal Affairs), the Ontario Electricity Financial Corporation and Whitesand First Nation dated August 9, 2011. The settlement lands to be transferred include: approximately 205 ha in the area referred to as the Old Whitesand Settlement area on the north shore of Lake Nipigon, approximately 170 ha at the community of Ferland, and approximately 80 ha at the community of Mud River.

## Land use intent

These lands are to be transferred to a designated nominee of Whitesand First Nation in the form of fee simple patent. The future use of the lands will be determined by Whitesand First Nation. Until the transfer occurs, these lands will remain within this holding category (G2640h) and Ontario retains management responsibility. Ontario will ensure that only land uses compatible with the terms of the agreement with Whitesand First Nation are permitted.

## Permitted uses — preface

The Settlement Agreement dated August 9, 2011 between Whitesand First Nation, HMQ in Right of Ontario as represented by the Minister of Natural Resources and the Minister of Aboriginal Affairs, and the Ontario Electricity Financial Corporation provides the management direction for this holding category. The transfer (disposition) of settlement lands to a designated nominee of Whitesand First Nation will convey title of the designated lands in full fee simple, without any reservations, exceptions, conditions or provisos except as may be required by law. Any reservations, exceptions, conditions or provisos required by law to patent the settlement lands will subsequently be removed from title.
