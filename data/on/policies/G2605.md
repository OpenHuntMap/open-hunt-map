## Land area description

This area includes the Eastern Peninsula of the Lake of the Woods and that portion of the lake in the vicinity of Big Narrows Island and Falcon Island. Portions of the Lake of the Woods Islands Conservation Reserve are located within this area. This part of the lake presents a more remote and isolated recreational experience. Resource capability and use is very limited for uses other than sport and commercial fishing. There are seven main base lodges and two Indian reserves (numbers 37 and 32B) located in this area.Water Management Areas have been removed from this Policy Report area as revisions, to more accurately reflect First Nation reserve lands affected by flooding.

## Land use intent

Future development in this area will be limited. The priorities will be for Crown land recreation and day use related to sport fishing and boating. All other resource uses future developments will recognize these roles.
