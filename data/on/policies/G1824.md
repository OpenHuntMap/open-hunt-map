## Land area description

The Tatachikapika Complex includes the lands and waters situated southwest of Timmins. It is a large area where resource harvesting and extraction along with recreation activities are high. Cottaging is a major use in the area. There are two canoe routes, the Kamiskotia and the Tatachikapika River canoe routes. This area lies entirely within one licenced forest Management Unit. Kenogaming Lake is the only identified tourism lake in this area.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Priority use within the complex will be a combination of public recreation and cottaging. Resource production and extraction will be a priority in non-shoreline areas. Resource management plans will contain guidelines for the protection of significant recreational and tourism sites, historical sites and fish and wildlife habitat. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Timmins District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
