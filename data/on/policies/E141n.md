## Land area description

This area in site district 5E-5 is adjacent to the Boulter-Depot Creek Conservation Reserve. The provincially significant earth and life science features contained in the conservation reserve extend into this enhanced management area, however, the enhanced management area is interspersed with forest plantations. The entire area is unorganized. Brook trout angling opportunity exists within this site.

## Land use intent

Forest management is permitted, provided that it is consistent with the protection of earth science natural heritage values. Aggregate extraction will be controlled, so as to not significantly adversely affect earth and life science natural heritage values and ground water discharge/recharge areas.Land use direction and resource management activities to be compatible with protection and management of the proposed Boulter-Depot Creek Conservation Reserve.The intent is for no forest management activity within the core life science areas of this Enhanced Management Area. Detailed prescription will be prepared to provide direction for forest management activities within non-core life science areas and core earth science areas. Enhanced Management Area management planning to recognize the importance of this site as a brook trout habitat. Several sites (heronries, cold water habitat etc.) are found in the area. These and other sites which may be found in the future, will be protected during resource management operations and will be identified as values requiring protection in municipal planning.MNR is responsible for the management Crown land and natural resources in unorganized townships.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Land Use Strategy (1999).

## Permitted uses — preface

Natural heritage enhanced management areas are intended to protect areas with significant natural values while allowing a range of resource activities.
