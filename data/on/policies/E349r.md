## Land area description

This area is located within the Temagami River/Lady Evelyn River watershed. The values and uses of the area include significant area of jack pine, the existing restricted access, and the significant aggregate deposits within management area.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Inland Ontario Lakes Designated for Lake Trout Management (2006) accessible in the Source of Direction portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The land use intent is to encourage forest renewal and resource extraction.Concerns include the impacts of resource extraction activities (e.g., noise, etc.) on the high value canoe routes of Bob Lake and Obabika River Waterway Park and the protection of the Small Lake canoe route connection between Diamond Lake and Bob Lake.The management area objectives are to continue restricted public motorized access, to restrict new roads within 350-metres of park boundaries, to consider non-consumptive recreation opportunities (i.e., mountain biking hut-to-hut), to follow conditions on fuelwood removal using ATVs from Lake Temagami, and to ensure resource extraction activities minimize impacts on canoe routes (i.e., aesthetics, noise).The strategies include applying road management strategies which maintain current restrictions to public motorized access and applying viewscape and Area of Concern planning to extractive activities.

## Permitted uses — preface

Land use and resource management activities for this area are also governed by the policies of the Temagami Land Use Plan (TLUP) (1997). The policies from the TLUP are integrated into this area report. Refer to the TLUP for background information and additional details.
