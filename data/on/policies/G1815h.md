## Land area description

This land use area is comprised of two areas adjacent to Matachewan First Nation Reserve 72., a section of lands immediately to the south and lands immediately to the east.

## Land use intent

This area is being prepared for transfer from Ontario to Canada for addition to reserve purposes for Matachewan First Nation. The land in the Holding Category General Use Area will be managed according to existing land use direction subject to the Matachewan Treaty Land Claim Entitlement Settlement Agreement until such a time that the transfer of lands has been completed. When the lands are transferred, this policy report no longer applies to the lands and will be removed from CLUPA.MNRF will maintain management responsibility of the area until such time the lands are transferred to Canada.The land use intent of this area is to prevent activities or land uses that may impact Canada’s acceptance of the lands to be set apart as reserve lands.Once the transfer be determined to be proceeding, existing provincial interests on the lands will be discontinued or transferred to federal permits, where appropriate.Should the transfer be determined not to be proceeding, an amendment will be sought to remove the holding category from the lands and return the lands to their original land use area.

## Permitted uses — preface

Management decisions must prevent activities or land uses that may impact Canada’s acceptance of the lands to be set apart as reserve. New approvals in this area will generally not be granted. Some maintenance of existing infrastructure may be permitted. Prior to any approval, it must be determined that the activity or land use would not impact Canada’s acceptance of the lands to be set apart as reserve. Confirmation from Canada may be warranted.
