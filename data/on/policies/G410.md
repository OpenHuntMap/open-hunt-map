## Land area description

This area comprises the north section of Lanark County and includes a portion of the Mazinaw Lanark Forest Management Unit. Much of the Lanark County Forest also is located in this area. The predominant land use is forestry. General recreation activities including hunting, fishing, back-country travel and cottaging are also significant in this area. Special features include:- Major deer concentration areas; Lavant Township, Bellamy Road, Clayton Lake, Dalhousie LakeProblems and issues:- A potential for conflict between the Forestry and Wildlife programs exists due to the concentration of forestry activities and deer wintering areas. - Pressure for development on waterbodies in this area is putting increasing stress on the fisheries resource.

## Land use intent

This area is the most significant timber production area in the district. It also is an area of significant habitat for wildlife and fisheries. The area will be managed for resource production, specifically forest management. Forestry activities will be carried out in recognition of the requirements of the other programs. In specific areas, forestry activities will be restricted. Discussion:Within this primary forest production area are productive Crown land forests. Ministry programs within this area must not compromise the availability of timber. The Lanark County Forest, an area of approximately 4,616 hectares also contributes to the achievement of district objectives and targets. Primary deer winter concentration areas are found within this area, making it the focus for intensive wildlife management. For those areas on Crown land, MNR can direct its programs to enhancing the wildlife resource, while on private land MNR will indicate preferred wildlife management actions. Specific strategies:-undertake forestry activities in areas identified as deer winter concentration areas, however, both cutting and regeneration operations will require consultation with wildlife staff before implementation; -support resource management in the Lanark County Forest -administer that portion of the area within the designated forest fire region for forest fire suppression under the Forest Fires Prevention Act. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Carleton Place District Land Use Guideline (1983).
