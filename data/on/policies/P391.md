## Land area description

The park is located within the Canadian Shield, 40 kilometres north of Bancroft in Site District 5E-11. Both mature and immature forest stands occur, as well as a series of wetland communities. A campground and day use area are located in the southern portion of the park along Lake St. Peter.Lake St. Peter Provincial Park was regulated as a provincial park in 1971.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The park was established to provide recreational opportunities.A 453 ha addition was regulated in 1989, providing good representation of upland deciduous forests as well as opportunities for wildlife viewing.
