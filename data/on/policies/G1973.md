## Land area description

This area is located within the Temagami River/Matabitchuan River watershed. The values and uses of this area include the community of Temagami (urban and rural residential), the historic Sherman Mine, and the historical fire tower on Caribou Mountain.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Inland Ontario Lakes Designated for Lake Trout Management (2006) accessible in the Source of Direction portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

In an area comprised primarily of patented land and urban development under the jurisdiction of the municipality, the intent is to provide a diverse range of resource uses (forestry, mining related activities, and aggregates) and recreational and tourism opportunities. The land use and resource management direction for this management area pertains only to Crown land and other resources under the Ontario government's jurisdiction; therefore, additional land use intent for this area will be to protect cultural heritage values and enhance fish and wildlife habitats with a focus on Sherman Mine rehabilitation.A concern of this area includes the long-term impact of Sherman Mine on water quality. A management area objective is to ensure that provincial interests will be considered in municipal planning.The strategies of this area include developing partnerships between the Ministry of Natural Resources and the municipality in regard to resource issues, promoting various resource policies, and encouraging resource stewardship on private lands.

## Permitted uses — preface

Land use and resource management activities for this area are also governed by the policies of the Temagami Land Use Plan (TLUP) (1997). The policies from the TLUP are integrated in this area report. Refer to the TLUP for background information and additional details.
