## Land area description

This area is located within the Matabitchuan River/Montreal River watershed. The values and uses of this area include the Sunrise, Reuben, and Fourbass lakes which are naturally reproducing lake trout lakes. In addition, the values include the existing and potential canoe routes (including heritage routes) accessible by water from the town of Temagami and east shore of Rabbit, Cassels, and Obashkong lakes and portions of Fourbass and Montreal River shorelines. The whitewater canoe route on the Matabitchuan River is also considered a value. The Ottawa-Temiskaming Highland Trail passes through this management area and provides significant recreational and natural heritage appreciation opportunities.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Inland Ontario Lakes Designated for Lake Trout Management (2006) accessible in the Source of Direction portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The land use intent of this area is to allow for forestry and mining related activities while providing for backcountry recreational opportunities including all terrain vehicle (ATV) use.The concerns of this area include the ATV use and trail development outside of identified areas where ATV use is permitted. The objectives of this management area include allowing new access for mining related activities and forest management only as well as protecting existing canoe routes and retaining the potential for new routes. Other objectives include managing for old growth characteristics in white and red pine stands, retaining backcountry hiking opportunities, and protecting existing tourism values. In this management area, identified ATV use will not expand nor be upgraded to public car/truck access (ATV trails in south portion will not be connected to roads outside the management area).The strategies include harvesting pine stands to maintain old growth characteristics and applying viewscape management and Area of Concern planning for extractive activities. In addition, the strategies also include mitigating impacts on backcountry recreation and remote tourism by managing access to minimize conflicts. A strategy was developed to prohibit further use of existing portage trails by ATVs within this management area as part of the Crown Land Recreation Plan Temagami Integrated Planning process.
