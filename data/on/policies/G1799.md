## Land area description

This area covers the Planer area adjacent to the Municipality of Chapleau, and the seven small communities of Biscotasing, Devon, Foleyet, Island Lake, Pineal Lake, Ramsey, and Sultan. Initially these communities developed as small settlements surrounding either sawmills or some other facet of the woods industry. Tourism is now an important component in the economies of Biscotasing and Foleyet.

## Land use intent

MNR recognizes the communities as local service centres for resource users. MNR's main role in these areas is to protect the safety of their inhabitants from threats such as flooding, or fire on surrounding forest areas. MNR will assist the Ministry of Municipal Affairs and Housing to ensure that all development proposals on private land protect as much as possible the land use intent of adjacent Crown land areas.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Chapleau District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
