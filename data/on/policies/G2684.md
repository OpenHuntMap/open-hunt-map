## Land area description

This area is located east of Lake Nipigon and consists of the townships of Eva and Summers. Present uses include residential, commercial, industrial and Crown land recreation. The area is entirely within the Lake Nipigon Forest. Mineral exploration and extraction are very important to the local economy. No major resource management issues are anticipated in this area. Beardmore is part of the Municipality of Greenstone.This area is located within the Lake Nipigon Basin Signature Site, one of 9 such areas featured in the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999). Signature Sites are identified for their range of natural and recreational values and their potential to contribute to future recreation and tourism.This area is subject to the Range Management Policy in Support of Woodland Caribou Conservation and Recovery (2014). The Range Management Policy is accessible via the “Source of Direction.”

## Land use intent

The primary use of this area will continue to be urban. Resource extraction (mining, aggregate removal and forestry) will continue to be acceptable activities. All development and uses will conform to the approved zoning by-laws. Road access will be managed to facilitate the overall land use intent.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Nipigon District Land Use Guidelines (1983).Beardmore General Use Area is located in an area in which one or more First Nations have asserted Aboriginal rights and title, including an unextinguished Aboriginal right to exclusive use and occupancy of the land. The First Nations’ claims are the subject of litigation and negotiations. Dispositions in Beardmore General Use Area may be subject to heightened Crown consultation and accommodation obligations.This may mean a greater degree of engagement with First Nation communities asserting rights and title prior to a decision regarding potential authorizations or dispositions of public lands or resources. For a map of areas affected by these claims, please go to the Notice of Caution on the Mining Lands Administration System (MLAS) at https://www.geologyontario.mndm.gov.on.ca/mines/lands/withreop/notices_e.html .
