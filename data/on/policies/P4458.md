## Land area description

Lake on the Mountain Provincial Park was established in 1957. Situated on the crest of a 62 m high limestone escarpment in Prince Edward County, the lake is a physiographic anomaly of regional significance. The park provides scenic views of the Bay of Quinte, and is a popular summer picnic spot.

## Land use intent

This recreational class park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act.
