## Land area description

The Brighton Provincial Wildlife Area is located along the north side of Provincial Highway 401, approximately 5 kilometres north of the village of Brighton and 15 kilometers west of the town of Trenton. The area is within the Municipality of Brighton and the City of Quinte West.The Brighton Provincial Wildlife Area was identified as a provincially significant Area of Natural and Scientific Interest (ANSI) as part of the Raised Beaches Theme group in the Earth Science Framework for the ANSI Program in Ontario. This site was selected based on its representation of topography and rock forms deposited by water, wind or ice, which have been interpreted to be associated with the existence of Glacial Lake Iroquois. The diverse landscape within the Brighton Provincial Wildlife Area provides excellent habitat for a varietyof wildlife species and results in an extensive range of uses and user groups. This is an area of open fields, old orchards, wooded bottomland and beaver ponds which provide a varied and interesting landscape in which to hunt or view wildlife. Ruffed grouse, woodcock, ducks, cottontails, European hare, wild turkey, white-tailed deer and other species are found here.

## Land use intent

The primary land use intent for the Brighton Provincial Wildlife Area is to maximizerecreational benefits, specifically for hunting, and to demonstrate wildlife management techniques. Secondary uses relate to other non-motorized recreational opportunities including hiking, biking, nature appreciation, education, fishing, and horseback riding.Snowmobiling is permitted on designated trails.The property will be managed in a sustainable manner for the benefit of present and future generations. All recreation and management activities must be practiced in a manner that is environmentally sustainable. Areas of the property that contain species-at-risk or sensitive habitats will be managed accordingly, for example by directing activities (e.g. non-motorized multi-use trails) to less sensitive areas of the property.
