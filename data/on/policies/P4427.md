## Land area description

Bass Lake Provincial Park is located in the Georgian Lakelands Tourist Region four kilometres west of Orillia and 140 km north of Toronto. Initially a farm for more than 100 years, the former farm fields are good for skiing and snowshoeing in the winter. Bass Lake Provincial Park was regulated in 1957.

## Land use intent

This recreational class park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act.
