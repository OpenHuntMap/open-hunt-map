## Land area description

This area represents a watershed unit containing significant cold water (trout) habitat. It encompasses a series of cold water esker lakes and trout creeks (i.e. Boyle Lake, Mowbray, Bradley, Cosens and Boyle Creeks). Forestry is the primary activity in the area. Mineral potential is low. The esker in Guilfoyle and Boyle Townships is an extension of the Fred Flatt Esker and contains high quality aggregate.This area is subject to the Range Management Policy in Support of Woodland Caribou Conservation and Recovery (2014). The Range Management Policy is accessible via the “Source of Direction.”

## Land use intent

Extraction activities (i.e. forestry, mining) are priorities in non-shoreline areas. Public recreation, particularly cold water angling, will be a priority in shoreline areas. Resource management plans will contain guidelines for the protection of fish and wildlife and recreational values. Modified forest management will be practiced in selected area.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Kapuskasing District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
