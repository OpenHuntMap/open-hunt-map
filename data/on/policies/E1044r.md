## Land area description

This area encompasses those parts of the Ramsey Lake and Biscotasi Lake basins which are not included in the Mississagi River Provincial Park and Addition, and the Biscotasi Lake Provincial Park Addition. This includes the area between Ramsey Lake's East Arm and Northwest Arm of Biscotasi Lake. In addition, the zone extends southward from the above noted areas to include 120 metres back from the east shore of Drefal Lake, Houghton Lake, Skelton Lake and Mishap Lake to Metagama Road, then west on the Metagama Road to the southern boundary of Earl Township, then north to Southern Bay. Apart from the community of Biscotasing, the area now contains 12 cottages, 16 private camps on land use permits, 9 commercial outpost camps and 3 lodges. Two Ministry water access points are a further indication of the recreational use in this area. Other major values are extensive wood supplies held under SFL, some high mineral potential in the northern part of the Biscotasi Lake basin and the water storage reservoir system that INCO Ltd. has developed here for its hydraulic generating stations downstream on the Spanish River system.A large area southwest of Biscotasi Lake is designated as an Enhanced Management Area for recreation.This area is located within the Spanish River Signature Site, one of 9 such areas featured in the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999). Signature Sites are identified for their range of natural and recreational values and their potential to contribute to future recreation and tourism.Part of this area is subject to the Range Management Policy in Support of Woodland Caribou Conservation and Recovery (2014). The Range Management Policy is accessible via the “Source of Direction.””

## Land use intent

This mixed use Area will be managed within the context of maintaining a semi-remote area. This land use intent is designed to assist the economic development of Biscotasing by reinforcing the townsite as a principal point of access to Biscotasi Lake. Resource management plans will contain guidelines to protect significant recreational and tourism values, heritage resources, fisheries values and wildlife habitat. This area will be managed in a manner complementary to the adjacent Mississagi River Provincial Park and Addition, and the Biscotasi Lake Provincial Park Addition. Water levels in Biscotasi, Ramsey, and Indian Lakes will be managed in accordance with the Spanish River Watershed Management Plan, 1992.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Land Use Strategy (1999) and the Chapleau District Land Use Guidelines (1983).

## Permitted uses — preface

Recreation enhanced management areas have been applied to areas with high recreational use of significant recreation values for activities such as angling, hunting, motorized and pedestrian trail use, and canoeing. The enhanced management area can be used for a wide range of recreation values and management intents, ranging from areas where relatively substantial recreation development is permitted, to areas providing low-density, high-quality recreation in a natural setting.Where the recreation enhanced management Area has been identified to protect remote recreation values, all activities need to be carried out in such a way as to maintain or enhance the remote recreation qualities.

## Addendum

The following lakes are designated as tourism lakes: Biscotasi, Earl, Houghton, Indian, Ramsey, Skelton and Zak. In addition to provincial and regional policies, the following local policies will apply to the management of these lakes: - On Tourism lakes, tourism will be promoted as a primary land use objective. Road access restrictions and other policies may be applied to protect these lakes from increased access.
