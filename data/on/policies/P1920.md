## Land area description

W.J.B. Greenwood Provincial Park is a 465 hectare recreation class park located within the Municipality of Latchford. The park encompasses an early succession birch dominated forest as well as a number of hike-in campsites. The location of the park allows travellers to stop and enjoy a low intensity recreational experience.W.J.B. Greenwood Provincial Park was regulated as a provincial park in 1985.

## Land use intent

W.J.B. Greenwood Provincial Park will be managed to protect an early succession birch dominated forest and provide walk-in camping and hiking opportunities with minimal capital infrastructure supported by partnerships with cooperating associations.

## Permitted uses — preface

Management of this provincial park is carried out in accordance with Ontario Provincial Parks: Planning and Management Policies (1992) and the park management statement (2009). The permitted uses associated with provincial parks are found in approved management direction, which are available on the Ontario Parks website.
