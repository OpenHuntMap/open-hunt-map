## Land area description

North Hinkler Road Enhanced Management Area was designated through the Ontario's Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999) as a remote access enhanced management area, in recognition of the importance of remote areas on the landscape. This site is situated east of Highway 129 and north of Hinkler Lake Road, and is an important remote tourism, recreation and resource sector (forestry, mining) area with limited road access. Numerous remote commercial outpost camps are located throughout the EMA and comprise the major tourism use in the area. North Hinkler Road surrounds Wagong Lake Forest Conservation Reserve (C243) and abuts a portion of Wenebegon River Provincial Park (P247) and Mississagi River Provincial Park (P238). The southern boundary of the site is defined by Hinkler Lake Road, and the northern boundary follows the MNR District boundary between Chapleau and Sault Ste. Marie Districts. The vegetation in the North Hinkler Road Enhanced Management Area is composed of jack pine, black spruce, poplar and white birch mixedwoods, with a scattering of white and red pine. This area is one of the best representations of boreal forest in Sault Ste. Marie District. The central and western sections of the site were burnt over in the Mississagi Fire of 1948, and the eastern portion of the site contains a large wetland complex which provides important habitat for wildlife. This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The intent of this area is to encourage remote based recreation, including remote resource-based tourism while providing for forest management activities. Land use direction and resource management activities within this area will be compatible with protecting the natural and recreational values of Mississagi River Additions Provincial Park (P238), Wenebegon River Provincial Park (P247) and Wagong Lake Forest Conservation Reserve (C243).Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Ontario's Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999) and the Blind River District Land Use Guidelines (1983).

## Permitted uses — preface

Any new uses or commercial activities associated with this enhanced management area will be considered on a case-by-case basis and may be subject to the Class Environmental Assessment for MNR Resource Stewardship and Facility Development Projects (2003). Those uses or management activities not listed in the following table are governed by the existing policy for remote access category enhanced management areas. This policy states: “Remote access enhanced management areas are intended to maintain the remote character of selected areas. Typically, these are relatively large areas which provide the public and tourism operators with high-quality remote recreational experiences including hunting, fishing, canoeing, and camping. Given the large size, remoteness, and relative absence of roads, these areas will play a significant role in protecting wilderness values outside the parks and protected areas system (OLL LUS, 1999).”
