## Land area description

The Kidd Creek Complex is a broad band traversing the northern most part of the Timmins District accessed by Highway 655 and resource extraction roads. Generally the area is poorly accessed due to the numerous swamps and bogs. Development is sparse. The mining potential of the area is high. The most predominant development is the Kidd Creek Mine Site in Kidd Township. This area lies within two separate licenced forest Management Units.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.Part of this area is subject to the Range Management Policy in Support of Woodland Caribou Conservation and Recovery (2014). The Range Management Policy is accessible via the “Source of Direction.””

## Land use intent

The primary use in this area will be resource extraction in non-shoreline lands with emphasis on the continued management of the sport fisheries and existing recreational values in shoreline areas. Resource management plans will contain guidelines for the protection of significant recreation and fish and wildlife values. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Timmins District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
