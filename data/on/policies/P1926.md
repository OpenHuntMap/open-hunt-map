## Land area description

Manitou Islands Provincial Nature Reserve is located 20 kilometres southwest of the City of North Bay. This nature reserve includes four islands in Lake Nipissing and consists of 1,926 hectares.Manitou Islands Provincial Nature Reserve contains significant natural and cultural features such as geological and archaeological features, wildlife nesting sites and submarine landforms.Manitou Islands Provincial Nature Reserve was regulated as a provincial park in 1989.

## Land use intent

Manitou Islands Provincial Nature Reserve will be managed according to Provincial Park policy and Park Management Plan (1990). The nature reserve is managed to protect its provincially significant, special and representative features of Lake Nipissing’s natural history. The park’s natural and cultural features will be protected. Scientific research is encouraged.
