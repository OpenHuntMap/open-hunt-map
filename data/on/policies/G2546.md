## Land area description

This area, north of the Township of Ignace, extends to the English River the Sandbar Lake designated canoe route. The area has a large number of sport fishing lakes and includes the Sandbar. Resident and non-resident recreation has focused on this area since about 1960. Five tourist lodges, five outpost camps, four cottage subdivisions, one remote cottage and a number of individual cottages are found in the area. The area also has a high number of historical sites; eight pictographs and three historical quarries.The following are designated as tourism lakes: Mameigwess, Indian, Paguchi, Godden, Basket, Abamategwia, Notman.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The primary purpose of this area will be the maintenance of existing public, private and commercial recreation facilities in harmony with existing and proposed timber harvesting operations.Expansion of commercial tourism will be permitted in this area. New cottaging sub-divisions may be considered on any lakes as identified and reviewed. District’s commercial food fishing licences will be continued on Basket, Mameigwess and Sowden Lakes.Sensitive areas will be recognized and protected in resource management plans.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Ignace District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
