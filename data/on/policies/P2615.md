## Land area description

Rushing River Provincial Park is located on the east side of Highway 71 approximately 5.5 kilometres south of Highway 17. The Rushing River also abuts the proposed Eagle-Dogtooth Provincial Park.This recreation class park is 340 hectares in size and was established in September 1958.Further extensions to the south of existing park and to the west of Highway 71 (35 hectares) took place in 2000.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The intent is to manage this park as a recreation class provincial park, in accordance with MNR and Ontario Parks Policy.The park will continue to be managed to provide a variety of recreational opportunities including camping, day use, hiking and nature interpretation.
