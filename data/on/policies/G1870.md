## Land area description

The Montreal River is a multiple use waterway. It is controlled by an Ontario Hydro dam at Latchford. Recreational activities are boating, tourist camps, canoeing, hunting, fishing and cottaging. It is the source of water for the Town of Latchford and the Refinery Townsite. It is an important pickerel fishery. Numerous mineral occurrences have been identified in the past along the river. Road access to the river can be gained via Indian Lake and Mowat's Landing. Cottage lot development and water level control are future management issues which need to be dealt with.

## Land use intent

Wildlife and fisheries management, commercial tourism, recreation and water supply are permitted uses. Ministry programs will encourage multi-use of the river with an emphasis on recreation and tourism. All activities will take into consideration the presence of a historic feature being Rennebester Settlement- Mowatt's Landing. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Temagami District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
