## Land area description

Representative features within this site include medium aged red/silver maple, young poplar and open muskeg on weakly broken bedrock.Gem Lake Maple Bedrock Provincial Nature Reserve was regulated as a provincial park on May 8, 2002.

## Land use intent

Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Ontario's Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999).
