## Land area description

Located 4 km northeast of the Town of Aylmer, in the Township of Malahide, Elgin County, Aylmer Wildlife Management Area provides visitors with many recreational opportunities, including: hunting, education and wildlife viewing. Upon acquisition of the property from the Royal Canadian Air Force in 1962, it was restored to provide habitat for waterfowl, shore birds, song birds and game birds and to act as a demonstration site for wildlife management on farm land. The Ontario Police College occupies part of the property and is not included within the provincial wildlife area boundary.The property provides excellent migratory bird hunting and viewing opportunities. Large numbers of Tundra Swans can be viewed at close range during their spring migration. Other popular uses of the site include hiking, bird watching, dog walking and public education events.The property contains a Provincially Significant Wetland and is recognized as a federal designated Important Bird Area. The area offers excellent opportunities for day use outdoor recreation. The management of the area is currently supported by the Elgin Stewardship Council and management of the water levels in the sanctuary ponds is supported by Ducks Unlimited.

## Land use intent

The Aylmer Wildlife Management Area will be managed to provide breeding and migratory habitat for waterfowl, shorebirds and other priority species, as well as, to provide associated wildlife-based recreational activities, including hunting, hiking, research, environmental education, nature appreciation/interpretation and to act as a demonstration site of wildlife management on farm land. Activities and projects that protect and enhance wildlife and their habitat, as well as provide educational/research opportunities, will be considered. Specific emphasis is placed on management and development to support migratory waterfowl and shorebirds. Unless there is a valid case that any other proposed activity is wildlife-oriented or compatible, no other activities will be permitted on the property.
