## Land area description

Voyageur Provincial Park is a recreational class park that currently encompasses 1 464.53 ha within the municipality of East Hawkesbury, County of Prescott and Russell. Established in 1966, the park offers a high quality recreational opportunities including camping, hiking, swimming and cycling. Since the park was established, one addition has been made. One acquired parcel (an inholding) was added to consolidate the park boundary. Adding the acquired parcel to the regulated boundary will allow it to be managed consistent with provincial park regulations and management policies.

## Land use intent

MNRF intends to regulate this area as part of Voyageur Provincial Park, which is a recreational class park under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act, 2006.
