## Land area description

This area is located within the Lady Evelyn River/Wabi River watershed. The values and uses of this area include the access to the Montreal River and Lady Evelyn Lake as well as the backcountry parks and Crown land provided by the Mowat Landing access point. In addition, the area is dominated by young forest and contains large road-accessible recreation area, especially for hunting. This area also contains Bay Lake and a portion of the Montreal River.

## Land use intent

The land use intent is to provide a diverse range of resource uses (forestry, mining related activities and aggregates) while maintaining recreational and tourism opportunities and recognizing the area's important access point for Lady Evelyn Lake.The concerns include maintaining fisheries and the aesthetics of Bay Lake/Montreal River and managing the Mowat Landing access point issues (parking, waste disposal, fee collection, etc.). Other concerns include the proposed forest access road crossing across Montreal River near Mowat Landing and the potential unplanned access for public motorized recreation. The management area objectives include retaining a scenic corridor adjacent to Highway 558 west of Highway 11 as well as the aesthetics of Montreal River for recreation. Objectives also include ensuring continued water access at Mowat Landing while ensuring public access does not occur in the area north of Lady Evelyn Lake via road crossing of the Montreal River. The strategies include applying access strategy developed in the Elk Lake Forest Management Plan for Montreal River crossing as well as planning access and developing road use strategies by coordinating with the Kirkland Lake District where necessary. Strategies also include integrating Mowat Landing into future access management and visitor distribution system for the Temagami Recreation Area and area parks. Applying viewscape management and Area of Concern planning for extractive activities is considered to be another strategy..
