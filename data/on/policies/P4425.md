## Land area description

Awenda Provincial park is located on Georgian Bay at the north tip of Tiny Township near Penetanguishene. The park consists of the mainland portion of the park and Giant's Tomb Island located approximately three kilometres north of the park in Georgian Bay. The park is located approximately 80 km north of Barrie and 150 km north of Toronto. The 60 metres high Nipissing Bluff dominates the parks landscape. Below the bluff a series of sand, cobble and boulder beaches offer the best swimming in the area. Across the water on Giant's Tomb Island rests the spirit Kitchikewana, the protector of Georgian Bay. Awenda Provincial Park was regulated in1975.

## Land use intent

This natural environment class park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act.
