## Land area description

This area consists of Marmion Lake, a large water body north of the Town of Atikokan. The area generally includes the lake and its backshore a distance of 120 metre form the water's edge. A part of the Seine River system, the lake was created as a result of a hydro development in the early 1900's. The lake is a heavily used recreational area. Angling, boating, canoeing and camping by both local residents and non-residents occur throughout the open water season. The lake is a warm water lake and produces excellent angling for walleye and northern pike. Existing activities include logging, mineral exploration, trapping, commercial bait fishing and recreation.The area is well accessed by existing and former resource access roads. Marmion Lake is in the Crossroute Forest and Sapawe Forests.No specific problems have been identified except the conflicts between resource extraction and recreation.Marmion Lake is designated as a tourism lake.

## Land use intent

This area will provide an easily accessible, highly productive angling area for Atikokan residents and the tourist industry. Extraction activities such as logging and mining will occur but the recreational qualities of the area will not be adversely affected.The southern portion of the lake is the water source for the Atikokan Generating Station. The station takes in water from the southwestern part of the lake and discharges it through several small lakes, back into Marmion, approximately four miles east of the intake.Areas of Concern will be applied around recreational and tourism development. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Atikokan District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
