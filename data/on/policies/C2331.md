## Land area description

The Manitou Conservation Reserve is situated approximately 60 kilometres northeast of the Town of Fort Frances. The site encompasses most of the islands, major peninsulas and some adjacent lands of Lower Manitou Lake, Vickers Lake, Harris Lake, Manitou Stretch, Esox Lake, Dogfly Lake and Bat Lake.This site includes dispersed areas of old growth red pine, white pine and cedar on islands and peninsulas of the Crossroute Forest, as well as a variety of landform types. Harris, Manitou Stretch, Lower Manitou, Esox, Dogfly, Vickers and Grant are designated tourism lakes.Manitou Conservation Reserve was regulated on May 21, 2003.The Manitou Conservation Reserve (C2331) is an amalgamation of the recommend Ontario Living Legacy Highrock Conservation Reserve (C2331) and the recommended Ontario Living Legacy Manitou Conservation Reserve (C2337). These two conservation reserves have been combined for management purposes.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Crown land recreational uses will be permitted to continue with the emphasis on canoeing. No new access points or public facilities are proposed. Vehicular access will be managed to maintain low density recreational use.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Land Use Strategy (1999).

## Addendum

Management of this conservation reserve is carried out under the provisions of the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act and within the context of conservation reserve policy. Any new uses, and commercial activities associated with conservation reserves, will be considered on a case by case basis, and they must pass a test of compatibility to be acceptable. Compatibility is normally determined through a planning process.
