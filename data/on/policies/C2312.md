## Land area description

This site contains a provincially significant patterned (ridge and swale) peatland.Side Lake is situated 62 kilometres northwest of the Town of Atikokan on the west side of the Eagle-Finlayson moraine. This site lies approximately 1 kilometre to the west of Highway 622. The site is bordered on the south by a forest access road, which is excluded.Side Lake Conservation Reserve was regulated on May 21, 2003.

## Land use intent

Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Land Use Strategy (1999).

## Addendum

Management of this conservation reserve is carried out under the provisions of the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act and within the context of conservation reserve policy. Any new uses, and commercial activities associated with conservation reserves, will be considered on a case by case basis, and must pass a test of compatibility to be acceptable. Compatibility is normally determined through a planning process.
