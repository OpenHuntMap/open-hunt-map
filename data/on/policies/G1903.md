## Land area description

Significant and critical aggregate areas are found within, immediately north and east of the city of Sault Ste. Marie, and on St. Joseph Island. Deposits are located in Prince, Korah, Tarentorus, Macdonald, Tarbutt, Laird, Tarbutt Additional, and Jocelyn Townships. There is also a significant deposit located offshore of Gros Cap in Lake Superior.Coastal portions of this area are located within the Great Lakes Heritage Coast Signature Site, one of 9 such areas featured in the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999). Signature Sites are identified for their range of natural and recreational values and their potential to contribute to future recreation and tourism.

## Land use intent

The land use intent is to manage these areas for existing and potential aggregate production. Uses that do not permanently alienate these aggregate deposits from production are considered permissible. Other activities may occur in a sequential manner after extraction and rehabilitation have been completed (e.g. rural residential developments), or aggregate production may occur sequentially after another short-term land use (e.g. forest cropping) has been completed. Disposition of Crown land for uses which do not conform with management intents for these areas will be discouraged. Compatible long term activities on private lands will be encouraged. All activities in the area will take into consideration the known nesting sites. The specific location can be found in a compendium at the District office. Aggregate production will be managed so that critical spawning and fisheries habitat are not permanently adversely affected. Non-aggregate related industrial developments will be discouraged until aggregate removal is complete.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Sault Ste. Marie District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
