## Land area description

This nature reserve is composed of five separate parcels of land in the geographic Township of Brougham. The largest is an upland area around Oakhill Lake. There are three small island parcels on Black Donald Lake. The reserve protects representative pine, oak and maple forests in Site District 5E-11 as well as rare plant species and habitats growing on marble outcrops.Centennial Lake Provincial Nature Reserve was regulated as a provincial park in 1989.

## Land use intent

This nature reserve was established to protect provincially significant earth and life sciences features.Management will be in accordance with the Centennial Lake Provincial Nature Reserve Interim Management Statement (2002).

## Permitted uses — preface

Management of this provincial park is carried out in accordance with Ontario Provincial Parks: Planning and Management Policies (1992), the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999) and the Interim Management Statement. The Interim Management Statement provides direction to ensure custodial management of park resources until such time as a park management planning process is conducted, which includes public review of proposals. At that time, significant decisions regarding resource stewardship, operations, development and permitted uses are made. The long-term management direction will be determined through planning. Such uses that are existing may continue in the interim, unless park values are threatened. The permitted uses associated with provincial parks are found in approved management direction, which are available on the Ontario Parks website.
