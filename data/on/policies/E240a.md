## Land area description

Kirkpatrick Lake Northwest Enhanced Management Area is located approximately 56 km northwest of the City of Blind River. It is situated primarily in Le Caron, Sayer, Simons and Wagg Townships, with small sections of the site extending into Jackson, Rioux and Slievert Townships. The Enhanced Management Area is bisected and partially bounded by Little White River Provincial Park (P261) and is adjacent to Kirkpatrick Lake South and Northeast Enhanced Management Area (E237r).Kirkpatrick Lake Northwest Enhanced Management Area is located in the vicinity of a number of high quality lake trout lakes which are managed through access restrictions and extended closed seasons. Some fly-in remote operations also exist which contribute to the high quality fishing experience. Traditional uses include sportfishing, hunting, baitfishing and trapping. Forestry and mining operations also occur. Any new access will have restrictions to maintain the intent of this area.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The intent of this area is to encourage remote based recreation, including remote resource based tourism, while providing for forest management activities. Management of this important remote tourism, recreation and resource sector (forestry, mining) area, including the continued use of existing vehicular access controls, will be consistent with the policies for remote access category enhanced management areas, contained in the Ontario's Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999), and the existing remote access policies in the MNR Blind River District Land Use Guidelines (1983) Land use direction and resource management activities within this area will be compatible with protecting the natural and recreational values of Little White River Provincial Park.and Kirkpatrick Lake South and Northeast Enhanced Management Area

## Permitted uses — preface

Any new uses or commercial activities associated with this enhanced management area will be considered on a case-by-case basis and may be subject to the Class Environmental Assessment for MNR Resource Stewardship and Facility Development Projects (2003). Those uses or management activities not listed in the following table are governed by the existing policy for remote access category enhanced management areas. This policy states: "Remote access enhanced management areas are intended to maintain the remote character of selected areas. Typically, these are relatively large areas which provide the public and tourism operators with high-quality remote recreational experiences including hunting, fishing, canoeing, and camping. Given the large size, remoteness, and relative absence of roads, these areas will play a significant role in protecting wilderness values outside the parks and protected areas system "(OLL LUS, 1999)In addition to provincial and regional policies, the following local policies will apply to the management of Natural lake trout lakes:­ no additional access will be permitted
