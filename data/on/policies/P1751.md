## Land area description

Sextant Rapids Provincial Nature Reserve is located in Pitt Township on the Abitibi River, about 130 kilometres north of Cochrane.The area is notable for its Lamprophyre sills, slabs of volcanic rock that have been injected into Paleozoic rock. The lamprohure sills found here are the youngest known Paleozoic igneous occurrence in the Moose River basin. Nearby sedimentary rock contains fossils.There are no facilities in the park.Sextant Rapids Provincial Nature Reserve was regulated as a provincial park in 1985.This area is subject to the Range Management Policy in Support of Woodland Caribou Conservation and Recovery (2014). The Range Management Policy is accessible via the “Source of Direction.”

## Land use intent

Sextant Rapids Provincial Nature Reserve will be managed according to Provincial Park policy and Interim Management Statement (1985). The parks earth science features will be protected. Recreation opportunities are limited to nature viewing.
