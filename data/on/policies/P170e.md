## Land area description

Mashkinonje Provincial Park is located on the West Arm of Lake Nipissing, approximately 90 kilometres (by road) south-east of Sudbury and 85 kilometres west of North Bay. The park is accessed from Highway 64, which is accessible from either Highway 69 south or Highway 17 East.Mashkinonje Provincial Park remains undeveloped. The rugged landscape consists of vast complex of healthy wetlands and long undulating granite ridge of Pre-Cambrian Shield. These highly metamorphosed gneissic ridges are sheltered with mature Red, White and Jack Pine and blanketed with moss and lichens.Mashkinonje Provincial Park was regulated as a provincial park in 1963.

## Land use intent

Mashkinonje Provincial Park will be managed according to Provincial Park policy and Interim Management Statement (1994). Management of the park will protect its natural and cultural values while offering low-intensity recreational activities such as hiking and ecotourism-based opportunities to complement existing local and regional tourism.
