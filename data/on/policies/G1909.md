## Land area description

This area is northeast of Sault Ste. Marie, generally east of the Canadian National Railway (CNR) and encompasses Ranger and Megisan lakes. Access through the area decreases on a south to north gradient, from being relatively easy in the southern part of the area to almost non-existent in the northern portion. Forest cover also changes in a northerly direction from mostly deciduous to coniferous. Development is minimal in the area and is usually associated with recreational activities. The land use activities presently occurring include resource production (mostly timber extraction and trapping), public recreation and remote commercial tourism. Various forest research areas are located in this area. They are found in LaVerendrye, Gaudette, Hynes, Lamming, Butcher, Handleman, Carton, Jollineau and Plummer Townships and are operated by various government agencies both at the Provincial and Federal levels. Recognition will be made of these sites to reduce possible resource user conflicts.The following are designated as Tourism Lakes: Lodestone, Watson, Forester, Lake24 (Lunkie), Penelope, Sheldon, Snyder and Spike.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The general land use intent for this area is mixed uses reflecting existing forest harvesting, remote commercial tourism and general public recreation. Designated tourist lakes located in this zone include: Spike, Lodestone, Penelope, Snyder, Forester, Lake #24 (Lunkie), Watson and Shelden Lakes. Access planning will have regard for the need to maintain remoteness of all designated tourist lakes. Other land uses will be permitted; however, there will be a reduction of Crown land recreation facilities in this area. Limited Crown land disposition will occur providing no conflicts with resource production activities ties arise. A number of critical fisheries habitat have been identified. Their locations include: the Goulais River, Golf, Nod, LaVerendrye #1, LaVerendrye #2, LaVerendrye #4, LaVerendrye #5, Boundary, Laughing, Upper Laughing Tujack, Shadow, South Branch, Welcome, #1 (Lunkie), Horne, Little Horne, Hodgson, Camel, Lawer, Mirror, Little Saddle, Saddle, and Stag Lakes. All activities in the area will take into consideration the known nesting sites, historical sites and spawning areas. The specific locations for each can be found in a compendium at the District office. A white spruce seed production area is located in Gaudette Township. MNR will not dispose of this land and will protect it against physical disturbance.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Sault Ste. Marie District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
