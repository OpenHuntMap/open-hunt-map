## Land area description

Big Basswood Lake, Bright Lake and Hagen Lake comprise a major recreational area between Thessalon and Iron Bridge. These lakes contain hundreds of cottages, numerous commercial lodges and provide excellent angling, boating and day use opportunities.The management area includes the above three lakes and their backshore areas a distance of approximately 120 metres from the shoreline, except those areas designated under Ontario's Living Legacy.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The intent is to preserve the existing mix of recreational uses in the area, and to maintain the lake trout fishery on Big Basswood Lake.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Blind River District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
