## Land area description

This area includes all built-up urban areas and adjacent lands in Sudbury District (i.e. the City of Greater Sudbury, Cartier, Markstay, Hagar, St. Charles, Appleby Corners, Noelville, Ouellette, Alban, Estaire and Killarney). None of these areas have been considered in the achievement of any Ministry program objectives, with the exception of some fisheries management program efforts in Ramsey and Nepahwin Lakes, the development of a few aggregate deposits on private land near Blezard Valley, and the mineral potential of the Sudbury Nickel Irruptive.A portion of this area is located within the Killarney Great Lakes Heritage Coast Signature Site.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

MNR will continue to support normal urban uses to develop in these areas according to approved official plans, secondary plans, zoning by-laws, zoning orders and planning strategies, as amended. Further, MNR will continue to co-operate with the Greater City of Sudbury to ensure that: aggregate deposits are given priority for pit and quarry operations and related or dependent industrial uses. Other uses, such as agriculture, recreation, wildlife management, etc. which do not preclude future extraction will also be encouraged; the Nickel Irruptive and high and medium mineral potential lands are given priority for mining and mining-related activities. Other uses such as forestry, recreation, wildlife management, etc. which do not preclude future extraction will also be encouraged; in response to heavy sport fishing pressure, Ministry efforts to increase fisheries management in Ramsey and Nepahwin Lakes will continue, wherever feasible; and, existing sport fishing and hunting activities will continue to be encouraged according to regulations.Management of this area is also governed by the general polices contained in Sudbury District Guidelines (1983)
