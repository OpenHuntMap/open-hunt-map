## Land area description

This area is located generally west of Highway 596 and north of Highway 17. There are a number of cottages within the area and timber harvesting is conducted in several locations throughout this portion of the Kenora Forest. Several secondary logging roads and cottage access roads are found throughout the area. The Scot River sensitive area which is important as waterfowl habitat is located in the area. There are presently nine main base lodges and two outposts located in the area.A portion of this area includes the Wabaseemoong Traditional Land Use Area. Consultation will be undertaken with the First Nation respecting Crown Land disposition and other resource use, consistent with the (1983) Ontario Islington Band Agreement.The following are designated as tourism lakes: Catherine, Cygnet, Jadel, Musk and Royal.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The primary uses in this area will be general recreation in association with forest harvesting activities. The emphasis will be towards maintaining existing use. Prior to any development proposal within two km. of the Manitoba border being approved, discussions will be held with the Manitoba Government to avoid potential conflicts and minimize any negative impacts. In addition, no management action will be taken on boundary waters without advisement and discussion with the Manitoba Government.Management of this area is also governed by the Ontario Islington Band Agreement and the Kenora Forest Management Plan.
