## Land area description

Sibbald Point Provincial Park is situated on the south shore of Lake Simcoe in Georgina Township in the Regional Municipality of York. The park is located approximately 40 km northeast of Newmarket and 60 km north of the Greater Toronto Area. The park was home to generations of the Sibbald family for 130 years before becoming a park. Many of the original buildings still remain.Sibbald Point Provincial Park was regulated in 1957.

## Land use intent

This recreational class park is protected by regulation under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act.
