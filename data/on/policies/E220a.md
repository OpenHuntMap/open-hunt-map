## Land area description

Encompasses an area currently being managed as a remote access area including stocked lakes and stable wildlife populations. Access in the area is mainly by snowmobile and ATV and provides remote hunting and angling opportunities for the local tourism industry and public. Forestry and mining are also important. New access is limited to ensure protection of the remote character of the area and its moose population.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Manage this important recreational and resource sector (forestry, mining) area consistent with the enhanced management area (remote access) policies and the existing limited access area provisions of the MNR Espanola District Land Use Guidelines and local resource management plans. Traditional uses such as hiking, camping, canoeing, hunting, angling, trapping, wildlife viewing, etc. will be encouraged. Timber extraction, mineral exploration and aggregate extraction activities will be permitted provided that long term, all season or year round public access is not implored. Minor boundary adjustments may be made to this area through further planning exercises.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Land Use Strategy (1999) and the Espanola District Land Use Guidelines (1983)

## Permitted uses — preface

Remote access enhanced management area are intended to maintain the remote character of selected areas. Typically, these are relatively large areas which provide the public and tourism operators with high-quality remote recreational experiences including hunting, fishing, canoeing, and camping. Given the large size, remoteness, and relative absence of roads, these areas will play a significant role in protecting wilderness values outside the parks and protected areas system.
