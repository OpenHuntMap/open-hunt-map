## Land area description

This area contains the major deer yarding sites in Wildlife Management Units 43 and 44. Technically, WMU 43 is comprised of 2 sub-units, 43a and 43b. It includes large portions of the south shores of Cockburn and Manitoulin Islands plus yards north of Ice Lake, Lake Manitou and one to the west of LaCloche Channel. Most of the land base in this area is patented. The existing uses are, generally, forestry and recreational; some farming and aggregate extraction also occurs. Ministry interest in this area centres around the maintenance of winter habitat for deer; this interest may conflict with the desires of some landowners and municipalities for increased development opportunities.This area is located within the Great Lakes Heritage Coast Signature Site, one of 9 such areas featured in the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999). Signature Sites are identified for their range of natural and recreational values and their potential to contribute to future recreation and tourism.

## Land use intent

The primary management intent of MNR in this area is to maintain viable deer wintering habitat. Most other uses in this area will be supported provided that they consider, incorporate and account for the habitat concern. Some specific development may have to be prohibited because of unacceptable levels of impact. MNR has worked closely with the local municipalities and planning boards over the issue of development control and will continue to do so. Crown land disposition will be, normally, in accordance with municipal official plans and the proper development of existing natural resources. New activity related to aggregate extraction, agriculture, rural residential, commercial fishing and tourism will be in accordance with approved official plans and by-laws and will not be encouraged by this Ministry.
