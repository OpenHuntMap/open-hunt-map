## Land area description

This area receives low intensity recreational use, generally in the Mendelssohn Lake vicinity and along the Cooke Lake Road. The eastern area has good concentration of moose. Mineral potential is moderate to high. Timber locations support local mills.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

Continued resource production (logging and mining) activity will be encouraged. The road network will have to be upgraded and extended to access allocated forest stands. Commercial tourism and recreation are existing uses which will be accommodated. Ministry programs will encourage the continued production of resources. Several lake trout lakes require special consideration in resource extraction proposals. All activities will take into consideration the presence of a known geological features at Anna Lake Cliff.Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Temagami District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
