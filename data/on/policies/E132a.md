## Land area description

This relatively remote area contains high value forest resources and remote access resource tourist operations dependent upon the remote character of the area. The area abuts the existing Ottertail Creek Conservation Reserve and the area governed by the Temagami Land Use Plan. The entire area is unorganized.A small portion of the Enhanced Management Area is in the northwest corner of Parkman Township which was a part of the McConnell Lakes Recreational Area.Greenwood and Richer lakes are recognized as tourist outpost camp lakes.

## Land use intent

Continue to manage this area to retain its remote access characteristic and remote tourism land use, while managing the area for its high quality forest and recreational / tourism values. Resource stewardship agreement to be encouraged between local forest and tourism industries.The remote character of this area will be retained through planning and establishing standards in keeping with the remote access of this area. Specific management direction will be provided for the area located in Parkman Township, formerly part of the McConnell Lakes Recreation Area. Naturally reproducing lake trout fisheries in Opimika Creek will be protected by limiting both licences plus the use of live bait.Any proposed planning on activities adjacent to any conservation reserve will consider the management intent and direction of these protected areas. MNR is responsible for the management of Crown land and natural resources in unorganized townships. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in the Land Use Strategy (1999) and including provisions for McConnell Lakes Plan (1983).

## Permitted uses — preface

Remote access enhanced management areas are intended to maintain the remote character of selected areas. Typically, these are relatively large areas which provide the public and tourism operators with high-quality remote recreational experiences including hunting, fishing, canoeing, and camping. Given the large size, remoteness, and relative absence of roads, these areas will play a significant role in protecting wilderness values outside the parks and protected areas system.
