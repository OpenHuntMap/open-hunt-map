## Land area description

This area is located on the southern end of the Western Peninsula and is bounded on the west by the Ontario-Manitoba border and on the south by the International boundary. This area contains Indian Reserves No. 31-J, 37-B, 34-C and 33-B and the community of Northwest Angle which has an approximate population of 250.Water Management Areas have been removed from this Policy Report area as revisions, to more accurately reflect First Nation reserve lands affected by flooding.

## Land use intent

This area is designated for mixed use recognizing the permanent residential development and potential commercial tourist development on Indian Reserve lands. Development proposals on Crown land will be discussed with the Manitoba Government to avoid potential conflicts and minimize any negative impacts.
