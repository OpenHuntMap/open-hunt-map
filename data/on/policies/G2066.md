## Land area description

This area is located within the Temagami River/Sturgeon River watershed. The values and uses of this area include the mainly young forests and the road areas adjacent to the Lake Temagami Skyline and Cross Lake Management Areas.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Inland Ontario Lakes Designated for Lake Trout Management (2006) accessible in the Source of Direction portion of this policy report.NOTE: Part or all of this Management Area contains lands set aside pending resolution of the Temagami area aboriginal land claim. These lands will not be re-opened for staking and will not be included in the forestry land base.

## Land use intent

The land use intent is to provide opportunities for forestry and mining related activities while mitigating impacts on motorized recreation and remote tourism as well as maintain fishing and hunting opportunities.The concerns include the unauthorized access to Lake Temagami that may occur from this management area. The management area objectives are to ensure no unplanned road/trail development to Lake Temagami or Cross Lake and to ensure a full range of resource extraction activities.The strategies are to ensure public motorized access is subject to road strategies. Access will be planned and road use strategies should be developed to restrict public motorized access to Lake Temagami to minimize conflicts. Strategies also include applying viewscape and Area of Concern planning to extractive activities.

## Permitted uses — preface

Land use and resource management activities for this area are also governed by the policies of the Temagami Land Use Plan (TLUP) (1997). The policies from the TLUP are integrated into this area report. Refer to the TLUP for background information and additional details.
