## Land area description

This area is composed largely of the highway corridor east and south of Kirkland Lake. The corridor contains a population of about 15,000 residents and includes parts of four municipalities, namely the Township of Gauthier, the Township of Larder Lake, the Township of McGarry and the Town of Kirkland Lake. The area was settled because of its mineral potential and the mining industry continues to be a major employer. The major recreation developments are two municipal parks, two ski areas, three commercial campgrounds and various snowmobile and cross country ski trails. Timber harvesting and regeneration are ongoing activities. Areas allocated for harvesting occur throughout the area. The Town of Kirkland Lake has a sanitary control order for the Gull-McTavish Lakes drainage basin. The intent of this order is to protect the Town's water supply. There are five earth science representation sites, and two cultural-historic sites in the area.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The intent for this area is to provide opportunities for industrial development, particularly mineral development, and for urban development. Secondary activities would be general recreation, tourism, forestry and aggregate extraction. Industrial development will be encouraged to locate within or adjacent to existing urban areas. Land use activities will be in conformity with local official plans and zoning by-laws. Structural development will be discouraged on hazard lands through Ministry input to official plans and zoning by-laws and through review of specific development proposals. The Ministry will support the prevention of unnecessary restriction to mineral exploration and development. Urban development allowed. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Kirkland Lake District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
