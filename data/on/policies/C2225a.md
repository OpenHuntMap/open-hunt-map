## Land area description

Gravel River Conservation Reserve Addition is located approximately 40 kilometers east of Nipigon. The addition has similar characteristics to those of the adjacent Gravel River Conservation Reserve which contains a major geologic fault structure that defines the rugged terrain with extensive uplands and mixed boreal forests and the Gravel River, an important coldwater fishery that follows the geologic fault. Cavers, Cloutier, Upper Pays Plat and McGoey Lakes are tourism lakes wholly contained within the Conservation Reserve. Sinclair, Chance, Greenhedge, Pays Plat and a portion of Dickinson Lakes are tourism lakes that form the boundary of the Conservation Reserve. This area is located within the Great Lakes Heritage Coast Signature Site, one of 9 such areas featured in the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999). Signature Sites are identified for their range of natural and recreational values and their potential to contribute to future recreation and tourism.Gravel River was regulated as a conservation reserve on May 8, 2002.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The intent is to regulate this area as a Conservation Reserve.Gravel River Conservation Reserve Addition is located in an area in which one or more First Nations have asserted Aboriginal rights and title, including an unextinguished Aboriginal right to exclusive use and occupancy of the land. The First Nations’ claims are the subject of litigation and negotiations. Dispositions in Gravel River Conservation Reserve Addition may be subject to heightened Crown consultation and accommodation obligations.This may mean a greater degree of engagement with First Nation communities asserting rights and title prior to a decision regarding potential authorizations or dispositions of public lands or resources. For a map of areas affected by these claims, please go to the Notice of Caution on the Mining Lands Administration System (MLAS) at https://www.geologyontario.mndm.gov.on.ca/mines/lands/withreop/notices_e.html .
