## Land area description

The Valhalla -Trough Lake Dedicated Protected Area originates at the headwaters of the Chukuni River,which flow south through the community in Red Lake, and stretches to the northeast corner of WoodlandCaribou Provincial Park. The Trough Lake Patterned Peatlands are a provincially significant life sciencefeature included in the northern portion of the Dedicated Protected Area. This lowland habitat supportsblack spruce dominated bog, tamarack and sedge dominated fen, and open sphagnum bog. Anothervaluable earth science feature of this dedicated protected area is a series of northwest-southeasttrending, low De Geer moraine ridges. These ridges formed in Lake Agassiz where it abutted the icefrontand today are covered by jack pine dominated forest interspersed with lowland black spruce swampor treed bog. Mature black spruce dominated forest and occasional expanses of bare bedrock also occuron uplands throughout the Valhalla -Trough Lake Dedicated Protected Area. First Nation people conductvarious traditional activities on this land for livelihood and cultural purposes. This area can supportscientific research, and nature appreciation.Development within the Valhalla - Trough Lake Dedicated Protected Area currently consists of a touristoutpost camp, trap cabins, and boat caches. Management boundaries relevant to the area includebaitfish blocks, bear management areas, traplines, and a wildlife management unit. The ValhallaRiver/Trough Lake Dedicated Protected Area provides woodland caribou wintering habitat, calving sites,and refuge habitat. This area is also used as a travel corridor by caribou.

## Land use intent

The intent is to regulate the Valhalla -Trough Lake Dedicated Protected Area as a provincial park orconservation reserve, although the specific designation and category have not been determined. .Thisarea can be designed to incorporate outstanding recreational water routes with representative naturalfeatures and historical resources to provide high quality recreational and educational experiences.Management will also be governed by the Land Use Strategy for the Whitefeather Forest and AdjacentAreas, MNR policies, and/or new policies developed through cross-cultural dialogue.

## Permitted uses — preface

The dedicated protected area designation (i.e. Park Class or Conservation Reserve) for Valhalla -TroughLake is yet to be determined. Interim direction for this area is provided in the Land Use Strategy for theWhitefeather Forest and Adjacent Areas (WFAA Strategy) and in the table below. Additional specificdirection will be determined by the final designation assigned to this dedicated protected area throughcross-cultural dialogue, and by future resource management planning.In the absence of resource management planning, new proposals for specific activities will be subject to areview by MNR in consultation with Pikangikum First Nation. This review will consider the intent of thearea, direction provided in the WFAA Strategy, impacts on resources and values associated with theValhalla - Trough Lake Dedicated Protected Area, compatible economic opportunities, and potential foreconomic benefit to Pikangikum First Nation.Values associated with the Valhalla - Trough Lake Dedicated Protected Area include the natural andcultural heritage features of the area, special features, and existing land uses. Maintenance of naturaldisturbance regimes and remoteness will be given high importance as will the conservation of fish andwildlife species, including woodland caribou, and wolverine.
