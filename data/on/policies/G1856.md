## Land area description

This area is accessed by Highway 101. The main features of the area are Perry Lake and Munro Lake. Both lakes have heavy concentrations of cottages, about 70 lots on Munro Lake and 50 lots on Perry Lake. Both lakes have an official access point. There is a tourist lodge on Perry Lake and it is stocked with lake trout. Both lakes are popular recreation areas for local residents and tourists. Mineral potential is high in this area and exploration is ongoing. The area also has high aggregate potential. Some timber harvesting will occur in the general area of Perry Lake. Portions of the Munro Lake area are very sandy. Erosion problems arise when the duff layer is damaged by such factors as fire or heavy recreational or extractive uses. Conflicts also may arise between recreation users and resource extraction concerns and between different recreational user groups.This area contains lake(s) designated for lake trout management. For a current list of designated lakes associated with this area, refer to the Lake Trout Lake Search Index accessible via the ‘Source of Direction’ portion of this policy report.

## Land use intent

The intent in this area is to provide cottaging and general recreation including tourism opportunities. Mineral exploration, timber harvesting and aggregate extraction are secondary activities. Modified forest management will be used within 120 metres of the lakes in the area. All development proposals or plans will be reviewed with regard for the potential erosion problems in the Munro Lake area. Management of this area is also governed by the general policies contained in Kirkland Lake District Land Use Guidelines (1983).
