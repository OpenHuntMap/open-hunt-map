## Land area description

Regulated under the Provincial Parks and Conservation Reserves Act, Upper Madawaska River Provincial Park is classified as a Waterway park. Situated in the Algonquin Highland physiographic region of the southern Canadian Shield. The rocky shorelines are commonly vegetated by alder, winterberry, holly and other various shrubs and black ash forests.Non operating park administrated by Ontario Parks.

## Land use intent

Originally identified in the 1983 Bancroft District Land Use Guidelines as a candidate Provincial Park.To protect the river and its associated natural and cultural features and to provide a variety of quality outdoor recreational experiences. Presently, four seasonal hunt camps, one cottage, a commercial tourist camp and one trapping cabin exist.

## Permitted uses — preface

Management of this provincial park is carried out in accordance with Ontario Provincial Parks: Planning and Management Policies (1992), the Ontario’s Living Legacy Land Use Strategy (1999) and the Interim Management Statement, where available. The Interim Management Statement provides direction to ensure custodial management of park resources until such time as a park management planning process is conducted, which includes public review of proposals. At that time, significant decisions regarding resource stewardship, operations, development and permitted uses are made.
